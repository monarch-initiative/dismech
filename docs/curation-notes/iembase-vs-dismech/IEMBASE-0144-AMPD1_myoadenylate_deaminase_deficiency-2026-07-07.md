## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents this as AMPD1-related myoadenylate deaminase deficiency, with
alternate label adenosine monophosphate deaminase deficiency and abbreviation
AMPD. Treatability is marked yes.

increased plasma creatine kinase, and decreased plasma ammonia response during
ischemic muscle exercise testing. Clinical rows include exercise intolerance,
muscle cramps, and an acquired or associated form in neuromuscular or
rheumatologic disorders.
