## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents this as HYAL1-related hyaluronidase deficiency, abbreviated
MPS IX. The record is autosomal recessive and treatability is marked unknown,
with no treatment rows in the cached JSON.

Biochemical rows include decreased hyaluronidase activity in plasma, increased
urinary hyaluronic acid, and normal total urinary glycosaminoglycans. The
clinical section has no additional rows in the cached JSON. Characteristic rows
include cutaneous nodules, cutaneous swelling, degenerative hip dysplasia,
joint contractures, and scoliosis.
