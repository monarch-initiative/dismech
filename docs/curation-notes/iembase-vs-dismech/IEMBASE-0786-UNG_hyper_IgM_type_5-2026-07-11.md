## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase labels this autosomal recessive record as UNG-related uracil-DNA
glycosylase deficiency, with alternate name immunodeficiency with hyper-IgM type
5 and abbreviation HIGM5. The phenotype signal resembles AICDA/HIGM2:
recurrent bacterial infections, lymphoid hyperplasia, giant germinal centers in
lymph nodes, impaired immunoglobulin class-switch recombination, low IgG, low
IgA, low IgE, normal B-cell counts, and normal or increased IgM.
