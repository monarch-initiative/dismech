## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents this as SLC39A4-related acrodermatitis enteropathica, with
alternate labels zinc-deficiency type and AEZ/AE. Treatability is marked yes.

The biochemical rows include decreased or low-normal serum zinc, decreased
alkaline phosphatase, and decreased duodenal zinc uptake. Characteristic
clinical rows include alopecia, anorexia, dermatitis, diarrhea, failure to
thrive, and irritability. Additional rows include apathy, depression, and
recurrent infections. The treatment row lists zinc supplementation increasing
serum zinc.
