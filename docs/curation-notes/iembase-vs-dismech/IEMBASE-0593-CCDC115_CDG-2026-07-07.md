## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents CCDC115-related congenital disorder of glycosylation,
also labelled CCDC115-CDG and CDG-IIo. The record is autosomal recessive,
classified under disorders of multiple glycosylation pathways, has unknown
treatability, and has no treatment rows.

Biochemical rows include very increased plasma alkaline phosphatase, increased
plasma transaminases, apolipoprotein CIII hypoglycosylation, serum
sialotransferrin type 2 pattern, decreased serum ceruloplasmin, increased serum
cholesterol, and increased plasma LDL cholesterol. Clinical rows include
behavioral disorder, cholestasis, facial dysmorphism, hypotonia, liver failure,
long face, ptosis, seizures, hepatosplenomegaly, and psychomotor delay.
