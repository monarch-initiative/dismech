## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents TMEM199-related congenital disorder of glycosylation /
CDG-IIp as an autosomal recessive disorder with unknown treatability and no
treatment rows.

Biochemical rows include markedly increased alkaline phosphatase from neonatal
life through adolescence, increased transaminases through adolescence with
normal adult values, type 2 sialotransferrin pattern, possible apolipoprotein
CIII hypoglycosylation, and decreased serum ceruloplasmin. The only clinical
row is optional childhood hepatomegaly.
