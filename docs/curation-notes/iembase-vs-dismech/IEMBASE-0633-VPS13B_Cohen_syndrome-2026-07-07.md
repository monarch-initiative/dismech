## IEMbase phenotype signal

VPS13B-CDG as an autosomal recessive disorder with unknown treatability and no
treatment rows.

Biochemical rows include increased serum agalactosotransferrin and
asialotransferrin in adolescence/adulthood with normal serum transferrin.

optional neutropenia, chorioretinal degeneration, joint laxity, microcephaly,
and obesity.
