## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents autosomal recessive COG5-CDG, also listed as CDG-IIi and
COG5-related conserved oligomeric Golgi complex subunit 5 deficiency.

Clinical rows include abnormal ApoC-III isoelectrofocusing, ataxia, blindness,
deafness, dwarfism, hypotonia, psychomotor delay, strabismus, transferrin
glycan hyposialylation, cerebellar and cerebral atrophy, dry scaly skin,
epilepsy, finger flexion contractures, hepatomegaly, microcephaly, neurogenic
bladder, and scoliosis. Biochemical rows include increased asialotransferrin,
disialotransferrin, monosialotransferrin, trisialotransferrin, type II
sialotransferrins, and ApoC-III hypoglycosylation, with decreased
tetrasialotransferrin.
