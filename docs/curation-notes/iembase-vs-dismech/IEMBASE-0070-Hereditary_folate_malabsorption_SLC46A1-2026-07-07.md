## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents this as autosomal recessive SLC46A1-related proton-coupled
folate transporter deficiency, with alternate labels hereditary folate
malabsorption and HFM. Treatability is marked yes.

The characteristic biochemical signal includes abnormal 5-methyltetrahydrofolic
acid in CSF, abnormal plasma folate, and abnormal plasma homocysteine. Other lab
rows include urinary FIGLU, immunoglobulins, and plasma cobalamin.

Characteristic clinical rows include megaloblastic anemia, ataxia, neurologic
deterioration, and recurrent infections. Additional rows include diarrhea,
failure to thrive, heart failure, intellectual disability, movement disorder,
peripheral neuropathy, pancytopenia, seizures, and stomatitis. The treatment row
is levofolinic or folinic acid.
