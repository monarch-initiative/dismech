## IEMbase phenotype signal

sterol C5-desaturase deficiency and SC5D. Treatability is marked yes.

and childhood periods. Characteristic clinical rows include broad alveolar
ridges, cataracts, growth retardation, hypotonia, microcephaly, micrognathia,
and toe syndactyly affecting 2-3 or 2-4 toes. Additional rows include anteverted
nares, bitemporal narrowing, cleft palate, clubfoot, corneal clouding,
hematuria, hypospadias, intellectual disability, postaxial polydactyly, and
ptosis. No treatment rows are listed despite the treatability flag.
