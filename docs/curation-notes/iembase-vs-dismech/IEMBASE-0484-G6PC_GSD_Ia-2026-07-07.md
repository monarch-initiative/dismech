## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents autosomal recessive G6PC-related glucose-6-phosphatase
deficiency as GSD Ia / von Gierke disease. Treatments are fasting avoidance,
uncooked cornstarch, and liver transplantation. Biochemical rows include
decreased hepatic glucose-6-phosphatase activity, increased liver glycogen,
decreased fasting plasma glucose, increased fasting plasma and urine lactate,
increased cholesterol, triglycerides, and uric acid, low fasting ketones,
increased or normal transaminases, and increased biotinidase. Clinical rows
include doll-like adiposity, bleeding tendency, delayed tooth eruption,
diarrhea, glomerulosclerosis, hyperfiltration, liver adenoma/carcinoma,
osteopenia, pancreatitis, pulmonary hypertension, renal enlargement, short
stature, tachypnea, and taurodontism.
