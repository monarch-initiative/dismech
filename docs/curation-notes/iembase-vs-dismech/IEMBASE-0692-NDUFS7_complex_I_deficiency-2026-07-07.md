## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents autosomal recessive NDUFS7-related NADH dehydrogenase
iron-sulfur protein 7 deficiency, also labeled mitochondrial complex I
deficiency, nuclear type 3.

Biochemical rows include decreased fibroblast complex I activity and increased
plasma lactate from neonatal through childhood ages. Clinical rows include
ataxia, epilepsy, feeding difficulties, lactic acidosis, liver dysfunction,
myopathy, and characteristic cardiomyopathy, encephalopathy, and Leigh syndrome.
