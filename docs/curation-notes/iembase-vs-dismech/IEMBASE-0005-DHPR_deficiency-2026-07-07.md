## IEMbase phenotype signal

Characteristic clinical features include axial hypotonia, basal ganglia
calcifications on CT, intellectual disability, and myoclonic seizures.
Additional features include choreoathetosis, cortical/subcortical atrophy,
drooling, abnormal EEG with generalized slowing or spike-wave discharges,
hypopigmented hair, microcephaly, pneumonia, psychomotor delay, eczematous rash,
tonic-clonic seizures, sudden death, and temperature instability.

The biochemical pattern includes increased phenylalanine, low CSF 5-HIAA and
homovanillic acid, low DHPR activity in dried blood spots, BH4 loading response,
increased biopterin and BH2, generally normal neopterin, and increased
prolactin.

Treatments listed are sapropterin/BH4, phenylalanine-reduced diet,
levodopa/carbidopa, 5-hydroxytryptophan, and folinic acid.
