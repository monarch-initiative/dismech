## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase labels this autosomal recessive record as AMPD2-related adenosine
monophosphate deaminase deficiency, with alternate name pontocerebellar
hypoplasia type 9 (severe) and abbreviation PCH9. The phenotype signal includes
microcephaly, seizures, psychomotor delay or retardation, dysmorphic features,
midface hypoplasia, broad nasal bridge, short upper lip, and abnormally shaped
ears.
