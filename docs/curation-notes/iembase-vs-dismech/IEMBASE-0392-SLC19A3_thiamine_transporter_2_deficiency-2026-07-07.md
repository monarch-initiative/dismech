## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents autosomal recessive SLC19A3-related thiamine transporter 2
deficiency, with alternate names Wernicke-like encephalopathy and BRBG,
biotin and thiamine basal ganglia disease, and SLC19A3.

Characteristic clinical rows include basal ganglia MRI lesions, acute
encephalopathic crisis, and focal epilepsy. Additional rows include dystonia and
intractable epilepsy. Biochemical rows include normal-to-increased lactate,
CSF lactate, alanine, urinary 2-ketoglutaric acid, pyruvate, hemoglobin, and
plasma vitamin B1/thiamine. Treatment rows list thiamine and biotin.
