## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents SLC35A1-CDG/CDG-IIf, caused by CMP-sialic acid transporter
deficiency. Characteristic rows include ataxia, bleeding tendency, epilepsy,
intellectual disability, and macrothrombocytopenia. Additional clinical rows
include behavioral disorder, hypotonia, and microcephaly.

The biochemical rows include serum N-glycans, type II sialotransferrins, and
altered sialylation of platelet glycoproteins. No treatment rows are present.
