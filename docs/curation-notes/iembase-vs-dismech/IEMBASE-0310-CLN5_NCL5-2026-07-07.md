## Concordance and completeness

local coverage is only partial through the broad NCL umbrella.

The umbrella file agrees with IEMbase on CLN5 being an NCL gene and on the
shared NCL phenotype scaffold: visual/retinal disease, seizures, regression,
myoclonus, motor deterioration, and lysosomal storage biology. It does not
capture CLN5-specific disease identity, late-infantile CLN5 natural history,
optic atrophy, speech abnormality, MRI/electrophysiology findings, dystonia,
spasticity, or storage-material ultrastructure as CLN5-specific rows.
