## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents PIGG-related glycosylphosphatidylinositol biosynthesis
defect 13 / PIGG-CDG / autosomal recessive mental retardation 53 as an
autosomal recessive disorder with unknown treatability and no treatment rows.

represented as normal in infancy/childhood. Clinical and characteristic rows

and intellectual disability.
