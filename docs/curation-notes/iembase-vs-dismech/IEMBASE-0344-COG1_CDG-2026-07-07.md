## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents COG1-CDG/CDG-IIg, an autosomal recessive conserved
oligomeric Golgi complex disorder. Characteristic rows include abnormal
apolipoprotein C-III isoelectrofocusing, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy,
dwarfism, facial dysmorphism, microcephaly, psychomotor delay, transferrin
hypogalactosylation, transferrin hyposialylation, and a type II

disialotransferrin, monosialotransferrin, trisialotransferrin, decreased

apolipoprotein CIII. No treatment rows are present.
