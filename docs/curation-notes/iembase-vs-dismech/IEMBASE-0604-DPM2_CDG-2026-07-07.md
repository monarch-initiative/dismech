## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase represents DPM2-related dolichol-P-mannose synthase-2 deficiency,
labelled DPM2-CDG. The record is autosomal recessive, classified under
disorders of multiple glycosylation pathways, has unknown treatability, and has
no treatment rows.

Biochemical rows include increased creatine kinase, increased transaminases,
serum sialotransferrin type 1 pattern, and increased dolichol-linked
Man5GlcNAc2. Clinical rows include muscular dystrophy, hypotonia, epilepsy,
cerebral atrophy on MRI, microcephaly, hepatomegaly, dysmorphic features, joint
contractures, scoliosis, strabismus, respiratory distress, and recurrent
respiratory infections.
