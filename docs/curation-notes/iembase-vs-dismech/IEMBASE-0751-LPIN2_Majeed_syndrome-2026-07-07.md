## IEMbase phenotype signal

IEMbase labels this autosomal recessive record as LPIN2-related lipin 2
deficiency, with alternate name Majeed syndrome. The phenotype signal combines
inflammatory bone disease, anemia, and skin findings: recurrent fever, bone
pain, psoriasiform dermatitis, short stature, cholestatic or episodic jaundice,
neutropenia, microcytic hypochromic anemia, dyserythropoietic anemia, and
hepatosplenomegaly. Biochemical and laboratory rows include elevated
transaminases, elevated erythrocyte sedimentation rate, low hemoglobin, and
normal serum iron.
