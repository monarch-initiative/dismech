Title: Request for new term WAPL-related developmental disorder

### Label

WAPL-related developmental disorder

### Synonyms

WAPL-related disorder

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A disorder featuring developmental delay, intellectual disability and risk of other developmental anomalies, caused by heterozygous damaging variants in WAPL, which encodes a cohesin release factor. [PMID:42431198]

### Parent term

MONDO:0100500 Mendelian neurodevelopmental disorder

### Children term(s)

None proposed.

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has no class linked to WAPL. `MONDO:0012830` chromosome 10q23 deletion syndrome is the closest, and the cited study nominates WAPL as a driver gene in that region, but a single-gene disease is a different concept from a contiguous gene deletion. Gene: WAPL.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- 27 individuals with heterozygous predicted damaging variants, with human cell and mouse models (PMID:42431198).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0012830` chromosome 10q23 deletion syndrome
- `MONDO:0016033` Cornelia de Lange syndrome

**Search.** No matching label, synonym or cross-reference in Mondo release 2026-11-03, and no matching open or closed issue.

**Context.** Requested from curation of the [dismech](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech) knowledge base, where `WAPL-Related_Developmental_Disorder` currently has no `disease_term`.
