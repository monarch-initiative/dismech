### Label

amatoxin poisoning

### Synonyms

amatoxin syndrome, phalloides syndrome, cyclopeptide mushroom poisoning

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A poisoning that is caused by ingestion of mushrooms containing amatoxins, which occur in about 35 species of the genera Amanita, Galerina and Lepiota. A gastrointestinal phase of abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting and watery diarrhea begins 6 to 24 hours after ingestion. Amanitins are carried into hepatocytes by organic anion transporting polypeptides and cause extensive centrolobular necrosis. [PMID:12475187, PMID:39684738, PMID:16495352]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has no mushroom poisoning class of any kind. This is the form that matters most: amatoxin-containing species account for more than 90% of deaths from mushroom poisoning (PMID:17503263).

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Clinical outcome studies: PMID:15664245, PMID:22352731, PMID:41378447.
- The hepatic uptake transporter is identified as OATP1B3 (PMID:16495352).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0043230` ciguatera fish poisoning
- `MONDO:0019140` acute ackee fruit intoxication
- `MONDO:0035511` ricin poisoning

**Poisoning design pattern.** Suggested stimulus: `CHEBI:37415` alpha-amanitin (ChEBI has no term labelled amatoxin).
