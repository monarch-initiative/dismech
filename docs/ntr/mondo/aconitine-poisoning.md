### Label

aconitine poisoning

### Synonyms

aconite poisoning, Aconitum poisoning

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A poisoning that is caused by ingestion of aconitine and related alkaloids from Aconitum species, which are cardiotoxins and neurotoxins. It follows accidental ingestion of the wild plant or use of herbal preparations of aconite root, with higher risk from inadequately processed roots, large doses and tinctures. Aconitine suppresses the inactivation of voltage-dependent sodium channels. [PMID:19514874, PMID:15111916, PMID:9760702]
