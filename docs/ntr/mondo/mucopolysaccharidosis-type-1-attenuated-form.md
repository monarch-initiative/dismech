### Label

mucopolysaccharidosis type 1, attenuated form

### Synonyms

attenuated MPS I, attenuated mucopolysaccharidosis type I

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

The less severe forms of mucopolysaccharidosis type 1, comprising the Hurler-Scheie and Scheie phenotypes of autosomal recessive alpha-L-iduronidase deficiency caused by biallelic IDUA variants. [PMID:20301341, PMID:32764324, PMID:31194252]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo already makes this exact distinction for type 2, with `MONDO:0016316` mucopolysaccharidosis type 2, attenuated form, but not for type 1. Clinicians and published cohorts routinely classify MPS I patients as severe or attenuated.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- A genotype-phenotype study classified patients as severe or attenuated (PMID:31194252).
- The subtypes range from mild (Scheie) through moderate (Hurler-Scheie) to severe (Hurler) (PMID:32764324).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0016316` mucopolysaccharidosis type 2, attenuated form
- `MONDO:0016315` mucopolysaccharidosis type 2, severe form
