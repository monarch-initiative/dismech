### Label

Usutu virus disease

### Synonyms

Usutu virus infection

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A mosquito-borne infection of humans caused by Usutu virus, a flavivirus of the Japanese encephalitis serocomplex. Infection is mainly asymptomatic, and less often causes a febrile illness or neuroinvasive disease, including meningoencephalitis. [PMID:37589592, PMID:32858963, PMID:40694555]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has the veterinary term `MONDO:1015078` usutu virus infection, non-human animal, but no human counterpart, although human disease has been reported.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Clinical cases of neuroinvasive disease and Usutu fever, and seroconversion in blood donors, have been reported in Europe since 2009 (PMID:32858963).
- Human infections are reported in several EU/EEA countries (PMID:37589592).
