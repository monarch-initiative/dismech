### Label

carbamate poisoning

### Synonyms

carbamate intoxication, carbamate insecticide poisoning

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A poisoning that is caused by exposure to a carbamate insecticide. Carbamates inhibit acetylcholinesterase, leading to excess acetylcholine at nerve terminals and a cholinergic crisis. Unlike organophosphate poisoning, the enzyme does not undergo aging. [PMID:25455666, PMID:26563788, PMID:27252029]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has organophosphate and nerve agent poisoning but nothing for carbamates. The two insecticide classes share a target and differ in reversibility and in subacute and chronic effects (PMID:25455666), so organophosphate poisoning cannot stand in for this one.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- A prospective study followed 1,288 patients self-poisoned with carbamate insecticides at six Sri Lankan hospitals (PMID:27252029).

- `MONDO:0100331` nerve agent poisoning
