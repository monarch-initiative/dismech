### Label

diethylene glycol poisoning

### Synonyms

DEG poisoning, diethylene glycol intoxication

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A poisoning that is caused by ingestion of diethylene glycol, typically in outbreaks where it has contaminated a liquid medicine. Patients may present with gastrointestinal symptoms and anion gap metabolic acidosis, followed by renal failure and, later, encephalopathy and neuropathy, which can include lower motor neuron facial palsy. [PMID:18949211, PMID:37186212, PMID:25170933, PMID:15902788]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has ethylene glycol poisoning but not this agent, and the two should not be merged. In rats, the main urinary metabolite of diethylene glycol is 2-hydroxyethoxyacetic acid, with minor diglycolic acid (PMID:20530232), and diglycolic acid accumulation tracks with kidney injury (PMID:33475432). This differs from the oxalate route of ethylene glycol.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Outbreak with laboratory confirmation: diethylene glycol in patient samples and 8% contamination of cough syrup (PMID:18949211).
- Further human series: PMID:15902788, PMID:22246572, PMID:25170933.
