### Label

spaceflight associated neuro-ocular syndrome

### Synonyms

SANS

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A syndrome of neurologic and ophthalmic findings in astronauts during and after long-duration spaceflight, clinically defined by the development of optic disc edema during prolonged exposure to microgravity. Findings also include globe flattening, choroidal folds, cotton wool spots and retinal nerve fiber layer thickening. Proposed causes are elevated intracranial pressure and compartmentalization of cerebrospinal fluid. [PMID:37072472, PMID:31162175, PMID:21849212, PMID:33117025]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has `MONDO:0003147` space motion sickness, so spaceflight-caused conditions are already in scope, but not this better-characterized syndrome. A more specific parent such as `MONDO:0002135` optic nerve disorder may be preferable.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- The first series reported ophthalmic findings in 7 astronauts after 6 months in space (PMID:21849212).
- In-flight imaging documented choroidal thickening in all tested astronauts (PMID:32047839).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0003147` space motion sickness
- `MONDO:0006625` altitude sickness
- `MONDO:0020797` decompression sickness
