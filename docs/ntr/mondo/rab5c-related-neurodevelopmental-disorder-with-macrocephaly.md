### Label

RAB5C-related neurodevelopmental disorder with macrocephaly

### Synonyms

_No response_

### Synonym type

None

### Definition

An autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental disorder caused by heterozygous de novo missense variants in RAB5C, characterized by macrocephaly and mild-to-moderate developmental delay through disruption of the endocytic pathway. [PMID:37552066]

### Parent term

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has no class linked to RAB5C. The cited report also describes two patients with loss-of-function variants, who had refractory epilepsy and intellectual disability with normal head circumference; this definition covers the missense presentation only. Gene: RAB5C.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- 12 individuals with nine different de novo variants, supported by in vitro and in vivo studies (PMID:37552066).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0700368` DDX17-related neurodevelopmental disorder
- `MONDO:0800477` SETD2-related neurodevelopmental disorder without or with macrocephaly/overgrowth
