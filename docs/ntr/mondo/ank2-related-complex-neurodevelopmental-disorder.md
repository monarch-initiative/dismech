Title: Request for new term ANK2-related complex neurodevelopmental disorder

### Label

ANK2-related complex neurodevelopmental disorder

### Synonyms

ANK2-related neurodevelopmental disorder

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

An autosomal dominant complex neurodevelopmental disorder caused by heterozygous loss-of-function variants in ANK2, comprising intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorders and early onset epilepsy. [PMID:37195288]

### Parent term

MONDO:0100038 complex neurodevelopmental disorder

### Children term(s)

None proposed.

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** ClinGen classifies ANK2 and complex neurodevelopmental disorder as Definitive, autosomal dominant (2020-10-21), recorded against the general class `MONDO:0100038`. The only ANK2 class in Mondo is `MONDO:0010958` cardiac arrhythmia, ankyrin-B-related, a different disease. Gene: ANK2.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- 12 individuals with heterozygous de novo loss-of-function variants (PMID:37195288).
- A further family is reported in PMID:39962910.

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0100254` CACNA1A-related complex neurodevelopmental disorder
- `MONDO:0700350` GRIN2B-related complex neurodevelopmental disorder

**Search.** No matching label, synonym or cross-reference in Mondo release 2026-11-03, and no matching open or closed issue.

**Context.** Requested from curation of the [dismech](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech) knowledge base, where `ANK2_Related_Complex_Neurodevelopmental_Disorder` currently has no `disease_term`.
