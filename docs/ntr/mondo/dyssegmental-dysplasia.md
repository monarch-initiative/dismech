### Label

dyssegmental dysplasia

### Synonyms

dyssegmental dwarfism

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A severe autosomal recessive skeletal dysplasia comprising two subtypes, the lethal Silverman-Handmaker type and the nonlethal Rolland-Desbuquois type, caused by biallelic variants in HSPG2. [PMID:38424183, PMID:3605216, PMID:11279527]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** The literature uses this name for the disorder as a whole, but Mondo has only its two subtypes. Separately, `MONDO:0009140` is under `MONDO:0016151` neuromuscular disease caused by qualitative or quantitative defects of perlecan and `MONDO:0009139` is not, which you may wish to review.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Two autosomal recessive forms were delineated on clinical, radiographic and morphologic grounds (PMID:3605216).
- Fewer than forty cases were reported as of PMID:29526034.

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0009139` dyssegmental dysplasia, Rolland-Desbuquois type
