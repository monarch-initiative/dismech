### Label

FLNC-related dilated cardiomyopathy

### Synonyms

FLNC truncating variant cardiomyopathy

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A familial dilated cardiomyopathy with autosomal dominant inheritance caused by truncating variants in FLNC, which encodes filamin C. It is associated with a reportedly high risk of ventricular arrhythmia. [PMID:27908349, PMID:33978673, PMID:32112656]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** ClinGen classifies FLNC and dilated cardiomyopathy as Definitive, autosomal dominant (assertion dated 2025-05-30), but has to record it against the general `MONDO:0005021` dilated cardiomyopathy. Mondo has FLNC-specific classes for other phenotypes but none for this one. `MONDO:0016189` covers the filaminopathies as a group and is not a cardiomyopathy class. Gene: FLNC.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Truncating variants cosegregated with disease with a combined LOD score of 9.5 (PMID:27908349).
- FLNC variants are now among the more prevalent causes of genetic dilated cardiomyopathy (PMID:32112656).

- `MONDO:0007269` dilated cardiomyopathy 1A
