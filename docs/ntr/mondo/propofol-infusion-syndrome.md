### Label

propofol infusion syndrome

### Synonyms

PRIS, propofol-related infusion syndrome

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A rare, often fatal drug toxicity of propofol infusion, characterized by acute refractory bradycardia leading to asystole in the presence of one or more of metabolic acidosis, rhabdomyolysis, hyperlipidemia, and enlarged or fatty liver. It is associated with doses above 4 mg/kg/h for more than 48 hours. Proposed causes are mitochondrial respiratory chain inhibition or impaired mitochondrial fatty acid metabolism. [PMID:17567345, PMID:20544610]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** The syndrome has a published clinical definition and a known culprit drug but no Mondo class. Drug toxicities such as `MONDO:0033170` statin toxicity sit under `poisoning`, so I have proposed the same parent; please redirect if another parent fits better.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- A review of 108 publications documented 168 cases (PMID:30857601).
- A prospective estimate of incidence slightly above 1% in critically ill patients on propofol (PMID:19874582).
- Most reported cases arose during ICU sedation, with 10% during anaesthesia (PMID:26558513).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0033170` statin toxicity
- `MONDO:0100335` local anesthetic poisoning
- `MONDO:0019790` neuroleptic malignant syndrome

**Poisoning design pattern.** Suggested stimulus: `CHEBI:44915` propofol.
