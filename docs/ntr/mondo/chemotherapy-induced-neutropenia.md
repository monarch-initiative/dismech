### Label

chemotherapy-induced neutropenia

None

### Definition

Neutropenia caused by cancer chemotherapy, resulting from damage to proliferating hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells in the bone marrow. It is a major risk factor for infection-related morbidity and mortality and a significant dose-limiting toxicity in cancer treatment. [PMID:34307165, PMID:31504118, PMID:21095116]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** `MONDO:0001475` neutropenia covers inherited forms such as `MONDO:0015134` constitutional neutropenia, which makes it a poor stand-in for a drug toxicity. These existing terms are classified under their organ-level disease rather than under `MONDO:0800390` chemotherapy-induced toxicity, so you may wish to review that branch. Mondo previously held `MONDO:0015822` acquired neutropenia, now obsolete; this request is for a narrower, drug-caused concept, and please tell us if the reason for that obsoletion applies here too.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Pharmacogenetic risk is well documented, for example a 3.4-fold higher risk of grade IV neutropenia with partial DPD deficiency (PMID:12209976).
- Trilaciclib reduced the duration and occurrence of severe neutropenia (PMID:31504118).
