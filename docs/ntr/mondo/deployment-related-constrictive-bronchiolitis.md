### Label

deployment-related constrictive bronchiolitis

### Synonyms

DRCB

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

Constrictive bronchiolitis in military personnel with inhalational exposures during service in Iraq and Afghanistan, presenting as exertional dyspnea and diagnosed by lung biopsy. Dyspnea persists on long-term follow-up, with a decline in total lung capacity. [PMID:21774710, PMID:34081035]

### Parent term

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo divides bronchiolitis obliterans by cause (transplant, post-infectious, toxic), and this is a named cause-specific form with its own literature.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Of 49 soldiers who underwent lung biopsy, 38 had changes diagnostic of constrictive bronchiolitis (PMID:21774710).
- Follow-up of 29 soldiers over 1 to 15 years showed persistent dyspnea in all (PMID:34081035).
