### Label

e-cigarette or vaping product use-associated lung injury

### Synonyms

EVALI, vaping-associated pulmonary illness

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

An acute lung injury in a person who has used e-cigarette or vaping products, with pulmonary infiltrates on imaging that are not attributed to another cause. It was recognized during a 2019 outbreak in the United States. Most patients had used products containing tetrahydrocannabinol, and vitamin E acetate is strongly implicated. [PMID:31491072, PMID:31860793, PMID:32320569]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** This disease has a published case definition and its own ICD-10-CM code, `U07.0` Vaping-related disorder, but no Mondo class. Mondo has no term containing vaping, e-cigarette or EVALI.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- 2,558 hospitalized nonfatal and 60 fatal cases had been reported to the CDC by January 7, 2020 (PMID:32320569).
- Vitamin E acetate was found in bronchoalveolar lavage fluid from 48 of 51 case patients and not in healthy comparators (PMID:31860793).

- `MONDO:0005960` silicosis
