### Label

arsenic poisoning

### Synonyms

arsenic intoxication

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A poisoning that is caused by exposure to inorganic arsenic. Acute ingestion causes gastrointestinal cramps, vomiting, diarrhea, ECG changes and disturbed liver function, can cause shock, and can be followed 10 days to 3 weeks later by a peripheral neuropathy. Chronic exposure, commonly through contaminated drinking water, causes skin lesions including melanosis and keratosis, and neoplastic changes. [PMID:15533028, PMID:3578979, PMID:196051, PMID:11218669, PMID:21576319]

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** Mondo has classes for the other common toxic metals but none for arsenic, even though the definition of `MONDO:0023305` names arsenic as an example.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Acute poisoning is documented in case reports and series (PMID:15533028, PMID:3578979, PMID:196051).
- Chronic poisoning is documented in population studies, where drinking-water exposure raised the incidence of skin lesions even below 100 μg/L (PMID:21576319).

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0018019` lead poisoning
- `MONDO:0018020` mercury poisoning
- `MONDO:0043523` cadmium poisoning
- `MONDO:0041996` thallium poisoning
