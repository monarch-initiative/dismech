Title: Request for new term KIF5B-related kyphomelic dysplasia
Template: Add new term (generic)

### Label

KIF5B-related kyphomelic dysplasia

### Synonyms

kyphomelic dysplasia, Itai-Ikegawa type

### Synonym type

exact

### Definition

A kyphomelic dysplasia, with severe bowing of the limbs, caused by de novo heterozygous variants in KIF5B, which encodes kinesin-1 heavy chain. [PMID:35342932]

### Parent term

MONDO:0019698 bent bone dysplasia

### Children term(s)

None proposed.

### Additional comments

**Why this term is needed.** OMIM has split this condition: 621644 is the KIF5B form and 211350 is the CCN2 form. Mondo has `MONDO:0008881` kyphomelic dysplasia, which carries OMIM:211350 and CCN2, and no term carrying OMIM:621644. Its definition states recessive inheritance, so the dominant KIF5B form cannot go underneath it. If you would rather make `MONDO:0008881` a grouping class over both forms, that would also meet our need. Gene: KIF5B.

**Evidence that the concept exists.**

- Four individuals had de novo KIF5B variants (PMID:35342932). This is the only cited report, so OMIM:621644 is the main support for the concept.

**Similar terms already in Mondo.**

- `MONDO:0008881` kyphomelic dysplasia
- `MONDO:0031615` familial bent bone dysplasia syndrome

**Search.** No matching label, synonym or cross-reference in Mondo release 2026-11-03, and no matching open or closed issue.

**Context.** Requested from curation of the [dismech](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech) knowledge base, where `KIF5B-Related_Kyphomelic_Dysplasia` currently has no `disease_term`.
