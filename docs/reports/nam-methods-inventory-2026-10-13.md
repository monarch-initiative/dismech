# DisMech assay and NAM inventory: issues and PRs by gingin77

Written 2026-10-13 against dismech `main` at commit `3eb0796817`, from the GitHub history of monarch-initiative/dismech (first compiled 2026-10-08). Scope: issues and PRs authored, assigned or commented on by gingin77 whose focus is a method, assay, model system, NAM, or a framework for recording how a mechanism is measured. Disease curation work is left out. An issue and the PRs that answered it share one header. Status is as of the compile date. 
- The Adverse Outcome Pathway framework work (AOP and EMOD alignment, and seeding AOPs from dismech) is inventoried separately in [the AOP framework inventory](data/aop-framework-inventory-2026-10-13.md).

This report is aware of issue [#13602, NAMs inventory - what fits and what's fringe](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13602), which sorts non-animal models by what ties the model to the disease. This report was not created to align with that analysis approach: it is organised by topic and by issue-PR pair, and it does not classify models by tie.

### NSCLC lung organ-chip as a structured experimental model ([#9792](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9792), closed; PR [#9931](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9931), merged)

The Hassell 2017 lung organ-chip (PMID:29020635) was cited only as treatment evidence, so the model was invisible to the mechanism graph. PR [#9665](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9665) added the ORGAN_ON_CHIP record; [#9931](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9931) grounded its tyrosine kinase inhibitor readout in GO:0007169, keeping the relationship at MEASURES because the chip never claims to reproduce a driver lesion. ### Esophageal squamous cell carcinoma patient-derived organoid ([#9793](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9793), closed; PR [#9797](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9797), merged)

ESCC had no organoid, chip or iPSC evidence while its adenocarcinoma sibling had a tumour-chip citation. The PR added a 24-patient ESCC organoid library (Nakagawa 2025, PMID:40169778) linked to a new Chemotherapy Resistance node with cisplatin, 5-FU and fedratinib readouts, and to TP53 Dysfunction, both RECAPITULATES at MODERATE fidelity. Blockers: no ESCC organ-chip literature exists, so an organoid stood in; a batched Europe PMC query returned the wrong abstract, forcing per-PMID fetches; the new node is isolated because the paper shows no upstream cause; organoids lack stroma and immune cells.

### Scope decision for purely computational NAM evidence ([#9794](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9794), open)

Filed because the in-silico strand of NAMeRS, virtual imaging trials, had no obvious home and needed a maintainer call. Nothing has been added and no decision is recorded. The triage bot argued the design decisions already exclude acquisition parameters and radiology decision support, which a virtual imaging trial simulates, and proposed judging by what a paper claims rather than its method, pointing instead at atrial fibrillation in-silico trials on the existing `computational_models` slot. COMPUTATIONAL evidence was thin when the issue was triaged on 2026-08-28 (642 items against 9,065 IN_VITRO) and remains so: at `3eb0796817`, `kb/disorders/` carries 1,136 COMPUTATIONAL against 20,943 IN_VITRO evidence_source lines, about 5 percent either way.

Thirteen non-animal models were curated for the NAMO bridge, but seven in [Stargardt](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Stargardt_Disease.html), [Noonan](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Noonan_Syndrome.html) and [Ulcerative Colitis](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Ulcerative_Colitis.html) had no `modeled_mechanisms`. Blockers: the issue's candidate targets were wrong in three places, a RAF1 paper refuted the ERK to hypertrophy edge, the healthy-tissue colon-on-chip was capped at MEASURES and LOW, and no coverage metric exists for model wiring.

### Tracking high-value NAM target diseases ([#802](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/802), open, authored by cmungall, commented; PR [#12582](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/12582), merged)

Blockers: the reviewer caught a mislabelled TSC limitation, opposite second-hit claims, an overstated LCA10 link; Wilson organoid fidelity stays UNKNOWN; Usher waits on [#10822](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/10822).

## 3. Liver fibrosis MPS and the fibrotic_response module

### First liver fibrosis NAM on [`Liver_Cirrhosis`](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Liver_Cirrhosis.html) and the [DILI module](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/modules/drug_induced_liver_injury.html) (PR [#9935](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9935), merged)

The three existing liver models were all drug-toxicity models and no fibrosis entry or module had an experimental model. Added the Akura Twin 384-well liver fibrosis MPS (PMID:40754287): HepaRG microtissues with THP-1 monocytes paired with stellate microtissues, challenged with TGF-beta1, methotrexate and acetaminophen, linked as CO_CULTURE to stellate activation, inflammation, hepatocyte injury and TGF-beta signalling. Blockers: no NAMO class fit until `namo:CoCulture` was found; `MicrophysiologicalSystem` requires microfluidics the plate lacks; monocyte activation was TGF-beta1-driven rather than drug-driven, so fidelity is LOW; the paper is abstract-only.

### Experimental models for the [`fibrotic_response`](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/modules/fibrotic_response.html) module ([#9997](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9997), closed; PR [#10282](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10282), merged)

The most-reused module had no experimental model, and the two organ-specific fibrosis NAMs were not fair stand-ins for fibrosis generally. [#10282](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10282) added the organ-agnostic Scar-in-a-Jar macromolecular crowding fibroblast assay (PMID:34028741, with dermal and lung demonstrations) linked to Mesenchymal Cell Activation and Excessive ECM Deposition at MODERATE fidelity. Blockers: the fibrosis chip literature is entirely organ-partitioned; precision-cut slices were rejected as murine with no NAMO class; `tissue_term` had to be omitted; three CHANGES_REQUESTED rounds over readouts asserting direction on methods-only quotes; exogenous TGF-beta1 forces PERTURBS rather than RECAPITULATES.

### Liver fibrosis NAM as the test case for the AOP Assay/NAM bridge ([#10051](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/10051), closed; PRs [#10257](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10257) and [#10299](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10299), merged)

The alignment page named Assay/NAM to `ExperimentalModel` as the cheapest bridge to AOP-Wiki and nothing had exercised it. [#10257](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10257) mapped AOP 38, protein alkylation leading to liver fibrosis, onto the Akura Twin nodes: five of seven Key Events reach a node, both endpoints miss, and eight `biological_scale` tags were added. [#10299](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10299) corrected the adverse outcome row, which had conflated fibrosis with cirrhosis. Blockers: nothing in the schema can reference a KE or KER and AOP-Wiki is not citable; the acetaminophen MIE link rests on a paywalled paper; `biological_scale` is single-valued; dismech has no organ-level liver fibrosis event.

## 5. ToxCast and Tox21 assay endpoints

### ToxCast as a structured reference source ([#12682](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/12682), open)

A chemical to assay hit-call is a citable fact nothing in the repository can verify, so a fabricated bisphenol A activity claim passes every gate. The issue specifies a `StructuredSource` emitting one cache file per chemical keyed on DTXSID with a `TOXCAST:` prefix, sized on the Jeong and Choi 2020 plastic-additive screen. Nothing has merged; the hit-call work survives only on an archived local branch, and the metadata half was split to [#12858](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/12858). Blockers: the EPA host fails DNS from sandboxed environments, the key must be requested by email, the sizing paper is paywalled, an API source does not fit the sha256 manifest pattern, and the `assays` slot has no validation path.

### Map ToxCast assays to pathograph nodes using assay metadata ([#12858](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/12858), open; PRs [#12923](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/12923), [#13226](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13226), [#13228](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13228), [#13236](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13236), merged)

ToxCast assays are molecular-level NAMs whose EPA CTX metadata could map an assay to a node independently of any chemical result. [#12923](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/12923) added `just toxcast-refresh`, [a parser](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/src/dismech/toxcast_assays.py) for 1,570 endpoints and the [epa-ctx-api](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/.claude/skills/epa-ctx-api/SKILL.md) skill. [#13226](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13226) added [`projects/TOXCAST.md`](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/projects/TOXCAST.html) and a [coverage script](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/scripts/toxcast_pathograph_coverage.py) with its [report](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/docs/reports/toxcast-pathograph-coverage-2026-09-30.md): 552 endpoints share a gene with 452 nodes in 317 entries. [#13228](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13228) wrote the THRB TR-FRET coactivator endpoint against [Generalized_Resistance_to_Thyroid_Hormone](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Generalized_Resistance_to_Thyroid_Hormone.html) as a full model record that validates cleanly while asserting something false; [#13236](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13236) pinned it in fixtures. Blockers: models live on Disease and links target same-file nodes, so an endpoint is copied everywhere with no shared identity; gene joins fail by lesion versus state, direction and quantity; cell-free assays have no model type; the API is unversioned.

### GO-resolving biologicalProcessTarget values ([#13224](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13224), open) and mitochondrial depolarization as a narrow bridge ([#13225](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13225), open)

[#13224](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13224) tested whether the 38 `biologicalProcessTarget` values bridge assay to node through GO. Nine resolve, covering 507 endpoints, but the top two are too broad to license an edge, leaving about 115 honest endpoints, with the H295R steroidogenesis assay (OECD TG 456) as the one substantive bridge. [#13225](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13225) split out GO:0051882, six endpoints that are four measurements, reaching a [PRKN-Related_Juvenile_Parkinson_Disease](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/PRKN-Related_Juvenile_Parkinson_Disease.html) node and a [Diethylene_Glycol_Poisoning](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Diethylene_Glycol_Poisoning.html) node. No PR for either. Blockers: 29 values do not resolve, three carry EPA typos, H295R endpoints list the hormone's receptor as gene target, the HepG2 time series has no kinetic slot, and cmungall objected that GO cell death must not describe toxic cell death.

### Endocrine Disruptor Screening Program history ([#13161](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13161), closed; PR [#13219](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13219), merged)

The estrogen signalling page claimed ESR1 is among the most heavily assayed ToxCast targets and the receptor the US statute was written around, and cited nothing. [#13219](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13219) added a section with 17 verified identifiers covering the 1996 Food Quality Protection Act, OECD TG 455, 493 and 440 with the uterotrophic ring trial, the ToxCast ER pathway model EPA accepted in place of three EDSP Tier 1 assays, the androgen receptor model, and CERAPP and CoMPARA. ## 7. Caenorhabditis elegans models for Major Depressive Disorder

### Seed MDD animal models from C. elegans serotonin, insulin/FOXO and antidepressant pharmacology ([#13749](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13749), closed; PRs [#13755](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13755), [#13756](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13756), [#13757](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13757), [#13759](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13759) and [#13776](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13776), merged)

A scripted PubMed survey proposed worm work for the upstream half of the entry, never as a model of depression. Five PRs landed: the SSRI mechanism qualified with fluoxetine pharmacology showing reuptake inhibition is not a sufficient account; the tph-1 serotonin-null and daf-2 insulin-receptor immobility models as the first `animal_models`, at LOW fidelity with typed divergences; the risk-gene mutant panels recorded as a knowledge gap, not models; the serotonin-to-FOXO arm resolved as a HUMAN_MODEL_MISMATCH after an OpenScientist run; and the forced-swim validity dispute cited. Blockers: one-to-many worm-to-human orthology; the suicidality panels contain none of the entry's genes; the program rests on one laboratory and no divergence type expresses that; the FOXO chain holds only in the worm.

### Mood disorders record no models at all ([#13754](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13754), open)

None of the four mood-disorder entries recorded any model, yet together they already cite 14 model-organism papers inside their mechanism nodes, so the models exist but are invisible to queries and the pathograph. The issue lists what can be promoted without research, such as the GABA-A delta knockout mouse for postpartum depression and the Clock, GSK-3beta and Cacna1c models for bipolar disorder, and scopes deep research for hormone-withdrawal, mania, stress-paradigm and PMDD model families. Nothing has merged; the C. elegans work stayed on [#13749](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13749). ## 8. NAM scope, the lithium models, and the valproate and lithium report

### NAMs inventory: what fits and what is fringe ([#13602](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13602), open)

Filed because no rule says when a NAM belongs in a disease entry. It sorts by what ties the model to the disease: cells carrying the lesion fit, unaffected cells plus a causal exposure fit, unaffected cells plus a risk factor is undecided, unaffected cells plus the treatment is fringe, a wild-type target plus a chemical with no disease is fringe (the ToxCast case), and statistical enrichment is not a model link. Counts on main: 1,251 models in 600 entries, 212 links with no relationship. Nothing has merged. Blockers: no structured exposure, dose, duration or donor population, no field for the tie, one model nested in one file. Needs a maintainer decision.

### Bipolar Disorder lithium-exposed neural progenitor and patient iPSC neuron models ([#13600](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13600), open; PR [#13788](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13788), merged; PR [#13603](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13603), draft held)

[`Bipolar_Disorder`](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Bipolar_Disorder.html) had no experimental or animal models. The issue proposes lithium-exposed primary human cortical progenitors from 83 genotyped unaffected donors (Wolter 2023, Valone 2026) plus the already-cited Mertens 2015 patient iPSC neurons. [#13788](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13788) landed the iPSC-derived dentate gyrus-like neurons as the entry's first patient-derived model: RECAPITULATES on Neuronal Hyperexcitability at MODERATE fidelity with a lithium-responder readout, MEASURES on Mitochondrial Dysfunction because the paper reports enhanced function. [#13603](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13603) was narrowed to the progenitor model and stays held. Blockers: donors are unaffected, so it models lithium's action rather than the disease; no neurogenesis node; genotype-by-treatment effects have no field; the scope decision in [#13602](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13602) is open.

### Valproic acid and lithium gene-by-treatment report ([#13787](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13787), merged; [#13794](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13794), merged)

Opened to ask what one 2026 paper on valproate and lithium in genotyped human neural progenitors could add to dismech. The [report](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/docs/reports/vpa-lithium-gxt-paper-dismech-fit-2026-10-05.md) names five entry gaps, four of which became work including [#13600](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13600), and three schema limits: `ExperimentalModel` has no structured place for an exposure dose, a genotyped donor panel or a gene-by-exposure interaction, so the fetal valproate syndrome entry dropped the paper's headline response QTL result. [#13794](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13794) corrected the claim that none were filed: the perturbation gap is [#13768](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13768), the donor-panel gap sits in [#13602](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13602), and the gene-by-exposure row has no issue and may belong in EnviroMech rather than dismech. Blockers: supplementary tables unread; two knowledge-base claims went stale between drafts.

## 9. Model organism, gene namespace and perturbation schema

### AnimalModel cannot bind its organism or record a non-genetic perturbation ([#13768](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13768), open; [#13774](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13774), open)

Filed as the follow-on to [#13548](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13548) after its organism and perturbation structures were set aside. `AnimalModel.species` is free text, written 66 ways across 2,381 models, and nothing structured can say what was done to a toxin-exposed zebrafish or a chronic-social-defeat mouse, so 351 procedure-based models bury the procedure in `genotype`, `description` or `category`. The proposal adds an NCBITaxon-bound `organism` and a `perturbations` list whose entries need no gene. [#13774](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13774) asks for a report on the 50 whole-organism models still parked in `experimental_models` as OTHER. Nothing has merged. Blockers: `species` is an anchor key for entity references; migrating bound-organism records today would lose the binding; [#13450](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13450) and [#13754](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13754) are blocked on the slot.

### Non-human gene identifiers on model genes ([#13548](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13548), open, authored by caufieldjh, commented; [#11140](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/11140), open, authored by dragon-ai-agent, commented)

[#13548](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13548) lets model genes be named in the model's own species through NCBIGene and UniProtKB, with an offline label gate. [#11140](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/11140) asked to document the older convention of recording the human ortholog. gingin77's comments, prompted by the worm survey in [#13749](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13749), showed that convention is underdetermined for invertebrate and fish models: tph-1 has two human candidates, daf-2 three, daf-16 four, and two curators would pick differently. The namespace PR answers that by recording the worm gene as itself, with orthology moved to a planned structured link. Blockers: the PR awaits maintainer sign-off as a schema change; nine MGI bindings fall through as unchecked; a mouse dataset binding a mouse gene sits outside the gate's scope; documentation written against the old rule would be wrong within weeks.

## 10. Model fidelity and readout grading

### Bgee cross-species model fidelity check (PR [#10513](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10513), merged, authored by another contributor, commented)

A [prototype script](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/scripts/bgee_model_fidelity.py) checks whether a model organism's ortholog of a disease gene is expressed in the anatomy where the human mechanism operates, a necessary but never sufficient condition for fidelity, with a [proposal](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/blob/main/docs/bgee-integration-proposal.md) explaining why Bgee presence calls cannot be imported as evidence. gingin77's comment endorsed the check in toxicology terms as context of use: a NAM is chosen to match its context, and a model for advancing understanding of a complex disease differs from the minimal model for chemical safety screening. Blockers reported: 79 percent of links not evaluable from anatomy mismatch, 4 percent cell-type coverage, Ensembl returning empty ortholog lists for real genes.

### Inverted readout grading on the Ganab mouse (PR [#10350](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/10350), merged)

Two evidence items on the Ganab heterozygous mouse's FAILS_TO_RECAPITULATE link in [`Polycystic_Kidney_Disease_3`](https://dismech.monarchinitiative.org/pages/disorders/Polycystic_Kidney_Disease_3.html) were graded REFUTE where the quoted sentence is the null observation the readout reports. The PR regraded both to SUPPORT with DIRECT directness, since the negative sense is already carried by the relationship and the UNCHANGED direction. Not a new assay, but a rule for grading animal-model readouts. Blockers: nothing gates this error because `check-snippet-grading` deliberately does not gate `supports`; the reviewer found 14 occurrences across 12 other files; merge was blocked for days by a stale-base check.

### A phenotype as the readout of a mechanism ([#11427](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/11427), open)

Curators wanting to say a phenotype is the readout of a mechanism were borrowing `sequelae`, which compiles to a phenotype-to-target edge and reverses causation; a census found nine such edges in six files. Nothing has merged. The bot showed the construct already exists as `Phenotype.reports_on` with READOUT_OF, CORRELATES_WITH, PREDICTS and PHARMACODYNAMIC_MARKER_OF, used in over 150 files, so this is a discoverability gap rather than a schema gap. The recommended fix is a `reports_on` migration and one sentence in CLAUDE.md.

### Evidence strength, study design and assay typing ([#9421](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9421), open, authored by another contributor, commented)

A generic evidence-model issue proposing study design, sample size and strength fields. gingin77's 2026-09-04 comment tied it to [#10772](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/10772): ECO types the evidence while OBI types the assay, 564 assay entries bind no OBI term and only two sit on a pathophysiology node, OBI has no adapter, and letting an evidence item point at its model would match EMOD's indirection, so one schema change should cover both issues. cmungall ruled that agents were redoing a hackathon discussion and that trial tiers do not suit pathograph internals built from converging animal, in vitro and computational lines. No change has merged.

## Blockers that recur across the inventory

- No disease-agnostic assay record. `experimental_models` lives on Disease and a link must target a node in the same file, so a reusable endpoint is copied per disease with no shared identity ([#12858](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/12858), [#13225](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13225), [#13450](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13450), [#13602](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13602)).
- The `assays` slot is bound to OBI with no OAK adapter or enum cache, and ECO is absent, so how a claim was measured cannot be recorded structurally ([#10772](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/10772), [#11394](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/11394), [#12682](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/12682), [#9421](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9421)).
- Several of the primary papers behind the models curated here (the NSCLC organ-chip, the Akura Twin liver MPS, the ToxCast sizing paper and the zebrafish VAMR assay) were available only as abstracts or behind a paywall. Because dismech evidence must quote the cached reference, the quotable text in those cases ran out before the model's readouts were all covered ([#9792](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9792), [#9935](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9935), [#12682](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/12682), [#13450](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13450)).
- Models involving co-cultures, gravity-linked plates and ex vivo slices, revealed some NAMO vocabulary gaps. Specifically, NAMO has no class for a non-perfused, gravity-linked microphysiological plate (its MPS class is abstract and requires microfluidics) or for ex vivo tissue slices, and the project page's in-scope list showed only four of NAMO's 56 classes, so one model was first left unbound for the wrong reason ([#9935](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/9935), [#9997](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/9997)).
- `AnimalModel` binds no organism and has no perturbation slot, so procedure-based and exposure-based whole-organism models have nowhere structured to say what was done to them ([#13768](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13768), [#13754](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13754), [#13450](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13450), [#13774](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13774)).
- Worm-to-human and fish-to-human gene orthology is one-to-many, and the human-ortholog convention on model genes forces a pick the biology does not support ([#11140](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/11140), [#13548](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13548), [#13749](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13749)).
- No structured exposure, dose, duration, kinetic series or donor population on a model ([#13225](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13225), [#13602](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13602), [#13600](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/issues/13600), [#13787](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/dismech/pull/13787)).
