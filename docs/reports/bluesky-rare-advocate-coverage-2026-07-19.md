# Bluesky rare-disease advocacy account as a curation-lead source (2026-07-19)

Review of the Bluesky profile **[@rarectadvocate.com](https://bsky.app/profile/rarectadvocate.com)**
("CT Rare Advocate") as a candidate lead-generation source for dismech curation,
with a coverage cross-check of the diseases it features against `kb/disorders/`.

## What the account is

- A Connecticut-based **rare-disease awareness** account (DID `did:plc:sdy3iizxbedjaaxdtytw5l2c`),
  active since Feb 2025, ~560 posts, 86 followers. Companion site `rarectadvocate.com`.
- Format: **one rare disease per day** — a 1-2 sentence lay summary (often naming the
  causative gene, prevalence, and inheritance) plus a link to a patient-advocacy resource,
  signed "You may be rare, but you're not alone!" `#rareDisease #rareDiseaseAwareness`.
- Editorially curated by a patient advocate (self-identifies as a hereditary-angioedema
  patient); committed to adding image alt-text from July 2025. It is an **awareness feed,
  not a scientific source** — summaries are lay-audience and uncited.

## How this maps to dismech

The feed is a broad, human-curated **worklist of named rare diseases** — useful in the same
way the `literature-scan` / `knowledge-gap-scan` agents are: as a source of *candidate
entities to curate*, not as evidence. Treat every claim (gene, prevalence, mechanism) as a
**lead only**, exactly as the CLAUDE.md SOP requires for deep-research outputs: verify each
PMID/gene/ontology term against primary sources before curation, and run the
**Named-Entity-Confusion preflight** — the feed leans heavily on **eponyms** (Aarskog,
Björnstad, Freiberg, Dercum, Field, Naxos, Uner Tan…), a documented NEC-risk class.

## Coverage cross-check

Method: pulled the account's full public post history via the AT-Protocol `getAuthorFeed`
API (430 posts → ~389 distinct named diseases), extracted the leading disease name from each
post, and matched (normalized + fuzzy + substring, then hand-verified the boundary cases)
against the top-level file names in `kb/disorders/` (1,602 entries). Counts are approximate
(lay naming, abbreviations, and subtype phrasing make exact matching imperfect).

- **~255 (≈65%) are already represented** in dismech (e.g. Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis,
  Loeys-Dietz, Wilson, Behçet, Waardenburg spectrum, CADASIL, Alkaptonuria, Pompe, Tay-Sachs,
  Huntington, Sickle Cell, Rett, Fragile X, most metabolic/lysosomal disorders). Good overlap
  — the account and dismech share a rare-Mendelian centre of gravity.
- **~127 are genuine gaps** (no dedicated `kb/disorders/` entry), listed below. Several are
  strong fits for **existing mechanism modules** (fibrosis, channelopathy, antimicrobial,
  lysosomal storage, cancer hallmarks), which lowers their curation cost.

This is a **candidate worklist**, not a mandate: some entries are out of dismech's
molecular-mechanism scope (functional/psychiatric curiosities), and a handful flagged as
"gap" may already exist under a subtype/grouping name (e.g. Scheie = MPS I attenuated;
DiGeorge is present as 22q11.2 Deletion Syndrome).

## Recommendation

1. **Adopt as a low-cost lead feed, not evidence.** The account surfaces well-scoped rare
   diseases daily; its main value to dismech is the ~127-entry gap worklist above.
2. **Prioritise the module-fit gaps first** — the Mendelian/mechanistic, cardiac,
   antimicrobial, and immune buckets can reuse existing modules and drug-target patterns.
3. **Enforce the DR-grade verification + NEC preflight** for anything sourced here: the
   summaries are uncited lay text and eponym-heavy. Never quote a post as an evidence
   `snippet`; use it only to pick the entity, then curate from primary literature.
4. **De-scope the functional/psychiatric curiosities** (Alice-in-Wonderland, Capgras,
   Cotard, Foreign Accent, Jumping Frenchman, Morgellons, Hyperthymesia) unless a molecular
   pathophysiology is established — they fall outside dismech's mechanism-KB remit.
