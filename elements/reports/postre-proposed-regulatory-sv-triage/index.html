    POSTRE regulatory structural variant curation triage

    Assessment date: 2026-09-21. Scope: the six Table 1 reference alleles and all 41 Table 2 proposed long-range cases, compared with the DisMech worktree. This is a curation worklist, not a pathogenicity adjudication or patient-level phenotype dataset. The initial Table 2 triage changed no KB entries; the follow-up below records subsequent curation.

    Inputs and identity checks

    • POSTRE paper, PMID:36999617, especially the Table 2 discussion explaining incomplete experimental validation.
    • Authors’ Table 2 input TSV, pinned to its last modifying commit. SHA-256: 23f0c5d434bf6810a74310cf92a1a100e2d14d373250f49c71eaf2229b855fc8.
    • Publisher supplementary bundle via Europe PMC: SupplementaryData_3_Sanchez_Gaya_et_al_revision.xlsx, worksheets Table1_SVs and Table2_SVs; SHA-256 69cbe3e363f63917725f77f3fc6f8343a8b92c97d8c22ef45a2235d473e87997.
    • The same bundle’s SupplementaryData_4_Sanchez_Gaya_et_al_revision.xlsx, worksheet Table2_SV_results, was checked to distinguish proposed literature targets from positive POSTRE predictions. No model was rerun.

    The 41 input rows are 41 distinct chromosome/breakpoint/type tuples: 21 translocations, 15 deletions, three inversions and two duplications. They concern nine target-gene groups. All input coordinates are hg19/GRCh37, as specified by POSTRE. A comma-separated pair at one breakpoint is an interval supplied by the authors, not two SVs; it must not be converted to a nucleotide-resolved junction. For an intrachromosomal SV, the two breakpoint columns delimit the rearrangement. The table below preserves the deposited coordinate strings without coordinate-system conversion.

    All 13 cited source abstracts were read. Relevant clinical/variant/assay sections of 12 full texts were inspected; the Ishibashi ARX paper remained abstract-only after its author-manuscript download returned HTTP 403. This is targeted triage, not a complete reassessment of every source’s supplementary data. Missing original patient IDs, individual phenotype assignments or assay results are explicitly unassessed below. Table2_SVs contains no detailed-phenotype column: only neurodevelopmental or limbs. Clinical summaries below come from identified original-paper passages or D’Haene’s coordinate-linked Table 1; a cohort description is not silently assigned to an individual.

    Table 2 contains prior-literature mechanistic proposals with incomplete validation. POSTRE’s published standard-mode output calls 39 of these 41 pathogenic; it does not call NR2F2_Nr1 or CTNNA2_Nr1 pathogenic. Agreement with earlier proposals is not independent experimental confirmation. The paper’s LOF/GOF terminology includes changes in expression and cannot be copied directly into DisMech’s coding functional-impact or spatial/temporal expression enums.
