# Toxicologic phenocopies of Mendelian disease

**48 exposure–gene pairs · 73 distinct genes or loci · 15 organ systems · 20 halt or regress on withdrawal**

Toxic and pharmacologic exposures that reproduce a Mendelian phenotype closely enough to mislead a phenotype-driven pipeline. Deeper phenotyping does not resolve these — it makes the wrong gene rank *higher*. What resolves them is the exposure history and a specific non-genetic test.

## Caveats

- Tier assignments are a judgement about mechanistic tightness, **not a graded evidence rating**. They would need review before curation.
- Two entries (anthracyclines/`TTN`, silica/`TERT`) are arguably gene–environment interaction rather than phenocopy proper. Kept in and flagged, but they may belong in a separate category.
- Valproate/`POLG` is deliberately both: a phenocopy of Alpers-Huttenlocher in people with normal `POLG`, and a fatal trigger in those without. If the schema expresses one relation per pair, that case needs a second edge type.
- This is a literature compilation, not a systematically curated set. Treat it as a starting list, not a reference.
