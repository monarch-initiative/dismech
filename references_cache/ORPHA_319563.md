# ORPHA:319563  Mendelian susceptibility to mycobacterial diseases due to complete ISG15 deficiency

**ORPHA:319563** — Mendelian susceptibility to mycobacterial diseases due to complete ISG15 deficiency (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- MSMD due to complete ISG15 deficiency

## Definition

Mendelian susceptibility to mycobacterial diseases (MSMD) due to complete ISG15 deficiency is a genetic variant of MSMD characterized by Bacille Calmette-Guérin (BCG) infections.
