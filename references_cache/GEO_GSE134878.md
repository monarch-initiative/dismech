# Gene Expression Analysis of Essential Tremor via RNA-sequencing

## Content

RNA-seq evaluation of post-mortem human cerebelllum from 33 patients with diagnosed Essential tremor, compared to 22 age-matched control patients. Two samples were under-sequenced and therefore removed from the final analysis. The raw data has been included in this submission.
