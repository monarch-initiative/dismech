# Transcriptome analysis of naive CD4T cells from Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis(GPA) patients and healthy control (HC)

## Content

Analysis of transcriptional profile of GPA and HC naive CD4 T cells to understand intrinsic molecular differences after early TCR activation that may explain different effector T cell genereation.
