## Definition

Primary care 1. Diagnosis of AAA during a consultation 2. Performance of emergency AAA repair procedure recording during a consultation Secondary care 1. Diagnosis of AAA as the primary diagnosis of any hospitalization 2. Performance of emergency AAA repair procedure recorded Mortality 1. Leaking or ruptured AAA as underlying cause of death 2. AAA without leak or rupture as underlying cause of death
