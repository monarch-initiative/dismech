INTRODUCTION: Recently, You, Hoover-Fong, and colleagues described a disease
caused by a deficiency of the telomere maintenance 2 gene (TELO2) function. The
clinical spectrum includes early-onset global delay, dysmorphic facial features,
auditory disorder, and reduced vision.
MATERIALS AND METHODS: We report two siblings, diagnosed with You-Hoover-Fong
syndrome at the age of 28 and 14 months. Both were genetically studied to find
the cause of their developmental delay and microcephaly.
RESULTS: The identical compound heterozygous missense mutations in the TELO2gene
were found in each. Ophthalmologically, both siblings were diagnosed with
progressive congenital bilateral nuclear-lamellar cataracts.
CONCLUSIONS: We report nuclear-lamellar cataracts in two siblings diagnosed with
You-Hoover-Fong syndrome.
