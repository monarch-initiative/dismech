# Nitrogen Dioxide Toxicity.
**Authors:** Amaducci A, Launico MV

## Content

Nitrogen is a colorless, tasteless, and odorless gas comprising approximately 78% of air by volume. This substance exists primarily as an inert diatomic molecule with no direct physiologic toxicity. Nitrogen oxides, including nitric oxide and nitrogen dioxide, are gases generated from nitrous or nitric acid as byproducts of reactions between nitric acid and organic materials, burning of nitrocellulose, detonations, and decomposition of rocket fuel. Exposure to nitrogen oxides can also occur during electric arc welding, electroplating, and engraving. Engine exhaust contains nitrogen oxides, which are additionally produced when filling silos with grain containing high nitrite levels. Nitric oxide reacts with oxygen to form nitrogen dioxide.
