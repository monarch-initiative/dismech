Chung-Jansen Syndrome is a recently identified obesity syndrome, characteristic 
clinical features of which are global developmental delay, intellectual 
disability, obesity and dysmorphism (DIDOD). We present a child with the 
syndrome who also had hypothyroidism and renal involvement in form of small 
kidneys on one side. The next generation sequencing done in the child showed a 
nonsense variant in the PHIP gene leading to premature chain termination in the 
protein on bioinformatic analysis.
