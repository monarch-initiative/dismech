# PTPN11 gene mutations: linking the Gln510Glu mutation to the "LEOPARD syndrome phenotype".

PTPN11 gene mutations: linking the Gln510Glu mutation to the "LEOPARD syndrome 
phenotype".

Digilio MC(1), Sarkozy A, Pacileo G, Limongelli G, Marino B, Dallapiccola B.

Author information:
(1)Medical Genetics, Bambino Gesù Hospital, Rome, Italy, digilio@opbg.net.

We describe the "LEOPARD syndrome (LS) phenotype" associated with the Gln510Glu 
mutation of the PTPN11 gene in two patients presenting with rapidly progressive 
severe biventricular obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and structural 
abnormalities of the mitral valve, facial anomalies, café-au-lait spots and 
multiple lentigines.
