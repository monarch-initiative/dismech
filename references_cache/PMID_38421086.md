# 3q29 duplications: A cohort of 46 patients and a literature review.
**Authors:** Massier M, Doco-Fenzy M, Egloff M, Le Guillou X, Le Guyader G, Redon S, Benech C, Le Millier K, Uguen K, Ropars J, Sacaze E, Audebert-Bellanger S, Apetrei A, Molin A, Gruchy N, Vincent-Devulder A, Spodenkiewicz M, Jacquin C, Loron G, Thibaud M, Delplancq G, Brisset S, Lesieur-Sebellin M, Malan V, Romana S, Rio M, Marlin S, Amiel J, Marquet V, Dauriat B, Moradkhani K, Mercier S, Isidor B, Arpin S, Pujalte M, Jedraszak G, Pebrel-Richard C, Salaun G, Laffargue F, Boudjarane J, Missirian C, Chelloug N, Toutain A, Chiesa J, Keren B, Mignot C, Gouy E, Jaillard S, Landais E, Poirsier C

## Content

Duplications of the 3q29 cytoband are rare chromosomal copy number variations (CNVs) (overlapping or recurrent ~1.6 Mb 3q29 duplications). They have been associated with highly variable neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs) with various associated features or reported as a susceptibility factor to the development of learning disabilities and neuropsychiatric disorders. The smallest region of overlap and the phenotype of 3q29 duplications remain uncertain. We here report a French cohort of 31 families with a 3q29 duplication identified by chromosomal microarray analysis (CMA), including 14 recurrent 1.6 Mb duplications, eight overlapping duplications (>1 Mb), and nine small duplications (<1 Mb). Additional genetic findings that may be involved in the phenotype were identified in 11 patients. Focusing on apparently isolated 3q29 duplications, patients present mainly mild NDD as suggested by a high rate of learning disabilities in contrast to a low proportion of patients with intellectual disabilities. Although some are de novo, most of the 3q29 duplications are inherited from a parent with a similar mild phenotype. Besides, the study of small 3q29 duplications does not provide evidence for any critical region. Our data suggest that the overlapping and recurrent 3q29 duplications seem to lead to mild NDD and that a severe or syndromic clinical presentation should warrant further genetic analyses.

Abstract:

(overlapping or recurrent ~1.6 Mb 3q29 duplicatons). They have been associated with highly variable
neurodevelopmental disorders (NDD) with various associated features, or reported as a susceptbility

Introducton
The advent of chromosomal microarray analysis (CMA) has allowed the discovery of reciprocal
recurrent copy number variatons (CNVs), which are fanked by segmental duplicaton (SD) and
mediated by non-allelic homologous recombinaton (NAHR). For most of these reciprocal CNVs, the
duplicatons’ pathogenicity was assessed later than for deletons and the phenotype may be less
severe.  At the 3q29 cytoband, a ~1.6 Mb recurrent reciprocal CNV is found from 195,6 to 197,3 Mb

4
(GRCh37/Hg19) fanked by SD [Yilmaz et al., 2023] . This region contains 22 protein-coding genes
currently without strong evidence for candidate critcal genes. The recurrent 3q29 deleton is found
with a frequency of 0.002% in the UK biobank populaton [Crawford et al., 2019] . It has been
established to display incomplete penetrance and variable expressivity, characterized by
developmental delay (DD), mild to moderate intellectual disability (ID), neuropsychiatric disorders
(NPD) such as atenton-defcit/hyperactvity disorder (ADHD), autsm spectrum disorder (ASD),
anxiety, and schizophrenia, pediatric feeding problems, cardiac and cerebral malformatons, ocular
and dental abnormalites [Sanchez Russo et al., 2021]. 
The reciprocal 3q29 duplicaton is less frequent. In the UK biobank populaton, the estmated
frequency is 0.001% [Crawford et al., 2019] . In the DGV and gnomAD SV databases, the recurrent
duplicaton has not been reported [Collins et al., 2020]. 3q29 duplicatons have been associated with
highly variable neurodevelopmental disorders (NDD) ranging from mild to severe ID, learning
difcultes, neuropsychiatric disorders, and associated to miscellaneous clinical features such as
cardiac, cerebral, or ocular malformatons. Patents described had heterogeneous phenotype, and
some are reported as asymptomatc [Holinski-Feder et al., 2000; Ballif et al., 2008; Lisi et al., 2008;
Goobie et al., 2008; Aleixandre Blanquer et al., 2011; Schilter et al., 2013; Fernández-Jaén et al.,
2014; Quintela García et al., 2015; Kessi et al., 2018; Tassano et al., 2018; Streata et al., 2020; Coyan
and Dyer, 2020; Bauleo et al., 2022] . Furthermore, a case-control study suggested the 3q29
duplicaton might be a susceptbility locus for NDD (mainly learning disability and neuropsychiatric
disorders such as ASD) [Pollak et al., 2020]. 
In large NDD cohorts (DD, ID and/or ASD), the 3q29 duplicaton prevalence is not signifcantly
increased compared to controls [Moreno-De-Luca et al., 2013; Kushima et al., 2018; Stefansson et al.,
2014; Coe et al., 2014; Zarrei et al., 2019] . However, meta-analysis of rare CNV in nearly one million
individuals identfed an associaton between 3q29 duplicaton and neurodevelopmental phenotype
(p=10-4)  [Collins et al., 2022].

5
To date, the impact of the 3q29 duplicaton remains unclear. The hypothesis of multple molecular
diagnoses has been raised in recent reports to explain the variable expressivity [Coyan and Dyer,
2020; Bauleo et al., 2022]. To improve phenotype delineaton and consequently the interpretaton of
this CNV, we here present a cytogenetc and clinical descripton of a French cohort with a duplicaton
in the 3q29 region (chr3: 194803300-198810000, GRCh37/Hg19). We also investgate the implicaton
of additonal CNVs in our cohort, as well as SNVs as identfed by exome sequencing. 
Methods
Our multcenter retrospectve study was performed from December 2018 to October 2022. Patents
with a duplicaton in the chr3:194800000-198810000 (GRCh37/hg19) region were recruited from 14
French genetc centers, thanks to a natonal collaboraton within the ACLF network (associaton of
French speaking cytogenetcists). Clinical features and genetc data were collected using a
questonnaire completed by clinical genetcists and cytogenetcists. The collected data included: age
at diagnosis, age at last follow-up, medical history, clinical signs, para-clinical exams, the
chromosomal and molecular genetc analyses performed, the characteristcs of 3q29 duplicaton
(size, locaton, inheritance), and the presence of additonal genetc variatons (CNVs and SNVs). 
Sixty-four patents were recruited, including 3q29 duplicaton carrier relatves.  Eighteen patents
were excluded from our cohort due to lack of clinical informaton or due to the cytogenetc data
(locaton of the duplicaton found in very low proportons in the cohort) (fgure 1). 
CNVs were identfed by CMA analysis and confrmed by qPCR, FISH or qMPSF. Exome sequencing
(ES) or a targeted gene sequencing of 286 genes involved in intellectual disability (ID genes panel)
were performed in 10 families. Trio analyses were prioritzed when parent samples were available.
For one family, symptomatc siblings were also analyzed. Genomic mapping was performed on the
Human GRCh37/hg19 assembly, with a custom track tool of University of California Santa Cruz (UCSC)
Genome Browser custom track tool.

Results

Discussion

 This study reports a large cohort of 3q29 duplicaton carriers with familial data and additonal
genetc events identfed by CMA and exome sequencing recruited among French laboratories. 
3q29 duplicatons are rare CNVs with much lower frequency than other recurrent CNVs  associated
with NDD of variable expressivity, such as 16p11.2 BP4-BP5 duplicaton, 16p12.2 deleton or 15q11.2
deleton [D’Angelo et al., 2016; Girirajan et al., 2010; Crawford et al., 2019; Jønch et al., 2019]
Previously, 86 patents with 3q29 duplicatons have been reported in the literature  [Holinski-Feder et
al., 2000; Ballif et al., 2008; Lisi et al., 2008; Goobie et al., 2008; Aleixandre Blanquer et al., 2011;
Schilter et al., 2013; Fernández-Jaén et al., 2014; Quintela García et al., 2015; Kessi et al., 2018;
Tassano et al., 2018; Streata et al., 2020; Coyan and Dyer, 2020; Pollak et al., 2020; Bauleo et al.,
2022]. To discuss patents reported in the literature with our cohort, we separated individuals from
the literature with >1 Mb 3q29 duplicaton overlapping the recurrent locus and those with smaller
duplicaton (fgure S1 and S2). Similarly, we focused on the reported individuals with apparently
isolated duplicaton for clinical presentaton.
Recurrent and overlapping 3q29 duplicatons: Cohort A and literature review
Seventy-seven individuals have been previously reported with duplicaton encompassing the region:
chr3:195345000-197745100 (GRCh37/Hg19) (fgure S1 and S2). Among them, 46 patents from 23
families were described in case reports or small cohorts [Holinski-Feder et al., 2000; Ballif et al.,
2008; Lisi et al., 2008; Goobie et al., 2008; Aleixandre Blanquer et al., 2011; Schilter et al., 2013;
Fernández-Jaén et al., 2014; Quintela García et al., 2015; Streata et al., 2020; Coyan and Dyer, 2020] .
Additonally, Pollak et al. compared 31 patents with 3q29 duplicaton with 64 controls without
duplicaton and with 117 patents with 3q29 deleton [Pollak et al., 2020] . In this study, the 3q29
duplicatons’ locaton and size were not specifed.
In cohort A, we mostly found recurrent intersttal ~1.6 Mb duplicatons mediated by NAHR [Carvalho
and Lupski, 2016; Yilmaz et al., 2023] . The recurrent duplicaton was previously reported in 17
patents (fgure S2) [Lisi et al., 2008; Ballif et al., 2008; Aleixandre Blanquer et al., 2011; Fernández-

Jaén et al., 2014; Quintela García et al., 2015; Coyan and Dyer, 2020].  A derivatve chromosome from
an unbalanced reciprocal translocaton can lead to a 3q29 terminal duplicaton with an associated
variable deleton, but only one large family has yet been reported with a chromosomal translocaton
[Holinski-Feder et al., 2000] . Other duplicatons reported in our cohort (n=2) and the literature are
the result of diferent mechanisms leading to nonrecurrent CNV (fgure S2)  [Goobie et al., 2008;
Schilter et al., 2013; Streata et al., 2020; Carvalho and Lupski, 2016]. 
In cohort A, around a third of patents (n=11/33) have additonal genetc fndings (CNV or SNV).
Among them, three unrelated patents have pathogenic deletons related to well-described
syndromes: the terminal 11q24.1q25 deleton (Jacobsen syndrome, OMIM #147791), the 2q37
deleton (OMIM #600430), and the 12p13.33p13.32 deleton [Han and Park, 2021] . These deletons
unequivocally partcipate to the phenotype. One patent carries the recurrent 15q11.2 deleton BP1-
BP2 known as a susceptbility locus for NDD (OMIM #615656), and four families (3, 8, 16 and 19)
have a chromosomal deleton or a SNV of uncertain signifcance, for which we cannot exclude a
contributon to the phenotype.  
In the literature review, few 3q29 duplicaton carriers appear to have undergone exome sequencing.
In some reports, additonal CNVs were reported. A large family was reported with an unbalanced
translocaton t(3;16)(q29;p13.3) in 10 siblings, leading to a 16p13.3 deleton associated with
Rubinstein-Taybi syndrome [Holinski-Feder et al., 2000] . Two reports include a patent with an
additonal CNV of uncertain signifcance [Goobie et al., 2008; Coyan and Dyer, 2020] . Environmental
factors such as fetal alcohol exposure have also been mentoned in two families [Coyan and Dyer,
2020]. In the case-control study, patents with an additonal genetc diagnosis were excluded [Pollak
et al., 2020].
Additonal genetc events are an important factor to consider in 3q29 duplicaton interpretaton.
Multple molecular diagnoses have already been well-established to explain phenotype expansions or
phenotypic heterogeneity [Karaca et al., 2018; Posey et al., 2017]  and notably with pathogenic CNVs

3q29 genes 
The identfcaton of critcal phenotype driving genes is a challenge in the study of pathologic CNVs.
The recurrent 3q29 CNV encompasses 22 protein-coding genes (3q29 genes), including 19 OMIM
genes, currently without strong evidence for one or more candidate critcal genes. Some genes have
been more ofen discussed such as DLG1, PAK2, NCBP2, BDH1 but with inconclusive functonal data
or without a clearly established link with NDD [Carroll et al., 2011; Rutkowski et al., 2021;
Antonarakis et al., 2022; Singh et al., 2020]. Triplosensitvity (i.e. sensitvity to increased DNA dosage)
of 3q29 genes could be a guiding element towards the identfcaton of phenotype-driving genes.
Among the 1,559 triplosensitve genes predicted by Collins et al., two genes UBXN7 and PAK2 are in
the recurrent 3q29 locus [Collins et al., 2022] . Furthermore, the impact assessment of 3q29 deleton
on the human neural transcriptome prioritzed nine genes i nvolved in diferent biological processes :
BDH1, CEP19, DLG1, FBXO45, PIGZ, RNF168, SENP5, WDR53, and in partcular UBXN7 as major driver
genes in 3q29 deleton phenotype [Sefk et al., 2021] . A recent functonal study on human cortcal
organoids and a mouse model suggests that the 3q29 deleton can lead to mitochondrial dysfuncton.
This dysfuncton was partally reproduced by  PAK2  knockout, suggestng a partal role for PAK2 in
3q29 deleton phenotype [Purcell et al., 2023] . Thus, these studies suggest a probable complex
impact of 3q29 deleton on several biological processes, involving several 3q29 genes, which may
also be the case for duplicaton.

Conclusion
Individuals in our cohort and previously reported carriers of 3q29 duplicatons are heterogeneous
regarding their clinical features and the duplicatons’ size and locaton. However, afer
harmonizaton, overlapping or recurrent ~1.6 Mb 3q29 duplicaton could lead to a mild
neurodevelopmental phenotype with variable expressivity and incomplete penetrance, supported by
several observatons: the clinical presentaton of patent with isolated 3q29 duplicaton (cohort
Aisolated), the inheritance patern, the consistent segregaton in mild NDD families, and the proportons
of additonal genetc events. This highlights the need for further genetc investgatons when patents
present a severe or syndromic phenotype.
