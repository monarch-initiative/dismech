# Acantholytic Blistering of the Oroesophageal Mucosa Due to Homozygous DSG3 Pathogenic Missense Variant.
**Authors:** Avitan-Hersh E, Mao X, Solimani F, Zhao G, Boyango I, Bergman R, Payne AS
**Journal:** JAMA Dermatol (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1001/jamadermatol.2026.3186](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamadermatol.2026.3186)

## Content

This genetic association study provides new data on a prior case report of persistent neonatal pemphigus, reclassifying it as genetic rather than autoimmune in origin.
