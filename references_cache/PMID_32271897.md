# Efficacy of ruxolitinib in subcutaneous panniculitis-like T-cell lymphoma and hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis.
**Authors:** Lévy R, Fusaro M, Guerin F, Chetouani A, Moshous D, Fischer A, de Saint Basile G, Sepulveda FE, Neven B
**Journal:** Blood Adv (2020)
**DOI:** [10.1182/bloodadvances.2020001497](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2020001497)

## Content

First evidence of ruxolitinib efficacy for subcutaneous panniculitis-like T-cell lymphoma with hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis. Supporting rationale for ruxolitinib use, not more aggressive treatment, in this context, questioning this condition’s neoplastic nature.
