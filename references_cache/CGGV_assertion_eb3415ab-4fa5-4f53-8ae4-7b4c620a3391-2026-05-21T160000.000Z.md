## Evidence summary

Biallelic germline variants in RELB were first reported in relationship to combined immunodeficiency (CID) in a consanguineous family in 2015 (Merico et al., 2015; DOI: 10.14785/lpsn-2015-0005). Subsequent reports have described affected individuals presenting with a dysplastic thymus, abnormal T-cell repertoire, dysfunctional T and B cells, autoimmune manifestations and susceptibility to opportunistic infections (PMID: 26385063, 33791233). In total, there have been a reported seven loss-of-function (LOF) variants (four truncating and three missense) across multiple probands (PMID:26385063, 33083013, 33791233, 36402602, 39231201). Heterozygous carriers do not manifest clinical symptoms.
