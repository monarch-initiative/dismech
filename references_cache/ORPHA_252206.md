# ORPHA:252206  Melanoma and neural system tumor syndrome

**ORPHA:252206** — Melanoma and neural system tumor syndrome (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Melanoma-astrocytoma syndrome

## Definition

Melanoma and neural system tumor syndrome is an extremely rare tumor association characterized by dual predisposition to melanoma and neural system tumors (typically astrocytoma).

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant
- Unknown

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adult
- Age of onset: Elderly
