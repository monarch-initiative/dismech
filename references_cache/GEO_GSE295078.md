# Single-nucleus transcriptomics of inducible Ngly1-/- cerebellum

## Content

To explore the cell type-specific effects of NGLY1 deficiency and the STING pathway in the cerebellum, we performed single-nucleus RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq) of cerebella from Ngly1fl/fl, iNgly1-/- and iNgly1-/-Sting1-/- mice
