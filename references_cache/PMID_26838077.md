BACKGROUND: TWINKLE (c10orf2) gene is responsible for autosomal dominant 
progressive external ophthalmoplegia (PEO). In rare cases, additional features 
such as muscle weakness, peripheral neuropathy, ataxia, cardiomyopathy, 
dysphagia, dysphonia, cataracts, depression, dementia, parkinsonism, and hearing 
loss have been reported in association with heterozygous mutations of the 
TWINKLE gene.
METHODS: We have studied a large Iranian family with myopathy, dysphonia, 
dysphagia, and behavior change in addition to PEO in affected members.
RESULTS: We identified a missense mutation c.1121G > A in the c10orf2 gene in 
all affected members. Early death is a novel feature seen in affected members of 
this family that has not been reported to date.
CONCLUSION: The association of PEO, myopathy, dysphonia, dysphagia, behavior 
change and early death has not been previously reported in the literature or 
other patients with this mutation.
