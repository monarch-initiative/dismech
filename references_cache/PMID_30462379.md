# Japanese patient with Cole-carpenter syndrome with compound heterozygous variants of SEC24D.

Cole-Carpenter syndrome is a rare skeletal dysplasia associated with low-bone 
mass or an osteogenesis imperfecta (OI)-like syndrome. Only 3 and 6 variants in 
SEC24D have been reported in patients with Cole-Carpenter syndrome type 2 and 
autosomal recessive OI, respectively. We describe a 15-year-old Japanese boy 
with short stature of the short-trunk type and craniofacial abnormalities 
including ocular proptosis, marked frontal bossing, midface hypoplasia, and 
micrognathia. These features were consistent with a diagnosis of Cole-Carpenter 
syndrome. He had low-bone mineral density and basilar impression. Whole exome 
sequencing analysis identified biallelic variants in SEC24D (p.Arg484* and 
p.Arg313His) in the patient. We will report a patient with compound heterozygous 
variants of SEC24D causing Cole-Carpenter syndrome type 2.
