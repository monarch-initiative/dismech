# An in vitro study of the development of resistance to FGFR inhibition in two urothelial carcinoma cell lines

## Content

Expression array data was used to compare parental FGFR3-TACC3 fusion-driven urothelial cell lines with their FGFR inhibitor-resistant derivatives. In this dataset, we include RT112 and RT4 parental cells, RT112 cells acutely treated with PD173074 (24 h), RT112 and RT4 resistant derivatives cultured with drug and their resistant derivatives cultured for four to six passages out of drug.
