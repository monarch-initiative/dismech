# ORPHA:1231  Barber-Say syndrome

**ORPHA:1231** — Barber-Say syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Hypertrichosis-atrophic skin-ectropion-macrostomia syndrome

## Definition

A rare congenital malformation syndrome with neonatal onset characterized by congenital generalized hypertrichosis, redundant skin and facial dysmorphism such as ectropion and macrostomia.
