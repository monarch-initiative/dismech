## Health condition(s) or problem(s) studied

Pleural infection
Infections and Infestations
Pleural infection

## Intervention(s)

Patients will be randomly assigned in the ratio 1:1:1:1 to either of the following regimes: Alteplase 10 mg bd (twice a day) intrapleurally + DNase 5 mg bd intrapleurallly; or
Alteplase placebo intrapleurally + DNase 5 mg bd intrapleurally; or
Alteplase 10 mg bd intrapleurally + DNase placebo intrapleurally; or
Alteplase placebo intrapleurally + DNase placebo intrapleurally.

## Key inclusion & exclusion criteria

Inclusion criteria: 1.A clinical presentation compatible with pleural infection
2. Has pleural fluid requiring drainage which is either purulent, gram stain positive, culture positive or acidic with a pH
Exclusion criteria: 1. Age
Age minimum: Age maximum: Gender: Both

## Primary outcome(s)

The primary endpoint will be radiographic improvement in area of pleural collection (between the area of the pleural collection on the CXR at randomisation and the chest radiograph taken on day 6/7).

## Secondary outcome(s)

Secondary outcome measure(s): 1. Changes in blood CRP from baseline to day 6/7
2. Time from randomisation to remain apyrexial for 36 hrs
3. Total pleural fluid drainage
4. Survival and need for surgery at 3 and 12 months
