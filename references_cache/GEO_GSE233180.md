# Thymic B lineage cell landscape in Myasthenia gravis

## Content

Here, we provide a comprehensive and unbiased atlas of hematopoietic cell colonization in thymi derived from 12 immunotherapy-naïve patients with early-onset AChR-Ab+ MG (EOMG) using single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA‑seq).
