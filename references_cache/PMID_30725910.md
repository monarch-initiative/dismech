# Amitriptyline.
**Authors:** Thour A, Alag P, Patel P, Marwaha R

## Content

Amitriptyline is an FDA-approved tricyclic antidepressant used to treat major depressive disorder and is widely prescribed off-label for chronic pain syndromes, migraine prophylaxis, insomnia, anxiety disorders, and other neurologic and psychiatric disorders. Although effective, its anticholinergic, antihistaminic, and cardiovascular effects, clinically significant drug interactions, pharmacogenomic variability, and risk of toxicity require careful patient selection, individualized dosing, and ongoing monitoring. This activity reviews the pharmacology of amitriptyline, including its mechanism of action, approved and off-label indications, dosing strategies, contraindications, adverse effects, pharmacogenomic considerations, and clinically important drug interactions. Participants review monitoring recommendations, recognition and management of overdose, and special considerations for older adults and patients with hepatic or renal impairment and during pregnancy and lactation. Emphasis is placed on applying evidence-based prescribing practices, recognizing adverse effects, optimizing medication safety, and promoting effective interprofessional collaboration to improve therapeutic outcomes and patient safety.
