# CGGV:assertion_3531e2eb-71cd-446c-8956-acb15c3adc7b-2025-01-24T170000.000Z  ROBO4 / aortic valve disease 3

**CGGV:assertion_3531e2eb-71cd-446c-8956-acb15c3adc7b-2025-01-24T170000.000Z** - ROBO4 / aortic valve disease 3 (Moderate)

## Evidence summary

ROBO4 was first reported in relation to autosomal dominant aortic valve disease 3 (AOVD) in 2019 (Gould et al., PMID: 30455415). AOVD is characterized by aortic stenosis and/or bicuspid aortic valve (BAV). It is associated with aortic root aneurysm, ascending aortic aneurysm, and atrial septal defect (ASD) in some individuals. TAAD has appeared independent of BAV in patients with ROBO4 variants (PMID: 30455415 and 32748548). The condition is incompletely penetrant (both in terms of aortic valve disease and aortic aneurysm) with greater penetrance in males compared to females. The mechanism of pathogenicity appears to be loss of function. At least 9 unique variants reported in individuals with AOVD were included in this curation; this included missense, in-frame and splicing variants. In one family the splice-site variant segregated in 7 additional affected family members and 2 unaffected family members (PMID: 30455415). There are other individuals reported with variants in ROBO4 in the literature, however, the variants were not included due to high frequencies in gnomAD v4.1.0 (PMIDs: 30455415, 33631418, 35737725). This gene-disease relationship is also supported by evidence of biochemical function of the gene, multiple expression data, and 3 animal models (a knockout zebrafish, a knockout mouse, a knock-in mouse) replicating the disease phenotype (PMIDs: 18345009, 30455415). In summary, there is moderate evidence supporting the relationship between ROBO4 and autosomal dominant aortic valve disease 3. While more evidence is needed to establish this relationship definitively, no convincing contradictory evidence has emerged. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Congenital Heart Disease GCEP on the meeting date September 9th, 2024 (SOP Version 11). The evidence summary was updated by the Heritable Thoracic Aortic Aneurysm and Dissection subgroup of the Hereditary Cardiovascular Disease Gene Curation Expert Panel on 1/24/2025.
