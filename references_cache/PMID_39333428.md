Pathogenic SOX11 variants have been associated with intellectual developmental 
disorder with microcephaly, and with or without ocular malformations or 
hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (HH) (IDDMOH, OMIM # 615866). In this article, we 
report seven new patients with de novo SOX11 variants. Five of the variants are 
missense, one nonsense, and one whole-gene deletion, most of them are novel 
variants. The main clinical features included neurodevelopmental delay (7/7) and 
intellectual disability (5/7), autism/attention deficit hyperactivity disorder 
(5/7), microcephaly (4/7), short stature (4/7), hypotonia (4/7), and 
clinodactyly of the 5th fingers (5/7). HH was confirmed in two female patients 
with primary amenorrhea, nonvisualized/prepubertal size of the uterus, and 
nonvisualized ovaries. Two of the male patients presented with micropenis, two 
had cryptorchidism, and one had decreased testicular size, which are suggestive 
findings of HH. This article contributes to the clinical characterization of 
patients with SOX11 variants and supports the role of this gene in HH.
