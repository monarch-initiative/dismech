# CGGV:assertion_5e2bc680-f1d4-4b65-baaa-22b4ff22b56e-2026-02-23T170000.000Z  APPL1 / monogenic diabetes

**CGGV:assertion_5e2bc680-f1d4-4b65-baaa-22b4ff22b56e-2026-02-23T170000.000Z** - APPL1 / monogenic diabetes (Refuted)

## Evidence summary

APPL1 was first reported in relation to apparent autosomal dominant diabetes in 2015 (Prudente et al., PMID: 26073777). Evidence supporting this gene-disease relationship includes case level data, segregation data, and experimental data. Summary of genetic and experimental evidence: 7.0 points.

This gene- disease relationship was re-evaluated in October 2025. As a result of this re-evaluation, no further convincing genetic or experimental evidence supporting a gene-disease relationship is available in the literature. Moreover, Siriam et al (PMID: 40779032) reported a lack of enrichment of ultra-rare (i.e., MAF<1:10,000) protein-truncating and predicted-damaging missense variants in APPL1 within a MODY testing cohort of 2,571 patients as compared to population controls (UKBiobank n=155,501 and n= 4,029 Non-Finnish Europeans from gnomAD v3.1.2 all p>0.05). Furthermore, APPL1 pedigrees showed that more than 10% of family members aged 25 or older without diabetes carried the variant. This large-scale study evaluating gene-level burden tests in a MODY cohort (PMID: 40779032) provides strong evidence against APPL1 pathogenicity for monogenic diabetes.

As a result of this reevaluation, APPL1 is refuted as a monogenic diabetes gene with the addition of new genetic evidence (PMID: 40779032).

This classification was approved by the Monogenic Diabetes GCEP on February 13, 2026. Gene Clinical Validity Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) - SOP11.
