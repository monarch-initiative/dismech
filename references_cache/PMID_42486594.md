Biliary atresia is a complex cholangiopathy of infancy characterized by 
extrahepatic biliary obstruction and progressive intrahepatic fibroinflammatory 
injury. It remains the leading indication for liver transplant in children 
without any disease-modifying medical therapies. Herein the authors review 
updated literature relevant to the patient journey from diagnosis through 
end-stage liver disease up to liver transplantation. They focus on the burden of 
disease throughout this course including a focus on actionable areas of 
potential future study that could help transform patient care.
