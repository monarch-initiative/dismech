AMPK (adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase) is phosphorylated 
(AMPK-P) in response to low energy through allosteric activation by Adenosine 
mono- or diphosphate (AMP/ADP). Folliculin (FLCN) and the FLCN-interacting 
proteins 1 and 2 (FNIP1, 2) modulate AMPK. FNIP1 deficiency patients have a 
AMPK-P gain of function phenotype with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, 
Wolff-Parkinson-White pre-excitation syndrome, myopathy of skeletal muscles and 
combined immunodeficiency.
