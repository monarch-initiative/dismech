# Fanconi anemia patient with bilaterally hypoplastic scapula and unilateral winging associated with scoliosis and rib abnormality.

Fanconi anemia is an autosomal recessive disease characterized by bone marrow 
failure, variable congenital physical abnormalities, and predisposition to 
hematologic malignancy and several solid tumors. The most frequently associated 
congenital malformations are those of the skeletal system, mainly radius and 
thumb. Herein, we report bilaterally hypoplastic scapula with unilateral winging 
associated with scoliosis and rib abnormality, a previously unreported skeletal 
abnormality in Fanconi anemia patients.
