# Comprehensive expression profiles of mRNAs, lncRNAs and miRNAs in Kashin-Beck Disease identified by RNA-sequencing

## Content

RNA‐seq technology to detect the differentially expressed mRNAs, lncRNAs and miRNAs in KBD patients. The database of Gene Ontology (GO) and the Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEEG) were employed to conduct functional annotation and pathway enriched analysis for differential expression.
