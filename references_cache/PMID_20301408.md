# Pulmonary Fibrosis Predisposition Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Garcia CK, Talbert JL

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Briefly describe the clinical characteristics of pulmonary fibrosis. 2.. Review genetic causes of pulmonary fibrosis. 3.. Review the differential diagnosis of pulmonary fibrosis. 4.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of pulmonary fibrosis in a proband. 5.. Review management of pulmonary fibrosis. 6.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with a genetic predisposition to pulmonary fibrosis.
