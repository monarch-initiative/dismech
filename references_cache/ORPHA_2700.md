# ORPHA:2700  Noma

**ORPHA:2700** — Noma (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Cancrum oris

## Definition

An orofacial gangrenous disease characterized by the rapid, severe destruction of the soft and osseous tissues of the face.

## Natural history
