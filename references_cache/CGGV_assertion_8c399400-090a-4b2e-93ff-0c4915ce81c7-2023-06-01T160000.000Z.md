**CGGV:assertion_8c399400-090a-4b2e-93ff-0c4915ce81c7-2023-06-01T160000.000Z** - SLC17A8 / nonsyndromic genetic hearing loss (Definitive)

## Evidence summary

The SLC17A8 gene was first associated in relation to autosomal dominant nonsyndromic hearing loss in 2008 (Ruel et al., PMID: 18674745). At least 8 unique variants (missense, frameshift, splice site) have been reported in humans in 9 probands in 8​ publications (PMIDs: 18674745, 26797701, 28647561, 24082139, 26969326, 34440452, 33724713, 34145196). Variants in this gene segregated with disease in 28 additional members in two families (PMID: 18674745). This gene-disease relationship is supported by expression studies, two mouse models, a mouse rescue, and a zebrafish model (PMIDs: 18674745, 18215623, 18305245, 22841313, 24676347, 25122905). In summary, SCL17A8 is definitively associated with autosomal dominant nonsyndromic hearing loss. This has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic settings, and has been upheld over time. This gene-disease pair was originally evaluated as Strong by the ClinGen Hearing Loss Working Group on 9/12/2018 as per SOP v6. It was reevaluated on 6/1/2023 as per SOP v9. As a result of this reevaluation, the classification increased from Strong to Definitive due to additional case level evidence (PMIDs: 24082139, 26969326, 34440452, 33724713).
