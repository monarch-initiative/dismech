# ORPHA:140952  Syndactyly-telecanthus-anogenital and renal malformations syndrome

**ORPHA:140952** — Syndactyly-telecanthus-anogenital and renal malformations syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- STAR syndrome

## Definition

A rare malformation syndrome characterized by the association of toe syndactyly, facial dysmorphism including telecanthus (abnormal distance between the eyes) and a broad nasal tip, urogenital malformations and anal atresia.

## Inheritance

- X-linked dominant

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
