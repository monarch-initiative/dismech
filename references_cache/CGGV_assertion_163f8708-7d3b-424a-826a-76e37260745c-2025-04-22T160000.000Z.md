# CGGV:assertion_163f8708-7d3b-424a-826a-76e37260745c-2025-04-22T160000.000Z  LMX1A / autosomal dominant nonsyndromic hearing loss

**CGGV:assertion_163f8708-7d3b-424a-826a-76e37260745c-2025-04-22T160000.000Z** - LMX1A / autosomal dominant nonsyndromic hearing loss (Definitive)

## Evidence summary

LMX1A was first reported in relation to autosomal dominant nonsyndromic deafness in 2018 (Wesdorp et al., PMID: 29754270). In affected probands, the degree of hearing loss ranges from mild to profound, and the age of onset from congenital to middle adulthood. Also, the hearing loss may be symmetrical or asymmetrical. 12 variants (missense, nonsense, frameshift) that have been reported in at least 26 probands in 7 publications (PMIDs: 29754270, 32840933, 35254497, 35711095, 36140227, 36519758, 38056549) are included in this curation. The mechanism of pathogenicity is reported to be loss-of-function. This gene-disease relationship is also supported by experimental evidence (mouse models; PMIDs: 10693804, 23226461; https://www.jax.org/strain/002624). Three homozygous mutant mouse models show deafness, inner ear structural anomalies, shortened tails, white belly spotting, and atypical behavior. In summary, there is definitive evidence supporting the relationship between LMX1A and autosomal dominant nonsyndromic deafness. This has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic settings, and has been upheld over time. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Hearing Loss GCEP on the meeting date 4/16/25 (SOP Version 11).
