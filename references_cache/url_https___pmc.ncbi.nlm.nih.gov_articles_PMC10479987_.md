<div><div>This corrects the article "<a>Expanding the phenotype: Four new cases and hope for treatment in Bachmann‐Bupp syndrome</a>" in  volume 185 on page 3485.</div></div>
</div></section></section><section><section><hr/>
<p>In the originally published article, Table 1 contained the variant c.1342A&gt;G. This variant should have been 1342A&gt;T.</p>
<p>We apologize for this error.</p>

<p>VanSickle, E. A., Michael, J., Bachmann, A. S., Rajasekaran, S., Prokop, J. W., Kuzniecky, R., Hofstede, F. C., Steindl, K., Rauch, A., Lipson, M. H., &amp; Bupp, C. P. (2021). Expanding the phenotype: Four new cases and hope for treatment in Bachmann–Bupp syndrome. <em>American Journal of Medical Genetics Part A</em>, 185, 3485–3493. <a>https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.a.62473</a>.</p></section><footer><hr/>
