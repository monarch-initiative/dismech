# Bordetella bronchiseptica and Bordetella pertussis: Similarities and Differences in Infection, Immuno-Modulation, and Vaccine Considerations
**Authors:** Beatriz Miguelena Chamorro, Karelle De Luca, Gokul Swaminathan, Stéphanie Longet, Egbert Mundt, Stéphane Paul
**Journal:** Clinical Microbiology Reviews (2023)
**DOI:** [10.1128/cmr.00164-22](https://doi.org/10.1128/cmr.00164-22)

## Content

Bordetella pertussis
            and
            Bordetella bronchiseptica
            belong to the genus
            Bordetella
            , which comprises 14 other species.
            B. pertussis
            is responsible for whooping cough in humans, a severe infection in children and less severe or chronic in adults. These infections are restricted to humans and currently increasing worldwide.
            B. bronchiseptica
            is involved in diverse respiratory infections in a wide range of mammals.
