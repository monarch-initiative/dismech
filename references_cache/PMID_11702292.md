# Histologic proof of acalculous cholecystitis due to Cyclospora cayetanensis.
**Authors:** Zar FA, El-Bayoumi E, Yungbluth MM
**Journal:** Clin Infect Dis (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1086/324586](https://doi.org/10.1086/324586)

Histologic proof of acalculous cholecystitis due to Cyclospora cayetanensis.

Zar FA(1), El-Bayoumi E, Yungbluth MM.

Author information:
(1)Saint Francis Hospital, Evanston, IL 60202, USA. fredzar@aol.com

It is suspected that Cyclospora cayetanensis can infect the biliary tract in 
persons with human immunodeficiency virus infection, but histologic proof has 
never been published. We report a case of acalculous cholecystitis in a person 
with acquired immunodeficiency syndrome who required cholecystectomy and 
demonstrated C. cayetanensis in the gallbladder epithelium.
