# Eladocagene Exuparvovec: First Approval.

Eladocagene exuparvovec (Upstaza™) is a gene therapy developed by PTC
Therapeutics for the treatment of human aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase
(AADC) deficiency. Eladocagene exuparvovec comprises an adeno-associated virus
vector that delivers the dopa decarboxylase (DDC) gene, the gene for human AADC.
Eladocagene exuparvovec was approved in July 2022 in the EU for the treatment of
patients aged 18 months and older with a clinical, molecular, and genetically
confirmed diagnosis of AADC deficiency with a severe phenotype (i.e. patients
who cannot sit, stand or walk). This article summarizes the milestones in the
development of eladocagene exuparvovec leading to this first approval for the
treatment of patients aged 18 months and older with AADC deficiency.
