# A translation re-initiation variant in KLHL24 also causes epidermolysis bullosa simplex and dilated cardiomyopathy via intermediate filament degradation.

This study shows that gain-of-function variants in KLHL24 causing EBS and DCM, 
do not only originate in the start-codon and suggest that any nonsense-inducing 
variant affecting nucleotides c.4_84 will likely cause the same effect on 
protein level and a similar potential lethal phenotype.
