## Disease description

X-linked isolated growth hormone deficiency (GHD) or combined pituitary hormone deficiency (CPHD) patients with or without intellectual disability [@pmid:24346842].

## Mechanism

Polyalanine expansions leading to aggresome formation and impaired transcriptional activity [@pmid:17127446].

## Age of onset

Typical: 0-3 (small sample size) [@pmid:19654509; @pmid:21289259]; range: 0-9 [@pmid:19654509].

## Prevalence

- Details: 3 families reported, however they were distributed across the world [@omim:300123].

## Details

Expansion to 22-26 repeats or contraction to 8 repeats can cause disease, as reported in 3 families [@genereviews:NBK535148].
