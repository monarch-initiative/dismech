# Transcriptomic differences underlying the Activin-A induced large osteoclast formation in both healthy control and Fibrodysplasia Ossificans Progressiva osteoclasts

## Content

Activin-A has been identified as the driver for heterotopic ossification in FOP. It's effect on osteoclast formation is not extensively studied in control or FOP samples.
