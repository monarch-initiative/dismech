# Spatial Transcriptomics of Patients with Kaposi Sarcoma Identifies Mechanisms of Immune Evasion

## Content

We performed Visium Spatial on Seven Skin Samples with Kapiosi Sarcoma tumor. For four of the samples, we included custom probes for five KSHV genes that allowed us to measure human and KSHV expression patterns at the same time.
