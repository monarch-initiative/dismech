# A Phase I First-in-human Study to Investigate Safety, Tolerability, and Pharmacokinetics of AZD4063 in Adults With Phospholamban R14del Dilated Cardiomyopathy

## Content

The purpose of the study is to assess the safety, tolerability and the pharmacokinetics (PK) of AZD4063 after single dose administration in participants with phospholamban (PLN) R14del dilated cardiomyopathy.
