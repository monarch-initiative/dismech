# Primary Pyruvate Dehydrogenase Complex Deficiency Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Ganetzky R, McCormick EM, Falk MJ

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of primary pyruvate dehydrogenase complex deficiency (PDCD). 2.. Review the genetic causes of primary PDCD. 3.. Review the differential diagnosis of primary PDCD. 4.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of primary PDCD in a proband (when possible). 5.. Inform (when possible) medical management of primary PDCD based on genetic cause. 6.. Inform genetic risk assessment of family members of a proband with primary PDCD.
