# Shashi XLMR syndrome: report of a second family.
**Authors:** Castro NH, dos Santos RC, Nelson R, Beçak W, Hane B, Lindsey CJ, Lubs HA, Stevenson RE, Schwartz CE
**Journal:** Am J Med Genet A (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1002/ajmg.a.10888](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.a.10888)

## Content

1. Am J Med Genet A. 2003 Apr 1;118A(1):49-51. doi: 10.1002/ajmg.a.10888.

Shashi XLMR syndrome: report of a second family.

Castro NH(1), dos Santos RC, Nelson R, Beçak W, Hane B, Lindsey CJ, Lubs HA, 
Stevenson RE, Schwartz CE.

Author information:
(1)Laboratorio de Genetica, Instituto Butantan, Sao Paulo, Brazil.

This report describes a family with mental retardation in two brothers. The 
pedigree is consistent with either X-linked mental retardation or autosomal 
recessive inheritance. The clinical features consist of coarse face, prominent 
lower lip, large testes, and obesity. This same constellation of findings was 
observed in a family with X-linked mental retardation (XLMR) reported by Shashi 
et al. [2000: Am J Hum Genet 66:469-479]. Furthermore, haplotype analysis was 
consistent with localization of the Shashi XLMR syndrome in Xq26-q27. Thus, the 
family likely represents a second occurrence of the Shashi XLMR syndrome.
