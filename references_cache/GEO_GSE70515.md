# Gene expression signature of bone lesion waldenstrom macroglobulinemia and myeloma

## Content

Bone lesion in waldenstom macroglobulinemia (WM) is less common. In the present study, we compaired gene expresion signature of bone lesion in WM compaired to multiple myeloma (MM). In WM, APRIL and HBGB1 were up-regulated compaired to MM.
