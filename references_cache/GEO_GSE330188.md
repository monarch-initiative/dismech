# Crnic Institute Human Trisome Project - Tofacitinib for Immune Skin Conditions in Down Syndrome: PolyA RNAseq from whole blood

## Content

Analysis of steady-state mRNA levels in whole blood of subjects with Down syndrome (trisomy 21) and qualifying moderate-to-severe immune skin conditions. This dataset is part of the Human Trisome Project run by the Linda Crnic Institute for Down Syndrome at University of Colorado Anschutz. http://www.trisome.org/
