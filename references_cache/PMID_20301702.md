The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of 
holoprosencephaly; 2.. Review the genetic causes of holoprosencephaly; 3.. 
Provide an evaluation strategy to identify (when possible) the genetic cause of 
holoprosencephaly in a proband; 4.. Inform genetic counseling of family members 
of an individual with holoprosencephaly.
