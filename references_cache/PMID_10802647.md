# Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 4B is caused by mutations in the gene encoding myotubularin-related protein-2.
**Authors:** Bolino A, Muglia M, Conforti FL, LeGuern E, Salih MA, Georgiou DM, Christodoulou K, Hausmanowa-Petrusewicz I, Mandich P, Schenone A, Gambardella A, Bono F, Quattrone A, Devoto M, Monaco AP

## Content

1. Nat Genet. 2000 May;25(1):17-9. doi: 10.1038/75542.

Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 4B is caused by mutations in the gene encoding 
myotubularin-related protein-2.

Bolino A(1), Muglia M, Conforti FL, LeGuern E, Salih MA, Georgiou DM, 
Christodoulou K, Hausmanowa-Petrusewicz I, Mandich P, Schenone A, Gambardella A,

Author information:
(1)Wellcome Trust Centre for Human Genetics, Oxford, UK.

A gene mutated in Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 4B (CMT4B), an autosomal 
recessive demyelinating neuropathy with myelin outfoldings, has been mapped on 
chromosome 11q22. Using a positional-cloning strategy, we identified in 
unrelated CMT4B patients mutations occurring in the gene MTMR2, encoding 
myotubularin-related protein-2, a dual specificity phosphatase (DSP).
