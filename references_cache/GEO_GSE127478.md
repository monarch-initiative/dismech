# Mitochondrial 3243A > G mutation confers pro-atherogenic and pro-inflammatory properties in MELAS iPS derived endothelial cells

## Content

We obtained three independent sets of endothelial cells differentiated from iPSCs derived from a Mitochondrial encephalomyopathy, lactic acidosis, and stroke-like episodes (MELAS) patient as well as iPSCs that has been corrected for the mitochondrial mutation.
