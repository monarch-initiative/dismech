In the original version of this article, the listed MO (tMO: 
GTC TCG ATA TGC TGC CAA GGT CAT G) for zebrafish 
experiment in the publication is incorrect. The authors 
revised this as follows:
"For morpholino (MO) microinjection, fertilized embryos 
at one-cell stage were injected with 2 ng antisense MO 
designed against agtpbp1 (sMO: 5’-GGT GTT CTG TAC GAA 
AAC ATC AAG T-3’) by using a nanoliter injector (World 
Precision Instrument Inc., Sarasota, FL).
The original article has been corrected.
