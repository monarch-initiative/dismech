# Megacystis-Microcolon-Intestinal Hypoperistalsis Syndrome Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Ambartsumyan L

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of megacystis-microcolon-intestinal hypoperistalsis syndrome (MMIHS); 2.. Review the genetic causes of MMIHS; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of MMIHS in a proband (when possible); 4.. Review management of MMIHS; 5.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with MMIHS.
