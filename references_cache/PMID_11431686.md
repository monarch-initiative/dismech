# Mutation of POLG is associated with progressive external ophthalmoplegia characterized by mtDNA deletions.
**Authors:** Van Goethem G, Dermaut B, Löfgren A, Martin JJ, Van Broeckhoven C
**Journal:** Nat Genet (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1038/90034](https://doi.org/10.1038/90034)

## Content

1. Nat Genet. 2001 Jul;28(3):211-2. doi: 10.1038/90034.

Mutation of POLG is associated with progressive external ophthalmoplegia 
characterized by mtDNA deletions.

Van Goethem G(1), Dermaut B, Löfgren A, Martin JJ, Van Broeckhoven C.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Genetics, Flanders Interuniversity Institute for 
Biotechnology, Born-Bunge Foundation, University of Antwerp, Universiteitsplein 
1; B-2610 Antwerpen, Belgium.

Progressive external ophthalmoplegias (PEO) characterized by accumulation of 
large-scale mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) deletions are rare human diseases. We 
mapped a new locus for dominant PEO at 15q22-q26 in a Belgian pedigree and 
identified a heterozygous mutation (Y955C) in the polymerase motif B of the 
mtDNA polymerase gamma (POLG). We identified three additional POLG missense 
mutations compatible with recessive PEO In two nuclear families. POLG is the 
only DNA polymerase responsible for mtDNA replication.
