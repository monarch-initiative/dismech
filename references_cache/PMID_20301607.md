# Genetic Hearing Loss Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Shearer AE, Hildebrand MS, Odell AM, Smith RJH

## Content

The purpose of this GeneReview is to: 1.. Describe the audiometric and clinical aspects of genetic hearing loss; 2.. Review the causes of genetic hearing loss; 3.. Review the differential diagnosis of genetic hearing loss; 4.. Explain the evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of hearing loss in a proband; 5.. Review the management of genetic hearing loss; 6.. Inform the genetic counseling of an individual with genetic hearing loss and their family members.
