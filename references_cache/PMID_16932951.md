# Mutations in the CLCN2 gene are a rare cause of idiopathic generalized epilepsy syndromes.

Mutations in the chloride channel gene CLCN2 have been reported in families with 
generalized and focal epilepsy syndromes. To evaluate the contribution of 
mutations in the CLCN2 gene to the etiology of epilepsies in our population, we 
screened 96 patients with different epilepsy syndromes and a putative genetic 
background. No definite mutations were found in our study population. We 
conclude that mutations in the CLCN2 gene are only a rare cause of idiopathic 
generalized epilepsy.
