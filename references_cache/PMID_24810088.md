A 7-year-old boy presented with episodic blindness for the last 2 months with 
occipital paroxysms and fixation-off sensitivity on electroencephalography 
(EEG). The clinico-EEG features were suggestive of idiopathic childhood 
occipital epilepsy of Gastaut. The interesting phenomenon of fixation-off 
sensitivity is discussed.
