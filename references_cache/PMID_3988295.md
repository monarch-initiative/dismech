# Sneezing on exposure to bright light as an inherited response.
**Authors:** Forrester JM

## Content

1. Hum Hered. 1985;35(2):113-4. doi: 10.1159/000153527.

Sneezing on exposure to bright light as an inherited response.

Forrester JM.

Sneezing in response to bright light has been found in 25% of a sample of the
British population, and pedigrees are compatible with an autosomal dominant mode
of inheritance. The mechanism of the response is discussed.
