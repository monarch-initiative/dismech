# Melatonin for sleep disorders in people with autism: Systematic review and meta-analysis.

Melatonin is a potential therapeutic intervention for improving sleep quality in
people with autistic spectrum disorder (ASD). We investigate the effect of using
melatonin as a sleep disorder treatment in people with ASD. Interventionist
studies were searched in seven databases. A total of 595 references were
identified, 15 of which were eligible for the systemic review and meta-analysis.
Melatonin use presented a positive effect on total sleep time (standardized mean
difference- SMD = 0.78; 95%CI = 0.35; 1.21; I2 = 91%), on sleep latency
(SMD = 1.23; 95%CI = 0.35; 2.11; I2 = 94%), and on sleep efficiency
(SMD = -0.70; 95%CI = -1.23; -0.16; I2 = 91%) when comparing the intervention
group with the placebo/control group via the global analysis. According to the
global analysis, the wake after sleep onset and night awakening parameters were
not statistically significant. Melatonin has possible efficacy over total time,
latency, and efficiency sleep parameters.
