Bardet-Biedl syndrome (BBS) is an autosomal recessive ciliopathy with a wide 
range of symptoms including obesity, retinal dystrophy, polycystic kidney 
disease, polydactyly, hypogonadism and learning difficulties. Here we describe 
the successful generation of an induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) KCi001-A 
from a BBS patient compound heterozygous for two disease causing BBS1 variants 
c.1169T>G, p. (Met390Arg)/c.1135G>C, p.(Gly370Arg). Resource table.
