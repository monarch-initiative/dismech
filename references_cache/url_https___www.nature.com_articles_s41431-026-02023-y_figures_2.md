<div>Fig. 2: Deletion of exon 3 and 4 of CCDC32 in affected individuals. | European Journal of Human Genetics</div><article><header><h1>Fig. 2: Deletion of exon 3 and 4 of <i>CCDC32</i> in affected individuals.</h1><p>From: <a>Two siblings with <i>CCDC32</i>-related cardiofacioneurodevelopmental syndrome diagnosed by clinical RNA-sequencing and review of literature</a></p></header><div><figure><div><div><picture><source/><img/></picture></div></div><div><p><b>A</b> Volcano plot for RNA-seq of individual 1 shows z-scores (X-axis) and log10(p-values) (Y-axis) for assessed genes. No gene in the ID and MCA panels showed significant downregulation (Z-score &lt; −4), but <i>CCDC32</i> (not included in the ID and MCA panels) exhibited notable downregulation with a Z-score of -8.20. <b>B</b> Sashimi plot at the <i>CCDC32</i> locus showing RNA-seq of fibroblast cultured with or without cycloheximide (CHX) for individual 1 and unrelated controls, combined with WES tracks for both affected children and carrier parents, identifying the deletion in both affected children. <b>C</b> Confirmation of the exon 3 and 4 deletion. Left panel: Deletion-specific PCR for affected individuals, heterozygous parents, and unrelated control. Arrows indicate the wildtype (wt) band at 2425 bp and deletion specific (variant) band at 330 bp. 1 Kb Plus DNA ladder (Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc, Waltham, MA, USA) was used. Upper panel: Sanger sequencing of deletion-specific PCR product for individual 1, reveals breakpoints and genomic coordinates of the biallelic <i>CCDC32</i> deletion. Lower panel: Schematic representation of the predicted effect of the encountered genomic deletion in <i>CCDC32</i>. Splicing occurs between exons 2 and 3, splicing to a 3’ predicted acceptor splice site ~33 kb upstream. This splicing event results in an altered <i>CCDC32</i> mRNA lacking exons 3 and 4.</p></div><div><a><span>Back to article page</span><svg><use></use></svg></a></div></figure></div></article>
