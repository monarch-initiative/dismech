## Disease description

A condition that primarily affects the development of the bones and teeth. Characteristic features include underdeveloped or absent collarbones (clavicles); dental abnormalities; and delayed closing of the spaces between the skull bones (fontanels). Other features may include decreased bone density (osteopenia), osteoporosis, hearing loss, bone abnormalities of the hands, and recurrent sinus and ear infections. [@mondo:0007340].

## Mechanism

Polyalanine expansion leading to haploinsufficiency [@pmid:26220009].
