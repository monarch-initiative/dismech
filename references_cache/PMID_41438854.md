Findings based on asbestosis patients treated between 2004 and 2024 reveal a
modest reduction in the rate of FVC decline (mean (95% CI) difference 7.0
(-5.8-19.7) mL·month-1, p=0.22) and D LCO decline (0.11% (-0.19-0.41%), p=0.30)
after starting pirfenidone https://bit.ly/45fZ5TR.
