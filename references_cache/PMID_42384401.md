# BRAF Inhibition in Congenital Nevi and Neural Melanosis.
**Authors:** Doolan BJ, Levy R, McKeown T, Das A, Ramaswamy V
**Journal:** JAMA Dermatol (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1001/jamadermatol.2026.1629](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamadermatol.2026.1629)

## Content

This case report describes sustained neurologic and cutaneous improvement following BRAF inhibition in a patient with BRAF V600E–variant neurocutaneous melanosis.
