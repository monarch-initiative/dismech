# Wilms Tumor Predisposition.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Turner JT, Brzezinski J, Dome JS

## Content

The purpose of this GeneReview is to: 1.. Briefly describe the clinical characteristics of Wilms tumor; 2.. Review the mechanisms of predisposition to Wilms tumor; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to determine if a proband with Wilms tumor has a predisposition to Wilms tumor, identify the genetic or epigenetic mechanism for Wilms tumor, and determine risks for additional medical complications; 4.. Review management (e.g., tumor screening) recommendations for individuals with a genetic predisposition to Wilms tumor; 5.. Review genetic counseling issues including mode of inheritance, recurrence risk, and evaluation of relatives at risk based on the underlying genetic mechanism.
