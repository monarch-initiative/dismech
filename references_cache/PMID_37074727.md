PURPOSE: To evaluate the efficacy and safety of dabrafenib-trametinib-131I for
the treatment of radioactive iodine refractory metastatic differentiated thyroid
cancer (DTC) with a BRAF p.V600E mutation.
PATIENTS AND METHODS: A prospective phase II trial including patients with
RECIST progression within 18 months and no lesion > 3 cm. Following a baseline
recombinant human (rh)TSH-stimulated diagnostic whole-body scan (dc1-WBS),
dabrafenib and trametinib were given for 42 days. A second rhTSH-stimulated dc
WBS (dc2-WBS) was done at day 28 and 131I (5.5 GBq-150 mCi after rhTSH) was
administered at day 35. Primary endpoint was the 6-month RECIST objective
response rate. In case of partial response (PR) at 6 or 12 months, a second
treatment course could be given. Among 24 enrolled patients, 21 were evaluable
at 6 months.
RESULTS: Abnormal 131I uptake was present on 5%, 65%, and 95% of the dc1-WBS,
dc2-WBS, and post-therapy scans, respectively. At 6 months, PR was achieved in
38%, stable disease in 52%, and progressive disease (PD) in 10%. Ten patients
received a second treatment course: one complete response and 6 PRs were
observed at 6 months. The median progression-free survival (PFS) was not
reached. The 12- and 24-month PFS were 82% and 68%, respectively. One death due
to PD occurred at 24 months. Adverse events (AE) occurred in 96% of the
patients, with 10 grade 3-4 AEs in 7 patients.
CONCLUSIONS: Dabrafenib-trametinib is effective in BRAF p.V600E-mutated DTC
patients for restoring 131I uptake with PR observed 6 months after 131I
administration in 38% of the patients.

Dear Editor, 47 
RAS mutations are present in one third of follicular-cell derived thyroid cancer ( TC) 48 
(1). They are more frequent in follicular TC and in follicular subtype of papillary TC compared 49 
with other subtypes, with NRAS being more frequently mutated than HRAS or KRAS (2, 3). 50 
In the case of RAS mutations, the activation of the Mitogen -Activated Protein (MAP) kinase 51 
pathway is less intense than in case of BRAFp.V600E mutation. Even though RAS mutated 52 
tumors are better differentiated than BRAFp.V600E mutated tumors, t he expression of 53 
proteins involved in the iodine metabolism  is decreased . These abnormalities  may be 54 
reverted by the inhibition of the MAPK pathway (4). In a pioneer study on redifferentiation of  55 
RAS mutated radioactive iodine refractory ( RAIR) TC with selumetinib (a MEK inhibitor),  56 
significant 131I tumor uptake reappeared in all 5 patients, resulting in partial tumor responses 57 
in 4 (80%) of the patients treated with 131I (5).  58 
In a recent phase II prospective trial in 21 patients with a BRAFp.V600E mutated 59 
RAIR DTC, dabrafenib-trametinib was associated with reinduction of  radioiodine uptake in 60 
95% of the patients with a 6-months response rate of 3 8%, after the administration of a high 61 
131I activity following rhTSH injections  (6). In the present study focused on metastatic RAIR 62 
RAS-mutated DTC, we investigated the efficacy and safety of trametinib treatment before the 63 
administration of a high 131I activity (following rhTSH injections). 64

 MERAIODE is a multicentre, prospective phase II trial in patients with RAIR DTC, with 66 
two independent cohorts with similar designs, one for BRAF p.V600E and one for RAS 67 
mutated patients (NCT 03244956). The results of BRAFpV600E-mutated cohort study were 68 
recently published (6). Detailed inclusion and exclusion criteria, procedures and objectives, 69 
available in the Supplementary Table 1, were similar to the BRAFp.V600E mutated cohort 70 
except for treatment consisting in trametinib-131I.   71

mutated tumors. Indeed, despite being RAIR,  3 of the 10 patients  had abnormal 131I uptake 156

administration of 131I, contrary to the association of anti BRAF and anti MEK in 165

Conclusion  188 
The treatment with tr ametinib in RAS mutated DTC is not highly effective for 189 
restoring/increasing 131I uptake and the administration of high activity  (150mCi, 3700MBq) of 190 
131I is followed by a low response rate. Other strategies than trametinib alone should be 191 
developed for the redifferentiation of RAS mutated RAIR DTC. 192
