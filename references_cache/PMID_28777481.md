Researchers have identified a subset of Holstein having a range of skeletal 
deformities, including vertebral anomalies, referred to as complex vertebral 
malformation due to mutations in the SLC35A3 gene. Here, we report the first 
case in humans of SLC35A3-related vertebral anomalies. Our patient had 
prenatally diagnosed anomalous vertebrae, including butterfly, and hemivertebrae 
throughout the spine, as well as cleft palate, micrognathia, patent foramen 
ovale, patent ductus arteriosus, posterior embryotoxon, short limbs, 
camptodactyly, talipes valgus, rocker bottom feet, and facial dysmorphism 
including proptosis, nevus flammeus, and a cupped left ear. Clinical exome 
sequencing revealed a novel missense homozygous mutation in SLC35A3. Follow-up 
biochemical analysis confirmed abnormal protein glycosylation, consistent with a 
defective Golgi UDP-GlcNAc transporter, validating the mutations. Congenital 
disorders of glycosylation, including SLC35A3-CDG, can present as a wide 
phenotypic spectrum, including skeletal dysplasia. Previously reported patients 
with SLC35A3-CDG have been described with syndromic autism, epilepsy, and 
arthrogryposis.
