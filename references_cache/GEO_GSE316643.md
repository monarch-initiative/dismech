# Spatial transcriptomic profiling of human heart tissue obtained from mRNA vaccine-associated myocarditis cases using GeoMx DSP

## Content

Spatial transcriptomic analysis was performed using the NanoString GeoMx Digital Spatial Profiler to characterize region-specific gene expression patterns in heart tissue sample obtained from mRNA vaccine-associated myocarditis cases.
