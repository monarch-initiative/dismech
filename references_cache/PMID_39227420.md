# Phenotypic spectrum of iron-sulfur cluster assembly gene IBA57 mutations: c.286 T > C identified as a hotspot mutation in Chinese patients with a stable natural history.
**Authors:** Jiang H, Xu C, Duan R, Liu Z, Ren X, Li J, Chen C, Wang H, Han T, Tian X, Duan X, Song M, Li T, Fang F
**Journal:** J Hum Genet (2025)
**DOI:** [10.1038/s10038-024-01291-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s10038-024-01291-0)

## Content

Mutations in IBA57 disrupt iron-sulfur clusters maturation, causing a rare mitochondrial disease. Clinical manifestations vary from neonatal lethality to childhood-onset spastic paraparesis, yet the ethnic heterogeneity and natural history remain unclear, necessitating further exploration. This study aimed to delineate the genotype-phenotype correlation of IBA57 mutations by analyzing diverse clinical presentations. We report 11 Chinese patients and include literature-reported cases, totaling 61 patients enrolled for analysis. Clinical, neuroimaging, genetic, and disease progression information were collected. Among these, 46 presented as multiple mitochondrial dysfunctions syndrome 3 (MMDS3), with 58.7% originating from Chinese population. Based on disease course, we propose three clinical subtypes: neonatal, infant and childhood subtypes. Neonatal cases universally displayed hypotonia and respiratory distress at presentation, deceased within three months. Most infancy and childhood cases exhibited developmental regression and impaired motor function. Cavitating leukoencephalopathy was a typical neuroimaging finding in MMDS3 patients. The c.286 T > C mutation was reported in 85.2% of Chinese patients. A significantly lower mortality rate was observed compared to the non-Chinese group (P = 0.002), with a survival rate exceeding 90% at 5 years, indicating a relatively stable disease progression. Fifteen cases from three families manifested the spastic paraplegia 74 phenotype, demonstrating normal development before onset, with common clinical manifestations including spastic paraplegia (14/15), visual impairment (10/13), and peripheral neuropathy (9/13). In conclusion, this study indicates a hotspot mutation in Chinese and analyses the disease progression with different clinical subtypes.

Abstract
Mutations in IBA57 disrupt iron-sulfur clusters maturation, causing a rare mitochondrial disease. Clinical
manifestations vary from neonatal lethality to childhood-onset spastic paraparesis, yet the ethnic
heterogeneity and natural history remain unclear, necessitating further exploration. This study aimed to
delineate the genotype-phenotype correlation of IBA57 mutations by analyzing diverse clinical
presentations. We report 11 Chinese patients and include literature-reported cases, totaling 61 patients
enrolled for analysis. Clinical, neuroimaging, genetic and disease progression information were collected.
Among these, 46 presented as multiple mitochondrial dysfunctions syndrome 3 (MMDS3), with 58.7%
originating from Chinese population. Based on disease course, we propose three clinical subtypes:
neonatal, infant and childhood subtypes. Neonatal cases universally displayed hypotonia and respiratory
distress at presentation, deceased within three months. Most infancy and childhood cases exhibited
developmental regression and impaired motor function. Cavitating leukoencephalopathy was a typical
neuroimaging �nding in MMDS3 patients. The c.286T > C mutation was reported in 85.2% of Chinese
patients. A signi�cantly lower mortality rate was observed compared to the non-Chinese group (P = 
0.002), with a survival rate exceeding 90% at 5 years, indicating a relatively stable disease progression.
Fifteen cases from three families manifested the spastic paraplegia 74 phenotype, demonstrating normal
development before onset, with common clinical manifestations including spastic paraplegia (14/15),
visual impairment (10/13), and peripheral neuropathy (9/13).
Conclusion: Despite a diverse phenotypic spectrum, neonatal-onset cases exhibit severe symptoms and a
poor prognosis. More than half of the patients were Chinese, and c.286T > C variant was a hotspot
mutation in Chinese population, associated with a stable natural history.

Introduction

Page 4/20
The IBA57 encodes a protein pivotal for the maturation of mitochondrial iron-sulfur clusters (ISC),
essential inorganic cofactors crucial for cellular viability across diverse life forms[1]. Disruptions in the
ISC biosynthesis pathway not only negatively impact the mitochondrial respiratory chain complexes but
also affect mitochondrial aconitase and lipoic acid synthase (LIAS)[2]. Lipoic acid serves as a necessary
cofactor for multiple mitochondrial proteins, including pyruvate dehydrogenase complex (PDHc), alpha-
ketoglutarate dehydrogenase ( α -KDH), branched-chain keto acid dehydrogenase (BCKDH), and the H
protein of the glycine cleavage system (H-GCS)[3, 4]. Consequently, de�ciencies in IBA57 can compromise
mitochondrial respiratory chain activity, the tricarboxylic acid cycle, and glycine metabolism.
Clinical manifestations resulting from IBA57 mutations exhibit a broad spectrum, ranging from neonatal
fatality to the onset of mild neurological symptoms in childhood[5]. Two distinct phenotypes are
described in OMIM database: multiple mitochondrial dysfunctions syndrome 3 (MIM#615330, MMDS3
reported in 2013) and spastic paraplegia 74 (MIM #616451, SP74 reported in 2015)[6, 7]. Despite vast
clinical heterogeneity, the majority patients present with bilateral symmetrical white matter lesions,
observed as focal cavities and restricted diffusion on brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)[8]. To
date, research on IBA57 variations has primarily comprised case report/series, with only a limited number
of literature reviews. Furthermore, these reviews lack comparative analyses of ethnic disparities, clinical
phenotype severity, and natural disease progression. According to our unpublished data, Chinese patients
appear to exhibit a stable disease progression and a low mortality rate. Therefore, the aim of this study is
to elucidate the correlation between genotypes and phenotypes of IBA57 mutations by analyzing
differences among various clinical manifestations and ethnic groups.
Materials and methods
Participants
This retrospective cohort study enrolled patients identi�ed from the Chinese Mitochondrial Disease
Network Registry (mitoC-NET, http://www.mitodb.com.cn), spanning January 2002 to October 2023.
Inclusion criteria involved: (1) Clinical diagnosis of mitochondrial leukoencephalopathy; (2) Other
mitochondrial diseases attribute to IBA57 variants; (3) Obtained informed consent from patients or their
guardians. Exclusion criteria included: (1) Absence of a pathogenic or likely pathogenic gene; (2)
Leukoencephalopathy caused by genes other than IBA57; (3) Insu�cient detailed medical records.
“Molecular genetic diagnosis and pathogenesis of mitochondrial diseases” (2017-k-60) was approved by
the Ethics Committee of Beijing Children’s Hospital.
Literature review
A literature search was conducted using keywords "IBA57", "iron-sulfur clusters", "multiple mitochondrial
dysfunction syndrome 3", "mitochondrial leukoencephalopathy" and "spastic paraplegia 74" across the
Wanfang (Chinese database), CNKI (Chinese database), PubMed and Embase database up to December
2023. Cases with incomplete clinical documents or duplicate reports were excluded from the analysis
(Fig. 1).

Collect clinical and genetic data
Clinical, metabolic, neuroimaging, genetic, ethnic and survival data were systematically collected. The
modi�ed Rankin score (mRS) was used to evaluate motor function at last contact. "Y" indicated explicit
documentation of the feature in case, while "N" denoted its explicit absence. "NA" was assigned to traits

Statistical analysis
Quantitative variables were summarized using median and interquartile ranges. Differences between
groups with non-normally distributed variables were assessed using the Mann-Whitney U test. Categorical
variables were presented as frequencies and percentages. Associations between categorical variables
were determined using the Fisher’s exact test. Survival analysis utilized the Log rank test. The
signi�cance level was set at P < 0.05. Data were analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics 20 software (IBM
Corp., Armonk, NY), and GraphPad Prism 8 software was used for graph preparation.
Results
Case series
Demographic features of Chinese patients
The mitoC-NET has recorded 850 cases of mitochondrial disease. Among them, 40 cases (4.7%) were
clinically diagnosed with mitochondrial leukoencephalopathy, and 36 exhibited pathogenic or likely
pathogenic variants in ten genes. IBA57 emerged as the predominant pathogenic gene, accounting for
30.6% cases (11/36). Additional identi�ed genes include NDUFS1, DARS2, NDUFV1, NDUFV2, ISCA1,
COX10, GLRX5, SDHAF1, and MT-ND6. Eleven patients (5 males, 6 females) with IBA57 variants, from ten
families, were enrolled for the analysis (Fig. 1). According to the OMIM phenotype classi�cation, all were
categorized as MMDS3. Maternal prenatal and birth histories were uneventful for all patients. The
median age of onset was 0.92 years (range: 0.42–3.50 years, IQR = 1.50 years), and at last contact, all
patients were alive (median age 5.25 years, range: 2.08-23.0 years, IQR = 2.67 years, median follow-up
time 3.67 years) (Fig. 2a).

pyramid sign, and seven had spastic gaits. Common symptoms included irritable and other emotional
problems (N = 7), feeding di�culty (N = 5) and impaired vision (N = 5). Two patients were diagnosed with
epilepsy 2.5 and 8 years after the onset, and seizures were effectively controlled with levetiracetam or

Genetic �ndings of Chinese patients
All patients underwent whole exon sequencing. Twelve unique variants were identi�ed, including 5
missense variants, 4 frameshift variants (3 deletions and 1 replication), 2 aberrant splicing events and 1

Outcomes at last follow-up
A follow-up ranging from 1.41 to 20 years was conducted on all patients. Rehabilitation training led to
signi�cant motor recovery for all patients. At the last contact, parents were asked to subjectively judge
their child’s disease course as “improvement”, “stable” or “progression”. Stable clinical conditions were
reported in 7 patients, while others showed improvement. The mildest case (P1) presented with
encephalopathy characterized by post-infection somnolence and irritability, returning to baseline two
weeks after onset. Among the remaining 10 cases, impaired motor function was the major symptoms,
while 4 experiencing quadriparesis (Fig. 2e). A median mRS of 3 (range:0–4) was recorded per patient
(Fig. 2c). Four out of 7 patients were enrolled in regular schools.

Discussion
Herein, we detailed eleven new cases exhibiting mitochondrial leukoencephalopathy attributed to IBA57
variants. Combining our cohort with previously reported 50 patients, we identify MMDS3 as the primary
clinical phenotype of IBA57 de�ciency, while a minority presenting as SP74. We �rstly indicate the
phenotype classi�cation of MMDS3 and provide survival data.
Multiple mitochondrial dysfunctions syndrome 3
According to the age of onset, clinical symptoms and prognosis at last follow-up, three clinical
phenotypes were proposed for the �rst time: neonatal, infant and childhood subtypes. The neonatal
subtype typically presents with hypotonia and respiratory distress shortly after birth, characterized by
elevated lactate and glycine levels, leading to death within three months. Due to the immature brain
development, identifying typical white matter lesions is challenging. In contrast to the poor survival of
neonatal subtype, over 70% of infants and children exhibit a normal developmental history and present
with initial symptoms of developmental regression. Key clinical features include impaired development
milestone, positive pyramidal signs, and dysphagia were recorded. Survival rates exceed 75% at 5 years
of age. MRI reveals diffuse intense signals in the white matter with cavitating lesions, predominantly
affecting periventricular and deep white matter, as well as the corpus callosum, while sparing the basal
ganglia. Some patients with normal development exhibited acute developmental regression after
infection, resembling the clinical presentation of acute disseminated encephalomyelitis (ADEM)[5].
Elevated lactate levels, lactate peaks on Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy, and bilateral symmetric
lesions in white matter with cavities assist in differential diagnosis[13]. Unlike most ADEM cases that
recover after immunotherapy, such outcomes are rare in patients with IBA57 mutations. Additionally,
cavitating leukoencephalopathies caused by other genes also require differential diagnosis[24–26]. Thus,

timely genetic screening is essential to determine the etiology and avoid unnecessary
immunosuppressive treatments.
As IBA57 is involved in multiple mitochondrial function and glycine metabolism, elevated lactate and
glycine levels were characteristic features in these patients. However, glycine elevation was absent in our
patients, possibly due to a milder clinical course with incomplete loss of IBA57 function. Reported cases
consistently demonstrated glycine elevation in all neonatal subtypes, while it remained normal in
childhood group, supporting the association of glycine elevation with severe phenotypes. Nevertheless, a
signi�cant association between genotype and phenotype was not found. Parith Wongkittichote et al.
observed an increase of 2-hydroxyadipic and 2-ketoadipic acids in urine organic acid analysis in one
deceased patient, proposing lysine and tryptophan metabolites as potential biomarkers for MMDS3[17].
Existing studies lack documentation on these metabolites. Retrospective analysis of our patients revealed
normal levels of these markers, underscoring the need for larger cohorts to validate their sensitivity.
The majority (76.1%) of patients originated from Asia, exhibiting a later onset age and lower mortality
rate compared to other ethnicities. The stable disease course in Asian suggest a potential correlation with
genetic background. While these �ndings have not been mentioned in previous literature, whether they are
related to ethnic distinctions needs further detailed study. More than 85% of Chinese patients carrying the
p.Y96H variant indicate a founder mutation in the Chinese population[15]. Our cases demonstrated a
phenotype characterized by developmental regression, coupled with a stable follow-up outcome, in
keeping with Jie Zhang reported in 2019[27]. Cases harboring the p.G104C and p.Q314P variants
presented with a neonatal subtype, when combined with a complete loss-of-function allele, a severe
phenotype was observed[5, 6, 11, 14, 17]. These variants appear to signi�cantly impair IBA57 function.
Spastic paraplegia 74
This rare phenotype was documented in only three pedigrees, two of which were Chinese. Two families
displayed typical symptoms of spastic paraplegia, optic atrophy, and peripheral neuropathy. However, the
third family presented with isolated infantile-onset optic atrophy and asymptomatic brain white matter
involvement, revealing an overlapping phenotype between MMDS3 and SP74[12]. The identi�cation of
the p.R8X variant in both phenotypes underscores the unclear correlation between genotype and
phenotype in patients with IBA57 mutations[15, 21].
Conclusion
Patients with IBA57 mutations manifest a diverse phenotypic spectrum, with no speci�c genotype-
phenotype correlation. Neonatal-onset cases display severe symptoms and a poor prognosis. More than
half of the patients were Chinese, with c.286T > C as a hotspot mutation. Survival rate was over 90% in
the Chinese population at age 5, suggesting a relatively stable disease course.
