# Complete Genome Sequence of Campylobacter fetus subsp. testudinum Strain 772, Isolated from Ascites of a Patient with Chronic Kidney Disease.
**Authors:** Hou SP, He P, Zhou Y, Wu XW
**Journal:** Genome Announc (2018)
**DOI:** [10.1128/genomeA.00432-18](https://doi.org/10.1128/genomeA.00432-18)

## Content

Campylobacter fetus subsp. testudinum originating in reptiles can cause invasive infections in humans. Here, we present the whole-genome sequence of C. fetus subsp. testudinum strain 772, isolated from a human patient in China.
