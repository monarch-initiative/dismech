Coffin-Siris syndrome (CSS) is characterized by growth deficiency, intellectual 
disability, microcephaly, dysmorphic features, and hypoplastic nails of the 
fifth fingers and/or toes. Variants in the genes encoding subunits of the BAF 
complex as well as in SOX11 encoding the transcriptional factor under the 
control of BAF complex are associated with CSS. We report a new patient with a 
novel SOX11 mutation. He showed the CSS phenotype and coarctation of the aorta. 
Sox11 is known to be associated with cardiac outflow development in mouse 
studies. Therefore, cardiac anomalies might be an important complication in 
patients with SOX11 mutations.
