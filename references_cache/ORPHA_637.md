# ORPHA:637  Full NF2-related schwannomatosis

**ORPHA:637** — Full NF2-related schwannomatosis (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Full NF2
- Full neurofibromatosis type 2
- Nonmosaic NF2-related schwannomatosis
- Nonmosaic neurofibromatosis type 2

## Definition

A rare inherited cancer-predisposing syndrome disorder characterized by the development of multiple schwannomas, meningiomas and ependymoma. Schwannomas typically affect both vestibular nerves but also other cranial and peripheral nerves, leading to tinnitus, hearing loss and balance dysfunction. Intradermal schwannomas and ocular involvement (cataract, retinal hamartoma) are also typical.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant

## Natural history

- Age of onset: All ages
