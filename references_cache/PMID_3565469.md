# Mental retardation, deafness, skeletal abnormalities, and coarse face with full lips: confirmation of the Fountain syndrome.
**Authors:** Fryns JP, Dereymaeker A, Hoefnagels M, Van den Berghe H
**Journal:** Am J Med Genet (1987)
**DOI:** [10.1002/ajmg.1320260307](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.1320260307)

## Content

1. Am J Med Genet. 1987 Mar;26(3):551-5. doi: 10.1002/ajmg.1320260307.

Mental retardation, deafness, skeletal abnormalities, and coarse face with full 
lips: confirmation of the Fountain syndrome.

Fryns JP, Dereymaeker A, Hoefnagels M, Van den Berghe H.

We describe 3 moderately to severely mentally retarded males (2 brothers and 1 
isolated patient) with congenital deafness due to an anatomical inner ear 
anomaly, the same manifestations that were present in the 4 sibs reported by 
Fountain [1974]: skeletal abnormalities with broad, stubby hands and feet and 
hyperkyphosis, and a peculiar "coarse" face with swelling of the subcutaneous 
tissue, particularly of cheeks and lips. According to the present observations 
early-onset, generalized seizures can be added to the symptom complex of this 
autosomal recessive trait.
