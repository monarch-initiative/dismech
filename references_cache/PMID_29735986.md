Spastic Paraplegia-79 (SPG79) is an autosomal recessive type of childhood onset 
complicated by hereditary spastic paraplegia. SPG79 is characterized by 
spasticity, paraplegia, optic atrophy, cerebellar signs, and other variable 
clinical features. Recessive, disease causing variants in Ubiquitin C-terminal 
hydrolase-L1 (UCHL1) gene have been implicated as a cause for SPG79 in two 
families till now. In this study, we report on a third family of SPG79 with two 
similarly affected siblings, harboring a novel homozygous splice-site variant in 
the UCHL1 gene (NM_004181.4: c.459+2T>C). The variant was identified by 
whole-exome sequencing and validated by Sanger sequencing in the family.
