# Comparison of cortical grey matter from Rasmussen Encephalitis patients and age-matched epileptic controls

## Content

We performed whole-genome transcriptome analysis of Rasmussen Encephalitis of the early disease stages for an overview of differentially expressed pathways leading to widespread neuroinflammation and degeneration.
