# Severe staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome.
**Authors:** Nakyeyune ML, Bongomin F
**Journal:** IDCases (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.idcr.2026.e02488](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.idcr.2026.e02488)

## Content

•Neonate presented with skin scaling and erythema suggestive of congenital ichthyosis.•Clinical features included ectropion, eclabium, and a positive Nikolsky sign.•A diagnosis of SSSS superimposed on congenital ichthyosis was made clinically.•Multidisciplinary care led to skin re-epithelialization and symptom resolution.•Ichthyotic changes persisted despite recovery from acute SSSS episode.
