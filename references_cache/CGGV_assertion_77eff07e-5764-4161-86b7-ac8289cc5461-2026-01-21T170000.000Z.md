# CGGV:assertion_77eff07e-5764-4161-86b7-ac8289cc5461-2026-01-21T170000.000Z  AKT2 / AKT2-related familial partial lipodystrophy

**CGGV:assertion_77eff07e-5764-4161-86b7-ac8289cc5461-2026-01-21T170000.000Z** - AKT2 / AKT2-related familial partial lipodystrophy (Limited)

## Evidence summary

AKT2 was first reported in relation to autosomal dominant familial partial lipodystrophy (FPLD) in 2004 (George et al., PMID: 15166380). George et al. described a rare, highly penetrant AKT2 mutation (NM_001626:6:c.821G>A, p.Arg274His) in a single kindred, co-segregating with insulin resistance in four individuals across three generations. Three of these also had diabetes, and the proband assessed in more detail showed reduced body fat compared to that predicted for height and weight. Two affected family members assessed later showed increased TG-enriched VLDL, hypertriglyceridemia, low HDL cholesterol levels, low leptin and adiponectin concentrations, while one assessed further also showed increased de novo lipogenesis and liver fat content (Semple et al., PMID: 19164855). No other penetrant disease-associated AKT2 alleles have been described in the literature, but at the population level, a rare coding variant (c.148C>A, p.Pro50Thr, rs184042322, gnomAD v4.1 Grpmax FAF = 0.000038) has been reported at higher frequency in the Finnish population (MAF = 0.012) than other populations. This variant is not associated with classic Mendelian disease but has measurable associations with metabolic traits and type 2 diabetes risk at the population level and demonstrated a mild deleterious impact on function in vitro (PMIDs: 28341696, 29141982, 36409629). Genetic evidence score: 0.5 points. The mechanism of pathogenicity is reported to be loss of function, evidenced by in vitro functional assays showed that the AKT2 Arg274His allele has little or no kinase activity. Limited evidence from reporter assays also raises the possibility of a dominant negative activity of the mutant allele (PMID: 15166380). AKT2 signaling is also important for adipocyte differentiation (PMID: 15166380). Akt2 knockout mice developed severe diabetes with age dependent partial lipoatrophy (PMIDs:12843127, 11387480). Also, Adipo-AKT-KO mice (PMID: 27408773) recapitulate many of the features observed in FPLD caused by the LoF variant p.Arg274His, including insulin resistance and hepatomegaly. Summary of experimental data: 5.5 points. Total score for genetic evidence and experimental evidence: 6 points.

A single variant in AKT2 (c.49G>A, p.Gly17Lys) has been reported in individuals with hypoinsulinemic hypoglycemia, some with mild hemihypertrophy (PMIDs: 21979934, 24285683, 26003998, 35602880, 38344362, 34673243). Per criteria outlined by the ClinGen Lumping and Splitting guidelines, we found differences in molecular mechanisms and phenotypic variability underlying AKT2 -related disease entities. Therefore, the curation of AKT2 has been split into: i) AKT2-related FPLD (MONDO:0019192), included in this curation and ii) hypoinsulinemic hypoglycemia and body hemihypertrophy (HIHGHH, MONDO:0009416).

In summary, while the mechanistic link between AKT2 dysfunction and lipodystrophic insulin resistance is highly plausible and well evidenced, current genetic evidence supporting the human gene-disease relationship is limited.
