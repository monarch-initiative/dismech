# Fusion of two novel genes, RBM15 and MKL1, in the t(1;22)(p13;q13) of acute megakaryoblastic leukemia.

Fusion of two novel genes, RBM15 and MKL1, in the t(1;22)(p13;q13) of acute
megakaryoblastic leukemia.

Ma Z(1), Morris SW, Valentine V, Li M, Herbrick JA, Cui X, Bouman D, Li Y, Mehta
PK, Nizetic D, Kaneko Y, Chan GC, Chan LC, Squire J, Scherer SW, Hitzler JK.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology, St Jude Children's Research Hospital, Memphis,
Tennessee 38105, USA.

t(1;22) is the principal translocation of acute megakaryoblastic leukemias. Here
we show this chromosomal rearrangement to result in the fusion of two novel
genes, RNA-binding motif protein-15 (RBM15), an RNA recognition motif-encoding
gene with homology to Drosophila spen, and Megakaryoblastic Leukemia-1 (MKL1), a
gene encoding an SAP (SAF-A/B, Acinus and PIAS) DNA-binding domain.
