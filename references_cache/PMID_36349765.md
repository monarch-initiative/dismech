# Rapid Emergence of Appetite and Hunger Resulting in Weight Gain and Improvement of Eating Disorder Symptomatology during and after Short-Term Off-Label Metreleptin Treatment of a Patient with Anorexia Nervosa.

Off-label treatment of a 15-year-old female patient with anorexia nervosa (AN) 
with human recombinant leptin (metreleptin) for nine days was associated with 
self-reported increments of appetite and hunger resulting in rapid weight gain 
and substantial improvement of eating disorder cognitions and of depression. The 
results further substantiate the effects of metreleptin on both AN and 
depression. We contrast these results with the widespread view that leptin is an 
anorexigenic hormone. Randomized controlled trials are warranted to confirm the 
described effects.
