# CGGV:assertion_d7edeea3-e12a-470a-83d3-05f950ca74e5-2024-06-20T160000.000Z  RTN4IP1 / optic atrophy 10 with or without ataxia, intellectual disability, and seizures

**CGGV:assertion_d7edeea3-e12a-470a-83d3-05f950ca74e5-2024-06-20T160000.000Z** - RTN4IP1 / optic atrophy 10 with or without ataxia, intellectual disability, and seizures (Definitive)

## Evidence summary

RTN4IP1 was first reported in 2015 in relation to isolated and syndromic optic neuropathies in families with early-onset recessive optic neuropathy (Angebault, et al ., PMID: 26593267). Whole exome sequencing detected a variant encoding p.Arg103His in the homozygous state in four affected individuals from three consanguineous families. Two affected compound heterozygous individuals were identified in a family of unrelated parents harboring the p.Arg103His and p.Lys201Ter variants. All parents and five unaffected siblings were heterozygous for their family variant(s) and four unaffected siblings were homozygous for the wildtype alleles. Subsequent studies investigating the causative variants for optic neuropathy identified disease-associated variants in RTN4IP1 in patients with autosomal recessive isolated and syndromic optic neuropathies. The disease entity associated with RTN4IP1 has been described as optic atrophy 10 with or without ataxia, impaired intellectual development and seizures (MIM# 616732), associated with autosomal recessive inheritance. This disease entity is referred to as RTN4IP1 -related optic atrophy with or without neurological features for the purposes of this gene curation.

This curation includes eighteen suspected disease-causing variants, including missense, nonsense, frameshift, splicing defect, and deletion variants, which have been reported in 25 probands in 9 publications (PMID: 26593267, PMID: 28638143, PMID: 29181510, PMID: 31077085, PMID: 33037779, PMID: 33136666, PMID: 33315831, PMID: 36231115, PMID: 35439212). The mechanism of pathogenicity appears to be loss of function, which is supported by expression studies and in vitro functional studies with patient-derived cells and by studies with non-human model organisms (PMIDs: 26593267, 29181510, 31077085, 37884807).

RTN4IP1 (OMIM #610502) is a nuclear gene encoding mitochondrial matrix protein RT4I1, which is a potent inhibitor of regeneration following spinal cord injury. RT4I1 protein plays a role in the regulation of retinal ganglion cell (RGC) neurite outgrowth and in the development of the inner retina and optic nerve. RT4I1 also has NAD(P)H oxidoreductase activity and required for CoQ biosynthesis.

In summary, RTN4IP1 is Definitively associated with RTN4IP1 -related optic atrophy with or without neurological features. This has been upheld over time without the emergence of contradictory evidence, leading to a Definitive classification. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Glaucoma and Neuro-Ophthalmology Gene Curation Expert Panel on June 20th, 2024 (SOP Version 10).
