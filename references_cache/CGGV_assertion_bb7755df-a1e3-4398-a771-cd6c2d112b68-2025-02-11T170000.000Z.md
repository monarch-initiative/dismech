# CGGV:assertion_bb7755df-a1e3-4398-a771-cd6c2d112b68-2025-02-11T170000.000Z  POPDC3 / autosomal recessive limb-girdle muscular dystrophy

**CGGV:assertion_bb7755df-a1e3-4398-a771-cd6c2d112b68-2025-02-11T170000.000Z** - POPDC3 / autosomal recessive limb-girdle muscular dystrophy (Definitive)

## Evidence summary

POPDC3 (Popeye Domain-Containing Protein 3) was first reported in relation to autosomal recessive limb-girdle muscular dystrophy (LGMD) in 2019 (Vissing et al., PMID: 31610034). POPDC3 is a Popeye domain-containing protein that is abundantly expressed in heart and skeletal muscle. Autosomal recessive LGMD (MONDO:0015152) is characterized by adult-onset weakness primarily affecting the proximal muscles of the lower limbs.

Seven homozygous probands from seven families have been described in five publications (PMID: 31610034, 35842834, 35075722, 37104941, 41026953; 9.8 pts). Co-segregation with the LGMD phenotype was observed in affected siblings in two families. Reported variants include two unique splice variants, one stop-gained variant, and four unique missense variants (9.8 pts). Three of the missense variants were shown to impair regulation of TREK-1 current in Xenopus oocytes, but the significance of this effect with respect to the disease mechanism is considered unclear. The mechanism for disease is though to be biallelic loss of function, but the exact pathogenic mechanisms are not yet known. This gene-disease association is also supported by experimental evidence (1.5 pts). POPDC3 is uniquely expressed in skeletal and cardiac muscle, providing evidence towards an association with this muscle-affecting disorder (PMID: 10882522), and co-IP studies have demonstrated a direct interaction between POPDC3 and BVES ( POPDC1 ), which is definitively associated with autosomal recessive LGMD (PMID: 36624536). In addition, a zebrafish knock-out mutant model demonstrated ventral tail curvature and dystrophic changes in myofibers (PMID: 31610034), although a significant phenotype was not observed by early adulthood in homozygous knock-out mice (C57BL/6N-Popdc3tm1.1(KOMP)Vlcg/MbpMmucd; https://www.mmrrc.org/catalog/sds.php?mmrrc_id=49150).

In summary, there is definitive evidence (11.3 pts) to support the relationship between POPDC3 and autosomal recessive LGMD. This relationship has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic settings and has been upheld over time.

This gene-disease pair was originally evaluated by the MDM GCEP in May 2023. It was reevaluated in February 2025. As a result of this reevaluation, the classification was upgraded from Moderate to Definitive with the addition of an additional case and protein interaction data (PMID: 36624536, 41026953).
