# A second locus for Rieger syndrome maps to chromosome 13q14.
**Authors:** Phillips JC, del Bono EA, Haines JL, Pralea AM, Cohen JS, Greff LJ, Wiggs JL
**Journal:** Am J Hum Genet (1996)

## Content

1. Am J Hum Genet. 1996 Sep;59(3):613-9.

A second locus for Rieger syndrome maps to chromosome 13q14.

Phillips JC(1), del Bono EA, Haines JL, Pralea AM, Cohen JS, Greff LJ, Wiggs JL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Ophthalmology, New England Medical Center, Boston MA 02111, 
USA.

Rieger syndrome is a genetically and phenotypically heterogeneous disorder 
typically characterized by malformations of the eyes, teeth, and umbilicus. The 
syndrome is inherited as an autosomal dominant trait and exhibits significant 
variable expressivity. One locus associated with this disorder has been mapped 
to 4q25. Using a large four-generation pedigree, we have identified a second 
locus for Rieger syndrome located on chromosome 13q14.
