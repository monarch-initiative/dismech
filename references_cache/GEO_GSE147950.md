# A positive feedback circuit between mTORC1 signaling and cathelicidin promotes skin inflammation in rosacea [mouse]

## Content

The goal of this study is to figure out the role of mTORC1 signaling in the pathogenesis of rosacea by comparing rosacea mouse model skin lesion transcriptome profiling (RNA-seq) to that of control mouse skin treated with or without rapamycin.
