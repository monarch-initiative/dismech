# A Phase IIa, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics, and Efficacy of INS018_055 Administered Orally to Subjects With Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF)

## Content

The goal of this clinical trial is to learn about INS018\_055 in adults with Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF).

The primary objective is to evaluate the safety and tolerability of INS018\_055 orally administered for up to 12 weeks in adult subjects with IPF compared to placebo.
