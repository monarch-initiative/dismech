# The mRNA-lncRNA-circRNA network profiling of Turner syndrome patient-derived induced pluripotent stem cells and their derived cardiomyocytes

## Content

Heart deformity is the leading cause of mortality in Turner syndrome (TS) patients. To investigate the dysregulated ceRNA network in cardiomyocytes (CMs) of TS patients, we employed a ceRNA microarray to profile the mRNA-lncRNA-circRNA network in induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) and their derived CMs from healthy donors (WT) and TS patients.
