# A Human Induced Pluripotent Stem Cell Array-Based Genome Wide Association Study Identifies Virus Susceptibility Locus and Variants

## Content

RNA-sequencing was used to compare the global gene expression profiles in WT and NDUFA4-/- iPSCs, iPSCs carrying non-risk (T/T; T/T) and risk (G/G; C/C) alleles, and WT_Δ and NDUFA4Δ iPSCs.
