forceful throw that was accompanied

He was neurologically and vascular-

lying lesion was identified to suggest a
pathological fracture. Test results for
serum calcium and thyroid-stimulating
and intact parathyroid hormones were

bone scan showed no evidence of other

hanging cast that was later converted to
a functional brace. Bony union was

Avascular necrosis of bone is a rare
but potentially severe complication of
prolonged corticosteroid therapy. It
has also been recognized after short-
term treatment (
CMAJ 2001;164:205-6).
The annual incidence of avascular nec-
rosis is about 5 per 10 000 population.

Psoriasis plaques often present as they

Pinpoint capillary bleeding, known as

when the scales are gently scraped away
