Large-scale systematic resequencing has been proposed as the key future strategy 
for the discovery of rare, disease-causing sequence variants across the spectrum 
of human complex disease. We have sequenced the coding exons of the X chromosome 
in 208 families with X-linked mental retardation (XLMR), the largest direct 
screen for constitutional disease-causing mutations thus far reported. The 
screen has discovered nine genes implicated in XLMR, including SYP, ZNF711 and 
CASK reported here, confirming the power of this strategy. The study has, 
however, also highlighted issues confronting whole-genome sequencing screens, 
including the observation that loss of function of 1% or more of X-chromosome 
genes is compatible with apparently normal existence.
