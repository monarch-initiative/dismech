# Kikuchi-Fujimoto disease mimicking lymphoma and tuberculosis in an endemic region: a case report.
**Authors:** Basnet A, Khanal A, Karmacharya S, Bista S, Mandal SK
**Journal:** Ann Med Surg (Lond) (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1097/MS9.0000000000005627](https://doi.org/10.1097/MS9.0000000000005627)

## Content

Kikuchi-Fujimoto disease is a benign, self-limiting condition primarily presenting with cervical lymphadenopathy, often accompanied by mild fever and night sweats. Because of its variable clinical features, it is frequently misdiagnosed as lymphoma, tuberculosis, lupus lymphadenitis, or infectious mononucleosis, leading to unnecessary investigations and overtreatment.
