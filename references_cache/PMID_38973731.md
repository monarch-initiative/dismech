OBJECTIVE: We provide evidence-based recommendations regarding the treatment of
interstitial lung disease (ILD) in adults with systemic autoimmune rheumatic
diseases (SARDs).
METHODS: We developed clinically relevant population, intervention, comparator,
and outcomes questions. A systematic literature review was then performed, and
the available evidence was rated using the Grading of Recommendations,
Assessment, Development, and Evaluation methodology. A panel of clinicians and
patients reached consensus on the direction and strength of the recommendations.
RESULTS: Thirty-five recommendations were generated (including two strong
recommendations) for first-line SARD-ILD treatment, treatment of SARD-ILD
progression despite first-line ILD therapy, and treatment of rapidly progressive
ILD. The strong recommendations were against using glucocorticoids in systemic
sclerosis-ILD as a first-line ILD therapy and after ILD progression. Otherwise,
glucocorticoids are conditionally recommended for first-line ILD treatment in
all other SARDs.
CONCLUSION: This clinical practice guideline presents the first recommendations
endorsed by the American College of Rheumatology and American College of Chest
Physicians for the treatment of ILD in people with SARDs.
