# Primary Familial Brain Calcification Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Balck A, Klein C, Westenberger A

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Briefly describe the clinical characteristics of primary familial brain calcification; 2.. Review the genetic causes of primary familial brain calcification; 3.. Review the differential diagnosis of primary familial brain calcification with a focus on genetic and treatable conditions; 4.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of primary familial brain calcification in a proband; 5.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with genetically confirmed primary familial brain calcification.
