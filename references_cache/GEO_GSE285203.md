# Transcriptome analyses of human corneal endothelial cell lines derived from patients with Fuchs Endothelial Corneal Dystrophy

## Content

We report differential gene expression profiles of the corneal endothelial cells from Fuchs dystrophy patients with or without the TCF4 gene mutation
