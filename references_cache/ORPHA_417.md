# ORPHA:417  Neonatal severe primary hyperparathyroidism

**ORPHA:417** — Neonatal severe primary hyperparathyroidism (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- NSHPT

## Definition

Neonatal severe primary hyperparathyroidism (NSHPT) is characterized by severe hypercalcemia (> 3.5 mM) from birth and associated with major hyperparathyroidism.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive
- Not applicable

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Neonatal
