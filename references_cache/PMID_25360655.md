Animal models of coronary microcirculation dysfunction are useful in research on 
the pathology of coronary microcirculation dysfunction and its intervention 
mechanisms. Currently, such animal modes are prepared mainly by mechanical 
obstruction and chemical damage form coronary microembolization or 
microthrombosis. This paper summarizes the currently available preparation 
techniques and compared their advantages and disadvantages.
