## Key inclusion & exclusion criteria

Inclusion criteria: 1) The corresponding clinical manifestations of Genodermatosis,as well as the genetic diagnosis was confirmed at the same time .
2) Any age male and female;
3) vital signs are stable;
4) The subject or guardian has signed the informed consent form.
Exclusion criteria: 1) Patients who have a history of allergies to any of the ingredients to be used;
2) penicillin, aminoglycoside allergic patients;
3) Patients with a history of bronchial asthma;
4) Patients with signs of acute infection;
5) HIV, HBV, HCV and other chronic infections in patients;
6) There is a eighth cranial nerve damage, myasthenia gravis or Parkinson's disease history;
7) Lactating women, pregnant women, or those who have potential pregnancies during treatment and who refuse contraception;
8) ECG, chest radiograph, ultrasound, liver and kidney dysfunction with clinical significance;
9) gene screening found deafness genes or the presence of hearing impairment;
10) At the discretion of the investigator, the subjects were either untrustworthy or poorly compliant.
Age minimum: Age maximum: Gender: Both
