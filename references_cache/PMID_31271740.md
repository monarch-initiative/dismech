BACKGROUND: Mitochondrial DNA depletion syndrome is a group of heterogeneous 
diseases with non-specific presentation. The common feature is the quantitative 
depletion of mitochondrial DNA without qualitative defects. Diagnosis of these 
diseases poses a challenge and whole exome sequencing is often needed for their 
diagnoses.
CASE: Two siblings of a quartet family, presenting with hypotonia, microcephaly 
and severe intellectual disability, have been diagnosed to harbor two 
heterozygous variants in trans in the DTYMK gene of the thymidine biosynthesis 
pathway. Mitochondrial DNA depletion has been demonstrated in silico in the more 
severe sibling.
CONCLUSIONS: We suggest the consideration of incorporating DTYMK as one of the 
associated genes of mitochondrial DNA depletion syndrome (MDDS). DTYMK may be 
the missing link in the mitochondrial nucleotide salvage pathway but further 
characterization and additional evidence would be needed.
