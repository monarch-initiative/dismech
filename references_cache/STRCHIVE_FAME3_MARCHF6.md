## Disease description

Cortical tremor, seizures with generalised motor (tonic-clonic) onset [@pmid:38876750]; Adult-onset cortical tremor with epilepsy [@pmid:39349043].

## Mechanism

Noted as unknown in literature [@omim:613608].

## Age of onset

Typical: 24-41 based on one 76 member pedigree [@pmid:19616813]; Range: 10 [@omim:613608] - 46 [@pmid:31664039].

## Prevalence

- Details: Overall FAME prevalence is < 1/35,000; MARCHF6-caused much smaller. Most cases have European ancestry [@pmid:38876750].

## Details

Healthy controls do not have pathogenic allele (TTTCA), but do have 9-20 benign motifs (TTTTA) [@genereviews:NBK535148]. Alleles from six probands spanned from 791-1035 repeats; an inverse relationship between allele size and age of onset was noted [@pmid:31664039].
