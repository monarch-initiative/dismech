# ORPHA:228305  Carnitine palmitoyl transferase II deficiency, severe infantile form

**ORPHA:228305** — Carnitine palmitoyl transferase II deficiency, severe infantile form (Clinical subtype, Subtype of disorder)

## Synonyms

- CPT2, hepatocardiomuscular form

- CPTII, hepatocardiomuscular form
- CPTII, severe infantile form
- Carnitine palmitoyl transferase II deficiency, hepatocardiomuscular form
- Carnitine palmitoyl transferase deficiency type 2, hepatocardiomuscular form
- Carnitine palmitoyl transferase deficiency type 2, severe infantile form

## Definition

The severe infantile form of carnitine palmitoyltransferase II (CPT II) deficiency, an inherited disorder that affects mitochondrial oxidation of long chain fatty acids (LCFA), is the early-onset form of the disease.
