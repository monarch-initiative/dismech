# CGGV:assertion_f1e2d1d2-b77c-4f1a-b185-31e3a95aae9b-2024-03-29T160000.000Z  DZIP1L / autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease

**CGGV:assertion_f1e2d1d2-b77c-4f1a-b185-31e3a95aae9b-2024-03-29T160000.000Z** - DZIP1L / autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (Definitive)

## Evidence summary

DZIP1L was first reported in relation to autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD) in 2017 (Lu H et al., PMID: 28530676). Patients with homozygous DZIP1L pathogenic variants were reported to exhibit polycystic kidneys, enlarged kidneys, reduced cortico-medullary differentiation, arterial hypertension and end-stage renal disease. Of note, there was limited evidence for a liver phenotype in this cohort of ARPKD patients. Functionally, DZIP1L has been shown to interact with septin2 and ensures proper localization and translocation of polycystin-1 and polycystin-2 within the cilium. Per criteria outlined by the ClinGen Lumping and Splitting Working Group, we found no difference in molecular mechanism OR inheritance pattern, therefore, we suggest referring to this single disease entity as “ARPKD - DZIP1L ". Four missense, 1 frameshift and 1 nonsense variants that have been reported in 7 probands in 2 publications (PMIDs: 28530676, 35211789) are included in this curation. Additionally, a pedigree segregating a DZIP1L missense and frameshift variant in two affected siblings was scored (unpublished). The mechanism of pathogenicity is reported to be loss of function (LOF). A total of 12.1 genetic evidence points was reached. This gene-disease relationship is also supported by LOF mouse and zebrafish models, in vitro functional assays, and expression studies (PMIDs: 28530676, 35211789).

This gene-disease pair was originally evaluated by the Kidney Cystic GCEP on 03/09/2022 and was reevaluated on 03/11/2024. As a result, the classification increased from Moderate to Definitive with the revision of previously scored evidence using ClinGen’s latest SOP version for gene-disease validity process. No new evidence was scored in this recuration. In summary, there is Definitive evidence to support the relationship between DZIP1L and ARPKD. Three years have elapsed from the first proposal of the relationship without any valid contradictory evidence. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Kidney GCEP on the meeting date 03/11/2024 (SOP Version 10).
