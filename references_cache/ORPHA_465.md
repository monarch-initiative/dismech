# ORPHA:465  Congenital plasminogen activator inhibitor type 1 deficiency

**ORPHA:465** — Congenital plasminogen activator inhibitor type 1 deficiency (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Congenital PAI-1 deficiency

## Definition

A rare hemorrhagic disorder due to a constitutional haemostatic factors defect characterized by premature lysis of hemostatic clots and a moderate bleeding tendency.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adolescent
- Age of onset: Adult
- Age of onset: Childhood
- Age of onset: Elderly
