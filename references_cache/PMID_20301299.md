The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Define the clinical characteristics of 
thoracic aortic disease; 2.. Review the causes of heritable thoracic aortic 
disease; 3.. Provide a strategy for evaluation and genetic risk assessment for 
heritable thoracic aortic disease in a proband; 4.. Review management 
recommendations for individuals with heritable thoracic aortic disease; 5.. 
Inform genetic counseling and risk assessment in family members of a proband 
with heritable thoracic aortic disease.
