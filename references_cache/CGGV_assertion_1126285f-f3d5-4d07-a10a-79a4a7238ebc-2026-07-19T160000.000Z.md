# CGGV:assertion_1126285f-f3d5-4d07-a10a-79a4a7238ebc-2026-07-19T160000.000Z  CIDEC / CIDEC-related familial partial lipodystrophy

**CGGV:assertion_1126285f-f3d5-4d07-a10a-79a4a7238ebc-2026-07-19T160000.000Z** - CIDEC / CIDEC-related familial partial lipodystrophy (Moderate)

## Evidence summary

CIDEC , also known as FSP27 , was first reported in association with autosomal recessive CIDEC -related familial partial lipodystrophy in 2009 (Rubio-Cabezas et al., PMID: 20049731). The affected individual was an Ecuadorean female with adolescent-onset lipodystrophy who was homozygous for the nonsense variant NM_001321142.2:c.556G>T (p.Glu186Ter). The inheritance pattern was curated as autosomal recessive, consistent with the homozygous loss-of-function variant identified in the affected individual.

The reported patient had partial lipodystrophy affecting the limbs, femorogluteal region, and abdominal subcutaneous fat, accompanied by severe insulin-resistant diabetes mellitus, hypertriglyceridemia, hepatic steatosis, and reduced circulating adiponectin (PMID: 20049731). Another patient was reported to have partial lipodystrophy, acanthosis nigricans, and marked insulin resistance and was found to be homozygous for the rare CIDEC missense variant NM_001199623.2:c.224G>T (p.Ser75Ile) (PMID: 41596298). However, she also carried a novel homozygous WRN missense variant, NM_000553.4:c.1856T>G (p.Leu619Arg), and both variants were classified as variants of uncertain significance. Because WRN -associated disease may also present with partial lipodystrophy phenotype and insulin resistance (PMID: 23849162) and both variants were novel missense variants, the specific contributions of both the CIDEC and *WRN *variants to this patient’s phenotype remain uncertain. Therefore, this case does not provide unequivocal independent evidence for CIDEC -related disease. Per the ClinGen Lumping and Splitting guidelines, the available evidence supports evaluation under a single disease entity: autosomal recessive CIDEC-related familial partial lipodystrophy.

One homozygous variant reported in one proband was included in this curation (PMID: 20049731). The case-level evidence received 3 points. This gene-disease relationship is further supported by expression studies, biochemical function, protein interaction, functional alteration, and mouse model data (PMIDs: 17884815, 20049731, 22144693, 23399566, 24627478, 18654663, 18682832, 25565658, 34508658, 35963433, 36800289). Total points for experimental evidence are 5, and the combined genetic and experimental evidence totals 8 points.

In summary, there is moderate evidence supporting the relationship between CIDEC and autosomal recessive CIDEC -related familial partial lipodystrophy. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Monogenic Diabetes GCEP on the meeting date July 15, 2026 (SOP Version 12).
