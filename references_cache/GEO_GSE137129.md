# RNA-seq of UGP2 mutant human embryonic stem cells and in vitro differentiated neural stem cells

## Content

We report transcriptome analysis of human embryonic stem cells and in vitro differentiated neural stem cells, comparing wild type H9, UGP2 homozygous knock-out and UGP2 mutant cells harboring a homozygous A>G nucleotide change affecting the start codon of UGP2 isoform 2 that was introduced by CRISPR-Cas9 engineering
