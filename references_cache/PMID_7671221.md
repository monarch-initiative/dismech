# Increased frequency of spontaneous skin tumors in transgenic mice which overexpress ornithine decarboxylase.
**Authors:** Megosh L, Gilmour SK, Rosson D, Soler AP, Blessing M, Sawicki JA, O'Brien TG
**Journal:** Cancer Res (1995)

## Content

1. Cancer Res. 1995 Oct 1;55(19):4205-9.

Increased frequency of spontaneous skin tumors in transgenic mice which 
overexpress ornithine decarboxylase.

Megosh L(1), Gilmour SK, Rosson D, Soler AP, Blessing M, Sawicki JA, O'Brien TG.

Author information:
(1)Lankenau Medical Research Center, Wynnewood, Pennsylvania 19096, USA.

Ornithine decarboxylase, a critical regulatory enzyme for polyamine 
biosynthesis, is highly inducible by growth-promoting stimuli in mouse epidermis 
but the enzyme level is only transiently elevated due to rapid turnover of the 
protein. Here we report that constitutive overexpression of the enzyme in the 
skin of transgenic mice causes several phenotypic abnormalities. Effects 
observed include development of dermal follicular cysts, excessive skin 
wrinkling, enhanced nail growth, alopecia, and spontaneous tumor development. 
These results indicate that up-regulation of polyamine biosynthesis can 
profoundly disturb skin homeostasis and alter susceptibility to neoplastic 
development.
