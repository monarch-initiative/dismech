# Phenotype-Genotype Correlations of &lt;i&gt;GH1&lt;/i&gt; Gene Variants in Patients with Isolated Growth Hormone Deficiency or Multiple Pituitary Hormone Deficiency
**Authors:** Ayşe Pınar Öztürk, Zehra Yavas Abali, Ayça Dilruba Aslanger, Firdevs Bas, Güven Toksoy, Volkan Karaman, Gulandam Bagirova, Sukran Poyrazoglu, Zehra Oya Uyguner, Feyza Darendeliler
**Journal:** Hormone Research in Paediatrics (2024)
**DOI:** [10.1159/000531113](https://doi.org/10.1159/000531113)

## Content

<b><i>Introduction:</i></b> Genetic forms of growth hormone deficiency (GHD) may occur as isolated GHD (IGHD) or as a component of multiple pituitary hormone deficiency (MPHD). This study aimed to present the clinical and molecular characteristics of patients with IGHD/MPHD due to the <i>GH1</i> gene variants. <b><i>Methods:</i></b> A gene panel accommodating 25 genes associated with MPHD and short stature was used to search for small sequence variants. Multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification was performed in patients with normal panel results to investigate gross deletion/duplications. Segregation in the family was performed by Sanger sequencing. <b><i>Results:</i></b> The <i>GH1</i> gene variants were detected in 5 patients from four unrelated families. One patient had IGHD IA due to homozygous whole <i>GH1</i> gene deletion and one had IGHD IB due to <i>novel</i> homozygous c.162C&gt;G/p.(Tyr54*) variant. Two patients from a family had previously reported heterozygous c.291+1G&gt;A/p.(?) variant in which clinical and genetic characteristics were compatible with IGHD II accompanying MPHD. One patient had clinical and laboratory characteristics of IGHD II with MPHD but the heterozygous c.468 C&gt;T/p.(R160W) variant had conflicting results about the relationship with the phenotype. <b><i>Conclusion:</i></b> Expanding our knowledge of the spectrum of <i>GH1</i> gene variants by apprehending clinical and molecular data of more cases, helps to identify the genotype-phenotype correlation of IGHD/MPHD and the <i>GH1</i> gene variants. These patients must be regularly followed up for the occurrence of additional pituitary hormone deficiencies.
