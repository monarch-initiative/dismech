# Unveiling Cancer-Related Metaplastic Cells in Both Helicobacter pylori Infection and Autoimmune Gastritis

## Content

We used 10X 5' single cell RNA sequencing (scRNAseq) technology to examine the transcriptional profiles of distinct gastric metaplastic cell types within the gastric corpus of mice chronically infected with Helicobacter pylori or chronically inflamed with autoimmune gastritis and humans with autoimmune gastritis.
