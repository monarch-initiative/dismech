Bullous pemphigoid (BP), although a rare disease, is the most frequent 
subepidermal autoimmune disorder. Treatment with gliptins, used for type 2 
diabetes, was reported as associated with BP onset. To identify HLA alleles that 
may reflect a higher susceptibility to BP in the Italian population, we analysed 
30 patients affected by idiopathic bullous pemphigoid (IBP) and 86 
gliptin-associated BP (GABP) patients. A significant association between 
HLA-DQB1*03:01 allele and IBP and GABP patients was found. Of note, both IBP and 
GABP were significantly associated with one of the following haplotypes: 
DRB1*11:01, DRB3*02:02, DQA1*05:05, DQB1*03:01 or DRB1*11:04, DRB3*02:02, 
DQA1*05:05 and DQB1*03:01. These data identify, for the first time, potential 
markers of susceptibility to BP in the Italian population, especially when 
associated with gliptin intake.
