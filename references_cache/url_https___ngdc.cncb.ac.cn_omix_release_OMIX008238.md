<th>Title</th>
<td>E3 Ubiquitin Ligase CHIP Facilitates cAMP and cGMP Signalling Cross-talk by Polyubiquitinating PDE9A</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<th>Description</th>
<td>Single-cell transcriptome sequencing expression matrix. Single-cell RNA Sequencing of CHIP p.T246M homozygous mutant Rattus norvegicus, CHIP p.T246M homozygous mutant Rattus norvegicus was received intraperitoneal injections of Bay 73-6691(2 mg/kg/day) for 15 consecutive days, CHIP p.T246M homozygous mutant Rattus norvegicus was intravenously injected with HBAAV2/BBB-CHIP virus (1.2 x 10^12 vg/mL), and Wild-type Rattus norvegicus.</td>
