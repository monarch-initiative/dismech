Holoprosencephaly (HPE) results from a lack of cleavage of the prosencephalon. 
It has a complex etiology, resulting from chromosome abnormalities or single 
gene variants in the Sonic hedgehog signaling pathway. A single variant, 
p.Arg535Cys in CNOT1, has been described in HPE in association with pancreatic 
agenesis and neonatal diabetes. Here, we report on a case of HPE and p.Arg535Cys 
in CNOT1 without pancreatic agenesis where the patient presented with diabetes 
mellitus in adolescence. This case reinforces the role of CNOT1 in pancreatic 
development. We suggest that individuals with p.Arg535Cys in CNOT1 with no 
pancreas abnormalities observed at birth should be screened for diabetes during 
follow-up.
