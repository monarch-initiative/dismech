# Genome-wide DNA methylation profiling in progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP)

## Content

Genome wide Methylation profiling of DNA extracted from post-mortem forebrains of PSP patients (n=94) and of controls (n=72). The Illumina Infinium HumanMethylation450 BeadChip was used representing 485,000 potentially methylated CpG sites throughout the genome.
