# Delays in Diagnosis of Powassan Virus Encephalitis-Vermont, 2022-2023.
**Authors:** Strelau KM, Pareles E, Padda H, Fitzpatrick KA, Laven JJ, Lehman JA, Lambert AJ, Staples JE, Gould CV, Kwit NA
**Journal:** Open Forum Infect Dis (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1093/ofid/ofag243](https://doi.org/10.1093/ofid/ofag243)

## Content

Tickborne encephalitic disease caused by Powassan virus (POWV) is increasing in the United States. We describe two recent POWV encephalitis cases reported in Vermonters; both experienced diagnostic delays. These cases provide insights into neuroinvasive symptoms, long-term sequelae, and laboratory tests for diagnosing POWV disease.
