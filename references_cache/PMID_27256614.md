We report clinical and biochemical finding from three unrelated patients 
presenting ONCE (Optic Neuropathy, Cardiomyopathy and Encephalopathy with lactic 
acidosis and combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency) syndrome. 
Whole-exome sequencing (WES) of the three patients and the healthy sister of one 
of them was used to identify the carry gene. Clinical and biochemical findings 
were used to filter variants, and molecular, in silico and genetic studies were 
performed to characterize the candidate variants. Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) 
defects involving mutations, deletions or depletion were discarded, whereas WES 
uncovered a double homozygous mutation in the MTO1 gene (NM_001123226:c.1510C>T, 
p.R504C, and c.1669G>A, p.V557M) in two of the patients and the homozygous 
mutation p.R504C in the other. Therefore, our data confirm p.R504C as pathogenic 
mutation responsible of ONCE syndrome, and p.V557M as a rare polymorphic 
variant.
