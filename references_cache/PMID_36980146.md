Autoimmune polyendocrine syndromes (APSs) encompass a heterogeneous group of
rare diseases characterized by autoimmune activity against two or more endocrine
or non-endocrine organs. Three types of APSs are reported, including both
monogenic and multifactorial, heterogeneous disorders. The aim of this
manuscript is to present the main clinical and epidemiological characteristics
of APS-1, APS-2, and IPEX syndrome in the pediatric age, describing the
mechanisms of autoimmunity and the currently available treatments for these rare
conditions.
