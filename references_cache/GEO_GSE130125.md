# Delineating the molecular pathways regulated by IFN-gamma and IL-17 in the small intestines of humanized mice during staphylococcal superantigen-induced toxic shock syndrome

## Content

RNASeq identified several pathways that were differentially expressed/regulated in the small intestines that correlated with intestinal failure during toxic shock syndrome caused by staphylococcal superantigen in a humanized mouse model
