Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease is the most prevalent, potentially 
lethal, monogenic disorder. It is associated with large interfamilial and 
intrafamilial variability, which can be explained to a large extent by its 
genetic heterogeneity and modifier genes. An increased understanding of the 
disorder's underlying genetic, molecular, and cellular mechanisms and a better 
appreciation of its progression and systemic manifestations have laid out the 
foundation for the development of clinical trials and potentially effective 
treatments.
