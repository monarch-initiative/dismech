# Neonatal Brachial Plexus Palsy.
**Authors:** Osorio M, Hartman KC, Mendoza-Sengco P
**Journal:** Phys Med Rehabil Clin N Am (2025)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.pmr.2025.03.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmr.2025.03.005)

## Content

The following article is an overview of neonatal brachial plexus palsy, including current literature on early recognition of glenohumeral dysplasia and trends in surgical intervention. Long-term concerns regarding pain, function, and quality of life are also discussed.
