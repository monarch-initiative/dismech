Implantable defibrillator therapy in Naxos disease.

Gatzoulis K(1), Protonotarios N, Anastasakis A, Tsatsopoulou A, Vlasseros J, 
Gialafos J, Toutouzas P.

Author information:
(1)University Cardiac Department, Hippokration General Hospital, Athens, Greece.

Naxos disease is a unique form of right ventricular cardiomyopathy with a high 
prevalence of malignant ventricular arrhythmias, including sudden cardiac death. 
As a hereditary systemic disease confined to a small island, it has been closely 
studied over the last 15 years. The implantation of an automatic defibrillator 
provides an alternative form of antiarrhythmic management to improve life 
expectancy in these high risk cardiac patients. We present the first two Naxos 
disease patients with malignant ventricular arrhythmias who had defibrillator 
implantation.
