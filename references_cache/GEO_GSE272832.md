# Study on peripheral blood methylation and transcriptome in patients with Graves' disease and Graves' ophthalmopathy

## Content

By studying the differences in transcriptional and methylation levels between the GO (Graves' ophthalmopathy) patient group and the GH (Graves' hyperthyroidism) patient group, we aim to explore the pathogenic mechanisms of GO in patients with GH, and provide intervention guidance for the prevention of GO.
