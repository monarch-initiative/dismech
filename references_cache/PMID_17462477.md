# The mast cell in interstitial cystitis: role in pathophysiology and pathogenesis.
**Authors:** Sant GR, Kempuraj D, Marchand JE, Theoharides TC
**Journal:** Urology (2007)

Current evidence from clinical and laboratory studies confirms that mast cells 
play a central role in the pathogenesis and pathophysiology of interstitial 
cystitis (IC). In this article, we focus on the role of the mast cell in IC and 
examine the ways in which mast cells and other pathophysiologic mechanisms are 
interrelated in this disease. Identifying the patients with IC who have mast 
cell proliferation and activation will enable us to address this aspect of 
disease pathophysiology in these individuals with targeted pharmacotherapy to 
inhibit mast cell activation and mediator release.
