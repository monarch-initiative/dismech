The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of 
Parkinson disease; 2.. Review monogenic causes of Parkinson disease; 3.. Review 
the differential diagnosis of monogenic Parkinson disease; 4.. Provide an 
evaluation strategy to identify the cause of monogenic Parkinson disease in a 
proband; 5.. Provide information on targeted therapeutic clinical trials for 
Parkinson disease; 6.. Inform genetic counseling for family members of an 
individual with monogenic Parkinson disease.
