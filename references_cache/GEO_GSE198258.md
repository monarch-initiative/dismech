# Bulk RNAseq analysis of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes (hiPSC-CMs) and the isogenic control

## Content

To investigate HCM mutation-associated molecular details at the early stage of disease development, we performed bulk RNA-seq analysis at Day 15 of differentiation using RNA isolated from the isogenic control and mutant hiPSC-CMs. We performed pair-end sequencing with >30 million reads per sample with clean read counts of more than 96% for each sample.
