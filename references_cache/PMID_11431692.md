The gene products involved in mammalian mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) maintenance 
and organization remain largely unknown. We report here a novel mitochondrial 
protein, Twinkle, with structural similarity to phage T7 gene 4 primase/helicase 
and other hexameric ring helicases. Twinkle colocalizes with mtDNA in 
mitochondrial nucleoids. Screening of the gene encoding Twinkle in individuals 
with autosomal dominant progressive external ophthalmoplegia (adPEO), associated 
with multiple mtDNA deletions, identified 11 different coding-region mutations 
co-segregating with the disorder in 12 adPEO pedigrees of various ethnic 
origins. The mutations cluster in a region of the protein proposed to be 
involved in subunit interactions. The function of Twinkle is inferred to be 
critical for lifetime maintenance of human mtDNA integrity.
