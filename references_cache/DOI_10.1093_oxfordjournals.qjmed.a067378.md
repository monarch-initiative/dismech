# Triglyceride Storage Disease
**Authors:** D. J. GALTON, C. GILBERT, J. P. D. RECKLESS, J. KAYE
**Journal:** QJM: An International Journal of Medicine (1974)
**DOI:** [10.1093/oxfordjournals.qjmed.a067378](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.qjmed.a067378)

## Content

Abstract
Four patients are described with metabolic defects on the paths of triglyceride breakdown associated with excessive storage of triglyceride in adipose tissue. In one infant noradrenaline(10−5M) poorly stimulated the activity of adenyl cyclase in subcutaneous adipose tissue in the periphery compared to abdominal tissue (P &lt; 0.05); and this was associated with excessive accumulation of triglyceride in the periphery. In three other generalized obese patients of a kindred, isoprenaline (10−5M) stimulated the activity of adenyl cyclase in subcutaneous adipose tissue as judged by normal increments in tissue levels of cyclic-AMP but this failed to activate the triglyceride lipase; seen as a reduced output of glycerol compared to obese controls (P &lt; 0.01). A provisional classification is proposed for the metabolic defects of triglyceride breakdown based on the separate stages of lipolysis and includes the rare defect of triglyceride breakdown already described in the literature as Wolman's disease.
