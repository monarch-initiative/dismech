# A de novo WBP11 Pathogenic Variant in a Fetus With Cystic Brain Malformation and Growth Restriction.
**Authors:** Gill G, O'Donovan K, Rubinfeld D, Meagher S, Goergen S, Hennington L, Fung A, Downie L
**Journal:** Prenat Diagn (2025)
**DOI:** [10.1002/pd.70015](https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.70015)

## Content

We report the first prenatal diagnosis of a de novo WBP11 variant in a fetus with growth restriction and structural brain anomalies. The case highlights the challenges of counselling with a new and evolving gene-disease association.
