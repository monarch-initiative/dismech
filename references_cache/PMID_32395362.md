# Unique Mutation in SP110 Resulting in Hepatic Veno-Occlusive Disease with Immunodeficiency.

Familial hepatic veno-occlusive disease with immunodeficiency (VODI, OMIM: 
235550) is a rare form of combined immune deficiency (CID) that presents in the 
first few months of life with failure to thrive, recurrent infections, 
opportunistic infections along with liver impairment. Herein, we are describing 
a Pakistani patient with a homozygous novel variant in the SP110 gene, 
presenting with classical phenotypic manifestations of VODI. He presented at the 
age of 3 months with opportunistic infections and later developed liver failure. 
Conclusion. Hepatic veno-occlusive disease with immunodeficiency is a rare cause 
of immunodeficiency, and this is the first case report from the Middle East in a 
patient of Pakistani origin. It is important to have a high suspicion for this 
disease, in patients presenting early life with a picture of CID and deranged 
liver function, as the earlier the diagnosis and treatment, the better the 
prognosis.
