# A family with distal arthrogryposis and cleft palate: possible overlap between Gordon syndrome and Aase-Smith syndrome.
**Authors:** Becker K, Splitt M
**Journal:** Clin Dysmorphol (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1097/00019605-200101000-00009](https://doi.org/10.1097/00019605-200101000-00009)

## Content

We report a family with distal arthrogryposis and cleft palate which demonstrates the clinical overlap between Gordon syndrome and Aase-Smith syndrome. The two syndromes may represent a single clinical entity.
