Patent ductus arteriosus (PDA) and coarctation of the aorta (CoA) are relatively 
common congenital heart defects. Pathogenic variants in PRDM6, which encodes a 
smooth-muscle-cell-specific transcription factor, have now been etiologically 
associated with non-syndromic PDA. We present three patients with PDA and CoA 
found to harbor PRDM6 variants, including a novel, likely-pathogenic variant.
