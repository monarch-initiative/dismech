# The FG syndrome from a pathological perspective.
**Authors:** Neri C, Moser K, Pysher TJ, Boettger DR, Neri G, Opitz JM
**Journal:** Fetal Pediatr Pathol (2011)
**DOI:** [10.3109/15513815.2011.520259](https://doi.org/10.3109/15513815.2011.520259)

1. Fetal Pediatr Pathol. 2011;30(2):71-6. doi: 10.3109/15513815.2011.520259.

The FG syndrome from a pathological perspective.

Neri C(1), Moser K, Pysher TJ, Boettger DR, Neri G, Opitz JM.

Author information:
(1)Istituto di Ginecologia e Ostetricia, Università Cattolica del S. Cuore, 
Rome, Italy.

We report on a case of FG syndrome in an almost 6-year-old boy, diagnosed 
post-mortem. The description of the intellectual and behavior phenotype provided 
by the mother, together with the evidence gathered at autopsy, were sufficient 
to reach a clinical diagnosis. The mother had mild manifestations, including a 
symptomatic tethered cord, which established her as a carrier of the putative 
mutation causing the syndrome in the son. The propositus' phenotype did not 
suggest involvement of the MED12 gene.
