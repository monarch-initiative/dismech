# ORPHA:234  Dubin-Johnson syndrome

**ORPHA:234** — Dubin-Johnson syndrome (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Dubin-Sprinz disease
- Hyperbilirubinemia type 2
- Sprinz-Nelson syndrome

## Definition

A rare metabolic liver disease characterized clinically by a benign, chronic, predominantly conjugated hyperbilirubinemia, and histopathologically by black-brown pigment deposition in parenchymal liver cells.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adolescent
- Age of onset: Adult
