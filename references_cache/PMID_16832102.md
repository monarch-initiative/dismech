Early progressive encephalopathy in boys and MECP2 mutations.

Kankirawatana P(1), Leonard H, Ellaway C, Scurlock J, Mansour A, Makris CM, Dure 
LS 4th, Friez M, Lane J, Kiraly-Borri C, Fabian V, Davis M, Jackson J, 
Christodoulou J, Kaufmann WE, Ravine D, Percy AK.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, University of Alabama at Birmingham, USA.

MECP2 mutations mainly occur in females with Rett syndrome. Mutations have been 
described in 11 boys with progressive encephalopathy: seven of nine with 
affected sisters and two de novo. The authors report four de novo occurrences: 
three pathogenic and one potentially pathogenic. Common features include failure 
to thrive, respiratory insufficiency, microcephaly, and abnormal motor control. 
MECP2 mutations should be assessed in boys with progressive encephalopathy and 
one or more of respiratory insufficiency, abnormal movements or tone, and 
intractable seizures.
