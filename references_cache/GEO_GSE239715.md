# Epigenome analysis of schwannomas arising as part of segmental schwannomatosis due to somatic mosaic SOX10 indel mutations

## Content

Genome-wide DNA methylation profiling of 6 schwannomas arising as part of segmental schwannomatosis due to somatic mosaic SOX10 indel mutations. The Illumina Infinium EPIC 850k Human DNA Methylation Beadchip was used to obtain DNA methylation profiles across approximately 850,000 CpG sites of genomic DNA extracted from formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tumor tissue of 6 schwannomas.
