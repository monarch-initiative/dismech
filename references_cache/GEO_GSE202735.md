# Gene expression from single cells of the retina and choroid in human MELAS (m.3243A>G) and control samples [scRNA-seq]

## Content

The mitochondrial m.3243A>G variant is known to cause retinal dystrophy and vision loss. We used single cell RNA sequencing to understand how the presence of this mutation affects cellular phenotype in a cell type-specific manner.
