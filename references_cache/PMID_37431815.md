Comment on
    EMBO Mol Med. 2023 Aug 7;15(8):e16090. doi: 10.15252/emmm.202216090.

Gal et al address the issues raised by Gerber et al and reiterate that patients 
in their study showed decreased Misato homolog 1 (MSTO1) mRNA and protein 
levels, but also confirm finding of Gerber et al that the mutation is in MSTO2p 
pseudogene. Whether MSTO2p variant contributes to the observed decrease in MSTO1 
levels in patients remains unclear.
