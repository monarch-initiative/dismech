# ORPHA:1552  Currarino syndrome

**ORPHA:1552** — Currarino syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Currarino triad

## Definition

A rare developmental defect during embryogenesis characterized by the triad of anorectal malformations, presacral mass and sacral anomalies.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant
- Not applicable

## Natural history

- Age of onset: All ages
