Recent progress in the methods of genetic diagnosis of inborn errors of immunity 
has contributed to a better understanding of the pathogenesis of chronic 
mucocutaneous candidiasis (CMC) and potential therapeutic options. This review 
describes the latest advances in the understanding of the pathophysiology, 
diagnostic strategies, and management of chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis.
