Bainbridge-Ropers syndrome (BRPS) is a rare and understudied developmental 
disorder associated with medical (e.g., sleep disruption) and behavioral (e.g., 
self-injury) challenges. There are no published treatments for BRPS. We targeted 
self-injury in a child with BRPS using a functional analysis and differential 
reinforcement, with several extensions to common procedures. Results present the 
first example of behavioral reduction for self-injury in BRPS. • ABA strategies 
can reduce self-injury in BRPS • Evaluating multiply maintained self-injury 
following identification of an automatic function is important. • Sleep deficits 
may complicate assessment.
