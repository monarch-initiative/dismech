Urinary bladder malformations associated with bladder outlet obstruction are a 
frequent cause of progressive renal failure in children. We here describe a 
muscarinic acetylcholine receptor M3 (CHRM3) (1q41-q44) homozygous frameshift 
mutation in familial congenital bladder malformation associated with a 
prune-belly-like syndrome, defining an isolated gene defect underlying this 
sometimes devastating disease. CHRM3 encodes the M3 muscarinic acetylcholine 
receptor, which we show is present in developing renal epithelia and bladder 
muscle. These observations may imply that M3 has a role beyond its known 
contribution to detrusor contractions. This Mendelian disease caused by a 
muscarinic acetylcholine receptor mutation strikingly phenocopies Chrm3 null 
mutant mice.
