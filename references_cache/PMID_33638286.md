# Novel USP9X variant associated with syndromic intellectual disability in a female: A case study and review.

Heterozygous variants in USP9X are associated with female-restricted X-linked 
mental retardation (MRXS99F), a rare syndrome characterized by 
neurodevelopmental delay, intellectual disability (ID), and a wide variety of 
additional congenital anomalies. Here, we report a girl harboring a novel de 
novo loss-of-function variant in USP9X (c.4091delinsAG, p.Thr1364Lysfs*7), and 
literature review revealed novel prenatal features associated with MRXS99F, 
expanding the genotypic and phenotypic landscape of the syndrome. It is 
important to consider X-linked diseases in girls with ID and perform directed 
molecular investigation to provide correct diagnosis and prognosis.
