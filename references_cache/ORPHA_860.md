# ORPHA:860  Congenitally uncorrected transposition of the great arteries

**ORPHA:860** — Congenitally uncorrected transposition of the great arteries (Morphological anomaly, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Discordant ventriculoarterial connections
- Transposition of the great vessels

## Definition

A rare congenital cardiac malformation characterized by a connection of the morphologically right ventricle to the aorta, and of the morphologically left ventricle to the pulmonary trunk (atrioventricular concordance and ventriculoarterial VA discordance).

## Inheritance

- Multigenic/multifactorial
- Not applicable

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Antenatal
- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
