# Aggressive gene expression signiture of waldenstrom macroglobulinemia with deletion 6q

## Content

Waldenstom macroglobulinemia (WM) with 6q del is still unknown. In the present study, we analyzed gene expression signiture of WM with 6q del. B-cell receptor signaling pathway, and IL21/IL21-R pathway were up-regulated in WM with 6q del.
