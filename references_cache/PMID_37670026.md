tRNA-histidine guanyltransferase 1-like protein (THG1L), located in the 
mitochondria, plays a crucial role in the tRNA maturation process. Dysfunction 
of THG1L results in abnormal mitochondrial tRNA modification and 
neurodevelopmental disorders. To date, few studies have focused on THG1L-related 
cerebellar ataxia. Whole-exome sequencing revealed compound heterozygous 
variants NM_017872.5: [c.224A > G]; [c.369-8T > G] in THG1L in a 6-year-old boy 
with moderate cerebellar ataxia. The variant c.224A > G was demonstrated to 
downregulate its RNA and protein expression, and c.369-8 T > G resulted in a 
7 bp insertion before exon 3. Our case expanded the gene variation and clinical 
spectrum of THG1L-related cerebellar ataxia.
