# Brain transcriptome analysis in the cortex and hippocampus of Cyfip2 Arg87Cys knock-in mice at four different postnatal stages

## Content

To investigate age-dependent molecular changes in the cortex and hippocampus of Cyfip2 Arg87Cys knock-in mice, we conducted transcriptome analysis at postnatal weeks 1, 7, 14, 28.
