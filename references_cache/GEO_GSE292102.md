# Brain transcriptome analysis in the cortex and hippocampus of Cyfip2 heterozygous mice at three different postnatal stages

## Content

To investigate age-dependent molecular changes in the cortex and hippocampus of Cyfip2 heterozygous mice, we conducted transcriptome analysis at postnatal weeks 1, 7, and 14.
