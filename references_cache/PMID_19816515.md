Sir,

Acute annular outer retinopathy (AAOR)1,2is a condition of unknown aetiology, which is considered by many to be a variant of acute zonal occult outer retinopathy (AZOOR).3This condition typically affects young adults and causes photopsia, scotoma, and electroretinographic abnormalities; it is rarely associated with systemic symptoms.
