Author information:
(1)Senior Resident, Department of Paediatrics, Lady Hardinge Medical College , 
New Delhi, India .

We report a case of Congenital Rubella Syndrome (CRS) in a newborn. The baby had 
blueberry muffin skin lesions, long bone metaphysitis and congenital heart 
defects. With this case report we would like to highlight the existence of 
congenital rubella syndrome in the community, prompt the clinicians to make a 
diagnosis of CRS in children with suggestive clinical signs, and to create 
awareness against this vaccine- preventable disease and consideration to include 
MMR vaccination in nation immunization schedule.
