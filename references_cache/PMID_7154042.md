# An autosomal dominant syndrome of uveal colobomata, cleft lip and palate, and mental retardation.
**Authors:** Kingston HM, Harper PS, Jones PW
**Journal:** J Med Genet (1982)
**DOI:** [10.1136/jmg.19.6.444](https://doi.org/10.1136/jmg.19.6.444)

## Content

1. J Med Genet. 1982 Dec;19(6):444-6. doi: 10.1136/jmg.19.6.444.

An autosomal dominant syndrome of uveal colobomata, cleft lip and palate, and 
mental retardation.

Kingston HM, Harper PS, Jones PW.

This report details a family in whom there is autosomal dominantly inherited 
uveal colobomata, associated eye defects, and cleft lip and palate occurring in 
twelve subjects over three generations. Considerable variability in expression 
of the gene is apparent, uveal colobomata being the most constant feature, and 
the full syndrome probably includes mental retardation of varying degree. The 
possibility of association with neural tube defect is discussed.
