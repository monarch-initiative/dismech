**CGGV:assertion_6fa6a520-ae6b-4638-a092-a61831d481bf-2020-12-15T002641.568Z** - KCNJ5 / long QT syndrome (Disputed)

## Evidence summary

KCNJ5 encodes an inward rectifying potassium channel. The gene is located within a 16 million base pair region (11q23.3-24.3) identified by linkage analysis in a large Chinese family with LQTS (PMID 20560207). The Gly387Arg variant identified in this family, however, is now recognized to be present in 1/200 individuals of East Asian descent, making it very unlikely to be a rare cause of a rare disease. Because there is very little other data supporting the association of KCNJ5 with LQTS, the level of evidence for this gene-disease association was classified as disputed.

Note: All LQTS genes were curated by 3 separate blinded teams. The evidence and scores reached by these 3 teams were reviewed by the LQTS Clinical Domain Expert Panel. An International, Multicentered, Evidence-Based Reappraisal of Genes Reported to Cause Congenital Long QT Syndrome. Circulation 2020;141(6):418-428. doi: 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.119.043132”
