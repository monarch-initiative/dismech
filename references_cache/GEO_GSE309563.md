# Differences in miRNAs profile in pregnant women with preeclampsia compared with healthy pregnant women

## Content

Individualized outcome prediction classifiers were successfully constructed through expression profiling of a total of 800 human miRNAs in serum samples of 12 pregnant women (4 samples from severe preeclampsia pregnancies, 4 samples from mild preeclampsia pregnancies and 4 samples from healthy pregnancies)
