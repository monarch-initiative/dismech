OBJECTIVE: Point mutations and deletions within the SOX11 gene have recently 
been described in individuals with a rare variant of Coffin-Siris syndrome, OMIM 
615866, an intellectual disability syndrome with associated features of nail 
hypoplasia, microcephaly, and characteristic facial features including a wide 
mouth and prominent lips.
PARTICIPANT: We describe a further patient with a mutation in SOX11 and 
phenotype resembling mild Coffin-Siris syndrome.
RESULTS: This boy had a cleft palate, a feature not previously seen in other 
patients with SOX11 mutations.
CONCLUSION: We discuss This adds to the current evidence that SOX11 is a gene 
involved in palatogenesis.
