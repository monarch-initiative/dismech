# Mechanically activated ion channel PIEZO1 is required for lymphatic valve formation
**Authors:** Keiko Nonomura, Viktor Lukacs, Daniel T. Sweet, Lauren M. Goddard, Akemi Kanie, Tess Whitwam, Sanjeev S. Ranade, Toshihiko Fujimori, Mark L. Kahn, Ardem Patapoutian
**Journal:** Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (2018)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.1817070115](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1817070115)

## Content

Significance

                    PIEZOs are mechanically activated cation channels. Recently, loss-of-function mutations of human
                    PIEZO1
                    were found among patients with familial lymphedema, suggesting a requirement of PIEZO1 in the lymphatic system. In this paper, utilizing mouse models lacking PIEZO1 in endothelial cells, we show that this ion channel is required for the formation of lymphatic valves, a key structure for proper circulation of lymph in the body. The requirement of PIEZO1 in valve formation provides mechanistic insight on how
                    PIEZO1
                    variants cause lymphatic dysfunction in patients. This study also extends the relevance of PIEZOs beyond acute signaling molecules (e.g., touch sensation) and highlights the importance of these ion channels in controlling morphological/structural specification during development.
