# Single-cell RNA sequencing reveals chondrocyte heterogeneity and fibrocartilage transition in osteonecrosis of the femoral head

## Content

This study investigates the role of chondrocyte heterogeneity in osteonecrosis of the femoral head (ONFH) through single-cell RNA sequencing of 50,851 chondrocytes from 10 ONFH patients and 10 controls.
