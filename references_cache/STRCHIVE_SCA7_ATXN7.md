## Disease description

Spinocerebellar ataxia-7 (SCA7) is an autosomal dominant neurodegenerative disorder characterized by adult onset of progressive cerebellar ataxia associated with pigmental macular dystrophy [@omim:164500].

## Mechanism

Polyglutamine expansion leading to gain of function; toxic misfolded intermediated suspected [@genereviews:NBK1256; @pmid:18418675].

## Age of onset

Typical: 4-48 [@pmid:20739808]; Range: 0-90 [@genereviews:NBK1256; @pmid:14571264].

## Prevalence

- Prevalence: 0.999/300000
- Details: <1/300,000: predominantly found in those with North European and African ancestry [@genereviews:NBK1256].

## Details

Benign alleles range from 4-27 [@pmid:37906407], with intermediate alleles ranging from premutations (28-33) to reduced penetrance (34-36) [@genereviews:NBK1256]. Interruptions observed include CAA [@pmid:35245110].
