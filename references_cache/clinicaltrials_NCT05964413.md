# A Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Multicenter, Adaptive Phase III Trial to Investigate Efficacy and Safety of Vilobelimab in the Treatment of Ulcerative Pyoderma Gangrenosum

## Content

A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter, adaptive phase III trial to investigate efficacy and safety of vilobelimab in the treatment of ulcerative pyoderma gangrenosum
