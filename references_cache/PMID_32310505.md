# Cryoglobulinemic Vasculitis (Archived).
**Authors:** Davuluri S, Vaqar S

## Content

Cryoglobulinemic vasculitis (CryoVas) is an immune complex-mediated inflammation of the small and medium-sized blood vessels, involving complement activation. In 1933, Wintrobe and Buell discovered “cryoprecipitate” in the blood from a patient suffering from multiple myeloma. In 1947, Lerner and Watson coined the term “cryoglobulin” and it applies to immunoglobulins that can precipitate in vitro, from plasma as well as serum when the temperature falls below 37 C and re-solubilize on warming. CryoVas is famously associated with Hepatitis C (Hep-C) as well as some B-cell related blood dyscrasias.
