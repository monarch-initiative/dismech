The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Briefly describe the clinical 
characteristics of hereditary ataxias (sometimes referred to as "primary 
hereditary ataxias") for which an adult with ataxia or the caregivers of a child 
with ataxia would seek diagnosis and management from a neurologist as part of a 
multidisciplinary team; 2.. Review common and notable genetic causes of 
hereditary ataxia; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic 
cause of hereditary ataxia in a proband; 4.. Review management of hereditary 
ataxia; 5.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with 
hereditary ataxia.
