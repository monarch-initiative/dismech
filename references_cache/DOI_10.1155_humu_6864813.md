## Content

Long‐chain fatty acid oxidation disorders (LC‐FAODs) are characterized by the inability to metabolize long‐chain fatty acids. Serious clinical manifestations occur, including cardiomyopathy, hypoglycemia, rhabdomyolysis, and liver failure. Confirming a diagnosis with genetic testing is complicated by the rarity of the disorders, genetic and phenotypic heterogeneity, and the high frequency of variants of uncertain significance.
