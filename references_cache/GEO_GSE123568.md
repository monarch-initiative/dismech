# Identification of Potential Biomarkers for Improving the Precision of Early Detection of Steroid-induced Osteonecrosis of the Femoral Head

## Content

Early diagnosis and prompt treatment are the key to prevent collapse and delay Steroid-induced Osteonecrosis of the Femoral Head(SONFH) progression.The aim of this study was to identify potential biomarkers for the diagnosis of SONFH, so to improve the efficiency of early diagnosis. Microarray analysis based on the 30 SONFH patients and 10 non-SONFH patients(following steroid administration) was performed to screen SONFH-related genes.
