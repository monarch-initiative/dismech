# Epigenetic profiling of neuronal and non-neuronal signal in major depressive disorder

## Content

Characterization of cell type specific (NeuN+/NeuN-) open chromatin by ATAC-seq assay in orbitofrontal cortext of postmortem tissue samples from 19 MMD cases and 18 controls.
