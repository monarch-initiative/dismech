# Hox-C9 activates the intrinsic pathway of apoptosis and is associated with spontaneous regression in neuroblastoma [tumor_genex_44k]

## Content

Single-color gene expression profiles from 649 neuroblastoma tumors were generated using 44K oligonucleotide microarrays. We aimed at determining the association of class I HOX gene expression patterns with prognostic markers in neuroblastoma.We investigated the functional consequences of HOXC9 re-expression on neuroblastoma growth and programmed cell death. One-color gene expression analysis.
