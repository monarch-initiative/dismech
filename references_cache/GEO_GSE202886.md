# Multimodal sequencing (gene expression, chromatin accessibility, and mtDNA genotyping) of single cells of the RPE and choroid in human MELAS (m.3243A>G) and control samples

## Content

The mitochondrial m.3243A>G variant is known to cause retinal dystrophy and vision loss. We used single cell multimodal sequencing to understand how the presence of this mutation affects cellular phenotype in a cell type-specific manner.
