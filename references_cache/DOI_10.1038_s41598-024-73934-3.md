# Clinical study on the intervention effect of digital therapy on children with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)
**Authors:** Sheng Huang, Tianhui Zhang, Qing Lu, Xueqin Xiong, Zhisheng Liu, Dan Sun
**Journal:** Scientific Reports (2024)
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41598-024-73934-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-73934-3)

## Content

effect of digital therapy on children 
with attention deficit hyperactivity

Sheng Huang1, Tianhui Zhang2, Qing Lu1, Xueqin Xiong1, Zhisheng Liu1 & Dan Sun1
To evaluate the efficacy and safety of digital therapy for children aged 6 to 12 years with attention 
deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD). From January to March 2023, 52 children aged 6 to 12 years 
with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) from Wuhan Children’s Hospital, Hubei Province, 
China were selected for intervention using the “MindPro1” attention training software developed 
by Jiangsu Ruinao Qizhi Medical Technology Co., Ltd. Before the intervention, the children were in 
a stable treatment state, and no modifications were made to the original treatment plan during the 
MindPro1 intervention. Subjects with severe mental illness or other conditions that may affect the 
implementation and evaluation of disease treatment were excluded. Subjects completed the planned 
4-week intervention, and changes in attention-related variables were assessed using the Test of 
Attention Variables (TOVA) and the parent version of the 18-item SNAP-IV scale (Swanson, Nolan, 
and Pelham, version IV scale) before and after the intervention. After 4 weeks of intervention, the 
lower limit of the 95% confidence interval of the response rate of the 18-item SNAP-IV-Parent scale, 
which was ≥ 30% improvement from baseline, was higher than 27.5% (better than similar products 
on the market); the SNAP-IV parent score improved (P < 0.001), with statistical significance; the 
TOVA-ACS score improved (P < 0.05), with statistical significance. The acceptance rate of parents of 
children was 100%, and the average compliance rate was 95%. There were 4 cases (7.69%) of adverse 
reactions that may be related to the device in this trial, which recovered spontaneously within 2 
days of discontinuation, and no serious adverse events occurred. After 4 weeks of treatment with 
ADHD auxiliary treatment software, the objective attention assessment data and attention function 
assessment scale were significantly improved. Parents had a high acceptance of the software, the 
average compliance rate of participants was high, and the incidence of related adverse events was low 
and mild.
Keywords Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, ADHD, Digital therapy, Children
Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) also known as Hyperkinetic disorder mainly manifests as age-
disproportionate attention deficit, attention span is shortened, and there is excessive activity and emotional 
impulsivity regardless of the situation, accompanied by cognitive impairment and learning difficulties, with 
normal or near-normal intelligence. ADHD is common in school-age children, but 70% of children have 
symptoms that persist into adolescence, and 30-50% of children have symptoms that persist into adulthood 1. 
ADHD is often comorbid with learning disabilities, oppositional defiant disorder, emotional disorders, and 
adjustment disorders, which have a wide range of negative effects on the patient’s academic, professional, and 
social life. At present, child psychiatrists generally believe that ADHD is a chronic disease with lifelong effects2.
The prevalence of ADHD is generally reported to be 7.2%, The male-to-female ratio is (4 ~ 9):1. The prevalence 
of ADHD in school-age children in China is reported to be 1.3–13.4%; a meta-analysis of 7 large studies showed 
that the prevalence was 4.31–5.83%3, making it the most prevalent psychiatric disorder in children4.
The symptoms of ADHD basically appear before school and are most prominent at the age of 9. With the 
increase of age, the probability of comorbid learning difficulties and other mental disorders increases significantly. 
The comorbidity rates are as follows: disruptive behavior disorders 23%~64%, mood disorders 10%~75%,

one or more comorbidities. The presence of comorbidities often leads to serious impairment of children’s social 
functions, reduced clinical efficacy, and poor prognosis. In order to make the academic level of children with 
ADHD consistent with their intellectual level, about 20% of children with ADHD need special education, and 
15% of children with ADHD need special behavior correction services5.
According to the “Guidelines for the Prevention and Treatment of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder 
in China” , the current treatment methods for ADHD generally include drug therapy and non-drug therapy. 
Recommended drugs include central nervous system stimulant methylphenidate and selective norepinephrine 
reuptake inhibitor atomoxetine. Non-pharmacological treatments include behavioral therapy, parent training, 
and school interventions 5. Psychopharmacological treatments are ineffective in 18–36% of patients and have 
serious side effects. Low patient compliance is a common problem for children diagnosed with ADHD.In 
addition, there is a shortage of pediatricians in China and extremely limited diagnostic and treatment resources. 
There is an urgent need to find alternatives for ADHD assessment and treatment6.
In order to solve these problems, child mental health experts around the world have been actively exploring 
other methods, such as applying new technologies in mental health care. Digital health technology (e-Health) 
can provide high-quality, low-cost and continuously optimized online tools for efficient and standardized 
management of more ADHD patients, which will help to significantly alleviate the huge contradiction between 
the supply and demand of child mental health care services. Patients with ADHD can focus on interesting 
interactive activities, which provides a very good foundation for digital interventions based on smartphone 
applications to increase patients’ compliance and acceptance of treatment interventions.
Digital interventions are usually cognitive training, including a series of tasks designed to improve one or 
more aspects of executive function, such as attention, working memory, reaction time, cognitive flexibility and 
motor performance7. In addition to training related cognitive functions, the treatment also introduces new 
experiences gained from previous training 8. Digital treatment can improve cognitive ability and help improve 
neuroplasticity and the reorganization of neural circuits 9. In addition, digital intervention changes the activity 
of neurons. Through EEG evaluation, studies have found that multi-task training can enhance the consistency 
of frontal lobe EEG and occipital lobe EEG 10. Studies have shown that digital training can stabilize cognitive 
control and improve attention and memory. The impairment of attention and cognitive control in ADHD is 
associated with insufficient activation of the attention network in the frontal and frontoparietal cortices. Studies 
have also shown that video training can significantly increase the gray matter volume of the right hippocampus 
and right dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, which are important for spatial memory, strategic planning, working 
memory, attention, and motor design11.
With the rise of digital therapies, more and more diseases are beginning to be treated using digital therapies. 
The US FDA approved a digital therapy software (EndeavorRx®) to treat ADHD in 2020. EndeavorRx® was shown 
to have a significant improvement in the attention performance index(API)of subjects using the software in 
a 4-week clinical trial, compared with the baseline, the score increased by 0.93, while the score increased by 
0.03 for subjects who used ordinary video games, indicating that this drug has a good therapeutic effect on 
ADHD12,13.
This time, a clinical trial (registration number: ChiCTR2200066452, December 6th, 2022, https://www.chictr.
org.cn/) was conducted on a digital therapy software independently developed in China (Mindpro1, a digital 
intervention software provided by Hunan Ruinao Qizhi Technology Co., Ltd., which has obtained a qualified 
test report approved by the China Food and Drug Administration) to verify the safety and effectiveness of digital 
therapy in the clinical treatment of ADHD.
This trial aims to use the SNAP-IV 18-item scale that can quantify the improvement of ADHD symptoms 
(the SNAP-IV rating scale is compiled according to the Diagnostic and Statistical Guide to Mental Disorders) as 
the primary efficacy indicator, and the attention diagnosis and efficacy evaluation test software Test of Variables 
of Attention (TOV A test) developed by a Shanghai company as a secondary indicator, while considering parents’ 
acceptance and children’s compliance.
Materials and methods
This study is in full compliance with the Declaration of Helsinki, and all subjects have obtained permission from 
themselves or their guardians to conduct this trial; this study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Wuhan 
Children’s Hospital in Hubei, China, and was conducted in strict accordance with the clinical trial requirements 
of the China National Medical Products Administration.
General information
Fifty-two children with ADHD admitted to Wuhan Children’s Hospital Affiliated to Huazhong University of 
Science and Technology in China from January 2023 to May 2023 were selected for inclusion in the study. 
Inclusion criteria: (1) Complying with the American Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 
Fifth Edition (DSM-V) ADHD diagnostic criteria, patients with attention deficit type or mixed type; (2) Age 
6–12 years old, no gender limit; (3) Children’s Wechsler Intelligence Scale ≥ 80; (4) Stable condition for 30 days or 
more before enrollment Follow the first- and second-line standard treatment regimen, and maintain the original 
regimen and dosage during the trial (5) Meet the conditions to comply with the research plan and complete 
treatment testing and other relevant requirements; (6) The patient and/or his guardian agrees to participate in 
this study and voluntarily sign Informed consent form, subjects 8 years old and above should also sign a children’s 
informed consent form. Exclusion criteria are: (1) Suffering from neurodevelopmental disorders such as cerebral 
palsy, epilepsy, adrenoleukodystrophy, etc.; (2) Suffering from severe mental illness such as schizophrenia; (3) 
Hearing impairment leading to similar attention deficit disorders in children – many (4) Patients who are still 
ineffective after systemic treatment with two different types of ADHD drugs; (5) People who are color blind and

Detection methods
Effectiveness evaluation
(1) Main efficacy indicator: The 18-item SNAP-IV-Parent Scale evaluation value after 4 weeks of intervention 
compared with the baseline treatment response rate;
Calculation method: After 4 weeks of intervention, the SNAP-IV measurement value improved by ≥ 30% 
compared with the baseline period is a treatment responder, treatment responder/all persons receiving 
treatment*100%=treatment response rate;
(2) Secondary efficacy indicator 1: Improvement of 18-item SNAP-IV-Parent Scale assessment values from 
baseline after 4 weeks of intervention;
Calculation method: SNAP-IV measurement value at baseline - SNAP-IV measurement value after 4 weeks 
of intervention.
(3) Secondary efficacy indicator 2: improvement in TOV A-ACS measurement data after 4 weeks of 
intervention compared with baseline.
Calculation method: TOV A-ACS measurement value after 4 weeks of intervention - TOV A-ACS measurement 
value during the baseline period;
(4) Secondary efficacy indicator 3: treatment acceptance rate.
Calculation method: A score of ≥ 80 on the self-made “ Acceptance Evaluation Scale for New Treatment 
Informed Consent Process” (100-point system) is defined as parents who accept the new treatment. The number 
of all parents who accept the new treatment/all parents who accept informed consent is considered parent 
acceptance. Rate.
(5) Secondary efficacy indicator 4: MindPro1 compliance rate.
Calculation method: The calculation method for the compliance rate of subjects S001-S019 is: the total 
number of days of training reaching the standard in a course of treatment/the number of planned training days; 
if the actual training of the child using the “MindPro1” game every day is ≥ 5 levels, the training standard is met 
on that day. The calculation method for the compliance rate of subjects S020-S052 is: the total number of levels 
actually completed/the total number of levels planned to be completed.
Safety evaluation
 (1)  (Severe) adverse event incidence rate (%) and frequency of occurrence (number of events).
 (2)  Device-related (serious) adverse event incidence rate (%) and frequency of occurrence (number of events).
 (3)  AE incidence rate: number of AE cases/total number of cases*100%.
 (4)   Incidence rate of device defects.
Test equipment
“MindPro1” training software: It is an independent software (APP) applied to mobile hardware terminals for 
ADHD auxiliary treatment provided by Jiangsu Ruinao Qizhi Medical Technology Co., Ltd. This software 
targets specific visual and spatial task challenges. The nervous system related to attention control in the brain is 
intensively trained to improve children’s training compliance.
Research process
After children who meet the inclusion criteria give informed consent, basic demographic, disease history, 
treatment history and other information are collected, TOV A is measured and SNAP-IV scale data is collected, 
and children and parents are trained on product usage and precautions., after the trial experience of the children, 
they completed the task game for 25 min of training according to the operating procedures every day, and then 
rested for 2 days after 5 consecutive days, and lasted for 4 weeks as a course of treatment.
Statistical analysis methods
Statistical analysis was performed using SAS version 9.4 software. Before and after comparisons between groups 
and within groups were performed using the t test (in line with normal distribution) or Wilcoxon rank sum test. 
For categorical data, the chi-square test or exact probability method was used (when at least one grid number has 
an expected frequency of less than 4). For hierarchical data, the Wilcoxon rank test was used. Sum test (without 
correction for center effect) or CMH test (with correction for center effect). For repeated measurement data, 
repeated measurement analysis of variance was used for statistical comparison. Code combined medication 
according to WHO Drug (WHO Drug Global B3/C3-format March 1 2023), and describe and count the 
occurrence of combined medication according to ATC1/ATC2 classification.
This study presets subgroups for whether combined with drug treatment.

(4) Secondary indicator 4: MindPro1 compliance rate.

Safety evaluation
Adverse events and their handling
This study collected a total of 15 adverse events, which occurred in 10 patients, with an incidence rate of 19.23% 
(10/52). Among them, 4 cases may be related to this device, and the incidence rate is 7.69% (4/52). All adverse 
events were mild. The subjects returned to normal after suspending training and observing for 2 days without 
other treatment, and were able to continue training. There were no subjects who requested to stop training. No 
serious adverse events occurred in this study.
Assessment of gaming addiction
None of the subjects who completed follow-up developed game addiction.
Device defects
A total of 3 cases of device defects occurred in this trial, with an incidence rate of 5.77% (3/52). The incidence 
rate was low and the operation interface was temporarily stuck. It did not affect normal use after restarting the 
equipment.
Discussion
In recent years, with the technological advancement and development of mobile Internet and electronic devices, 
digital therapy has received more and more attention. To date, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration has 
approved hundreds of digital health products. my country started relatively late. Since 2020, the China National 
Medical Products Administration has approved more than 17 digital therapeutic products. In digital therapy, 
products whose main target is to directly treat diseases are called “digital medicines” , and their main application 
areas are mental and psychological related diseases. The application of digital therapy provides a more convenient 
and safe treatment option for children with ADHD.
A recent meta-analysis included a total of 31 studies involving 2169 participants aged 4–17 years old. The 
results showed that digital intervention therapy improved symptoms of inattention, and executive functions 
and working memory were improved12. In a randomized, double-blind, parallel-controlled study involving 348 
patients, AKL-T01, a digital therapy software marketed in the United States, significantly improved objective 
attention indicators measured by TOV A after 4 weeks of training. Subsequently, the software extended the 
evaluation period in further research. After 4 weeks of training and 4 weeks of inactivity, the evaluation indicated 
that the indicators were stable and the treatment effect persisted. The evaluation effect after the second 4-week 
training after an interval of 1 month was further improved. This clinical trial evaluated the digital intervention 
software MindPro1. After 4 weeks of intervention, the participants’ ADHD symptom scale (parental score) and 
objective test attention level were significantly improved, indicating that the treatment is effective. The main

Digital therapy is more easily accepted by parents of children with ADHD because there are no concerns 
about side effects caused by drug metabolism in the body. The EndeavorRx® software reported an incidence rate 
of approximately 18% of device-related adverse events13. In this trial, the incidence rate of device-related adverse 
events was only 7.8%. All were mild adverse events and recovered spontaneously in the short term.
The research results show that MindPro1, like Endeavor Rx ® approved by the foreign FDA, can reduce the 
symptoms of ADHD children 13, improve the attention of ADHD children, thereby improving the cognitive 
ability of children, and the improvement has clinical significance. As a digital non-drug intervention method, 
it has high parent acceptance, good subject compliance, and is easy to use. It can be used as a supplementary 
method for clinical treatment.
This trial still has many limitations. First, the sample size of this trial is small, and it was conducted without 
randomization or blind control. If we want to improve its wide applicability, we need to set up a double-blind and 
controlled design in a larger sample size for randomization and more scientific trials. However, the results of the 
trial can still prove the significance of digital therapy in clinical practice, thereby providing a reference for further 
verification of its safety and effectiveness. Secondly, this study excluded children with severe comorbidities. 
Although children with certain diseases (such as tic disorders and language development disorders) were allowed 
to participate in this study (see Supplementary Table), the premise was that these diseases would not interfere 
with participation and ADHD remained the primary diagnosis. It is well known that comorbidities are common 
in children with ADHD. In the future, increasing research on this population will help guide clinical practice. 
Third, the treatment time of this study was 4 weeks, but it failed to provide information on the long-term effects 
of Mindpro1 on ADHD-related damage and function. ADHD is a chronic disease, and it is crucial to understand 
the long-term treatment effects. As far as we know, even with traditional treatment methods, research is relatively 
insufficient14,15. This study provides evidence that 4 weeks of treatment has at least 4 weeks of stable effects, and 
continued research can lead to continuous improvement of certain results. It will be important to continue 
studying the effects of repeated treatments to better guide the intensity of treatment.
