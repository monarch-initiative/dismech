Gerstmann-Sträussler-Scheinker syndrome (GSS) with the P102L mutation is a rare 
genetic prion disease caused by a pathogenic mutation at codon 102 in the prion 
protein gene. Cluster analysis encompassing data from 7 Czech patients and 87 
published cases suggests the existence of 4 clinical phenotypes (typical GSS, 
GSS with areflexia and paresthesia, pure dementia GSS, and Creutzfeldt-Jakob 
disease-like GSS); GSS may be more common than previously estimated. In making a 
clinical diagnosis or progression estimates of GSS, magnetic resonance imaging 
and real-time quaking-induced conversion may be helpful, but the results should 
be evaluated with respect to the overall clinical context. ANN NEUROL 
2019;86:643-652.
