# MECOM-associated syndrome: a heterogeneous inherited bone marrow failure syndrome with amegakaryocytic thrombocytopenia
**Authors:** Manuela Germeshausen, Phil Ancliff, Jaime Estrada, Markus Metzler, Eva Ponstingl, Horst Rütschle, Dirk Schwabe, Richard H. Scott, Sule Unal, Angela Wawer, Bernward Zeller, Matthias Ballmaier
**Journal:** Blood Advances (2018)
**DOI:** [10.1182/bloodadvances.2018016501](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2018016501)

## Content

Key PointsGerm line mutations in MECOM cause a heterogeneous bone marrow failure syndrome with congenital hypomegakaryocytic thrombocytopenia. MECOM-associated syndrome includes various organ malformations with variable penetrance, including radioulnar synostosis.
