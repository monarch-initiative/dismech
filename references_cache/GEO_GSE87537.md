# MICRORNA EXPRESSİON PATTERNS OF CD8+ T CELLS IN ACUTE AND CHRONIC BRUCELLOSIS

## Content

Using miRNA microarray, more than 2000 miRNAs were screened in CD8 + T cells of patients with acute or chronic brucellosis and healthy controls that were sorted from peripheral blood with flow cytometry and validated through qRT-PCR.
