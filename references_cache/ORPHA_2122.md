# ORPHA:2122  Kaposiform hemangioendothelioma

**ORPHA:2122** — Kaposiform hemangioendothelioma (Disease, Disorder)

## Definition

A rare, locally aggressive, cutaneous or visceral vascular tumor characterized by either superficial lesions presenting as blue-purple infiltrated ill-defined plaques, or deep lesions usually manifesting with coagulation abnormality. They are associated in about 3 out of 4 of cases with potentially lethal thrombocytopenia and consumption coagulopathy (Kasabach-Merritt phenomenon; KMP).
