Ebstein anomaly is a rare congenital heart defect, accounting for less than 1% 
of cardiac malformations and occurring in approximately 1 out of 210,000 live 
births. It is characterized by an abnormality of the tricuspid valve, where the 
valve is positioned lower than normal in the right ventricle. Although primarily 
a tricuspid valve defect, the right ventricle itself is often structurally 
abnormal and weakened (myopathic).
