# ORPHA:2849  Perlman syndrome

**ORPHA:2849** — Perlman syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Nephroblastomatosis-fetal ascites-macrosomia-Wilms tumor syndrome

## Definition

A rare overgrowth syndrome characterized by polyhydramnios, pre- and postnatal macrosomia, muscular hypotonia, renal anomalies, organomegaly, abdominal distension, cryptorchidism, typical facial dysmorphism, neurodevelopmental delay and high neonatal mortality. Individuals surviving infancy show a high risk of developing Wilms tumor.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Antenatal
- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
