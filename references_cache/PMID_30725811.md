# Neuroanatomy, Cranial Nerve 3 (Oculomotor).
**Authors:** Joyce C, Le PH, Peterson DC

## Content

The oculomotor nerve (the third cranial nerve) carries 3 primary motor functions: it provides parasympathetic innervation to the pupil and lens for pupillary constriction and accommodation, supplies somatic motor innervation to the levator palpebrae superioris muscle to elevate the upper eyelid, and innervates most of the extraocular muscles responsible for visual tracking and gaze fixation. Like other nerve fibers in the human body, the oculomotor nerve can be impaired by disease, leading to lifelong impairment of normal vision. Dysfunction can also be indicative of more serious underlying diseases, such as an aneurysm or a neoplasm.
