# miR-22 and miR-205 drive tumor aggressiveness of mucoepidermoid carcinomas of salivary glands [mRNA]

## Content

We used mRNA expression arrays and integrated analysis to study mucoepidermoid carcinomas (MEC) to identify potential drivers involved with its pathogenesis. Normal salivary glands were used as controls.
