# Phase I/IIa Intrathecal Gene Delivery Clinical Trial for IGHMBP2-Related Diseases

## Content

Open-label, single intrathecal injection study of a AAV9 vector carrying the IGHMBP2 gene for IGHMBP2-related diseases.
