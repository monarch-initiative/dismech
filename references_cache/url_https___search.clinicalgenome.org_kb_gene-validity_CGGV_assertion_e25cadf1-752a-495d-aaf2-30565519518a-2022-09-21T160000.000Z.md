<h3>CEACAM16 - nonsyndromic genetic hearing loss </h3>
<div>
<table>


<tr>
<td>Evidence Summary:</td>
<td colspan="3">
<p>CEACAM16 was first reported in relation to autosomal dominant nonsyndromic hearing loss in 2011 (Zheng et al., PMID: 21368133). The split curation for autosomal recessive hearing loss will be curated separately. Five variants (missense, frameshift) that have been reported in five probands in five publications (PMIDs: 21368133, 25589040, 26648831, 28000701, 35292975) are included in this curation. The mechanism of pathogenicity is unknown. This gene-disease relationship is also supported by mouse models and expression data (PMIDs:22544735, 21368133). In summary, there is moderate evidence to support this gene-disease relationship. While more evidence is needed to establish this relationship definitively, no convincing contradictory evidence has emerged. This gene-disease pair was originally evaluated by the Hearing Loss GCEP on 03/20/2018. It was reevaluated on 09/21/2022. A new case with hearing loss (35292975) was scored, but two new mouse model studies were not, due to inconsistent inheritance pattern (PMIDs: 25080593, 31249509) and the classification did not change (SOP Version 9).</p>
