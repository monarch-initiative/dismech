# ORPHA:79279  Alpha-N-acetylgalactosaminidase deficiency type 1

**ORPHA:79279** — Alpha-N-acetylgalactosaminidase deficiency type 1 (Clinical subtype, Subtype of disorder)

## Synonyms

- NAGA deficiency type 1
- Schindler disease type 1

## Definition

A very rare and severe type of NAGA deficiency characterized by infantile neuroaxonal dystrophy.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
