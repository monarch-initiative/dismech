# Single-cell transcriptomic analysis of the tumor ecosystem of adenoid cystic carcinoma.

Adenoid cystic carcinoma (ACC) is a malignant tumor that originates from 
exocrine gland epithelial cells. We profiled the transcriptomes of 49,948 cells 
from paracarcinoma and carcinoma tissues of three patients using single-cell RNA 
sequencing. Three main types of the epithelial cells were identified into 
myoepithelial-like cells, intercalated duct-like cells, and duct-like cells by 
marker genes. And part of intercalated duct-like cells with special copy number 
variations which altered with MYB family gene and EN1 transcriptomes were 
identified as premalignant cells. Developmental pseudo-time analysis showed that 
the premalignant cells eventually transformed into malignant cells. Furthermore, 
MYB and MYBL1 were found to belong to two different gene modules and were 
expressed in a mutually exclusive manner. The two gene modules drove ACC 
progression into different directions. Our findings provide novel evidence to 
explain the high recurrence rate of ACC and its characteristic biological 
behavior.
