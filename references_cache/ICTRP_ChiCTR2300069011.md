## Health condition(s) or problem(s) studied

## Key inclusion & exclusion criteria

Inclusion criteria: 1) Genodermatosis such as EB and NPPK were diagnosed by clinical and genetic tests in our hospital,carried with nonsense mutations;
2) Stable vital signs;
3) The subject or guardian signed the informed consent form.
Exclusion criteria: 1) Patients with a history of allergy to aminoglycosides;
2) Patients with other chronic concomitant diseases or infectious diseases;
3) Impairment of VIII nerves, deafness gene mutation through gene screening, or hearing impairment;
4) Abnormal ECG, chest X-ray, ultrasound, liver and kidney functions;
5) Other conditions, or poor compliance.
Age minimum: 0
Age maximum: 18
Gender: Both

## Primary outcome(s)
