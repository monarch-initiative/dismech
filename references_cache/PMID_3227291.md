# Trehalase deficiency in Greenland.
**Authors:** Gudmand-Høyer E, Fenger HJ, Skovbjerg H, Kern-Hansen P, Madsen PR
**Journal:** Scand J Gastroenterol (1988)
**DOI:** [10.3109/00365528809090759](https://doi.org/10.3109/00365528809090759)

## Content

1. Scand J Gastroenterol. 1988 Sep;23(7):775-8. doi: 10.3109/00365528809090759.

Trehalase deficiency in Greenland.

Gudmand-Høyer E(1), Fenger HJ, Skovbjerg H, Kern-Hansen P, Madsen PR.

Author information:
(1)Medical-Gastroenterological Dept. F, Gentofte Hospital, University of 
Copenhagen, Denmark.

Small-intestinal surgical biopsy specimens from 97 adult Greenlanders showed an 
incidence of trehalase deficiency in at least 8%. Trehalose tolerance tests 
performed in three individuals with low trehalase activity (6 IU/g protein) 
showed no increase in the blood glucose concentration. Three trehalase-deficient 
patients had lactase deficiency too. Trehalase deficiency is not considered to 
have any significance from a nutritional point of view.
