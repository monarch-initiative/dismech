# Neurofibrillary tangles of the Indiana kindred of Gerstmann-Sträussler-Scheinker disease share antigenic determinants with those of Alzheimer disease.
**Authors:** Giaccone G, Tagliavini F, Verga L, Frangione B, Farlow MR, Bugiani O, Ghetti B
**Journal:** Brain Res (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0006-8993(90)91304-y](https://doi.org/10.1016/0006-8993(90)91304-y)

## Content

1. Brain Res. 1990 Oct 22;530(2):325-9. doi: 10.1016/0006-8993(90)91304-y.

Neurofibrillary tangles of the Indiana kindred of Gerstmann-Sträussler-Scheinker 
disease share antigenic determinants with those of Alzheimer disease.

Giaccone G(1), Tagliavini F, Verga L, Frangione B, Farlow MR, Bugiani O, Ghetti 
B.

Author information:
(1)Division of Neuropathology, Istituto Neurologico Carlo Besta, Milano, Italy.

In the Indiana kindred of Gerstmann-Sträussler-Scheinker disease, 
neurofibrillary tangles (NFT) with paired helical filaments (PHF) are numerous, 
widespread and consistently present in the cerebral cortex and several 
subcortical nuclei. Such tangles share antigenic determinants with those of 
Alzheimer disease; in fact, they are recognized by Alz50, anti-PHF and 
anti-ubiquitin antibodies. Thus, NFT with structural and immunocytochemical 
similarities are present in two distinct forms of amyloidosis of the central 
nervous system, i.e. the Indiana kindred of Gerstmann-Sträussler-Scheinker 
disease and Alzheimer disease.
