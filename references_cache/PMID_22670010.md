Congenital syphilis is an infectious disease caused by Treponema pallidum 
transmitted by infected mother to her baby during pregnancy. Late congenital 
syphilis is recognised with 2 or more years after birth. One of the main aspects 
is observed with the triad of Hutchinson, characterised by the presence of 
interstitial keratitis, eighth nerve deafness and Hutchinson's teeth. This 
manuscript reports a case of late congenital syphilis presenting with 
Hutchinson's triad at an age of 7 years. These clinical features are related to 
syphilis present during pregnancy and at birth, however they commonly become 
apparent after 5-years of age.
