As our understanding of iron metabolism improves through the more accurate 
description of iron metabolism actors, new causes of iron overload are 
identified. We, here, report 16 cases of hereditary hypotransferrinemia related 
to 4 previously undescribed TF (transferrin) mutations (p.Val221Gly, 
p.Arg609Trp, p.Glu370Lys, p.Tyr533X and p.Cys421Arg). We show that, besides 
increasing serum transferrin saturation without iron overload, 
hypotransferrinemia, when associated to mutations in HFE or HAMP or to acquired 
factors, can lead to clinically relevant iron burden. These cases emphasize the 
usefulness of serum transferrin determination in the diagnostic evaluation of 
iron overload and the importance for clinicians to be aware of this syndrome.
