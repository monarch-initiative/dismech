# Immune profiling of premalignant lesions in patients with Peutz-Jeghers Syndrome

## Content

RNA sequencing was performed in a total of 26 colorectal tissues from 26 unrelated patients including PJS polyps (n=6), familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP) polyps (n=9), paracancerous colonic mucosae (ConA) (n=9) as well as non-PJS and non-FAP sporadic polyps (ConB) (n=5)
