# Germ line mutations in shelterin complex genes are associated with familial chronic lymphocytic leukemia
**Authors:** Helen E. Speedy, Ben Kinnersley, Daniel Chubb, Peter Broderick, Philip J. Law, Kevin Litchfield, Sandrine Jayne, Martin J. S. Dyer, Claire Dearden, George A. Follows, Daniel Catovsky, Richard S. Houlston
**Journal:** Blood (2016)
**DOI:** [10.1182/blood-2016-01-695692](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2016-01-695692)

## Content

Key Points
Germ line loss-of-function mutations in shelterin genes occur in a subset of families with CLL. Telomere dysregulation is further implicated in CLL predisposition.
