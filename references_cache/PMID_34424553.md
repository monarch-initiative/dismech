# Bi-allelic loss of function variants in GOLGA2 are associated with a complex neurological phenotype: Report of a second family.

GOGLA2/GM130 is a Golgin protein involved in vesicle tethering, cell
proliferation and autophagy. Recessive loss of function mutation in GOLGA2 has
been previously reported in a single family with muscular dystrophy and
microcephaly. Here we describe a second consanguineous family with the
bi-allelic loss of function mutations in GOLGA2. The patient exhibits
microcephaly, seizures, and myopathy similar to the previously reported patient
with GOLGA2 mutation. This report supports the critical developmental
requirement of GOLGA2 and emphasizes a similar and severe clinical presentation
with loss of function mutations in affected patients.
