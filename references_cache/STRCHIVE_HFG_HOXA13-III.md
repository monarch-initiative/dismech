## Disease description

Hand-foot-genital syndrome (HFGS) is a very rare multiple congenital abnormality syndrome characterized by distal limb malformations and urogenital defects [@mondo:0007698].

## Mechanism

Polyalanine expansions leading to haploinsufficiency [@genereviews:NBK1423]

## Age of onset

0 (birth)

## Prevalence

- Details: Extremely rare, published cases generally European ancestry or unknown [@pmid:15385446; @pmid:17935235; @genereviews:NBK1423].

## Details

Anticipation does not occur, and expansions appear fully penetrant; it is unknown if contractions also lead to phenotypic variation [@genereviews:NBK1423].
