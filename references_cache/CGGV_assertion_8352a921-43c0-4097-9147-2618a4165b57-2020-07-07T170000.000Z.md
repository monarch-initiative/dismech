## Evidence summary

TBL1XR1 has been reported in relation to autosomal dominant complex neurodevelopmental disorders including Pierpont syndrome (PMIDs: 26769062, 30365874, 23499424, 25102098, 30008475, 29777588). At least 6 unique missense variants have been reported in the literature. This gene-disease relationship is supported by case level data. More evidence is available in the literature, but the maximum score for genetic evidence (12 pts.) has been reached. In summary, TBL1XR1 is definitively associated with autosomal dominant complex neurodevelopmental disorder. This has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic

settings, and has been upheld over time.
