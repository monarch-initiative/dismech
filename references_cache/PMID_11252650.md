# Calcium channel antagonists for the treatment of bipolar disorder.

Calcium channel antagonists for the treatment of bipolar disorder.

Levy NA(1), Janicak PG.

Author information:
(1)Department of Psychiatry, University of Illinois at Chicago, 60612, USA.

Calcium channel antagonists (CCAs) have many clinical applications, including 
their possible use in the treatment of bipolar disorder. Two justifications for 
this last application are some overlap in physiological activities of CCAs with 
those of lithium, and a possible association between bipolar disorder and 
calcium dysregulation. While the data from earlier studies support the use of 
verapamil in treating bipolar mania. more recent better-controlled trials have 
not. This paper reviews the available body of data regarding CCAs in the 
treatment of bipolar disorder, concluding there is presently limited support for 
their efficacy.
