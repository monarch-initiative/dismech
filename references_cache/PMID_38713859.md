Congenital arhinia-microphthalmos syndrome or BOSMA syndrome is an exceptionally 
rare clinical syndrome characterized by unilateral or bilateral complete absence 
of the nasal cavity associated with several craniofacial, ocular, and systemic 
anomalies. Lacrimal drainage anomalies are secondary to absent nasolacrimal duct 
and usually present as dilated lacrimal sac or mucoceles. While 
navigation-guided dacryocystorhinostomies into the contralateral nasal cavity 
are described for unilateral arhinia, the way forward for the complete absence 
of the nose and nasal cavity is still unclear. A multidisciplinary team from the 
specialties of genetics, plastic surgery, ophthalmic plastics and reconstructive 
surgery, otorhinolaryngology, and endocrinology should get involved very early 
on for better continuity of care.
