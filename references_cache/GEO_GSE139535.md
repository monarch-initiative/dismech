# Single Cell Sequencing Analysis for Wolfram Syndrome (WS4) Unedited and Corrected Stem Cell-Derived Beta Cells

## Content

We revealed transcriptome differences between corrected and unedited (diseased) Wolfram Syndrome patient stem cell-derived beta cells. We also identified several endocrine, pancreatic, and non-pancreatic cell types in the samples populations.
