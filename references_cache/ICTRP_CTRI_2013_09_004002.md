- Scientific title: Efficacy of Scorpion Antivenom plus Prazosin compared with Prazosin alone for scorpion sting envenomation in children- a randomized controlled trial

## Health condition(s) or problem(s) studied

Health Condition 1: null- scorpio sting envenomation

## Intervention(s)

Intervention1: scorpion antivenom: 30 ML intravenous route once
Control Intervention1: prazosin: 30 microgram per kg per dose in children at 3 hourly interval till clearing of all autonomic syndrome 1 week
Control Intervention2: prazosin plus scorpion antivenom: prazosin 30 microgram per kg per dose in children repeated 3 hourly till clearing of autonomic syndrome interval Scorpion antivenom 30 Ml single dose by intravenous route once
