# Hereditary Hyperekplexia Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Balint B, Thomas R

## Content

The goals of this overview on hereditary hyperekplexia (HPX) caused by dysfunction of glycinergic inhibitory transmission is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of hereditary hyperekplexia; 2.. Review the genetic causes of hereditary hyperekplexia; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of hereditary hyperekplexia in a proband (when possible); 4.. Review management of hereditary hyperekplexia; 5.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with hereditary hyperekplexia.
