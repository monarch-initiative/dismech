# ORPHA:49042  Dentinogenesis imperfecta

**ORPHA:49042** — Dentinogenesis imperfecta (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- DGI
- DGI without OI
- DI
- Dentinogenesis imperfecta without osteogenesis imperfecta
- Non-syndromic DGI
- Non-syndromic dentinogenesis imperfecta
- Opalescent teeth without OI
- Opalescent teeth without osteogenesis imperfecta

## Definition

Dentinogenesis imperfecta (DGI) is a hereditary dentin defect characterized by abnormal dentin structure resulting in abnormal tooth development.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Childhood
