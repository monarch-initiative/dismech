OBJECTIVE: To investigate the incidence of endocrinopathies in holoprosencephaly 
(HPE) and correlate the severity of the endocrinopathies with the neuroanatomic 
abnormalities.
STUDY DESIGN: We reviewed the histories and medical records of 117 children with 
HPE for endocrinopathies and related treatments. Neuroimaging studies were 
graded for severity of HPE, hypothalamus non-separation, and pituitary 
abnormalities.
RESULTS: Diabetes insipidus (DI) occurred in 70% of patients with classic HPE. 
The severity of the DI correlated with the grade of HPE and hypothalamic 
non-separation (p < 0.0001). Anterior pituitary dysfunctions were much less 
common. Hypothyroidism was identified in 11% of patients, hypocorticism in 7%, 
and growth hormone deficiency in 5%. Only one patient with middle 
interhemispheric variant of holoprosencephaly (MIH) had any of these disorders.
CONCLUSIONS: Patients with HPE have a high incidence of DI that may be related 
to the failure of cleavage of hypothalamic nuclei. Anterior pituitary 
dysfunctions are much less common than DI.
