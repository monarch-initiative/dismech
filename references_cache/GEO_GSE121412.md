# Epigenetic intersection of BDNF Val66Met genotype with premenstrual dysphoric disorder transcriptome in a cross-species model of estradiol add-back

## Content

Ovariectomized BDNF Val66Met mice, a model that is genetically susceptible to stress, exhibited anxiety- and depression-like behavior when treated with estradiol when compared to wild type mice. Comparing ventral hippocampus of these mice with lymphoblastoid cell line cultures of control women and women with prementrual dysphoric disorder revealed common epigenetic biomarkers that transcend species and cell-type.
