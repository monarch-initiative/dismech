# Bowen-Conradi syndrome in an Indian infant: first non Hutterite case.
**Authors:** Gupta A, Phadke SR
**Journal:** Clin Dysmorphol (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1097/00019605-200104000-00016](https://doi.org/10.1097/00019605-200104000-00016)

## Content

An Indian male infant with the lethal Bowen Conradi syndrome is described. This is the first case in the literature who is not from the Hutterite population.
