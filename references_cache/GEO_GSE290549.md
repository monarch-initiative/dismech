# Common Connective Tissue Disorder and Anti-Cytokine Autoantibodies are Enriched in Idiopathic Multicentric Castleman Disease Patients [CTD Array version 3]

## Content

We measued IgG autoantibodies associated with Connective Tissue Diseases (CTDs) and Anti-Cytokine Antibodies (ACA) in idiopathic Multicentric Castleman Disease (iMCD) patients and healthy controls who received the BNT162b2 vaccine.
