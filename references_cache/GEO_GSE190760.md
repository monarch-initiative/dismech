# Single-Cell RNA sequencing reveals prophylactic Prednisone blunts cellular immunity in hemophilia B gene therapy

## Content

Seven hemophilia B gene therapy participants were taken blood for single cell sequencing at four timepoints, and then compared the immune status of T and antigen presentation cells of theses patients according to therapy efficacy and different time points. The results showed that cellular immunity was elevated in patients with FIX:C less then 40IU/dl at week 26, glucocorticoids (GCs) effectively suppressed T cell immunity and GCs inhibited T cell immunity indirectly by effecting on APCs.
