Lower urinary tract obstruction (LUTO) is a rare birth defect with a prevalence 
between 1 in 5,000 and 1 in 25,000 pregnancies. LUTO is one of the most common 
causes of congenital abnormalities of the renal tract. Several genetic 
conditions have been associated with LUTO. Most common causes of LUTO are 
posterior urethral valves and urethral atresia. Despite available prenatal and 
postnatal treatments, LUTO is a significant cause of morbidity and mortality in 
newborns causing significant end stage renal disease and pulmonary hypoplasia.
