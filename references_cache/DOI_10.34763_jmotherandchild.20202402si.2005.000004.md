# Congenital disorders of glycosylation (CDG), a multigenetic disease family with multiple subcellular locations
**Authors:** Jaeken, Jaak
**Journal:** Journal of Mother and Child. Medycyna Wieku Rozwojowego. 2020;24(2):14-20. Instytut Matki i Dziecka, Warszawa, Polska (2020)
**DOI:** [10.34763/jmotherandchild.20202402si.2005.000004](https://doi.org/10.34763/jmotherandchild.20202402si.2005.000004)

## Content

This review discusses a selection of congenital disorders of glycosylation that show peculiar features, such as an unusual presentation, different phenotypes, a novel biochemical/genetic mechanism, a relatively high frequency or a relatively efficient treatment.
