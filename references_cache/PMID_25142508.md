To date, four families with spinocerebellar ataxia type 5 (SCA5) with four distinct mutations in thespectrin, beta, nonerythrocytic 2gene(SPTBN2)have been reported worldwide. In the present study, we identified the first Japanese family with SCA5, and analyzed this family clinically and genetically. The clinical features of the five patients in this family revealed late-onset autosomal-dominant pure cerebellar ataxia. We collected DNA samples from the majority of the family members across two generations, and exome sequencing combined with Sanger sequencing revealed a novel heterozygous three-nucleotide in-frame deletion mutation (c.2608_2610delGAG) in exon 14 of theSPTBN2gene. This mutation cosegregated with the disease in the family and resulted in a glutamic acid deletion (p.E870del) in the sixth spectrin repeat, which is highly conserved in theSPTBN2gene. This is the first three-nucleotide in-frame deletion mutation in this region of the beta-3 spectrin protein highly likely to be pathogenic based on exome and bioinformatic data.
