# Single-cell transcriptomics analysis of pancreatic primary tumor and metastatic biopsy tissues

## Content

The single-cell RNA profiles of dissociated 10 pancreatic primary tumors and 6 metastatic biopsies were obtained using the 10x Genomics Chromium platform
