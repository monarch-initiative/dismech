# Hyperzincemia and hypercalprotectinemia: unsuccessful treatment with tacrolimus.
**Authors:** Isidor B, Poignant S, Corradini N, Fouassier M, Quartier P, Roth J, Picherot G
**Journal:** Acta Paediatr (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1651-2227.2008.01092.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1651-2227.2008.01092.x)

## Content

UNLABELLED: We report on a 5-year-old boy with hyperzincemia and hypercalprotectinemia. Treatment began with Tacrolimus at the age of 4 years and 6 months. Despite an initial correction of clinical and biological symptoms, zincemia and calprotectinemia progressively worsened with secondary reappearance of symptoms.

CONCLUSION: Tacrolimus seems to have a transient effect in the treatment of Hyperzincemia and hyperprolactinemia.
