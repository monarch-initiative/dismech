<div class="chapter" title="Recommendations" id="ng206-recommendations" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">
  <h2 class="title" id="recommendations">Recommendations</h2>
  <div class="panel panel--primary">
    <p>People have the right to be involved in discussions and make informed decisions about their care, as described in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/about/nice-communities/nice-and-the-public/making-decisions-about-your-care" target="_top">NICE's information on making decisions about your care</a>. </p>
    <p>
      <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/about/what-we-do/our-programmes/nice-guidance/nice-guidelines/using-NICE-guidelines-to-make-decisions" target="_top">Making decisions using NICE guidelines</a> explains how we use words to show the strength (or certainty) of our recommendations, and has information about prescribing medicines (including off-label use), professional guidelines, standards and laws (including on consent and mental capacity), and safeguarding.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="section" title="Box 1 Severity of ME/CFS" id="ng206-box-1-severity-of-mecfs">
    <h3 class="title" id="box-1-severity-of-mecfs">Box 1 Severity of ME/CFS</h3>
    <div class="panel panel--primary">
      <p>Unless stated otherwise, these recommendations apply to everyone with myalgic encephalomyelitis (or encephalopathy)/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS) regardless of symptom severity. There are also additional considerations in the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      <p>Definitions of severity are not clear cut because individual symptoms vary widely in severity and people may have some symptoms more severely than others. The definitions below provide a guide to the level of impact of symptoms on everyday functioning.</p>
      <p>
        <strong>Mild ME/CFS</strong>
      </p>
      <p>People with mild ME/CFS care for themselves and do some light domestic tasks (sometimes needing support) but may have difficulties with mobility. Most are still working or in education, but to do this they have probably stopped all leisure and social pursuits. They often have reduced hours, take days off and use the weekend to cope with the rest of the week.</p>
      <p>
        <strong>Moderate ME/CFS</strong>
      </p>
      <p>People with moderate ME/CFS have reduced mobility and are restricted in all activities of daily living, although they may have peaks and troughs in their level of symptoms and ability to do activities. They have usually stopped work or education, and need rest periods, often resting in the afternoon for 1 or 2 hours. Their sleep at night is generally poor quality and disturbed.</p>
      <p>
        <strong>Severe ME/CFS</strong>
      </p>
      <p>People with severe ME/CFS are unable to do any activity for themselves or can carry out minimal daily tasks only (such as face washing or cleaning teeth). They have severe cognitive difficulties and may depend on a wheelchair for mobility. They are often unable to leave the house or have a severe and prolonged after-effect if they do so. They may also spend most of their time in bed and are often extremely sensitive to light and sound.</p>
      <p>
        <strong>Very severe ME/CFS</strong>
      </p>
      <p>People with very severe ME/CFS are in bed all day and dependent on care. They need help with personal hygiene and eating, and are very sensitive to sensory stimuli. Some people may not be able to swallow and may need to be tube fed.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="section" title="1.1 Principles of care for people with ME/CFS" id="ng206-principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs">
    <h3 class="title" id="principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs">1.1 Principles of care for people with ME/CFS</h3>
    <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
    <div class="section" title="Awareness of ME/CFS and its impact" id="ng206-awareness-of-mecfs-and-its-impact">
      <h4 class="title" id="awareness-of-mecfs-and-its-impact">Awareness of ME/CFS and its impact</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_1_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.1.1</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Be aware that ME/CFS: </p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is a complex, chronic medical condition affecting multiple body systems and its pathophysiology is still being investigated</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>affects everyone differently and its impact varies widely – for some people symptoms still allow them to carry out some activities, whereas for others they cause substantial incapacity</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is a fluctuating condition in which a person's symptoms can change unpredictably in nature and severity over a day, week or longer</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>can affect different aspects of the lives of both people with ME/CFS and their families and <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a>, including activities of daily living, family life, social life, emotional wellbeing, work and education.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_1_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.1.2</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Recognise that people with ME/CFS may have experienced prejudice and disbelief and could feel stigmatised by people (including family, friends, health and social care professionals, and teachers) who do not understand their illness. Take into account:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>the impact this may have on a child, young person or adult with ME/CFS </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>that people with ME/CFS may have lost trust in health and social care services and be hesitant about involving them.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Approach to delivering care" id="ng206-approach-to-delivering-care">
      <h4 class="title" id="approach-to-delivering-care">Approach to delivering care</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_1_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.1.3</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Health and social care professionals should: </p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>take time to build supportive, trusting and empathetic relationships </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>acknowledge to the person the reality of living with ME/CFS and how symptoms could affect them </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>use a person-centred approach to care and assessment</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>involve families and carers (as appropriate) in discussions and care planning if the person with ME/CFS chooses to include them</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>be sensitive to the person's socioeconomic, cultural and ethnic background, beliefs and values, and their gender identity and sexual orientation, and think about how these might influence their experience, understanding and choice of management.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_1_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.1.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Recognise that people with ME/CFS need:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>timely and accurate diagnosis so they get appropriate care for their symptoms</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>regular monitoring and review, particularly when their symptoms are worsening, changing or are severe (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse">section on managing flare-ups in symptoms and relapse</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#review-in-primary-care" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#review-in-primary-care">review in primary care</a>).</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_1_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.1.5</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain to people with ME/CFS and their family or carers (as appropriate) that they can decline or withdraw from any part of their <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a> and this will not affect access to any other aspects of their care. They can begin or return to this part of their plan if they wish to.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_1_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.1.6</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>When working with <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with ME/CFS, ensure their voice is heard by:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>taking a child-centred approach, with the communication focusing on them</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>discussing and regularly reviewing with them how they want to be involved in decisions about their care</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>taking into account that they may find it difficult to communicate and describe their symptoms and may need their parents or carers (as appropriate) to help them</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>recognising that they may need to be seen on more than 1 occasion to gain trust (with or without their parents or carers, as appropriate).</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="panel">
        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs-2">rationale and impact section on principles of care for people with ME/CFS</a>.</p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in: </p>
        <div class="itemizedlist">
          <ul class="itemizedlist">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/a-information-education-and-support-for-people-with-mecfs-and-their-families-and-carers-pdf-9265183022" target="_top">evidence review A: information, education and support for people with ME/CFS and their families and carers</a>
              </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/c-accessing-health-and-social-care-services-pdf-9265183024" target="_top">evidence review C: accessing health and social care services</a>
              </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-2-involving-adults-with-severe-mecfs-symptoms-pdf-333546588759" target="_top">appendix 2: involving adults with severe ME/CFS</a>. </p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
        <p>Other supporting evidence and discussion can be found in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/b-information-education-and-support-for-health-and-social-care-professionals-pdf-9265183023" target="_top">evidence review B: information, education and support for health and social care professionals</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-1-involving-children-and-young-people-pdf-333546588758" target="_top">appendix 1: involving children and young people</a>.</p>
      </div>

  <div class="section" title="1.2 Suspecting ME/CFS" id="ng206-suspecting-mecfs">
    <h3 class="title" id="suspecting-mecfs">1.2 Suspecting ME/CFS</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_2_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.2.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Explain to people presenting with possible symptoms of ME/CFS that there currently is no diagnostic test for ME/CFS and it is recognised on clinical grounds alone.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_2_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.2.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Suspect ME/CFS if:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>the person has had all of the persistent symptoms in box 2 for a minimum of 6 weeks in adults and 4 weeks in <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> <strong>and</strong></p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>the person's ability to engage in occupational, educational, social or personal activities is significantly reduced from pre‑illness levels <strong>and</strong></p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>symptoms are not explained by another condition.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="section" title="Box 2 Symptoms for suspecting ME/CFS" id="ng206-box-2-symptoms-for-suspecting-mecfs">
      <h4 class="title" id="box-2-symptoms-for-suspecting-mecfs">Box 2 Symptoms for suspecting ME/CFS</h4>
      <div class="panel panel--primary">
        <p>All of these symptoms should be present:</p>
        <div class="itemizedlist">
          <ul class="itemizedlist">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>Debilitating <a class="link" href="recommendations#fatigue" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#fatigue">fatigue</a> that is worsened by <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a>, is not caused by excessive cognitive, physical, emotional or social exertion, and is not significantly relieved by rest.</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="recommendations#post-exertional-malaise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#post-exertional-malaise">Post-exertional malaise</a> after <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a> in which the worsening of symptoms:</p>
              <p>
                <div class="itemizedlist">
                  <ul class="itemizedlist">
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>is often delayed in onset by hours or days</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>is disproportionate to the activity</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>has a prolonged recovery time that may last hours, days, weeks or longer.</p>
                    </li>
                  </ul>
                </div>
              </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="recommendations#unrefreshing-sleep" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#unrefreshing-sleep">Unrefreshing sleep</a> or sleep disturbance (or both), which may include:</p>
              <p>
                <div class="itemizedlist">
                  <ul class="itemizedlist">
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>feeling exhausted, feeling flu-like and stiff on waking</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>broken or shallow sleep, altered sleep pattern or hypersomnia.</p>
                    </li>
                  </ul>
                </div>
              </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>Cognitive difficulties (sometimes described as 'brain fog'), which may include problems finding words or numbers, difficulty in speaking, slowed responsiveness, short-term memory problems, and difficulty concentrating or multitasking.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
      <article id="ng206-1_2_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.2.3</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>If ME/CFS is suspected, carry out:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>a medical assessment (including symptoms and history, comorbidities, overall physical and mental health)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>a physical examination</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>an assessment of the impact of symptoms on psychological and social wellbeing</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>investigations to exclude other diagnoses, for example (but not limited to):</p>
              <p>
                <div class="itemizedlist">
                  <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>urinalysis for protein, blood and glucose</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>full blood count</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>urea and electrolytes</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>liver function</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>thyroid function </p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>erythrocyte sedimentation rate or plasma viscosity</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>C-reactive protein</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>calcium and phosphate </p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>HbA1c</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>serum ferritin</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>coeliac screening</p>
                    </li>
                    <li class="listitem">
                      <p>creatine kinase.<br /><br />Use clinical judgement to decide on additional investigations to exclude other diagnoses (for example, vitamin D, vitamin B12 and folate levels; serological tests if there is a history of infection; and 9am cortisol for adrenal insufficiency).</p>
                    </li>
                  </ul>
                </div>
              </p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_2_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.2.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Be aware that the following symptoms may also be associated with, but are not exclusive to, ME/CFS:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="recommendations#orthostatic-intolerance-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#orthostatic-intolerance-2">orthostatic intolerance</a> and autonomic dysfunction, including dizziness, palpitations, fainting, nausea on standing or sitting upright from a reclining position</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>temperature hypersensitivity resulting in profuse sweating, chills, hot flushes, or feeling very cold</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>neuromuscular symptoms, including twitching and myoclonic jerks</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>flu-like symptoms, including sore throat, tender glands, nausea, chills or muscle aches</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>intolerance to alcohol, or to certain foods and chemicals </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>heightened sensory sensitivities, including to light, sound, touch, taste and smell</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>pain, including pain on touch, myalgia, headaches, eye pain, abdominal pain or joint pain without acute redness, swelling or effusion.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_2_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.2.5</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Primary healthcare professionals should consider seeking advice from an appropriate specialist if there is uncertainty about interpreting signs and symptoms and whether an early referral is needed. For children and young people, consider seeking advice from a paediatrician.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_2_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.2.6</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>When ME/CFS is suspected:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>continue with any assessments needed to exclude or identify other conditions</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>give the person advice on managing their symptoms in line with the <a class="link" href="recommendations#advice-for-people-with-suspected-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#advice-for-people-with-suspected-mecfs">section on advice for people with suspected ME/CFS</a>.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Referring children and young people with suspected ME/CFS" id="ng206-referring-children-and-young-people-with-suspected-mecfs">
      <h4 class="title" id="referring-children-and-young-people-with-suspected-mecfs">Referring children and young people with suspected ME/CFS</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_2_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.2.7</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>When ME/CFS is suspected in a child or young person based on the criteria in recommendation 1.2.2 and the assessment in recommendation 1.2.3: </p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>refer them to a paediatrician for further assessment and investigation for ME/CFS and other conditions</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>start to work with the child or young person's place of education or training to support flexible adjustments or adaptations.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="panel">
        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#suspecting-mecfs-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#suspecting-mecfs-2">rationale and impact section on suspecting ME/CFS</a>. </p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/d-identifying-and-diagnosing-mecfs-pdf-9265183025" target="_top">evidence review D: identifying and diagnosing ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="section" title="1.3 Advice for people with suspected ME/CFS" id="ng206-advice-for-people-with-suspected-mecfs">
    <h3 class="title" id="advice-for-people-with-suspected-mecfs">1.3 Advice for people with suspected ME/CFS</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_3_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.3.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>When ME/CFS is suspected, give people personalised advice about managing their symptoms. Also advise them:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>not to use more energy than they perceive they have − they should manage their daily <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a> and not 'push through' their symptoms</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>to rest and convalesce as needed (this might mean making changes to their daily routine, including work, school and other activities)</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>to maintain a healthy balanced diet, with adequate fluid intake.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_3_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.3.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Explain to people with suspected ME/CFS that their diagnosis can only be confirmed after 3 months of persistent symptoms. Reassure them that they can return for a review before that if they develop new or worsened symptoms, and ensure that they know who to contact for advice.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="panel">
      <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#advice-for-people-with-suspected-mecfs-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#advice-for-people-with-suspected-mecfs-2">rationale and impact section on advice for people with suspected ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/e-management-strategies-before-diagnosis-pdf-9265183026" target="_top">evidence review E: management strategies before diagnosis</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="section" title="1.4 Diagnosis" id="ng206-diagnosis">
    <h3 class="title" id="diagnosis">1.4 Diagnosis</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_4_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.4.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Diagnose ME/CFS in a child, young person or adult who has the symptoms in recommendation 1.2.2 that have persisted for 3 months and are not explained by another condition.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_4_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.4.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Primary healthcare professionals should consider seeking advice from an appropriate specialist if there is uncertainty about interpreting signs and symptoms at 3 months and whether further investigations are needed.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_4_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.4.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Refer adults directly to an ME/CFS specialist team (see box 3) to confirm their diagnosis and develop a <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_4_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.4.4</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Refer <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> who have been diagnosed with ME/CFS after assessment by a paediatrician (based on the criteria in recommendation 1.2.2) directly to a paediatric ME/CFS specialist team (see box 3) to confirm their diagnosis and develop a care and support plan.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="section" title="Box 3 ME/CFS specialist team" id="ng206-box-3-mecfs-specialist-team">
      <h4 class="title" id="box-3-mecfs-specialist-team">Box 3 ME/CFS specialist team</h4>
      <div class="panel panel--primary">
        <p>Specialist teams consist of a range of healthcare professionals with training and experience in assessing, diagnosing, treating and managing ME/CFS. They commonly have medically trained clinicians from a variety of specialisms (including rheumatology, rehabilitation medicine, endocrinology, infectious diseases, neurology, immunology, general practice and paediatrics) as well as access to other healthcare professionals specialising in ME/CFS. These may include physiotherapists, exercise physiologists, occupational therapists, dietitians, and clinical or counselling psychologists. </p>
        <p>Children and young people are likely to be cared for under local or regional paediatric teams that have experience of working with children and young people with ME/CFS in collaboration with ME/CFS specialist centres.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="panel">
        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#diagnosis-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#diagnosis-2">rationale and impact section on diagnosis</a>.</p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/d-identifying-and-diagnosing-mecfs-pdf-9265183025" target="_top">evidence review D: identifying and diagnosing ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="section" title="1.5 Assessment and care and support planning by an ME/CFS specialist team" id="ng206-assessment-and-care-and-support-planning-by-an-mecfs-specialist-team">
    <h3 class="title" id="assessment-and-care-and-support-planning-by-an-mecfs-specialist-team">1.5 Assessment and care and support planning by an ME/CFS specialist team</h3>
    <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
    <article id="ng206-1_5_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.5.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Carry out and record a holistic assessment to confirm the person's diagnosis of ME/CFS and inform their <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>. This should include:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>a medical assessment (including relevant symptoms and history, comorbidities, overall physical and mental health, anything that is known to exacerbate or alleviate symptoms, and sleep quality)</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>physical functioning</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>the impact of symptoms on psychological, emotional and social wellbeing</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>current and past experiences of medicines (including tolerance and sensitivities), vitamins and mineral supplements</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>dietary assessment (including weight history before and after their diagnosis of ME/CFS, use of restrictive and alternative diets, and access to shopping and cooking).</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_5_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.5.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Develop and agree a personalised care and support plan with the person with ME/CFS and their family or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> (as appropriate) informed by their holistic assessment. Include the following, depending on the person's needs:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>information and support needs (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#information-and-support" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#information-and-support">section on information and support</a>)</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>support for activities of daily living (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#access-to-care-and-support-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#access-to-care-and-support-2">section on access to care and support</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#social-care" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#social-care">recommendation 1.6.8 on accessing social care</a>)</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>mobility and daily living aids and adaptations to increase or maintain independence (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#aids-and-adaptations" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#aids-and-adaptations">recommendations on aids and adaptations</a>)</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">

    <h3 class="title" id="information-and-support">1.6 Information and support</h3>
    <div class="section" title="Communication" id="ng206-communication">
      <h4 class="title" id="communication">Communication</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.1</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Ensure information is provided to people with ME/CFS and their family or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> (as appropriate):</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>in a variety of formats, such as written materials, electronic and audio, and suitable for their needs (for example, in their preferred language or an accessible version)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>both in person in clinical settings and for them to use at home.<br /><br />Follow the principles on communication, information giving and shared decision making in the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg138/chapter/1-Guidance#enabling-patients-to-actively-participate-in-their-care" target="_top">NICE guidelines on patient experience in adult NHS services</a>, <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng86/chapter/Recommendations" target="_top">people's experience in adult social care services</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng197" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng197">shared decision making</a>.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.2</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>When providing information for <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with ME/CFS, take into account their age and level of understanding, symptoms and any disabilities or communication needs. Use interactive formats such as:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>one-to-one or group discussion</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>written materials and pictures</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>play, art and music activities</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>digital media, for example video or interactive apps.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Information about ME/CFS" id="ng206-information-about-mecfs">
      <h4 class="title" id="information-about-mecfs">Information about ME/CFS</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.3</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Give people with ME/CFS and their family or carers (as appropriate) up-to-date information about ME/CFS as soon as it is suspected. Tailor information to people's circumstances, including their symptoms, the severity of their condition and how long they have had ME/CFS. Ask people regularly if they would like more information or to revisit discussions.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain that ME/CFS:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is a fluctuating medical condition that affects everyone differently, in which symptoms and their severity can change over a day, week or longer</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>varies in long-term outlook from person to person – although a proportion of people recover or have a long period of remission, many will need to adapt to living with ME/CFS</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>varies widely in its impact on people's lives, and can affect their daily activities, family and social life, and work or education (these impacts may be severe)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>can be worsened by particular triggers – these can be known or new triggers or in some cases there is no clear trigger</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>can be self-managed with support and advice (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-management" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-management">section on energy management</a>)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>can involve <a class="link" href="recommendations#flare-up" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#flare-up">flare-ups</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#relapse">relapses</a> even if symptoms are well managed, so planning for these should be part of the energy management plan.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.5</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain to children and young people with ME/CFS and their parents or carers (as appropriate) that the outlook is better in children and young people than in adults.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.6</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Give people with ME/CFS and their family or carers (as appropriate) information about:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>self-help groups, support groups and other local and national resources for people with ME/CFS</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>where to access advice about financial support, including applying for benefits.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Social care" id="ng206-social-care">
      <h4 class="title" id="social-care">Social care</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.7</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Discuss sensitively with the person and their family or carers (as appropriate) how social care may benefit them. Explain that it can help the person living with ME/CFS as well as provide a route to support for families and carers through a formal carer's assessment. Also see <a class="link" href="recommendations#maintaining-independence" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#maintaining-independence">recommendations 1.8.5 and 1.8.6 on maintaining independence</a>.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_8" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.8</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain to people with ME/CFS and their family or carers (as appropriate) how to self-refer for a social care needs assessment from their local authority. Offer to make the referral for them if they prefer.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_6_9" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.6.9</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Advise children and young people with <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">moderate ME/CFS</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">severe or very severe ME/CFS</a> and their parents or carers (as appropriate) that they may be entitled to support from children's social care as children in need because of their disability.</p>
        </div>

  <div class="section" title="1.7 Safeguarding" id="ng206-safeguarding">
    <h3 class="title" id="safeguarding">1.7 Safeguarding</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_7_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.7.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Recognise that people with ME/CFS, particularly those with <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>, are at risk of their symptoms being confused with signs of abuse or neglect.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_7_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.7.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>If a person with confirmed or suspected ME/CFS needs a safeguarding assessment, directly involve health and social care professionals who have training and experience in ME/CFS as soon as possible.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_7_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.7.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>If a person with confirmed or suspected ME/CFS needs to be assessed under the <a class="link" href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents" target="_top">Mental Health Act 1983</a> or the <a class="link" href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents" target="_top">Mental Capacity Act 2005</a>, directly involve health and social care professionals who have training and experience in ME/CFS as soon as possible.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_7_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.7.4</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Recognise that the following are not necessarily signs of abuse or neglect in <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with confirmed or suspected ME/CFS:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>physical symptoms that do not fit a commonly recognised illness pattern</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>more than 1 child or family member having ME/CFS</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>disagreeing with, declining or withdrawing from any part of their <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>, either by them or by their parents or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> on their behalf</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>parents or carers acting as <a class="link" href="recommendations#advocate" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#advocate">advocates</a> and communicating on their behalf</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>reduced or non-attendance at school.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_7_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.7.5</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Be aware that recognising and responding to possible child abuse and neglect (maltreatment) is complex and should be considered in the same way for <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with confirmed or suspected ME/CFS as with any child with a chronic illness or disability. Follow the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg89" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg89">NICE guidelines on child maltreatment</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng76" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng76">child abuse and neglect</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_7_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.7.6</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Offer <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with ME/CFS a review of their care and support plan at least every 6 months, and more frequently if needed, depending on the severity and complexity of their symptoms.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="panel">
      <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#safeguarding-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#safeguarding-2">rationale and impact section on safeguarding</a>.</p>
      <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/b-information-education-and-support-for-health-and-social-care-professionals-pdf-9265183023" target="_top">evidence review B: information, education and support for health and social care professionals</a>. </p>
      <p>Other supporting evidence and discussion can be found in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/a-information-education-and-support-for-people-with-mecfs-and-their-families-and-carers-pdf-9265183022" target="_top">evidence review A: information, education and support for people with ME/CFS and their families and carers</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-1-involving-children-and-young-people-pdf-333546588758" target="_top">appendix 1: involving children and young people</a>.</p>

  <div class="section" title="1.8 Access to care and support" id="ng206-access-to-care-and-support">
    <h3 class="title" id="access-to-care-and-support">1.8 Access to care and support</h3>
    <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
    <article id="ng206-1_8_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.8.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Health and social care organisations should ensure that people with ME/CFS can use their services by:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>adapting the timing, length and frequency of all appointments to the person's needs</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>taking into account physical accessibility, such as how far the person has to travel, whether there is suitable transport and parking, and where rooms are for appointments</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>taking into account sensitivities to light, sound, touch, pain, temperature extremes or smells</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>providing care flexibly to the person's needs, such as by online or phone consultations or making home visits.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_8_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.8.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>If a person with ME/CFS misses an appointment:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>do not discharge them for not attending because it could be due to their symptoms worsening</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>discuss why they could not attend and how the multidisciplinary team can support them.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_8_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.8.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Be aware that people with ME/CFS are unlikely to be seen at their worst because:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>debilitating symptoms or the risk that their symptoms will worsen may prevent people from leaving their home</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>cognitive difficulties may often mean people wait until they feel they can speak and explain clearly before contacting services.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="section" title="Hospital care" id="ng206-hospital-care">
      <h4 class="title" id="hospital-care">Hospital care</h4>
      <p>For improving access to hospital inpatient and outpatient care for people with ME/CFS, see recommendation 1.8.1.</p>
      <article id="ng206-1_8_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.8.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Discuss with people who need inpatient care whether any aspects of where their care will be provided could cause problems for them, including:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>where a bed is situated on a ward (if possible, aim to provide a single room)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>the accessibility of toilets and washrooms</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>environmental factors such as lighting, sound, heating and smells.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Maintaining independence" id="ng206-maintaining-independence">
      <h4 class="title" id="maintaining-independence">Maintaining independence</h4>
      <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#social-care" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#social-care">recommendations on social care</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#supporting-families-and-carers-of-people-with-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#supporting-families-and-carers-of-people-with-mecfs">supporting families and carers of people with ME/CFS</a>. </p>
      <article id="ng206-1_8_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.8.5</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>If a person with ME/CFS needs support at home, carry out a social care needs assessment. As a minimum, record and provide information and support on:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>activities of daily living</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>mobility, including transferring from bed to chair, access to and use of toilet and washing facilities, use of stairs, and access to outside space</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>dexterity and balance, including avoiding falls</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>their home, including environmental controls to reduce light levels, sound levels and temperature fluctuations</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>the feasibility of equipment and adaptations</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>access to technology, including internet access</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>where to get financial support and advice, for example signposting to advice on money management and making personalised arrangements with banks or the Post Office to access personal finances, and how to claim carers' and disability benefits and grants.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_8_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.8.6</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Give families and <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> information on how to access training and resources about caring for the person with ME/CFS (see the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng150" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng150">NICE guideline on supporting adult carers</a>).</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="section" title="Aids and adaptations" id="ng206-aids-and-adaptations">
        <h5 class="title" id="aids-and-adaptations">Aids and adaptations</h5>
        <article id="ng206-1_8_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
          <h6 class="recommendation__number">1.8.7</h6>
          <div class="recommendation__body">
            <p>Enable prompt assessment for funding for home adaptation. If the person is not eligible for funding, continue to offer information and support in arranging home adaptations.</p>
          </div>
        </article>
        <article id="ng206-1_8_8" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
          <h6 class="recommendation__number">1.8.8</h6>
          <div class="recommendation__body">
            <p>For people with <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">moderate ME/CFS</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>, consider providing or recommending aids and adaptations (such as a wheelchair, blue badge or stairlift) that could help them maintain their independence and improve their quality of life, taking into account the risks and benefits. Include these in the person's <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>.</p>
          </div>
        </article>
        <article id="ng206-1_8_9" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
          <h6 class="recommendation__number">1.8.9</h6>
          <div class="recommendation__body">
            <p>Provide aids and adaptations identified in the person's social care needs assessment without delay, so that people with ME/CFS can carry out activities of daily living and maintain their quality of life as much as possible.</p>
          </div>
        </article>
        <div class="panel">
          <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#access-to-care-and-support-3" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#access-to-care-and-support-3">rationale and impact section on access to care and support</a>.</p>
          <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/c-accessing-health-and-social-care-services-pdf-9265183024" target="_top">evidence review C: accessing health and social care services</a>. </p>
          <p>Other supporting evidence and discussion can be found in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/a-information-education-and-support-for-people-with-mecfs-and-their-families-and-carers-pdf-9265183022" target="_top">evidence review A: information, education and support for people with ME/CFS and their families and carers</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-2-involving-adults-with-severe-mecfs-symptoms-pdf-333546588759" target="_top">appendix 2: involving adults with severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
        </div>

  <div class="section" title="1.9 Supporting people with ME/CFS in work, education and training" id="ng206-supporting-people-with-mecfs-in-work-education-and-training">
    <h3 class="title" id="supporting-people-with-mecfs-in-work-education-and-training">1.9 Supporting people with ME/CFS in work, education and training</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_9_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.9.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Offer to liaise on the person's behalf (with their informed consent) with employers, education providers and support services. Give them information about ME/CFS and discuss the person's <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a> and any adjustments needed.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_9_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.9.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Advise and discuss with people with ME/CFS that:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>they may be able to access reasonable adjustments or adaptations (in line with the <a class="link" href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/contents" target="_top">Equality Act 2010</a>) to help them continue or return to work or education</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>there may be times when they are unable to continue with work or education</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>some people find that going back to work, school or college worsens their symptoms.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_9_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.9.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Health and social care professionals should follow the <a class="link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/supporting-pupils-at-school-with-medical-conditions--3" target="_top">Department for Education's guidance on supporting pupils at school with medical conditions</a> or equivalent statutory guidance.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_9_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.9.4</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Health and social care professionals should work with training and education services to:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>provide information about ME/CFS and the needs and impairments of <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with ME/CFS, including the need for a balance of activities in their life</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>discuss the child or young person's care and support plan so that everyone has a common understanding of their priorities, hopes and plans</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>discuss a flexible approach to training and education – this could include adjustments to the school day, online learning or education at home and using assistive equipment.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_9_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.9.5</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Give parents and <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> information about education, health and care (EHC) plans and how to request one from their local authority.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_9_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.9.6</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Advise children and young people with ME/CFS and their parents or carers (as appropriate) that:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>training or education should not be the only <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a> they undertake</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>they should aim to find a balance between the time they spend on education or training, home and family life, and social activities.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="panel">
      <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#supporting-people-with-mecfs-in-work-education-and-training-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#supporting-people-with-mecfs-in-work-education-and-training-2">rationale and impact section on supporting people with ME/CFS in work, education and training</a>.</p>
      <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/a-information-education-and-support-for-people-with-mecfs-and-their-families-and-carers-pdf-9265183022" target="_top">evidence review A: information, education and support for people with ME/CFS and their families and carers</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-1-involving-children-and-young-people-pdf-333546588758" target="_top">appendix 1: involving children and young people</a>. </p>
    </div>

  <div class="section" title="1.10 Multidisciplinary care" id="ng206-multidisciplinary-care">
    <h3 class="title" id="multidisciplinary-care">1.10 Multidisciplinary care</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_10_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.10.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Provide care for people with ME/CFS using a coordinated multidisciplinary approach. Based on the person's needs, include access to health and social care professionals with expertise in the following as a minimum, with additional expertise depending on symptoms:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>medical assessment and diagnosis</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>developing personalised <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plans</a></p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>self-management strategies, including <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-management-3" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-management-3">energy management</a></p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>symptom management, including prescribing and medicines management</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>managing <a class="link" href="recommendations#flare-up" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#flare-up">flare-ups</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#relapse">relapses</a></p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>activities of daily living, including dental health</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>psychological, emotional and social wellbeing, including family and sexual relationships</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>diet and nutrition</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>mobility, avoiding falls and problems from loss of dexterity, including access to aids and rehabilitation services</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>social care and support</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>support to engage in work, education, social activities and hobbies.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_10_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.10.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Care for people whose ME/CFS is managed in primary care should be supported by advice and direct clinical consultation from an <a class="link" href="recommendations#mecfs-specialist-team" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#mecfs-specialist-team">ME/CFS specialist team</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_10_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.10.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Give adults, <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with ME/CFS and their family or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> (as appropriate) a named contact in their primary care and/or ME/CFS specialist team to coordinate their care and support plan, help them access services and support them during periods of relapse.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_10_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.10.4</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Provide children and young people with ME/CFS and their family or carers (as appropriate) with details of a named professional in the ME/CFS specialist team who they can contact with any concerns about the child or young person's health, education or social life.</p>
      </div>

    <div class="section" title="Energy management" id="ng206-energy-management">
      <h4 class="title" id="energy-management">Energy management</h4>
      <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.2</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Discuss with people with ME/CFS the principles of <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-management-3" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-management-3">energy management</a>, the potential benefits and risks and what they should expect. Explain that it:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is not curative</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is a self-management strategy led by the person themselves with support from a healthcare professional in an <a class="link" href="recommendations#mecfs-specialist-team" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#mecfs-specialist-team">ME/CFS specialist team</a></p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>includes all types of <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a> (cognitive, physical, emotional and social) and takes into account overall level of activity</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>helps people learn to use the amount of energy they have while reducing their risk of <a class="link" href="recommendations#post-exertional-malaise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#post-exertional-malaise">post-exertional malaise</a> or worsening their symptoms by exceeding their limits</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>recognises that each person has a different and fluctuating <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-limit" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-limit">energy limit</a> and they are experts in judging their own limits</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>can include help from a healthcare professional to recognise when they are approaching their limit (<a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> in particular may find it harder to judge their limits and can overreach them)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>uses a flexible, tailored approach so that activity is never automatically increased but is maintained or adjusted (upwards after a period of stability or downwards when symptoms are worse)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is a long-term approach − it can take weeks, months or sometimes even years to reach stabilisation or to increase tolerance or activity.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.3</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Help people with ME/CFS develop a plan for energy management as part of their <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>. Support them to establish realistic expectations and develop goals that are meaningful to them. Discuss and record the following in the plan along with anything else that is important to the person:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>cognitive activity</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>mobility and other <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-activity">physical activity</a></p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>ability to undertake activities of daily living</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>psychological, emotional and social demands, including family and sexual relationships</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>rest and relaxation (both quality and duration)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>sleep quality and duration</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>effect of environmental factors, including sensory stimulation.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Work with the person to establish an individual activity pattern within their current energy limits that minimises their symptoms. For example:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>agree a sustainable level of activity as the first step, which may mean reducing activity</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>plan periods of rest and activity, and incorporate the need for pre-emptive rest</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>alternate and vary between different types of activity and break activities into small chunks.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.5</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Agree how often to review the person's energy management plan with them and revise it if needed.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.6</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Advise people with ME/CFS how to manage <a class="link" href="recommendations#flare-up" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#flare-up">flare-ups</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#relapse">relapses</a> (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse">section on managing flare-ups in symptoms and relapse</a>).</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.7</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Make self-monitoring of activity as easy as possible by taking advantage of any tools the person already uses, such as an activity tracker, phone heart-rate monitor or diary.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_8" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.8</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Refer people with ME/CFS to a physiotherapist or occupational therapist working in an ME/CFS specialist team if they:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>have difficulties caused by reduced physical activity or mobility (also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-functioning-and-mobility" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-functioning-and-mobility">sections on physical functioning and mobility</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>) <strong>or</strong></p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>feel ready to progress their physical activity beyond their current activities of daily living (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#incorporating-physical-activity-and-exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#incorporating-physical-activity-and-exercise">section on physical activity and exercise</a>) <strong>or</strong></p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>would like to incorporate a physical activity or <a class="link" href="recommendations#exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#exercise">exercise</a> programme into managing their ME/CFS (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#incorporating-physical-activity-and-exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#incorporating-physical-activity-and-exercise">section on incorporating physical activity and exercise</a>).</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="panel">
        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#energy-management-4" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#energy-management-4">rationale and impact section on energy management</a>. </p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/g-nonpharmacological-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183028" target="_top">evidence review G: non-pharmacological management of ME/CFS</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/h-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183029" target="_top">evidence review H: appendices for the management of ME/CFS</a>.</p>
        <p>Other supporting evidence and discussion can be found in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/a-information-education-and-support-for-people-with-mecfs-and-their-families-and-carers-pdf-9265183022" target="_top">evidence review A: information, education and support for people with ME/CFS and their families and carers</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-2-involving-adults-with-severe-mecfs-symptoms-pdf-333546588759" target="_top">appendix 2: involving adults with severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      </div>

    <div class="section" title="Incorporating physical activity and exercise" id="ng206-incorporating-physical-activity-and-exercise">
      <h4 class="title" id="incorporating-physical-activity-and-exercise">Incorporating physical activity and exercise</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_9" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.9</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Do not advise people with ME/CFS to undertake <a class="link" href="recommendations#exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#exercise">exercise</a> that is not part of a programme overseen by an <a class="link" href="recommendations#mecfs-specialist-team" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#mecfs-specialist-team">ME/CFS specialist team</a>, such as telling them to go to the gym or exercise more, because this may worsen their symptoms.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_10" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.10</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Only consider a personalised <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-activity">physical activity</a> or exercise programme for people with ME/CFS who:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>feel ready to progress their physical activity beyond their current activities of daily living <strong>or</strong></p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>would like to incorporate physical activity or exercise into managing their ME/CFS.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_11" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.11</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Tell people about the risks and benefits of physical activity and exercise programmes. Explain that some people with ME/CFS have found that they can make their symptoms worse, for some people it makes no difference and others find them helpful.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_12" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.12</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>If a physical activity or exercise programme is offered, it should be overseen by a physiotherapist in an ME/CFS specialist team. </p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_13" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.13</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>If a person with ME/CFS takes up the offer of a personalised physical activity or exercise programme, agree a programme with them that involves the following and review it regularly:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>establishing their physical activity baseline at a level that does not worsen their symptoms </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>initially reducing physical activity to be below their baseline level</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>maintaining this successfully for a period of time before attempting to increase it</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>making flexible adjustments to their physical activity (up or down as needed) to help them gradually improve their physical abilities while staying within their energy limits</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>recognising a <a class="link" href="recommendations#flare-up" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#flare-up">flare-up</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#relapse">relapse</a> early and outlining how to manage it.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_14" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.14</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Do not offer people with ME/CFS:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>any therapy based on physical activity or exercise as a cure for ME/CFS</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>generalised physical activity or exercise programmes – this includes programmes developed for healthy people or people with other illnesses</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>any programme that does not follow the approach in recommendation 1.11.13 or that uses fixed incremental increases in physical activity or exercise, for example, <a class="link" href="recommendations#graded-exercise-therapy" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#graded-exercise-therapy">graded exercise therapy</a> (see box 4)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>physical activity or exercise programmes that are based on deconditioning and exercise avoidance theories as perpetuating ME/CFS.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Box 4 Graded exercise therapy definition" id="ng206-box-4-graded-exercise-therapy-definition">
      <h4 class="title" id="box-4-graded-exercise-therapy-definition">Box 4 Graded exercise therapy definition</h4>
      <div class="panel panel--primary">
        <p>Graded exercise therapy is a term used in varying ways by different services supporting people with ME/CFS. </p>
        <p>In this guideline, graded exercise therapy is defined as first establishing an individual's baseline of achievable exercise or physical activity, then making fixed incremental increases in the time spent being physically active. This definition of graded exercise therapy reflects the descriptions given in the evidence that was reviewed, and it is this approach that the guideline says should not be undertaken.</p>
        <p>An individualised approach that should be taken for people with ME/CFS who choose to undertake a physical activity or exercise programme is described in recommendations 1.11.10 to 1.11.13.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Flare-ups and relapse" id="ng206-flare-ups-and-relapse">
      <h4 class="title" id="flare-ups-and-relapse">Flare-ups and relapse</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_15" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.15</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Agree with the person how to adjust their physical activity during a flare‑up or relapse. This should include:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>providing access to review and support from a physiotherapist in an ME/CFS specialist team</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>stabilising their symptoms by reducing physical activity to within their current energy limits</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>only once symptoms stabilise and the person feels able to resume physical activity, establishing a new physical activity baseline.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_11_16" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.11.16</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Advise people with ME/CFS after a flare‑up that the time it takes to return to the level of physical activity they had before varies from person to person.</p>
        </div>
      </article>

    <h3 class="title" id="symptom-management-for-people-with-mecfs">1.12 Symptom management for people with ME/CFS</h3>
    <p>Refer to relevant NICE guidance for managing symptoms that are not covered in this section, taking into account the recommendations in the <a class="link" href="recommendations#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs">sections on principles of care for people with ME/CFS</a>, <a class="link" href="recommendations#access-to-care-and-support" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#access-to-care-and-support">access to care and support </a>and <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-management" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-management">energy management</a>.</p>
    <div class="section" title="Rest and sleep" id="ng206-rest-and-sleep">
      <h4 class="title" id="rest-and-sleep">Rest and sleep</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.1</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Advise people with ME/CFS:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>about the role of rest in ME/CFS</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>that rest periods are part of all management strategies for ME/CFS</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>how to introduce rest periods into their daily routine, including how often and for how long, as appropriate for each person</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>that relaxation techniques at the beginning of each rest period could be helpful.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.2</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Give people with ME/CFS personalised sleep management advice that includes:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>explaining the role and effect of sleep disturbance in ME/CFS</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>identifying the common changes in sleep patterns seen in ME/CFS (such as broken or shallow sleep, altered sleep pattern or hypersomnia)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>developing good sleep habits</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>taking into account the need for rest in the day, and balancing this against how the person is sleeping at night</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>introducing changes to sleep patterns gradually.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.3</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>If sleep management strategies do not improve the person's sleep and rest, think about the possibility of an underlying sleep disorder or dysfunction and whether to refer to an appropriate specialist.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Review the use of rest periods and sleep management strategies regularly as part of the person's <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="panel">

        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/g-nonpharmacological-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183028" target="_top">evidence review G: non-pharmacological management of ME/CFS</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/h-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183029" target="_top">evidence review H: appendices for the management of ME/CFS</a>.</p>

    <div class="section" title="Physical functioning and mobility" id="ng206-physical-functioning-and-mobility">
      <h4 class="title" id="physical-functioning-and-mobility">Physical functioning and mobility</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.5</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Include strategies to maintain and prevent deterioration of <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-functioning-and-mobility-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-functioning-and-mobility-2">physical functioning and mobility</a> in the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plans</a> of people with ME/CFS. These strategies may need to be carried out in small amounts and spread out throughout the day. Think about including the following:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>joint mobility</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>muscle flexibility</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>balance</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>postural and positional support</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>muscle function</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>bone health</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>cardiovascular health.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.6</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Assess at every contact people with <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">severe</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">very severe ME/CFS</a> or those with prolonged periods of immobility for:</p>

            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>deep vein thrombosis (see the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng158" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng158">NICE guideline on venous thromboembolic diseases</a>)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>risk of contractures.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.7</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Give people with ME/CFS and their family or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> (as appropriate) information, advice and support on how to recognise and prevent possible complications of long-term immobility.</p>
        </div>
      </article>

        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.8</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Give families and carers information, advice and support on how to help people with ME/CFS follow their care and support plan in relation to physical functioning and mobility. This may include:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>bed mobility</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>moving from lying to sitting to standing</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>transferring from bed to chair</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>using mobility aids</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>walking</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>joint mobility</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>muscle stretching</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>muscle strength</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>balance</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>going up and down stairs.<br /><br />For training to provide care and support, see <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng150" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng150">NICE's guideline on supporting adult carers</a>.</p>

    <div class="section" title="Orthostatic intolerance" id="ng206-orthostatic-intolerance">
      <h4 class="title" id="orthostatic-intolerance">Orthostatic intolerance</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_9" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.9</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">

    <div class="section" title="Dietary management and strategies" id="ng206-dietary-management-and-strategies">
      <h4 class="title" id="dietary-management-and-strategies">Dietary management and strategies</h4>
      <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_19" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.19</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Emphasise to people with ME/CFS the importance of adequate fluid intake and a well-balanced diet according to the <a class="link" href="https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/" target="_top">NHS eat well guide</a>.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_20" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.20</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Work with the person and their family or carers (as appropriate) to find ways of minimising complications caused by gastrointestinal symptoms (such as nausea), changes to appetite, swallowing difficulties, sore throat or difficulties with buying, preparing and eating food.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_21" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.21</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Encourage people with ME/CFS who have nausea to keep up adequate fluid intake and advise them to try to eat regularly, taking small amounts often. Explain that not eating or drinking may increase their nausea.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_22" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.22</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Refer people with ME/CFS for a dietetic assessment by a dietitian with a <a class="link" href="recommendations#special-interest-in-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#special-interest-in-mecfs">special interest in ME/CFS</a> if they are:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>losing weight and at risk of malnutrition</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>gaining weight</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>following a restrictive diet.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_23" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.23</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Be aware that people with ME/CFS may be at risk of vitamin D deficiency, especially those who are housebound or bedbound. For advice on vitamin D supplementation, see the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ph56" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ph56">NICE guideline on vitamin D</a>.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_24" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.24</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain to people with ME/CFS that there is not enough evidence to support routinely taking vitamin and mineral supplements as a cure for ME/CFS or for managing symptoms. If they choose to take a vitamin or supplement, explain the potential side effects of taking doses of vitamins and minerals above the recommended daily amount.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_25" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.25</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Refer children and young people with ME/CFS who are losing weight or have faltering growth or dietary restrictions to a paediatric dietitian with a special interest in ME/CFS. </p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_26" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.26</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>For advice on food allergies in children, see the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg116" target="_top" data-original-url="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg116">NICE guideline on food allergy in under 19s</a>. </p>

    <div class="section" title="Cognitive behavioural therapy" id="ng206-cognitive-behavioural-therapy">
      <h4 class="title" id="cognitive-behavioural-therapy">Cognitive behavioural therapy</h4>
      <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">section on care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</a>. </p>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_28" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.28</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Discuss cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) with adults, children and young people with ME/CFS (and their parents or carers, as appropriate). Explain:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>its principles, including that it may help them manage their symptoms but it is not curative (see box 5) <strong>and</strong></p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>any potential benefits and risks.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Box 5 Cognitive behavioural therapy" id="ng206-box-5-cognitive-behavioural-therapy">
      <h4 class="title" id="box-5-cognitive-behavioural-therapy">Box 5 Cognitive behavioural therapy</h4>
      <div class="panel panel--primary">
        <p>The committee wanted to highlight that cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) has sometimes been assumed to be a cure for ME/CFS. However, it should only be offered to support people who live with ME/CFS to manage their symptoms, improve their functioning and reduce the distress associated with having a chronic illness.</p>
      </div>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_29" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.29</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Only offer CBT to adults, children and young people with ME/CFS if, after discussing it (see recommendation 1.12.28), they would like to use it to support them in managing their symptoms.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_30" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.30</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>For children and young people with ME/CFS who would like to use CBT: </p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>involve parents or carers (as appropriate) in the therapy wherever possible </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>adapt the therapy to the child or young person's cognitive and emotional stage of development.<br /><br />Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs">section on principles of care for people with ME/CFS</a> (including the additional principles of care for children and young people with ME/CFS).</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_31" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.31</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>CBT should only be delivered by a healthcare professional with appropriate training and experience in CBT for ME/CFS, and under the clinical supervision of someone with expertise in CBT for ME/CFS.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_32" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.32</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain that CBT for people with ME/CFS:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>aims to improve their quality of life, including functioning, and reduce the distress associated with having a chronic illness </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>does not assume people have 'abnormal' illness beliefs and behaviours as an underlying cause of their ME/CFS, but recognises that thoughts, feelings, behaviours and physiology interact with each other.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_33" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.33</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Explain what CBT involves so people know what to expect. Tell them that it:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>takes a non-judgemental, supportive approach to the person's experience of their symptoms and the challenges these present</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>is a collaborative, structured, time-limited intervention that focuses on the difficulties people are having at that time</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>involves working closely with their therapist to establish strategies to work towards goals and priorities that they have chosen themselves</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>takes into account how symptoms are individual to each person, can fluctuate in severity and may change over time.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_12_34" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.12.34</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>CBT for people with ME/CFS should include the following components:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>developing a shared understanding with the person about the main difficulties and challenges they face</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>exploring the personal meaning of their symptoms and illness, and how this might relate to how they manage their symptoms</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>developing a self-management plan</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>working together to adapt and refine self-management strategies to improve the person's functioning and quality of life, for example their sleep, <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a> and rest</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>reviewing their plan regularly to see if their self-management strategies need to be adapted, for example if their symptoms or functioning change</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>developing a <a class="link" href="recommendations#therapy-blueprint" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#therapy-blueprint">therapy blueprint</a> collaboratively with their therapist at the end of therapy.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="panel">
        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#cognitive-behavioural-therapy-3" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#cognitive-behavioural-therapy-3">rationale and impact section on cognitive behavioural therapy</a>.</p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in:</p>
        <div class="itemizedlist">
          <ul class="itemizedlist">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/g-nonpharmacological-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183028" target="_top">evidence review G: non-pharmacological management of ME/CFS</a>
              </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/h-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183029" target="_top">evidence review H: appendices for the management of ME/CFS</a>
              </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/appendix-2-involving-adults-with-severe-mecfs-symptoms-pdf-333546588759" target="_top">appendix 2: involving adults with severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
            </li>

  <div class="section" title="1.14 Managing flare-ups in symptoms and relapse" id="ng206-managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse">
    <h3 class="title" id="managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse">1.14 Managing flare-ups in symptoms and relapse</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Explain that <a class="link" href="recommendations#flare-up" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#flare-up">flare-ups</a> and <a class="link" href="recommendations#relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#relapse">relapses</a> can happen in ME/CFS even if the person's symptoms are well managed.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Tell people with ME/CFS that: </p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>they are likely to be having a flare‑up if they experience a worsening of their symptoms beyond their normal day-to-day variation, which lasts a few days</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>a relapse is when there is a sustained and marked exacerbation of ME/CFS symptoms lasting longer than a flare‑up and needing substantial and sustained adjustment of <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-management-3" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-management-3">energy management</a>.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Include guidance on managing flare‑ups and relapses in the person's <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.4</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Evaluate and investigate any new symptoms or a change in symptoms and do not assume they are caused by the person's ME/CFS.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.5</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Discuss and agree self-management strategies with the person with ME/CFS to help them respond promptly if they have a flare‑up or relapse, and record these in their care and support plan. This should include:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>For a flare‑up:</p>
            <p>
              <div class="itemizedlist">
                <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>identifying possible triggers, such as acute illness or overexertion (in some cases, there may be no clear trigger)</p>
                  </li>
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>temporarily reducing their <a class="link" href="recommendations#activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#activity">activity</a> levels</p>
                  </li>
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>monitoring symptoms, recognising that although flare‑ups are transient, some will develop into a relapse</p>
                  </li>
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>not returning to usual activity levels until the flare‑up has resolved.</p>
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </div>
            </p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>For a relapse:</p>
            <p>
              <div class="itemizedlist">
                <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>reducing, or even stopping, some activities</p>
                  </li>
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>increasing the frequency or duration of rest periods</p>
                  </li>
                  <li class="listitem">
                    <p>reassessing <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-limit" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-limit">energy limits</a> to stabilise symptoms.</p>
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </div>
            </p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.6</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>If a flare-up or relapse cannot be managed using the person's self-management strategies outlined in their care and support plan or they are worried about new symptoms or a change in symptoms, advise the person to contact their named contact in primary care or the <a class="link" href="recommendations#mecfs-specialist-team" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#mecfs-specialist-team">ME/CFS specialist team</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_14_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.14.7</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>When a person with ME/CFS has a relapse, review their care and support plan with them (if needed), and discuss and agree a course of action, taking into account:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>possible causes of the relapse, if known</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>the nature of the symptoms</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>the severity and duration of the relapse (bearing in mind this can be years).</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="section" title="After a flare-up or relapse" id="ng206-after-a-flare-up-or-relapse">
      <h4 class="title" id="after-a-flare-up-or-relapse">After a flare-up or relapse</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_14_8" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.14.8</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Once a flare‑up or relapse has resolved or stabilised, discuss with the person: </p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>whether their care and support plan needs to be reviewed and adjusted to reflect their current symptoms and energy limit if this is different from before the flare‑up or relapse (for people participating in <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-activity">physical activity</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#exercise">exercise</a> programmes, see <a class="link" href="recommendations#flare-ups-and-relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#flare-ups-and-relapse">recommendations 1.11.15 and 1.11.16</a>)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>their experience of the flare‑up or relapse to determine whether strategies can be put in place to manage potential triggers in the future.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <div class="panel">
        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#managing-flare-ups-in-symptoms-and-relapse-2">rationale and impact section on managing flare-ups in symptoms and relapse</a>.</p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/g-nonpharmacological-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183028" target="_top">evidence review G: non-pharmacological management of ME/CFS</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/h-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183029" target="_top">evidence review H: appendices for the management of ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

  <div class="section" title="1.15 Review in primary care" id="ng206-review-in-primary-care">
    <h3 class="title" id="review-in-primary-care">1.15 Review in primary care</h3>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.1</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Offer adults with ME/CFS a review of their <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a> in primary care at least once a year. </p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.2</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Offer <a class="link" href="recommendations#children-and-young-people" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#children-and-young-people">children and young people</a> with ME/CFS a review of their care and support plan at least every 6 months. </p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.3</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Arrange more frequent primary care reviews for children, young people and adults with ME/CFS as needed, depending on the severity and complexity of their symptoms, and the effectiveness of any symptom management.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.4</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>When carrying out a review in primary care, ensure you have access to the person's care and support plan and any clinical communications from the <a class="link" href="recommendations#mecfs-specialist-team" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#mecfs-specialist-team">ME/CFS specialist team</a> (including their discharge letter, if relevant).</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_5" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.5</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>As part of the review, discuss with the person with ME/CFS (and their family or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a>, as appropriate) and record as a minimum:</p>
        <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>their condition, including any changes in their illness and the impact of this</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>symptoms, including whether they have experienced new symptoms</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>self-management − ask about their <a class="link" href="recommendations#energy-management-3" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#energy-management-3">energy management</a> plan and (if relevant) their <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-activity">physical activity</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#exercise">exercise</a> programme</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>who is helping them and how they provide support</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>psychological, emotional and social wellbeing</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>any future plans − ask if the person is considering any changes or if they have any challenges ahead.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_6" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.6</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Refer the person with ME/CFS to their named contact in the ME/CFS specialist team if there are any new or deteriorating aspects of their condition.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.7</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Consider seeking advice from an appropriate specialist if there is uncertainty about interpreting signs and symptoms and whether a referral is needed.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article id="ng206-1_15_8" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
      <h4 class="recommendation__number">1.15.8</h4>
      <div class="recommendation__body">
        <p>Evaluate and investigate whether new symptoms, or a change in symptoms, are due to the person's ME/CFS or whether they are due to another condition. </p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <div class="section" title="Additional principles for children and young people" id="ng206-additional-principles-for-children-and-young-people">
      <h4 class="title" id="additional-principles-for-children-and-young-people">Additional principles for children and young people</h4>
      <article id="ng206-1_15_9" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.15.9</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Ensure reviews are carried out or overseen by a paediatrician with expertise in ME/CFS. Involve other appropriate specialists as needed.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_15_10" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.15.10</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>When deciding how often reviews or reassessment might be needed for children and young people with ME/CFS, take into account:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>their developmental stage</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>transitions, such as changing schools or exams </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>the severity and complexity of symptoms</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>the effectiveness of any symptom management.<br /><br />Also see <a class="link" href="recommendations#approach-to-delivering-care" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#approach-to-delivering-care">recommendation 1.1.6</a> on ensuring the child's voice is heard and on involving their parents or carers.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>

        <p>For a short explanation of why the committee made these recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the <a class="link" href="rationale-and-impact#review-in-primary-care-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="rationale-and-impact" data-original-url="#review-in-primary-care-2">rationale and impact section on review in primary care</a>.</p>
        <p>Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/j-monitoring-and-reviewing-people-with-mecfs-pdf-333546588757" target="_top">evidence review J: monitoring and reviewing people with ME/CFS</a>.</p>

  <div class="section" title="1.17 Care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS" id="ng206-care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">
    <h3 class="title" id="care-for-people-with-severe-or-very-severe-mecfs">1.17 Care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS</h3>
    <p>This section supplements the rest of the guideline with additional considerations for people with <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">severe</a> or <a class="link" href="recommendations#box-1-severity-of-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#box-1-severity-of-mecfs">very severe ME/CFS</a>.</p>
    <div class="section" title="Awareness of severe and very severe ME/CFS and its impact" id="ng206-awareness-of-severe-and-very-severe-mecfs-and-its-impact">
      <h4 class="title" id="awareness-of-severe-and-very-severe-mecfs-and-its-impact">Awareness of severe and very severe ME/CFS and its impact</h4>
      <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#principles-of-care-for-people-with-mecfs">main section on principles of care for people with ME/CFS</a>.</p>
      <article id="ng206-1_17_1" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.17.1</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Be aware that people with severe or very severe ME/CFS may experience the following symptoms that significantly affect their lives, including their mobility, emotional wellbeing and ability to interact with others and care for themselves:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>severe and constant pain, which can have muscular, arthralgic or neuropathic features</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>hypersensitivity to light, sound, touch, movement, temperature extremes and smells</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>extreme weakness, with severely reduced movement </p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>reduced ability or inability to speak or swallow</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>cognitive difficulties that limit the person's ability to communicate and take in written or verbal communication</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>sleep disturbance such as <a class="link" href="recommendations#unrefreshing-sleep" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#unrefreshing-sleep">unrefreshing sleep</a>, hypersomnia and altered sleep pattern</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>gastrointestinal difficulties such as nausea, incontinence, constipation and bloating</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>neurological symptoms such as double vision and other visual disorders, dizziness</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>
                <a class="link" href="recommendations#orthostatic-intolerance-2" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#orthostatic-intolerance-2">orthostatic intolerance</a> and autonomic dysfunction, such as postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS) and postural hypotension.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_17_2" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.17.2</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Recognise that symptoms of severe or very severe ME/CFS may mean that people:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>need a low-stimulus environment, for example a dark quiet room with interaction at a level of their choice (this may be little or no social interaction)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>are housebound or bedbound and may need support with all activities of daily living, including aids and adaptations to assist mobility and independence in activities of daily living (for example, a wheelchair)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>need careful physical contact when supported with activities of daily living, taking into account possible sensitivity to touch</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>cannot communicate without support and may need to choose someone to be their <a class="link" href="recommendations#advocate" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#advocate">advocate</a> and communicate for them</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>are unable to eat and digest food easily and may need support with hydration and nutrition (see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#dietary-management-and-strategies" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#dietary-management-and-strategies">recommendations on dietary management and strategies</a>)</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>have problems accessing information, for example because of difficulty with screens, sound and light sensitivity, headaches affecting their ability to read, or brain fog affecting their concentration.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_17_3" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.17.3</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Personal care and support for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS should be carried out by health and social care practitioners who are:</p>
          <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>known to the person and their family or <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carers</a> wherever possible</p>
            </li>
            <li class="listitem">
              <p>aware of the person's needs.</p>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article id="ng206-1_17_4" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
        <h5 class="recommendation__number">1.17.4</h5>
        <div class="recommendation__body">
          <p>Risk assess each interaction with a person with severe or very severe ME/CFS in advance to ensure its benefits will outweigh the risks (for example, worsening their symptoms) to the person. For people with very severe ME/CFS, think about discussing this with the person's family or carers on their behalf (if appropriate), while keeping the focus of the engagement on the person with ME/CFS.</p>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>

      <p>Also see the <a class="link" href="recommendations#access-to-care-and-support" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#access-to-care-and-support">main section on access to care and support</a>.</p>

This could include home visits, online or phone consultations, supplying written communication, and supporting their applications for aids and appliances.</p>

      <div class="section" title="Hospital care" id="ng206-hospital-care-2">
        <h5 class="title" id="hospital-care-2">Hospital care</h5>
        <article id="ng206-1_17_7" class="numbered-paragraph recommendation">
          <h6 class="recommendation__number">1.17.7</h6>
          <div class="recommendation__body">
            <p>When planning hospital care for people with severe or very severe ME/CFS:</p>
            <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
              <li class="listitem">
                <p>discuss with the person (and their family or carers, as appropriate) what to expect when they come into hospital</p>
              </li>
              <li class="listitem">
                <p>aim to minimise discomfort and <a class="link" href="recommendations#post-exertional-malaise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#post-exertional-malaise">post-exertional malaise</a> during transfer to hospital, for example by planning the route in advance, avoiding noisy areas and admitting them straight to the ward on arrival</p>
              </li>
              <li class="listitem">
                <p>discuss the person's <a class="link" href="recommendations#care-and-support-plan" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#care-and-support-plan">care and support plan</a> with them, including information on comorbidities, intolerances and sensitivities, to plan any reasonable adjustments that are needed</p>
              </li>
              <li class="listitem">
                <p>aim to provide a single room if possible</p>
              </li>
              <li class="listitem">
                <p>keep stimuli to a minimum, for example by:</p>
                <p>
                  <div class="itemizedlist">
                    <ul class="itemizedlist indented">
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>seeing them one-to-one</p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>using calm movements and gestures</p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>not duplicating assessments</p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>being cautious about the pressure of touch</p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>keeping lights dimmed</p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>reducing sound</p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>keeping a stable temperature </p>
                      </li>
                      <li class="listitem">
                        <p>minimising smells.</p>
                      </li>
                    </ul>
                  </div>
                </p>
              </li>
            </ul>
          </div>
        </article>

  <div class="section" title="Terms used in this guideline" id="ng206-terms-used-in-this-guideline">
    <h3 class="title" id="terms-used-in-this-guideline">Terms used in this guideline</h3>
    <p>For other definitions, see the <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/glossary" target="_top">NICE glossary</a> and the <a class="link" href="http://www.thinklocalactpersonal.org.uk/Browse/Informationandadvice/CareandSupportJargonBuster/" target="_top">Think Local, Act Personal Care and Support Jargon Buster</a>.</p>
    <div class="section" title="Activity" id="ng206-activity">
      <h4 class="title" id="activity">Activity</h4>
      <p>Any effort that uses energy, which includes cognitive, emotional and social activity as well as physical activity. Different activities combine and interact to cause a cumulative impact for the person.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Advocate" id="ng206-advocate">
      <h4 class="title" id="advocate">Advocate</h4>
      <p>In this guideline, the role of an advocate is to support a vulnerable or disadvantaged person with ME/CFS and ensure that their rights are being upheld in a health and social care context. They are chosen by the person with ME/CFS and could be a family member, <a class="link" href="recommendations#carers" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#carers">carer</a>, friend or an independent advocate. They make sure that the person is heard. </p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Care and support plan" id="ng206-care-and-support-plan">
      <h4 class="title" id="care-and-support-plan">Care and support plan</h4>
      <p>The personalised collaborative care and support plan is developed by the ME/CFS specialist team based on a holistic assessment. It is the basis for other assessments and plans in areas such as social care, energy management, physical activity, physical functioning and mobility, cognitive behavioural therapy and dietary management.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Carers" id="ng206-carers">
      <h4 class="title" id="carers">Carers</h4>
      <p>In this guideline, a carer refers to someone who provides unpaid care and support to a family member, partner or friend with ME/CFS. This is distinct from care workers who are employed to provide support.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Children and young people" id="ng206-children-and-young-people">
      <h4 class="title" id="children-and-young-people">Children and young people</h4>
      <p>In this guideline, children and young people are aged under 18 (adults are 18 and above).</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Energy limit" id="ng206-energy-limit">
      <h4 class="title" id="energy-limit">Energy limit</h4>
      <p>The amount of energy a person has to do all activities without triggering an increase or worsening of their symptoms.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Energy management" id="ng206-energy-management-3">
      <h4 class="title" id="energy-management-3">Energy management</h4>
      <p>A self-management strategy that involves a person with ME/CFS managing their activities to stay within their energy limit, with support from a healthcare professional.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Exercise" id="ng206-exercise">
      <h4 class="title" id="exercise">Exercise</h4>
      <p>Exercise is planned, structured, repetitive and purposeful activity focused on improvement or maintenance of 1 or more components of physical fitness. Exercise is a subcategory of <a class="link" href="recommendations#physical-activity" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#physical-activity">physical activity</a>.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Fatigue" id="ng206-fatigue">
      <h4 class="title" id="fatigue">Fatigue</h4>
      <p>Fatigue in ME/CFS typically has the following components:</p>
      <div class="itemizedlist">
        <ul class="itemizedlist">
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>feeling flu-like, especially in the early days of the illness</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>restlessness or feeling 'wired but tired'</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>low energy or a lack of physical energy to start or finish activities of daily living and the sensation of being 'physically drained'</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>cognitive fatigue that worsens existing difficulties</p>
          </li>
          <li class="listitem">
            <p>rapid loss of muscle strength or stamina after starting an activity, causing for example, sudden weakness, clumsiness, lack of coordination, and being unable to repeat physical effort consistently.</p>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Flare-up" id="ng206-flare-up">
      <h4 class="title" id="flare-up">Flare-up</h4>
      <p>A worsening of symptoms, more than would be accounted for by normal day-to-day variation, that affects the person's ability to perform their usual activities. Flare‑ups may occur spontaneously or be triggered by another illness, overexertion or other triggers. Flare‑ups usually occur as part of post-exertional malaise but it is possible for other symptoms, such as pain, to flare-up without post-exertional malaise. The worsening of symptoms is transient and flare‑ups typically resolve after a few days, either spontaneously or in response to temporary changes in energy management or a change in treatment. A <a class="link" href="recommendations#relapse" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#relapse">relapse</a> lasts longer than a flare‑up.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Graded exercise therapy" id="ng206-graded-exercise-therapy">
      <h4 class="title" id="graded-exercise-therapy">Graded exercise therapy</h4>
      <p>In this guideline, graded exercise therapy is defined as establishing a baseline of achievable exercise or physical activity and then making fixed incremental increases in the time spent being physically active. It is a therapy based on the deconditioning and exercise avoidance theories of ME/CFS. These theories assume that ME/CFS is perpetuated by reversible physiological changes of deconditioning and avoidance of activity. These changes result in the deconditioning being maintained and an increased perception of effort, leading to further inactivity. This definition of graded exercise therapy reflects the descriptions of it included in <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/g-nonpharmacological-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183028" target="_top">evidence review G: non-pharmacological management of ME/CFS</a> and <a class="link" href="https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/evidence/h-management-of-mecfs-pdf-9265183029" target="_top">evidence review H: appendices for the management of ME/CFS</a>.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="ME/CFS specialist team" id="ng206-mecfs-specialist-team">
      <h4 class="title" id="mecfs-specialist-team">ME/CFS specialist team</h4>
      <p>These teams consist of a range of healthcare professionals with expertise in assessing, diagnosing, treating and managing ME/CFS. They commonly have medically trained clinicians from a variety of specialisms (including rheumatology, rehabilitation medicine, endocrinology, infectious diseases, neurology, immunology, general practice and paediatrics) as well as access to other healthcare professionals specialising in ME/CFS. These may include physiotherapists, exercise physiologists, occupational therapists, dietitians, and clinical or counselling psychologists. Children and young people are likely to be cared for under local or regional paediatric teams that have experience working with children and young people with ME/CFS in collaboration with ME/CFS specialist centres.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Orthostatic intolerance" id="ng206-orthostatic-intolerance-2">
      <h4 class="title" id="orthostatic-intolerance-2">Orthostatic intolerance</h4>
      <p>A clinical condition in which symptoms such as light-headedness, near-fainting or fainting, impaired concentration, headaches, dimming or blurring of vision, forceful beating of the heart, palpitations, tremulousness and chest pain occur or worsen on standing up and are improved (although not necessarily resolved) by sitting or lying down. Orthostatic intolerance may include postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS), which is a significant rise in pulse rate when moving from lying to standing, and postural hypotension, which is a significant fall in blood pressure when moving from lying to standing. People with severe orthostatic intolerance may find they are unable to sit up for any length of time. </p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Physical activity" id="ng206-physical-activity">
      <h4 class="title" id="physical-activity">Physical activity</h4>
      <p>Any bodily movement produced by skeletal muscles that results in energy expenditure. It should not be confused with <a class="link" href="recommendations#exercise" target="_top" rel="internal" data-chapter="recommendations" data-original-url="#exercise">exercise</a>. Physical activity in daily life can be categorised into occupational, sports, conditioning, household or other activities, and can be done during leisure time, to get around or as part of a person's work. See <a class="link" href="https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity" target="_top">World Health Organization advice on physical activity</a>. Physical activity has a health benefit for most people and many conditions, but in people with ME/CFS, physical activity may make their symptoms worsen. </p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Physical functioning and mobility" id="ng206-physical-functioning-and-mobility-2">
      <h4 class="title" id="physical-functioning-and-mobility-2">Physical functioning and mobility</h4>
      <p>The process of incorporating into daily activities a level of movement that helps to maintain joint and muscle flexibility without worsening symptoms of ME/CFS. This aims to support people to have as much independence as possible in their activities, ranging from personal hygiene to activities of daily living, working and social interaction. Such movement is undertaken within the person's energy limits and avoids pushing through their boundaries of tolerance.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Post-exertional malaise" id="ng206-post-exertional-malaise">
      <h4 class="title" id="post-exertional-malaise">Post-exertional malaise</h4>
      <p>The worsening of symptoms that can follow minimal cognitive, physical, emotional or social activity, or activity that could previously be tolerated. Symptoms can typically worsen 12 to 48 hours after activity and last for days or even weeks, sometimes leading to a relapse. Post-exertional malaise may also be referred to as post-exertional symptom exacerbation.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Relapse" id="ng206-relapse">
      <h4 class="title" id="relapse">Relapse</h4>
      <p>A sustained and marked exacerbation of symptoms lasting longer than a flare‑up and needing a substantial and sustained adjustment to the person's energy management. It may not be clear in the early stages of a symptom exacerbation whether it is a flare‑up or a relapse. Relapses can lead to a long-term reduction in the person's energy limits.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Special interest in ME/CFS" id="ng206-special-interest-in-mecfs">
      <h4 class="title" id="special-interest-in-mecfs">Special interest in ME/CFS</h4>
      <p>A special interest in ME/CFS refers to a healthcare professional who is not working in an ME/CFS specialist team service but has knowledge and experience in this area.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Therapy blueprint" id="ng206-therapy-blueprint">
      <h4 class="title" id="therapy-blueprint">Therapy blueprint</h4>
      <p>This summarises the therapy and provides a basis for future independent self-management. The blueprint may include the therapy formulation, strategies that have been helpful, 'warning signs' and triggers of flare‑ups and how to manage them, and goals for the future. It is important that the therapy blueprint is led by the person themselves and is in their own words, supported by guidance from the therapist.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="section" title="Unrefreshing sleep" id="ng206-unrefreshing-sleep">
      <h4 class="title" id="unrefreshing-sleep">Unrefreshing sleep</h4>
      <p>Unrefreshing sleep means sleep that is non-restorative. Even after a full night's sleep, people do not feel refreshed. People with ME/CFS often report waking up exhausted and feeling as if they have not slept at all, no matter how long they were asleep. </p>
