<div><div>This corrects the article "<a>Mutations in <em>SNX14</em> Cause a Distinctive Autosomal-Recessive Cerebellar Ataxia and Intellectual Disability Syndrome</a>" in  volume 95 on page 611.</div></div>

<p>(American Journal of Human Genetics <em>95</em>, 611–621; November 6, 2014)</p>
<p>In this article, mutation c.1894+1G&gt;A in Table 1 and on page 616 is incorrect and should have been c.1894+1G&gt;T, as correctly written in Figure 2. In addition, Figure 2C incorrectly shows that c.1894+1G&gt;T occurs in intron 17. The correct intron is 19, as noted on page 616. A revised Figure 2 appears below. The authors apologize for the errors.</p>
