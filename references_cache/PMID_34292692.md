# Nonsyndromic Tooth Agenesis Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Letra A, Chiquet B, Hansen-Kiss E, Menezes S, Hunter E

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of nonsyndromic tooth agenesis (NSTA); 2.. Review the genetic causes of NSTA; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of NSTA in a proband (when possible); 4.. Review management of NSTA following diagnosis: evaluations, treatment, and surveillance; 5.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with NSTA.
