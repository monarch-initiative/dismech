# ORPHA:500095  Tall stature-intellectual disability-renal anomalies syndrome

**ORPHA:500095** — Tall stature-intellectual disability-renal anomalies syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Thauvin-Robinet-Faivre syndrome

## Definition

A rare overgrowth syndrome associated with multiple congenital anomalies characterized by tall stature, large hands and feet with large thumbs and halluces, spatulate digits, developmental delay and facial dysmorphism.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Antenatal
- Age of onset: Neonatal
