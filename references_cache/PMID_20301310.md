The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of 
arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy (ARVC); 2.. Review the genetic 
causes of ARVC; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause 
of ARVC in a proband; 4.. Review management of ARVC; 5.. Inform genetic risk 
assessment in family members of a proband.
