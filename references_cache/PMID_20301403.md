# Primary Mitochondrial Disorders Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Chinnery PF

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of primary mitochondrial disorders. 2.. Provide evaluation strategies to identify the genetic cause of a primary mitochondrial disorder in a proband. 3.. Identify patient care guidelines for primary mitochondrial disorders. 4.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with a primary mitochondrial disorder.
