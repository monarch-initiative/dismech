The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of 
primary mitochondrial disorders. 2.. Provide evaluation strategies to identify 
the genetic cause of a primary mitochondrial disorder in a proband. 3.. Identify 
patient care guidelines for primary mitochondrial disorders. 4.. Inform genetic 
counseling of family members of an individual with a primary mitochondrial 
disorder.
