Inhibiting the cell-cycle kinases CDK4 and CDK6 results in significant 
therapeutic effect in patients with advanced hormone-positive breast cancer. The 
efficacy of this strategy is, however, limited by innate or acquired resistance 
mechanisms and its application to other tumor types is still uncertain. Here, 
through an integrative analysis of sensitivity and resistance mechanisms, we 
discuss the use of CDK4/6 inhibitors in combination with available targeted 
therapies, immunotherapy, or classical chemotherapy with the aim of improving 
future therapeutic uses of CDK4/6 inhibition in a variety of cancers.
