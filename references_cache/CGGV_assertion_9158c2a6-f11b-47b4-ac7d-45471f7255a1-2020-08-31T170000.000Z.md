## Evidence summary

HOMER2 was described in association with autosomal dominant nonsyndromic hearing loss in 2015 by Azaiez et al. (25816005). At least 1 missense variant and 1 frameshift variant have been reported in humans (25816005, 30047143). Variants in this gene segregated with disease in 17 additional family members. Evidence supporting this gene-disease association includes case-level, segregation and experimental evidence. In summary, there is moderate evidence to support this gene-disease association. While more evidence is needed to establish this association definitively, no convincing contradictory evidence has emerged. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Hearing Loss Working Group on 8/31/2020.
