## Disease description

Autosomal recessive distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-7 (HMNR7) is characterized by onset of lower leg weakness in the first decade. Affected individuals have difficulty climbing stairs and problems standing on the heels... Most patients have foot deformities, and some may have leg muscle atrophy. The disorder is slowly progressive and often involves the upper limbs [@omim:619216].

## Mechanism

Loss of function [@pmid:38467784].

## Age of onset

Typical: 1-3 [@pmid:33559681]; Range: 0-10 [@omim:619216].

## Prevalence

- Details: Biallelic variants found in 0.01% of 100 KGP participants; enriched in those with motor disease. 80% of VWA1 pathogenic variants are expansions/contractions [@genereviews:NBK535148]. The repeat expansion was found in several ethnicities; founder mutation 7,000 years prior [@pmid:33559681].

## Details

Any deviation from 2 motifs is thought to be pathogenic [@genereviews:NBK535148].
