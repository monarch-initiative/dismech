# Gene expression profile at single cell level of non-parenchymal cells from Fibrosis and Tumor (FAT) Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis (MASH) model vs healthy liver at 12- and 24-week timepoints.

## Content

MASH is a subtype of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, characterized by inflammation and fibrosis. Non-parenchymal cells in this dataset include mostly immune cells, as well as some other cell types like endothelial cells, cholangiocytes, stellate cells, etc.
