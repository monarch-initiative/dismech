# scRNAseq analysis of hepatocytes from p16-Tom mice following CCl4 treatment

## Content

We used the Flex single-cell RNA sequencing platform (10x Genomics) to investigate the transcriptomic differences between p16high (Tom+) and p16low (Tom-) hepatocytes from p16-CreERT2/R26-LSL-tdTomato mice following 4 weeks of CCl4 treatment.
