# X-linked motor-sensory neuropathy type-II with deafness and mental retardation: a new disorder.
**Authors:** Cowchock FS, Duckett SW, Streletz LJ, Graziani LJ, Jackson LG
**Journal:** Am J Med Genet (1985)
**DOI:** [10.1002/ajmg.1320200214](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.1320200214)

## Content

1. Am J Med Genet. 1985 Feb;20(2):307-15. doi: 10.1002/ajmg.1320200214.

X-linked motor-sensory neuropathy type-II with deafness and mental retardation: 
a new disorder.

Cowchock FS, Duckett SW, Streletz LJ, Graziani LJ, Jackson LG.

We report on a family with an apparently X-linked neuromuscular disease. 
Electrophysiologic tests and electron microscopic studies are consistent with 
the diagnosis of hereditary motor sensory neuropathy type II (HMSN-II), one form 
of Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease. The manner of inheritance, the observation that 
males are severely affected from infancy, and the frequent association of 
deafness and/or mental retardation with the neuromuscular disorder are not usual 
for HMSN-II and suggest that this family may have a previously undescribed 
genetic disorder. The peripheral neuropathy did not appear to be linked to the 
Xg blood group. Minor abnormalities of sensory nerve conduction, 
electromyography, and hearing were separately identified in female relatives in 
this family, but were not consistent enough to be useful in the identification 
of carriers for this gene.
