# ORPHA:441  Pure autonomic failure

**ORPHA:441** — Pure autonomic failure (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Bradbury-Eggleston syndrome
- Idiopathic orthostatic hypotension
- PAF
- Pure dysautonomia
- Pure idiopatic dysautonomia

## Definition

A rare sporadic neurodegenerative disease characterized by slowly progressive symptoms of orthostatic hypotension (OH), and other symptoms of generalised dysautonomia related to the impairment of the sympathetic component of the autonomic nervous system.

## Inheritance

- Not applicable

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adult
- Age of onset: Elderly
