# single cell RNA sequencing analysis of infected or bystander (noninfected) monocytes during M. marinum infection

## Content

Mice were infected with wild type, EspK::tn, or deltaRD1 (completely ESX-1-deficient) Mycobacterium marinum. 14 days post infection, infected and uninfected monocytes from the infected tissue were sorted for single cell RNA sequencing analysis.
