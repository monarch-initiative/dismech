Comment in
    Cell Rep. 2018 Jun 26;23(13):3699-3700. doi: 10.1016/j.celrep.2018.06.062.

The majority of supratentorial ependymomas (ST-ependymomas) have few mutations 
but frequently display chromothripsis of chromosome 11q that generates a fusion 
between C11orf95 and RELA (RELAFUS). Neural stem cells transduced with 
RELAFUSex vivo form ependymomas when implanted in the brain. These tumors 
display enhanced NF-κB signaling, suggesting that this aberrant signal is the 
principal mechanism of oncogenesis. However, it is not known whether RELAFUS is 
sufficient to drive de novo ependymoma tumorigenesis in the brain and, if so, 
whether these tumors also arise from neural stem cells. We show that RELAFUS 
drives ST-ependymoma formation from periventricular neural stem cells in mice 
and that RELAFUS-induced tumorigenesis is likely dependent on a series of cell 
signaling pathways in addition to NF-κB.
