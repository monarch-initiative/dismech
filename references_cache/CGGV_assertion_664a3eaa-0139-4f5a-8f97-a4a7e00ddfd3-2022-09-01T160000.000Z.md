## Evidence summary

CNGB1 was first reported in relation to autosomal recessive retinitis pigmentosa in 2001 (Bareil et al., PMID: 11379879). 8 publications were added with 5 included for evidence (PMIDs: 11379879, 15557452, 23661369, 28056120, and 23977260) in this curation. CNGB1 variants within the channel‐encoding part of the sequence result in olfactory symptoms, in addition to phenotypes associated with retinitis pigmentosa. Because of the broad spectrum of potential phenotypes, these cases with causal variants in CNGB1 have been curated under the inclusive disease name CNGB1 -related retinopathy (MONDO:0800403). More evidence is available in the literature, but the maximum score for genetic evidence (12 pts.) has been reached. The mechanism of pathogenicity is reported to be LOF. This gene-disease association is also supported by experimental evidence.

In summary, CNGB1 is definitively associated with CNGB1 -related retinopathy. This has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic settings, and has been upheld over time. This classification has been approved by the ClinGen Retina GCEP on September 1st, 2022 (SOP Version 9).
