Rasmussen encephalitis (RE) is a rare but severe immune-mediated brain disorder 
leading to unilateral hemispheric atrophy, associated progressive neurological 
dysfunction and intractable seizures. Recent data on the pathogenesis of the 
disease, its clinical and paraclinical presentation, and therapeutic approaches 
are summarized. Based on these data, we propose formal diagnostic criteria and a 
therapeutic pathway for the management of RE patients.
