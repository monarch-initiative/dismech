# [Autoimmune Autonomic Ganglionopathy and Acute Autonomic Sensory Neuropathy].

Autoimmune autonomic ganglionopathy (AAG) and acute autonomic sensory neuropathy
(AASN) are immune-mediated neuropathies that affect the autonomic and/or dorsal
root ganglia. Autoantibodies against the nicotinic ganglionic acetylcholine
receptor (gAChR) detected in the sera of patients with AAG play a key role in
the pathogenesis of this condition. Notably, gAChR antibodies are not detected
in the sera of patients with AASN. Currently, AAG and AASN are not considered to
be on the same spectrum with regard to disease concept based on clinical
symptoms and laboratory findings. However, extra-autonomic brain symptoms
(including psychiatric symptoms and personality changes) and endocrine disorders
occur in both diseases, which suggests shared pathophysiology between the two
conditions.
