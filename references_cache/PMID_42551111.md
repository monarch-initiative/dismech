# Intellectual disability, neuroregression and adult-onset progressive dystonia due to a DLG4 pathogenic variant.
**Authors:** Peraferrer-Montesinos L, Nagaratnam SA, Forwood C, Berry A, Ashton K, Dong J, Zhu Y, Arpone M, Becker P, Delves M, Morales Briceno H
**Journal:** Parkinsonism Relat Disord (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.parkreldis.2026.108909](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.parkreldis.2026.108909)

## Content

We report a 34-year-old male with childhood developmental delay, severe intellectual disability in adulthood, episodes of agitation with a previous diagnosis of schizoaffective disorder and adult-onset cognitive regression who developed progressive generalised dystonia due to a de novo DLG4 pathogenic loss-of-function variant. This case expands the phenotypic spectrum of recognised movement disorder manifestations associated with DLG4-related synaptopathy.
