# Neglected Myiasis Wound Presenting With Septic Shock: A Case Report.
**Authors:** Adhikari N, Das SK
**Journal:** Clin Case Rep (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1002/ccr3.72491](https://doi.org/10.1002/ccr3.72491)

## Content

Myiasis is larval infestation of tissues, particularly common in tropical, and subtropical regions, especially in pre-existing wounds. Poor hygiene, inadequate wound care, and exposed wounds, can predispose to secondary bacterial infection. We report a case of neglected myiasis wound in a laborer from rural Nepal presenting with septic shock, who was managed with intravenous fluids, antibiotics, wound care, and delayed skin grafting, resulting in complete functional recovery.
