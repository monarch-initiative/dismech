# Steatocystoma Multiplex Suppurativa-A Case Report and Review of Treatments.
**Authors:** Vu JT, Rivero ADL
**Journal:** Australas J Dermatol (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1111/ajd.70070](https://doi.org/10.1111/ajd.70070)

## Content

We report a case of Steatocystoma Multiplex Suppurativa (SMS), a rare condition that can mimic hidradenitis suppurativa (HS). We also present a review of the surgical and medical therapies, including potential biologic agents.
