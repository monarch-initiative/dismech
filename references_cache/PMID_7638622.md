Comment in
    Science. 1995 Aug 18;269(5226):917-8. doi: 10.1126/science.7638610.

A candidate gene for the chromosome 1 Alzheimer's disease (AD) locus was 
identified (STM2). The predicted amino acid sequence for STM2 is homologous to 
that of the recently cloned chromosome 14 AD gene (S182). A point mutation in 
STM2, resulting in the substitution of an isoleucine for an asparagine (N141l), 
was identified in affected people from Volga German AD kindreds. This N141l 
mutation occurs at an amino acid residue that is conserved in human S182 and in 
the mouse S182 homolog. The presence of missense mutations in AD subjects in two 
highly similar genes strongly supports the hypothesis that mutations in both are 
pathogenic.
