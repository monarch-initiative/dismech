# Comparison of retinal structure and schisis location in X-linked retinoschisis and enhanced S-cone syndrome.

PURPOSE: To provide a quantitative assessment of the primary retinal layers 
affected by macular cystoid spaces in X-linked retinoschisis (XLRS) and enhanced 
S-cone syndrome (ESCS).
METHODS: Twenty-two visually-normal controls, 14 XLRS and 7 ESCS (autosomal 
recessive inheritance) subjects participated. Macular B-scans were acquired by 
OCT and the thickness of the inner-nuclear layer (INL), outer-nuclear layer plus 
outer-plexiform layer (OPL+ONL), and photoreceptor outer segments (OS+) was 
calculated. ANOVA was used to compare mean thicknesses within each layer for the 
three subject groups.
RESULTS: Thirteen of 14 XLRS subjects and all 7 ESCS subjects had macular 
schisis by OCT. The INL was significantly thicker than normal for the XLRS 
group, but not significantly different from normal for the ESCS group. 
Conversely, the OPL+ONL was abnormally thick for all ESCS subjects and 
abnormally thin for all XLRS subjects. OS+ was significantly thinned for the 
XLRS group, but not significantly different from normal for the ESCS group.
CONCLUSION: In this sample of subjects, macular cystoid spaces were most 
apparent in the INL of XLRS subjects and in the OPL+ONL of ESCS subjects. 
Additionally, photoreceptor thinning was a characteristic of XLRS, which was 
less apparent in ESCS. These data provide insight into the location of macular 
cystoid spaces and highlight anatomical differences between XLRS and ESCS 
patients.
