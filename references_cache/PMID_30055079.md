Bloom Syndrome (BSyn) is an autosomal recessive disorder that causes growth 
deficiency, endocrine abnormalities, photosensitive skin rash, immune 
abnormalities, and predisposition to early-onset cancer. The available 
treatments for BSyn are symptomatic, and early identification of complications 
has the potential to improve outcomes. To accomplish this, standardized 
recommendations for health supervision are needed for early diagnosis and 
treatment. The purpose of this report is to use information from the BSyn 
Registry, published literature, and expertise from clinicians and researchers 
with experience in BSyn to develop recommendations for diagnosis, screening, and 
treatment of the clinical manifestations in people with BSyn. These health 
supervision recommendations can be incorporated into the routine clinical care 
of people with BSyn and can be revised as more knowledge is gained regarding 
their clinical utility.
