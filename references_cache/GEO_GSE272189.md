# Gene expression profile at single cell level of human nasal primary airway cells from DNAH5 subjects

## Content

DNAH5 is an cilia dynein motor . We used single cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) to analyze the changes in cells types and gene expression profile of subjects with variants in DNAH5 compared to healthy individuals
