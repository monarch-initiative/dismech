**CGGV:assertion_a593a104-5904-461f-956b-ad1e154df921-2024-05-15T170000.000Z** - FUT8 / congenital disorder of glycosylation with defective fucosylation 1 (Definitive)

## Evidence summary

FUT8 was first reported in relation to autosomal recessive congenital disorder of glycosylation with defective fucosylation 1 in 2018 (Ng et al., PMID: 29304374). 8 variants (nonsense, frameshift, splice site, and missense) that have been reported in 7 probands in 2 publications (PMIDs: 29304374, 32049367) are included in this curation. The maximum score for genetic evidence (12 pts.) has been reached. The mechanism of pathogenicity is known to be loss of function. This gene-disease relationship is also supported by an animal knockout model (PMID: 16236725) and a cell line knockout model (PMID: 27008861). Both models showed a lack of fucosylated N-glycans, which is also observed in affected patients. In summary, there is definitive evidence supporting the relationship between FUT8 and autosomal recessive congenital disorder of glycosylation with defective fucosylation 1. This has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic settings and has been upheld over time.
