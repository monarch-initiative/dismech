# Immune response of human lung tissue after infection with human parainfluenza virus-3 – canonical pathways and treatment

## Content

Ex vivo human precision-cut lung slices (PCLS) were infected with human parainfluenza virus 3 (hPIV-3). We used microarry to explore the changes in gene expression between infected and non-infected, same-donor PCLS. We particularly analyzed changes in mRNA and miRNA expression levels linked to inflammation and antiviral immunity.
