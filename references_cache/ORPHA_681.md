# ORPHA:681  Hypokalemic periodic paralysis

**ORPHA:681** — Hypokalemic periodic paralysis (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Westphall disease

## Definition

A rare genetic, muscle channelopathy characterized by recurrent episodic attacks of generalized muscle weakness associated with a decrease in blood potassium levels.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adolescent
- Age of onset: Childhood
