# ORPHA:230  Dopamine beta-hydroxylase deficiency

**ORPHA:230** — Dopamine beta-hydroxylase deficiency (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- DBH deficiency

## Definition

A very rare primary monoamine neurotransmitter synthesis disorder with norepinephrine and adrenaline deficiency that leads to young-onset severe orthostatic hypotension and eyelid ptosis.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adolescent
- Age of onset: Adult
- Age of onset: Childhood
- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
