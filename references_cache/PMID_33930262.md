The Mediator complex subunit 13-like is a part of the large Mediator complex. 
Recently, a large number of patients were diagnosed with mutations in this gene, 
which makes it one of the most frequent causes of syndromic intellectual 
disability. In this work, we report a patient with a novel de novo likely 
pathogenic variant c.5941C>T, p.(Gln1981*) in the MED13L gene with severe 
intellectual disability and facial dysmorphism. Uncommon findings like lack of 
speech, strabismus and self-destructive behaviour present in our patient allowed 
us to further define the phenotypic spectrum of mental retardation and 
distinctive facial features with or without cardiac defects syndrome.
