# MicroRNA (miRNA) Expression in Circulating Leukocytes and Bioinformatic Analysis of Patients with Moyamoya Disease (MMD)

## Content

To investigate the differential expression of miRNAs in circulating leukocytes of patients with MMD. The results showed 12 differentially expressed miRNAs in leukocytes of MMD patients compared with controls (fold change > 2.0 and P < 0.05), of which 7 were up-regulated and 5 were down-regulated.
