Hereditary catalase deficiencies and increased risk of diabetes.

Góth L, Eaton JW.

Comment in
    Lancet. 2001 Jan 27;357(9252):314. doi: 10.1016/S0140-6736(05)71763-3.

Partial or near-total lack of erythrocyte catalase activity is a rare condition,
generally thought to be benign. However, little is known of the frequency of
common diseases of adult onset in human beings with catalase deficiency. We
report that, in a series of Hungarian patients with catalase deficiency, there
is a higher frequency of diabetes than in unaffected first-degree relatives and
the general Hungarian population. We speculate that quantitative deficiency of
catalase might predispose to cumulative oxidant damage of pancreatic beta-cells
and diabetes.
