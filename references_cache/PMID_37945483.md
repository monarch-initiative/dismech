Until recently, the disease known to be associated with THOC2 mutations was 
Intellectual developmental disorder, X-linked 12 (MIM300957). However, recently, 
fetal arthrogryposis multiplex congenita has been associated with a specific 
splice site mutation in the THOC2 gene. We report a family with the same splice 
site mutation in the THOC2 gene involved in fetal arthrogryposis as well. We 
provide the first description of the muscular phenotype of this disease which 
reveals the presence of cytoplasmic bodies. Our findings expand the clinical 
phenotype of THOC2 gene related defects.
