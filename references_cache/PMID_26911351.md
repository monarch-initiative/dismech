Juvenile myelomonocytic leukemia (JMML) appears to be a life-threatening disease 
and showed poor prognosis even after hematopoietic stem cell transplantation 
(HSCT) because of high relapse rate. On the other hand, recent molecular 
analysis revealed the heterogeneity of JMML. Here we report that two JMML 
patients survived >20 years without HSCT and both patients had uniparental 
disomy of 11q23 where CBL is located without the phenomenon found in neither 
Noonan syndrome nor Noonan syndrome-like disorder. We think that some JMML 
patients with CBL mutation might show the good prognosis in later life after 
remission of JMML.
