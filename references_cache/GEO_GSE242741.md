# Single cell RNA analysis of lung endothelial cells in mouse model of Hereditary Hemorrhagic Telangiectasia 2

## Content

Arteriovenous malformations (AVMs) are characteristic of hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia (HHT). We used single cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) to analyzed the pulmonary ECs in mice with endothelial-specific deletion of Alk1 gene.
