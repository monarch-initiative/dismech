# Histiocytoid cardiomyopathy: three new cases and a review of the literature.
**Authors:** Shehata BM, Patterson K, Thomas JE, Scala-Barnett D, Dasu S, Robinson HB
**Journal:** Pediatr Dev Pathol (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s100249900007](https://doi.org/10.1007/s100249900007)

## Content

1. Pediatr Dev Pathol. 1998 Jan-Feb;1(1):56-69. doi: 10.1007/s100249900007.

Histiocytoid cardiomyopathy: three new cases and a review of the literature.

Shehata BM(1), Patterson K, Thomas JE, Scala-Barnett D, Dasu S, Robinson HB.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology, Children's Medical Center of Northwest Ohio, Toledo 
43606, USA.

Histiocytoid cardiomyopathy (HC), a rare arrhythmogenic disorder, presents as 
difficult-to-control arrhythmias or sudden death in infants and children, 
particularly girls. Three cases are described with autopsy findings. In two 
cases, yellow-tan nodules were grossly visible in the myocardium; in the third 
case, no gross lesions were identified. Microscopic examination in all three 
cases revealed multiple, scattered clusters of histiocytoid myocytes which on 
ultrastructural examination were filled with abnormal mitochondria, scattered 
lipid droplets, and scanty myofibrils. These pathologic findings are similar to 
those previously described. The pathogenesis of this entity remains 
controversial. It was recently proposed that this disorder is X-linked dominant 
with the associated gene located in the region of Xp22.
