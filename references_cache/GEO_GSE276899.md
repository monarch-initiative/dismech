# Circulating nucleic acids in plasma of individuals with human lymphatic filariasis

## Content

Circulating nucleic acids (RNA / DNA) found in body fluids have been shown to be good biomarkers for diagnosis of infections. The short half life of nucleic acids makes them ideal choice for detecting active infections and hence good targets for molecular testing.
