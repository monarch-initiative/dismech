## Key inclusion & exclusion criteria

Inclusion criteria: 1. Aged 18 to 70 years;
2.A definite history of consumption of poisonous mushrooms before the onset of the disease (morphologically confirmed to be amatoxin-containing mushrooms based on the provided mushrooms in kind or photographs, or detection amatoxin in blood or urine);
3. 6h after consuming the poisonous mushroom, digestive symptoms such as poor appetite, nausea, vomiting, abdominal distension, and progressive elevation of liver biochemical and coagulation indexes were observed. Liver function and coagulation were monitored every 8h, and the indexes met the diagnostic criteria for prehepatic failure.
4. duration of disease
