# Skeletal muscle transcriptomics dissects the pathogenesis of Friedreich's Ataxia

## Content

RNA sequencing of skeletal muscle biopsies from healthy controls and Friedreich's Ataxia (FRDA) patients before and after treatment with recombinant human erythropoietin (rhuEPO) to dissect the mechanisms of disease.
