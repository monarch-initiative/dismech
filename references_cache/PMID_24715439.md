Haploinsufficiency of HDAC4 does not cause intellectual disability in all
affected individuals.

Wheeler PG(1), Huang D, Dai Z.

Author information:
(1)Divison of Genetics, Nemours Children's Clinic, Orlando, Florida.

Haploinsufficiency of HDAC4 gene has been reported to result in
brachydactyly-"mental retardation" syndrome (BDMR), a condition with significant
intellectual impairment, brachydactyly type E, and typical facial features.
Presented here are three individuals with haploinsufficiency of HDAC4 who have
brachydactyly type E, non-dysmorphic facial features, and normal intelligence.
This is in contradistinction to previous reports that haploinsufficiency of
HDAC4 is sufficient to cause BDMR.
