# X-linked mental retardation associated with cleft lip/palate maps to Xp11.3-q21.3.
**Authors:** Siderius LE, Hamel BC, van Bokhoven H, de Jager F, van den Helm B, Kremer H, Heineman-de Boer JA, Ropers HH, Mariman EC
**Journal:** Am J Med Genet (1999)

## Content

1. Am J Med Genet. 1999 Jul 30;85(3):216-20.

X-linked mental retardation associated with cleft lip/palate maps to 
Xp11.3-q21.3.

Siderius LE(1), Hamel BC, van Bokhoven H, de Jager F, van den Helm B, Kremer H, 
Heineman-de Boer JA, Ropers HH, Mariman EC.

Author information:
(1)Clinical Genetics Center Utrecht, Utrecht, The Netherlands.

A family is described in which X-linked mild to borderline mental retardation 
(MR) is associated with cleft lip/palate. Linkage analysis showed a maximum LOD 
score of Z=2.78 at straight theta=0.0 for the DXS441 locus with flanking markers 
DXS337 and DXS990, defining the region Xp11.3-q21.3 with a linkage interval of 
25 cM.
