# Maturity-Onset Diabetes of the Young Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Naylor R, Knight Johnson A, del Gaudio D

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of maturity-onset diabetes of the young (MODY); 2.. Review the genetic causes of MODY; 3.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of MODY in a proband (when possible); 4.. Inform (when possible) medical management of MODY based on genetic cause; 5.. Inform risk assessment and surveillance of at-risk relatives for early detection and treatment of MODY.
