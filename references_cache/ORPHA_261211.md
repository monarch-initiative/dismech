# ORPHA:261211  16p11.2p12.2 microdeletion syndrome

**ORPHA:261211** — 16p11.2p12.2 microdeletion syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Del(16)(p11.2p12.2)
- Monosomy 16p11.2p12.2

## Definition

16p11.2-p12.2 microdeletion syndrome is a recently described syndrome characterized by developmental delay and facial dysmorphism.

## Inheritance

- Not applicable
- Unknown

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
