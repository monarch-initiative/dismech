# ORPHA:398189  Focal facial dermal dysplasia type IV

**ORPHA:398189** — Focal facial dermal dysplasia type IV (Clinical subtype, Subtype of disorder)

## Synonyms

- FFDD type IV
- FFDD4
- Focal facial dermal dysplasia 4
- Focal facial preauricular dysplasia

## Definition

Focal facial dermal dysplasia type IV (FFDD4) is a rare focal facial dysplasia (FFDD), characterized by congenital isolated preauricular and/or cheek blister scar-like lesions.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Antenatal
- Age of onset: Neonatal
