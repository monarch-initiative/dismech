# Triple A syndrome.
**Authors:** Bharadia L, Kalla M, Sharma SK, Charan R, Gupta JB, Khan F
**Journal:** Indian J Gastroenterol (2005)

## Content

1. Indian J Gastroenterol. 2005 Sep-Oct;24(5):217-8.

Triple A syndrome.

Bharadia L(1), Kalla M, Sharma SK, Charan R, Gupta JB, Khan F.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medical Gastroenterology, S R Kalla Memorial Gastro and General 
Hospital, Jaipur. lalitbharadia@rediffmail.com

Triple A syndrome (Allgrove syndrome) is an autosomal recessive disorder 
consisting of achalasia, alacrima and Addison insufficiency. We report an 
11-year-old girl with predominant symptom of achalasia who was diagnosed as 
Triple A syndrome almost 3 years after initial presentation.
