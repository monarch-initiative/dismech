Adrenal Cushing's syndrome is caused by excess production of glucocorticoid from 
adrenocortical tumors and hyperplasias, which leads to metabolic disorders. We 
performed whole-exome sequencing of 49 blood-tumor pairs and RNA sequencing of 
44 tumors from cortisol-producing adrenocortical adenomas (ACAs), 
adrenocorticotropic hormone-independent macronodular adrenocortical hyperplasias 
(AIMAHs), and adrenocortical oncocytomas (ADOs). We identified a hotspot in the 
PRKACA gene with a L205R mutation in 69.2% (27 out of 39) of ACAs and validated 
in 65.5% of a total of 87 ACAs. Our data revealed that the activating L205R 
mutation, which locates in the P+1 loop of the protein kinase A (PKA) catalytic 
subunit, promoted PKA substrate phosphorylation and target gene expression. 
Moreover, we discovered the recurrently mutated gene DOT1L in AIMAHs and CLASP2 
in ADOs. Collectively, these data highlight potentially functional mutated genes 
in adrenal Cushing's syndrome.
