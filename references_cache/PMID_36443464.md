The Dutch Pharmacogenetics Working Group (DPWG) aims to facilitate PGx 
implementation by developing evidence-based pharmacogenetics guidelines to 
optimize pharmacotherapy. This guideline describes the starting dose 
optimization of the anti-cancer drug irinotecan to decrease the risk of severe 
toxicity, such as (febrile) neutropenia or diarrhoea. Uridine diphosphate 
glucuronosyl transferase 1A1 (UGT1A1 encoded by the UGT1A1 gene) enzyme 
deficiency increases risk of irinotecan-induced toxicity. Gene variants leading 
to UGT1A1 enzyme deficiency (e.g. UGT1A1*6, *28 and *37) can be used to optimize 
an individual's starting dose thereby preventing carriers from toxicity. 
Homozygous or compound heterozygous carriers of these allele variants are 
defined as UGT1A1 poor metabolisers (PM). DPWG recommends a 70% starting dose in 
PM patients and no dose reduction in IM patients who start treatment with 
irinotecan. Based on the DPWG clinical implication score, UGT1A1 genotyping is 
considered "essential", indicating that UGT1A1 testing must be performed prior 
to initiating irinotecan treatment.

The Dutch Pharmacogenetics Working Group (DPWG) aims to facilitate PGx implementation by developing evidence-based pharmacogenetics guidelines to optimize pharmacotherapy. This guideline describes the starting dose optimization of the anti-cancer drug irinotecan to decrease the risk of severe toxicity, such as (febrile) neutropenia or diarrhoea. Uridine diphosphate glucuronosyl transferase 1A1 (UGT1A1 encoded by theUGT1A1gene) enzyme deficiency increases risk of irinotecan-induced toxicity. Gene variants leading to UGT1A1 enzyme deficiency (e.g.UGT1A1*6, *28 and *37) can be used to optimize an individual’s starting dose thereby preventing carriers from toxicity. Homozygous or compound heterozygous carriers of these allele variants are defined asUGT1A1poor metabolisers (PM). DPWG recommends a 70% starting dose in PM patients and no dose reduction in IM patients who start treatment with irinotecan. Based on the DPWG clinical implication score,UGT1A1genotyping is considered “essential”, indicating thatUGT1A1testing must be performed prior to initiating irinotecan treatment.
