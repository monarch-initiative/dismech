# Dual T cell– and B cell–intrinsic deficiency in humans [CD4+ T cell SubSeries]

## Content

Human RLTPR deficiency is a combined immunodeficiency affecting the CD28-responsive pathway in T cells and the BCR-responsive pathway in B cells. This SubSeries contains high throughput gene expression data from primary CD4+ T cells of patients with RLTPR deficiency and controls.
