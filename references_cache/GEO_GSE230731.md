# The role of serum miRs in IgA nephropathy

## Content

To ascetain whether a serum miR signature can distinguish patients with progressive IgAN from the stable form and to determine the signature specificity for IgAN by comparing serum miR profiles with subjects with membranous nephropathy and healthy subjects.
