# Synaptobrevin is essential for fast synaptic-vesicle endocytosis.
**Authors:** Deák F, Schoch S, Liu X, Südhof TC, Kavalali ET
**Journal:** Nat Cell Biol (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1038/ncb1185](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb1185)

## Content

1. Nat Cell Biol. 2004 Nov;6(11):1102-8. doi: 10.1038/ncb1185. Epub 2004 Oct 10.

Synaptobrevin is essential for fast synaptic-vesicle endocytosis.

Deák F(1), Schoch S, Liu X, Südhof TC, Kavalali ET.

Author information:
(1)Center for Basic Neuroscience, Howard Hughes Medical Institute, USA.

Synaptobrevin-2 (VAMP-2), the major SNARE protein of synaptic vesicles, is
required for fast calcium-triggered synaptic-vesicle exocytosis. Here we show
that synaptobrevin-2 is also essential for fast synaptic-vesicle endocytosis. We
demonstrate that after depletion of the readily releasable vesicle pool,
replenishment of the pool is delayed by knockout of synaptobrevin. This delay
was not from a loss of vesicles, because the total number of pre-synaptic
vesicles, docked vesicles and actively recycling vesicles was unaffected.
However, altered shape and size of the vesicles in synaptobrevin-deficient
synapses suggests a defect in endocytosis. Consistent with such a defect, the
stimulus-dependent endocytosis of horseradish peroxidase and fluorescent FM1-43
were delayed, indicating that fast vesicle endocytosis may normally be nucleated
by a SNARE-dependent coat. Thus, synaptobrevin is essential for two fast
synapse-specific membrane trafficking reactions: fast exocytosis for
neurotransmitter release and fast endocytosis that mediates rapid reuse of
synaptic vesicles.
