# Mutations in VANGL1 associated with neural-tube defects.

Comment in
    N Engl J Med. 2010 Jun 10;362(23):2232-5. doi: 10.1056/NEJMc0910820.

Neural-tube defects such as anencephaly and spina bifida constitute a group of 
common congenital malformations caused by complex genetic and environmental 
factors. We have identified three mutations in the VANGL1 gene in patients with 
familial types (V239I and R274Q) and a sporadic type (M328T) of the disease, 
including a spontaneous mutation (V239I) appearing in a familial setting. In a 
protein-protein interaction assay V239I abolished interaction of VANGL1 protein 
with its binding partners, disheveled-1, -2, and -3. These findings implicate 
VANGL1 as a risk factor in human neural-tube defects.
