# Efficacy and Safety Analysis of Camrelizumab, Sintilimab, and Tislelizumab in the Treatment of NSCLC in Xuanwei, China: A Retrospective Real-World Study
**Authors:** Long F, He J, Pan X, Li J, Liu X, Lu S, Wang X, Kong S
**DOI:** [10.21203/rs.3.rs-9373929/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9373929/v1)

## Content

<title>Abstract</title>  <p>Background  The lung cancer incidence in the Xuanwei region of Yunnan Province is the highest in China, characterized by region-specific features and unique mutation patterns. Immune checkpoint inhibitors have been increasingly used in Chinese non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) patients. However, the real-world efficacy and safety of programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitor in this population remain unknown. This study aimed to compare the clinical outcomes of camrelizumab, sintilimab, and tislelizumab between NSCLC patients from Xuanwei and non-Xuanwei regions using real-world data. Methods  Clinical data of patients with NSCLC who received PD-1 inhibitors in the First and Third Affiliated Hospitals of Kunming Medical University were retrospectively collected through the Chinese Pharmacovigilance System (CHPS). The patients were divided into Xuanwei and non-Xuanwei groups, and the clinical efficacy and adverse reactions of PD-1 inhibitors between the two groups were compared. Results  After propensity score matching, 156 NSCLC patients receiving camrelizumab, sintilimab, or tislelizumab were included, with balanced baseline characteristics (P > 0.05). objective response rate (ORR) was 31.50% and disease control rate (DCR) 85.04%. DCR was significantly higher in the Xuanwei group than in the non-Xuanwei group (95.59% vs. 72.88%, P < 0.001). particularly for camrelizumab (100% vs 58.33%, P = 0.006) and sintilimab (96.30% vs 75.00%, P = 0.027). In 127 palliative-care patients, median progression-free survival (PFS) was 27 months, with no between-group difference. Multivariate Cox analysis identified combined radiotherapy as an independent predictor of shorter PFS. Median overall survival for all 156 patients was 47 months. Adverse events occurred in 21.79% of patients, mostly grade 1–2 (grade 3–4: 1.28%), most commonly rash and hypothyroidism. Conclusion  This study reveals that patients with NSCLC from the Xuanwei region achieved a significantly higher DCR when treated with camrelizumab or sintilimab. This finding suggests that there may be unique characteristics influencing the therapeutic efficacy of different PD-1 inhibitors in this specific patient population.</p>

Background
Page 3/30

Lung cancer is the most common cause of cancer-related death worldwide. According to data released
by the National Cancer Center of China in the 2021 China Cancer Registry Annual Report, lung cancer
ranks ﬁrst in both incidence and mortality among all cancer types in China, with non-small cell lung
cancer (NSCLC) accounting for approximately 85% of all lung cancer cases (1). In recent years,
treatment regimens represented by programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitors have
demonstrated signiﬁcant eﬃcacy in patients with NSCLC, offering the potential for long-term survival (2).
However, traditional randomized controlled trials (RCTs) have stringent inclusion/exclusion criteria and
are conducted in speciﬁc clinical settings, which may limit their generalizability to real-world practice.
The real-world eﬃcacy and safety of these treatments, especially in speciﬁc subpopulations, remain
uncertain (3).
The Xuanwei region in Yunnan Province, China, has the highest incidence of lung cancer nationwide (4).
Lung cancer in Xuanwei exhibits distinct regional characteristics: high incidence and mortality rates, a
patient demographic predominantly characterized by younger age, non-smoking status, female gender,
and a high frequency of adenocarcinoma, along with notable familial aggregation (5, 6). Studies have
shown that the gene mutation spectrum and mutation patterns in NSCLC patients from Xuanwei differ
signiﬁcantly from those in patients from non-Xuanwei regions (7). Furthermore, research indicates that
the positive expression rate of programmed death-ligand 1 (PD-L1) (PD-L1 > 1%) in this population is
signiﬁcantly higher than that in the broader East Asian patient population (5, 8, 9). Despite these known
differences, no real-world study has yet compared the eﬃcacy and safety of PD-1 inhibitors between
NSCLC patients from the Xuanwei region and those from other regions.
Therefore, this study aims to retrospectively collect clinical data of NSCLC patients treated with
camrelizumab, sintilimab, and tislelizumab from the First Aﬃliated Hospital and the Third Aﬃliated
Hospital of Kunming Medical University using the Chinese Pharmacovigilance System (CHPS). Using
propensity score matching (PSM), we will compare the eﬃcacy and safety of PD-1 inhibitors between
patients from Xuanwei and those from other regions, thereby providing evidence to inform real-world
clinical decision-making for this unique population.
1 Methods
2. 1.1 Study Subjects and Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria
This study is a real-world retrospective analysis based on the CHPS. Approved by the institutional ethics
committee of our hospital, we collected and screened data from patients with NSCLC who were treated
with camrelizumab, sintilimab, and tislelizumab at the First Aﬃliated Hospital and the Third Aﬃliated
Hospital of Kunming Medical University between July 2019 and November 2023. A total of 283 patients
met the inclusion criteria and were ultimately enrolled in this study. Patients were categorized into the
Xuanwei group or the non-Xuanwei group based on their household registration location. The last follow-
up date was March 4, 2024.
Page 4/30

Inclusion criteria for this study were as follows: (1) conﬁrmed diagnosis of NSCLC by cytology or
histology; (2) normal function of major organs; and (3) having received at least two cycles of PD-1
inhibitor therapy.Exclusion criteria included: (1) concurrent use of two or more types of immunotherapy;
(2) pre-existing systemic autoimmune diseases; and (3) requirement for long-term corticosteroid therapy
(within two weeks prior to enrollment).

1.2 Eﬃcacy Evaluation
Treatment eﬃcacy was assessed using the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors version 1.1
(RECIST 1.1). Tumor responses were categorized as complete response (CR), partial response (PR),
stable disease (SD), and progressive disease (PD).
Eﬃcacy endpoints included the objective response rate (ORR) and disease control rate (DCR), calculated
as follows:
ORR = (CR + PR) / (CR + PR + SD + PD) × 100%
DCR = (CR + PR + SD) / (CR + PR + SD + PD) × 100%
Survival endpoints included progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).
1. 3 Adverse Events Assessment
Adverse drug reactions (ADRs) were graded for severity according to the Common Terminology Criteria
for Adverse Events version 6.0. The diagnosis and grading of immune-related ADRs were performed
following the 2021 guidelines for toxicity management related to immune checkpoint inhibitors issued
by the Chinese Society of Clinical Oncology (CSCO).
1.4 Statistical Analysis
PSM was performed using R software to achieve a balanced distribution of baseline characteristics
between the two groups, with a caliper width set at 0.03. All statistical analyses were conducted using
Page 5/30

SPSS software (version 26.0). Categorical variables, including patient clinical characteristics, clinical
eﬃcacy outcomes, and ADRs, were compared between groups using the Chi-square (χ²) test. PFS curves
were generated using the Kaplan-Meier method, and differences between groups were compared with
the Log-rank test. Multivariate regression analysis was performed using the Cox proportional hazards
model. A two-sided P value of less than 0.05 was considered statistically signiﬁcant.

2.2 Analysis of Clinical Eﬃcacy
2.2.1 Objective Response Evaluation
Following PSM, eﬃcacy was evaluated in the 127 patients who received palliative treatment. The results
(Table 5) showed that no patient achieved a complete response in either group. The overall ORR was
31.50%, and the DCR was 85.04%. Regarding the comparison of treatment eﬃcacy between the two
groups, no signiﬁcant difference in ORR was observed between the Xuanwei group and the non-Xuanwei
group (30.88% vs. 32.20%, P = 0.873). In contrast, the DCR was signiﬁcantly higher in the Xuanwei group
compared to the non-Xuanwei group (95.59% vs. 72.88%, P < 0.001).

Following PSM, the therapeutic eﬃcacy of the three PD-1 inhibitors was evaluated separately in the
Xuanwei and non-Xuanwei groups. The results (Table 6) showed that within the Xuanwei group, the DCR
for both camrelizumab and sintilimab were signiﬁcantly higher than those in the non-Xuanwei group (P = 
0.006 and P = 0.027, respectively). No other statistically signiﬁcant differences were observed.

2.2.2 Analysis of PFS outcomes in palliative care patients
Among the 127 patients receiving palliative treatment included after PSM, the median PFS was 27
months, with a mean PFS of 31.83 months. In the Xuanwei group, the median PFS was not reached, and
the mean PFS was 28.52 months. In contrast, the non-Xuanwei group had a median PFS of 20 months
and a mean PFS of 29.28 months (Fig. 2A). The Log-rank test showed no signiﬁcant difference in PFS
between the two groups (P = 0.249).
The PFS curves for the 127 patients receiving palliative treatment (Fig. 2B) revealed the following: the
camrelizumab group had a median PFS of 24 months and a mean PFS of 24.73 months; the sintilimab
group had a median PFS of 20 months and a mean PFS of 36.62 months; and the median PFS was not
reached in the tislelizumab group, with a mean PFS of 26.58 months. The Log-rank test indicated no
signiﬁcant difference in PFS among the three PD-1 inhibitors (P = 0.342).
2.2.3 Analysis of Factors Inﬂuencing PFS
To identify factors inﬂuencing PFS in the 127 patients receiving palliative treatment, Kaplan-Meier
survival curves were plotted according to different clinical characteristics, and the median PFS was
estimated. Univariate analysis was performed using the Log-rank test. The results (Table 7) showed that
PD-L1 expression status (P = 0.008) and the administration of concurrent radiotherapy (P < 0.001) were
Page 14/30

signiﬁcantly associated with patient PFS. No signiﬁcant associations were found between PFS and any
of the other evaluated indicators (P > 0.05).
To further analyze the independent impact of various factors on PFS, a multivariate Cox regression
analysis was performed, incorporating factors identiﬁed from the univariate analysis and those
considered clinically relevant. The results (Table 8) demonstrated that the administration of concurrent
radiotherapy was an independent prognostic factor for PFS (P = 0.001). None of the other factors
examined were identiﬁed as independent inﬂuencing factors for PFS.

2.2.4 OS outcomes in palliative care patients
OS was analyzed in the 156 patients included after propensity score matching. The median OS for the
entire cohort was 47 months, with a mean OS of 46.53 months. The results (Fig. 3) showed that in the
Xuanwei group, the median OS was not reached, and the mean OS was 38.44 months. In contrast, the
non-Xuanwei group had a median OS of 47 months and a mean OS of 45.82 months. The Log-rank test
revealed no signiﬁcant difference in OS between the two groups (P = 0.980).
2.3 Analysis of Treatment-Related Adverse Events
2.3.1 Overall Adverse Event Proﬁle
Adverse events (AEs) were assessed in all 156 enrolled patients. As summarized in Table 9, the overall
incidence of AEs was 21.79% (34/156). Most AEs were Grade 1 or 2 in severity, with an incidence of
20.51% (32/156). The incidence of Grade ≥ 3 AEs was 1.28% (2/156). No Grade 5 (fatal) AEs were
reported. A total of 18 AEs occurred in the Xuanwei group, compared with 16 AEs in the non-Xuanwei
group. A direct comparison of the overall AE incidence rates between the two groups (Table 10) showed
no statistically signiﬁcant difference (21.79% vs. 20.51%, P = 0.832).
Furthermore, the incidence of AEs for each individual PD-1 inhibitor (camrelizumab, sintilimab, and
tislelizumab) was compared between the Xuanwei and non-Xuanwei groups (Table 11). No signiﬁcant
differences were found for any of the three agents (P > 0.05).

3 Discussion
This study is the ﬁrst to compare the eﬃcacy and safety of camrelizumab, sintilimab, and tislelizumab in
patients with NSCLC from Xuanwei and non-Xuanwei regions using real-world data. The results showed
that in terms of eﬃcacy, the DCR was signiﬁcantly higher in the Xuanwei group than in the non-Xuanwei
group (P < 0.05), primarily manifested as better eﬃcacy of camrelizumab and sintilimab in the Xuanwei
group (P < 0.05). No signiﬁcant differences were observed in the ORR, OS, and PFS between the two
groups. Regarding safety, the incidence of adverse events was similar between the two groups and
predominantly mild to moderate in severity.
In terms of eﬃcacy evaluation, the overall ORR for all patients was 31.50%, and the DCR was 85.04%.
The ORR was lower than reported in previous clinical trials, while the DCR was similar (10, 11). A
retrospective study (12) reported an overall ORR of 58.1% and a DCR of 83.9% for PD-1 inhibitors
Page 20/30

combined with other drugs in a real-world setting, with the ORR being higher than in this study, but the
DCR being close to our results. The reason for this discrepancy may be that this study primarily included
NSCLC patients receiving ﬁrst-line treatment, although a small number of patients receiving second- and
third-line therapies were also included. Despite the relatively low number of patients achieving CR and PR
in this study, the majority achieved SD, indicating good disease control.
The DCR was signiﬁcantly higher in patients from the Xuanwei region compared to those from non-
Xuanwei regions. We speculate that this may be related to the unique genetic mutation proﬁle, high PD-
L1 expression, and immune microenvironment in the Xuanwei region. The gene mutation rate and
patterns in this region differ signiﬁcantly from those in non-Xuanwei areas (13, 14), potentially endowing
NSCLC patients from Xuanwei with distinctive features in their response to immunotherapy. Residents in
the Xuanwei region are chronically exposed to high concentrations of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon
carcinogens generated from indoor coal combustion. This not only increases their tumor mutational
burden (TMB) (15) but also places these patients in a state of chronic inﬂammation induced by
environmental exposure. Key cytokines released by activated immune cells, such as interferon-gamma,
can upregulate PD-L1 expression through activation of the classic JAK-STAT signaling pathway (16, 17).
Previous studies have also shown that the positive PD-L1 expression rate (PD-L1 > 1%) in NSCLC
patients from the Xuanwei region is signiﬁcantly higher than that in East Asian patients (5, 8, 9).
Therefore, when Xuanwei patients are treated with PD-1 monoclonal antibodies, the drugs can effectively
block the key immunosuppressive PD-1/PD-L1 signaling pathway, potentially leading to more
pronounced eﬃcacy.
Evaluating the eﬃcacy of the three PD-1 inhibitors separately in the two groups revealed that the DCR for
camrelizumab and sintilimab was signiﬁcantly higher in the Xuanwei group than in the non-Xuanwei
group, while no signiﬁcant difference in DCR was observed for tislelizumab between the two groups.
Notably, the DCR for camrelizumab and sintilimab in Xuanwei patients was also slightly higher than
reported in previous clinical trials and real-world studies (10, 12, 18, 19). We speculate that this may be
related to the antibody structure and the unique tumor immune microenvironment in the Xuanwei region.
Studies suggest that Fc segment engineering is a key factor inﬂuencing the eﬃcacy of immune
checkpoint inhibitors (20–22). Among the three PD-1 inhibitors used in this study, camrelizumab and
sintilimab retain the ability of their Fc segments to bind to Fc gamma receptors (FcγR), while
tislelizumab is designed with a silenced Fc segment. Research has conﬁrmed that compared to
durvalumab, which uses a silenced Fc segment, avelumab, which retains Fc segment binding to FcγR,
can more effectively activate natural killer (NK) cells and induce higher levels of tumor cell cytotoxicity,
which may contribute to its superior antitumor effects (20, 23). Therefore, we hypothesize that in the
unique inﬂammatory microenvironment characterized by high PD-L1 expression in Xuanwei NSCLC
patients, camrelizumab and sintilimab, with their functional Fc segments, can leverage their Fc
segment's ability to bind FcγR to activate immune effector cells such as NK cells, thereby yielding better
therapeutic outcomes for patients. In contrast, tislelizumab, with its silenced Fc segment, cannot exploit
this microenvironmental advantage, resulting in no signiﬁcant difference in eﬃcacy between the
Xuanwei and non-Xuanwei regions. This hypothesis provides a new direction for future research on PD-1
Page 21/30

inhibitors in treating NSCLC in the Xuanwei region. Whether this speculation holds true has not been
reported in related studies and requires further validation through basic experiments and clinical
research.
Long-term eﬃcacy evaluation showed no statistically signiﬁcant differences in PFS or OS between the
two groups. The PFS in this study was signiﬁcantly longer than that reported in prospective clinical
studies (24–26). This discrepancy may be attributed to the relatively short follow-up period, with most
patients not yet reaching the endpoint events, and the presence of some censored data. Previous studies
investigating the eﬃcacy and adverse events of camrelizumab (27, 28), sintilimab (29), and tislelizumab
(18, 30), combined with chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone as ﬁrst-line treatment for advanced
NSCLC have shown that PD-1 inhibitor combination therapy signiﬁcantly prolongs PFS, which is largely
consistent with our ﬁndings. The median OS for the 156 matched patients was 47 months, with no
statistically signiﬁcant difference between groups. The OS in this study was signiﬁcantly longer than in
previous clinical studies (24), possibly because this study included patients receiving neoadjuvant
therapy before surgery and adjuvant therapy after surgery. These patients had earlier stages and milder
disease, leading to signiﬁcantly longer OS compared to those receiving palliative care. Comparing the
PFS of patients using the three different inhibitors revealed no statistically signiﬁcant differences.
Analysis of factors inﬂuencing PFS showed that in univariate analysis, PD-L1 expression status (P = 
0.008) and whether combined with radiotherapy (P < 0.000) were signiﬁcantly associated with PFS, while
no signiﬁcant differences in PFS were found among other indicator groups. Subsequently, multivariate
Cox regression analysis was performed based on the univariate analysis results and clinical practice.
The results indicated that patients not receiving combined radiotherapy (P = 0.001) experienced disease
progression later and had longer PFS. None of the other factors were independent inﬂuencing factors for
PFS. In this study, due to limited sample size, PD-L1 expression levels were not further stratiﬁed and
were only categorized as positive or negative. Although 92.24% of patients had unknown PD-L1
expression status due to incomplete medical records, loss of data, or lack of genetic testing, the
univariate analysis still suggested an association between PD-L1 expression and PFS. Future studies
need to expand the sample size to explore whether differences in eﬃcacy and prognosis exist among
patients with different PD-L1 expression levels.
Regarding safety, the overall incidence of adverse events was 21.79%. Comparison of adverse event
incidence between the two groups showed no statistically signiﬁcant difference. The incidence was
similar between groups, with the vast majority being grade 1–2 (incidence 20.51%), and grade 3 or higher
adverse events occurring in only 1.28% of patients. PD-1 inhibitor combination therapy was generally
manageable with a controllable safety proﬁle, and no fatal adverse events occurred. The overall
incidence of adverse events observed in this study was similar to that in clinical trials, but the incidence
of severe adverse events was lower than reported in clinical trials (24–26). Log-rank univariate analysis
of the correlation between adverse events and PFS showed no signiﬁcant correlation between patients
who experienced adverse events and those who did not.
Page 22/30

This study is a retrospective investigation of PD-1 inhibitor use in NSCLC patients from Xuanwei and
non-Xuanwei regions in a real-world setting and has several limitations: As a retrospective analysis,
biases such as selection bias and information collection bias are inevitable and may impact the results.
Additionally, the high proportion of patients with unknown PD-L1 and other gene expression statuses, the
lack of detailed subgroup analysis in the study design, the relatively short follow-up for some patients
leading to immature PFS and OS data, and the presence of censored data in the survival analysis may all
affect the outcomes. It is important to note that the comparison of this study's results with those of
clinical trials is not a strict head-to-head comparison. Compared to randomized controlled trials, this
study involves more confounding factors. Future research needs to accumulate more real-world data for
a more in-depth investigation of eﬃcacy and adverse events.
To address these limitations, subsequent studies could further expand the sample size, extend the
follow-up period, obtain more complete data, and conduct detailed subgroup analyses to provide
stronger reference evidence for clinical medication decisions.
4 Conclusions
This study revealed that NSCLC patients from the Xuanwei region achieved a higher disease control rate
(DCR) with PD-1 inhibitor therapy. Furthermore, signiﬁcant differences in clinical eﬃcacy were observed
among different types of PD-1 monoclonal antibodies, particularly for camrelizumab and sintilimab.
These ﬁndings suggest that the therapeutic response to PD-1 inhibitors in NSCLC patients from the
Xuanwei region may possess distinctive characteristics.
