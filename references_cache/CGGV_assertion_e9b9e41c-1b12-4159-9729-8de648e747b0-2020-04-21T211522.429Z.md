## Evidence summary

SLITRK6 gene was reported in relation to autosomal recessive deafness and myopia in 2013 (Tekin et al.). At least 5 unique variants (nonsense, frameshift, in-frame indel etc.) have been reported in humans. This gene-disease association is supported by a mouse model and expression studies (Tekin et al. 2013 and Katayama et al. 2009) as well as case-level and segregation data. In summary, SLITRK6 is definitively associated with autosomal recessive deafness and myopia. This has been repeatedly demonstrated in both the research and clinical diagnostic settings, and has been upheld over time
