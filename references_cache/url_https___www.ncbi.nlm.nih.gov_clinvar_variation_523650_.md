<div>In a boy (patient 3), born of consanguineous parents originating from the Turkmen population, with developmental and epileptic encephalopathy-63 (DEE63; 617976), Redler et al. (2017) identified a homozygous c.382C-A transversion (c.382C-A, NM_006651.2) in exon 4 of the CPLX1 gene, resulting in a leu128-to-met (L128M) substitution at a highly conserved residue in the C-terminal domain in a region important for synaptic vesicle binding. The mutation, which was found by whole-exome sequencing and confirmed by Sanger sequencing, segregated with the disorder in the family. The variant was present in heterozygous state in 6 of 33,197 individuals in the ExAC database. Functional studies of the variant and studies of patient cells were not performed. The patient had onset of refractory myoclonic seizures at 2 years of age. <a>(less)</a></div>
