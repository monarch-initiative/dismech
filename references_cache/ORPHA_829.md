# ORPHA:829  Adult-onset Still disease

**ORPHA:829** — Adult-onset Still disease (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- AOSD
- Wissler-Fanconi syndrome

## Definition

A rare inflammatory multisystem disorder characterized clinically by four cardinal signs: fever of unknown origin, arthralgia or arthritis, hyperleucocytosis, and typical skin rash.

## Inheritance

- Not applicable

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adult
- Age of onset: Elderly
