# CGGV:assertion_41096010-f7fc-49a5-be97-a20fc73e7e33-2023-11-01T160000.000Z  LBR / Greenberg dysplasia

**CGGV:assertion_41096010-f7fc-49a5-be97-a20fc73e7e33-2023-11-01T160000.000Z** - LBR / Greenberg dysplasia (Moderate)

## Evidence summary

LBR was first reported in relation to AR Greenberg Skeletal Dysplasia in 2003 (Waterham et al., PMID: 12618959). Greenberg Skeletal Dysplasia is a prenatally lethal chondrodystrophy, characterized by fetal hydrops, short limbs, and abnormal chondro-osseous calcification. Variants in LBR have been reported in individuals with the following disease entities: Reynold’s syndrome, Greenberg Skeletal Dysplasia, Pelger-Huet anomaly, and Regressive Spondylometaphyseal Dysplasia. Per criteria outlined by the ClinGen Lumping and Splitting Working Group, we found a difference in molecular mechanism and phenotypic variability. Therefore, the following disease entities have been split into four disease entities, Reynold’s syndrome (OMIM:613471), Greenberg Skeletal Dysplasia (OMIM:215140), Pelger-Huet anomaly (OMIM:169400), and Regressive Spondylometaphyseal Dysplasia (OMIM:618019). The split curation for AR Regressive Spondylometaphyseal Dysplasia has been curated separately by the Skeletal Disorders GCEP. Three missense variants, three frameshift, and one indel variants that have been reported in six probands in five publications (PMIDs: 12618959, 18382993, 21327084, 30518689, 30561119) are included in this curation. The mechanism of pathogenicity appears to be loss of function. This gene-disease relationship is also supported by expression evidence (PMID: 21327084). In summary, there is moderate evidence to support this gene-disease relationship. While more evidence is needed to establish this relationship definitively, no convincing contradictory evidence has emerged. This classification was approved by the ClinGen Skeletal Disorders GCEP on the meeting date November 1st, 2023 (SOP Version 9).
