# Pathogenesis of Graves' Disease Using Single-Cell Sequencing with Thyroid Autoantigens Peptides Stimulation in B Cells

## Content

We used the latest technology, BD Rhapsody, to analyze BCR of PBMCs and intrathyroidal blood mononuclear cells from patients with Graves' disease at the single-cell level.
