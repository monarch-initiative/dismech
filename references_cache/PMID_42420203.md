# Hyperacute Progression of Sinonasal NUT Carcinoma After Pembrolizumab-Based Treatment: A Case Report.
**Authors:** Ye Z, Yang D, Xie F, Zhang M, Gao C, Xie Y, Li X, Yin M
**Journal:** Laryngoscope (2026)
**DOI:** [10.1002/lary.70733](https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.70733)

## Content

This case report describes hyperacute destructive progression of sinonasal NUT carcinoma with new osseous metastasis shortly after pembrolizumab-based immunochemotherapy. The clinical course highlights the importance of early NUT testing, proactive systemic surveillance, cautious interpretation of immunotherapy response, and timely consideration of BET inhibition or clinical trial referral.
