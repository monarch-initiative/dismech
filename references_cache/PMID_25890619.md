Neurosyphilis is a complication of systemic syphilis. This review of the 
clinical presentation, diagnostic laboratory findings, treatment and management 
of neurosyphilis discusses the impact of HIV and the specific challenges it 
brings, focusing on areas of controversy, and highlighting important questions 
that remain to be answered.
