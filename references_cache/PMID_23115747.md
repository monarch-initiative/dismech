# Acute chorioamnionitis at term caused by the oral pathogen Fusobacterium nucleatum.
**Authors:** Bohrer JC, Kamemoto LE, Almeida PG, Ogasawara KK
**Journal:** Hawaii J Med Public Health (2012)

## Content

1. Hawaii J Med Public Health. 2012 Oct;71(10):280-1.

Acute chorioamnionitis at term caused by the oral pathogen Fusobacterium 
nucleatum.

Bohrer JC(1), Kamemoto LE, Almeida PG, Ogasawara KK.

Author information:
(1)Department of Obstetrics, Gynecology and Women's Health, University of 
Hawai'i John A. Burns School of Medicine, Honolulu, HI, USA. 
justin.bohrer.md@gmail.com

Fusobacterium nucleatum is an oral pathogen associated with preterm birth. 
Presented is a case of acute chorioamnionitis that progressed to maternal sepsis 
in a term patient with intact membranes. In addition to its role in periodontal 
disease and preterm birth, our case demonstrates that intrauterine infection 
with Fusobacterium nucleatum can result in severe disease at term.
