# Alternative splicing induced by bacterial pore-forming toxins sharpens CIRBP-mediated cell response to Listeria infection

## Content

We aimed at identifying the targets of the protein Cold-Inducible RNA-Binding Protein in a human epithelial colon adenocarcinoma cell line (LoVo ATCC CCL-229) in cells infected by Listeria monocytogenes strain LL195 compared to non-infected cells. This set is linked to a previous one referenced on ENA (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJEB26593)
