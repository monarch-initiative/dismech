<div><div>This corrects the article "<a>The ARF GAPs ELMOD1 and ELMOD3 act at the Golgi and cilia to regulate ciliogenesis and ciliary protein traffic</a>", ar13.</div></div>
</div></section></section><section><section><hr/>
<p>The authors of “The ARF GAPs ELMOD1 and ELMOD3 act at the Golgi and cilia to regulate ciliogenesis and ciliary protein traffic” (Mol. Biol. Cell [2022] 33, 1–16; originally published in <em>MBoC</em> In Press as 10.1091/mbc.E17-04-0248) wish to make a correction to Figure 4E. In the original HTML and PDF versions, we mistakenly used the same images for the DKO (Elmod1 and Elmod3 double knockout) cells with and without serum (far right panels). This has been corrected to replace the images of the cells grown “with serum” with the correct images. Note that no text, figure legends, or conclusions are altered in any way by this correction.</p>
<p>The authors apologize for this error.</p>
<p>The HTML and PDF versions were corrected on the <em>Molecular Biology of the Cell</em> website on May 23, 2022. These corrections may not appear on copies of the article that reside on other websites.</p>
