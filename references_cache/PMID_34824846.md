We report the case of a patient who was initially presented with ischemic 
priapism to the emergency department. He was treated with adrenaline 
intracavernous injections and aspiration with irrigation of the corpora 
cavernosa and distal shunt. In the postoperative period, anemia, basophilia, 
eosinophilia, thrombocytosis and hyperleukocytosis were detected. The patient 
was subsequently diagnosed with chronic myeloid leukemia. Priapism is a rare 
manifestation of chronic myeloid leukemia (≤ 3%) and occurs mostly due to 
hyperleukocytosis, resulting in thrombus formation and corporal venous outflow 
obstruction. Priapism occurring in any setting is considered as a medical 
emergency that requires immediate local therapy because of resulting 
irreversible cell damage and fibrosis if not treated within the first 24-48 h.
