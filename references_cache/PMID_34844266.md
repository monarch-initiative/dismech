We report a patient affected by BCL11B-related disorder, providing the first 
extensive demonstration of clinical and neuroradiological progressive course of 
the disease, with possible implications on the way it is studied and 
followed-up. Never described clinical aspects such as toes abnormalities and 
hypospadias widen the range of dysmorphisms associated with this condition. Our 
data suggest that BCL11B mutations may be implicated not only in impaired 
morphogenesis and hematopoiesis but also in progressive central nervous system 
damage, which remains to be further investigated and clarified.
