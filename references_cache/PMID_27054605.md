Advanced Therapy in Eisenmenger Syndrome: A Systematic Review.

Hjortshøj CS(1), Jensen AS, Søndergaard L.

Author information:
(1)From the Department of Cardiology, Rigshospitalet, Copenhagen, Denmark.

Treatment options for patients with the Eisenmenger syndrome have until recently 
been scarce, but new knowledge in the field of pulmonary arterial hypertension 
has expanded the therapeutic possibilities for these patients. Advanced therapy 
with pulmonary vasodilators has become part of the standard treatment, offering 
long-term benefits on exercise capacity, clinical symptoms, and possibly 
survival. However, there are currently only few studies to guide the use of 
advanced therapies in this population, and important questions such as 
indications for initiation or escalation of advanced therapy and valid effect 
parameters and treatment goals remain unanswered. This review covers the 
pharmacology, therapeutic options, risk stratification, and treatment strategy 
of pulmonary arterial hypertension-specific drugs in patients with Eisenmenger 
syndrome.
