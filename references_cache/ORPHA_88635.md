# ORPHA:88635  Vacuolar myopathy with sarcoplasmic reticulum protein aggregates

**ORPHA:88635** — Vacuolar myopathy with sarcoplasmic reticulum protein aggregates (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Myopathy due to calsequestrin and SERCA1 protein overload
- Vacuolar aggregate myopathy

## Definition

A rare, genetic vacuolar myopathy characterized by mild myopathy or elevated levels of creatine kinase in the blood without associated symptoms.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant
- Unknown

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Adult
