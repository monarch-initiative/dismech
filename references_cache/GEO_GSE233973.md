# Comprehensive gene expression analysis using gastric mucosa with autoimmune gastritis

## Content

In this study, we aimed to reveal whether gastric mucosa with AIG has a specific gene expression profile, involving in its histology and chronic inflammation. To approach this, we performed comprehensive analysis of gene expression using gastric mucosa with atuoimmune gastritis, that with H. pylori-associated gastritis and healthy mucosa without any inflammation. Potential mechanisms of the gene expression changes in gastric mucosa with AIG were also explored.
