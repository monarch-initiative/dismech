# RNA-Seq analysis of human MEN1-associated thymic carcinoid and thymoma compared to normal thymus

## Content

RNA-Sequencing was performed on rRNA-depleted RNA samples that were isolated from formalin fixed paraffin embedded (FFPE) tissue sections of human normal thymus and human thymic tumors of patients with multiple endocrine neoplasia type 1 (MEN1) syndrome.
