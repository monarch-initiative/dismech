# Psittacosis.
**Authors:** Jenks JD, Yarrarapu SNS

## Content

Psittacosis is a zoonotic bacterial infection caused by the obligate intracellular bacterium Chlamydia psittaci. Psittacosis, also called parrot fever or ornithosis, is primarily transmitted through contact with infected birds and can cause a wide range of diseases. Psittacosis typically causes a mild to moderate infection, although infection can be severe. Birds serve as the major epidemiological reservoir, and psittacosis can occur after exposure to any bird species and has been documented in 467 species across 30 bird orders. The most significant risk factor for psittacosis is exposure to birds infected with C psittaci, with human transmission occurring through direct contact with infected birds or inhalation of aerosolized bacteria from feces, urine, respiratory tract secretions, or eye secretions.
