# Autosomal recessive intestinal lymphangiectasia and lymphedema, with facial anomalies and mental retardation.
**Authors:** Hennekam RC, Geerdink RA, Hamel BC, Hennekam FA, Kraus P, Rammeloo JA, Tillemans AA
**Journal:** Am J Med Genet (1989)
**DOI:** [10.1002/ajmg.1320340429](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.1320340429)

## Content

1. Am J Med Genet. 1989 Dec;34(4):593-600. doi: 10.1002/ajmg.1320340429.

Autosomal recessive intestinal lymphangiectasia and lymphedema, with facial 
anomalies and mental retardation.

Hennekam RC(1), Geerdink RA, Hamel BC, Hennekam FA, Kraus P, Rammeloo JA, 
Tillemans AA.

Author information:
(1)Clinical Genetics Center Utrecht, The Netherlands.

We report on two male and two female relatives with intestinal lymphangiectasia; 
severe lymphedema of limbs, genitalia, and face; facial anomalies; seizures; 
mild growth retardation; and moderate mental retardation. Main facial anomalies 
are a flat face, flat nasal bridge, hypertelorism, small mouth, tooth anomalies, 
and ear defects. Their parents are consanguineous. This disorder probably is an 
hitherto undescribed autosomal recessive syndrome.
