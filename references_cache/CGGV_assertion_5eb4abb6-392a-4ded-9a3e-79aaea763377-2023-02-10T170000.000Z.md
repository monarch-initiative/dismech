## Evidence summary

SLC38A8 has been repeatedly associated with autososomal recessive foveal hypoplasia 2 with or without optic nerve misrouting and/or anterior segment dysgenesis, also known as foveal hypoplasia, optic nerve decussation and anterior segment dysgenesis without albinism (FHONDA). Patients with the disorder often present with foveal hypoplasia, nystagmus, reduced visual accuity, and chiasmal misrouting. Evidence supporting this gene-disease relationship includes case-level data and experimental data. At least 19 variants (frameshift, splice site, nonsense, in-frame deletion, and missense) have been reported in 16 probands from 3 publications (PMID:32744312, PMID:24290379, PMID:24045842). A summed LOD score of 7.06 was calculated from two seperate publications (PMID:24290379, PMID:24045842).

The gene SLC38A8 encodes SNAT8 (sodium coupled neutral amino acid transporter) 8, which is expressed in all neurons and has a broad substrate profile for a number of amino acids. Due to its expression in the central nervous system and as a transporter of glutamine, it may be involved in glutamate/glutamine (GABA) cycling. This gene-disease association is further supported by its biochemical function as an amino acid transporter (PMID:25451601) and in SLC38A8 kock-down Medaka (PMID:24290379), which showed microphthalmia, coloboma, and lens defects.

A classification of definitive was reached on 1/27/2022 by the Aminoacidopathy GCEP.
