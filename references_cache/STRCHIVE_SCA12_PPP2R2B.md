## Disease description

Spinocerebellar ataxia type 12 (SCA12) is a very rare subtype of type I autosomal dominant cerebellar ataxia (ADCA type I). It is characterized by the presence of action tremor associated with relatively mild cerebellar ataxia. Associated pyramidal and extrapyramidal signs and dementia have been reported [@mondo:0011439].

## Mechanism

Polyalanine gain of function associated with RAN translation [@pmid:38467784].

## Age of onset

Typical: 26-50; Range: 8-56 [@omim:604326].

## Prevalence

- Details: Frequent in India; rare in other populations [@pmid:34711523].

## Details

Benign range  is 6-32 repeats, intermediate range 40-49, and pathogenic range is 51-78 [@pmid:37906407]; intermediate alleles are associated with reduced penetrance [@pmid:11198281].
