# Single-cell RNA sequencing of blood cells from patients with VEXAS syndrome.

## Content

We performed single cell RNA sequencing, and VDJ sequencing of TCR and BCR of peripheral blood samples (Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells[PBMCs]) from 9 patients with VEXAS to understand disease pathogenesis.
