# Mitochondrial DNA Maintenance Defects Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, El-Hattab AW, Craigen WJ, Wong LJC, Scaglia F

## Content

This purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the pathomechanism of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) maintenance defects; 2.. Review the genetic causes of mtDNA maintenance defects; 3.. Describe the clinical characteristics of mtDNA maintenance defects; 4.. Provide clinical and laboratory evaluation strategies to facilitate the diagnosis of a mtDNA maintenance defect and to establish a genetic cause in a proband (when possible); 5.. Summarize current management recommendations for individuals with mtDNA maintenance defects; 6.. Inform genetic counseling for mtDNA maintenance defects.
