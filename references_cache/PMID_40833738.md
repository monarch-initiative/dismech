# Targeted Next-Generation Sequencing for Etiologic Diagnosis of Cutaneous Mycobacterium marinum Infection.
**Authors:** Gu AK, Qin WH, Zhang XJ, Ma FK, Zhang LT
**Journal:** JAMA Dermatol (2025)
**DOI:** [10.1001/jamadermatol.2025.2905](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamadermatol.2025.2905)

## Content

This cross-sectional study evaluates the sensitivity and specificity of targeted next-generation sequencing in diagnosing cutaneous Mycobacterium marinum infection.
