The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Describe the clinical characteristics of 
Alzheimer disease (AD); 2.. Review the genetic causes of AD; 3.. Provide an 
evaluation strategy to identify the genetic cause of AD in a proband (when 
possible); 4.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with 
AD.
