Identifying neurometabolic disorders that lead to neonatal encephalopathy is 
difficult, and access to exome sequencing is a significant advantage in 
developing countries. We present a case of neonatal encephalopathy characterized 
by refractory seizures and significant apnea resulting from glutaminase 
deficiency, along with elevated levels of glutamine and glycine in the 
cerebrospinal fluid. Although the condition was fatal, it was possible to offer 
genetic counseling and recommendations for future pregnancies following exome 
sequencing.
