# Host eicosanoid signals define a granuloma-associated fibroblast population coordinating mycobacterial containment II

## Content

We used single-cell RNA sequencing to interrogate granulomas from Mycobacterium marinum infected zebrafish (Danio rerio) of a genetic mutant and matched WT siblings. Granulomas are comprised of several cell types and we sought to understand the consequences of genetic disruption of the gene apolipoprotein db (apodb) on granuloma cells types and cell-specific transcription.
