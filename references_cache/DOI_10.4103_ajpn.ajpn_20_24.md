# Kidney Involvement in Erythrocyte Membrane Protein Band 4.1-like 1-associated Autosomal Dominant Intellectual Developmental Disorder Type 11
**Authors:** Evgenia Gurevich, Alaa Asle, Alon Farfel, Evgenia Cherniavsky, Ohad Birk, Daniel Landau
**Journal:** Asian Journal of Pediatric Nephrology (2024)
**DOI:** [10.4103/ajpn.ajpn_20_24](https://doi.org/10.4103/ajpn.ajpn_20_24)

## Content

Autosomal dominant intellectual developmental disorder (ADIDD) is a genetically heterogeneous disease. Genetic variants in erythrocyte membrane protein band 4.1-like 1 ( EPB41L1 ) cause ADIDD type 11, which has not been reported to have a kidney phenotype. We describe a 3.6-year-old boy, born to nonconsanguineous healthy parents, who presented with failure to thrive, microcephaly, and moderate global developmental delay, but normal brain MRI. Bilateral normal-sized hyperechogenic kidneys were found, in association with normal blood pressure, glomerular filtration rate, and urinary concentrating capacity, but mild electrolyte abnormalities (hypocalciuric hypomagnesemia, mild metabolic alkalosis, and nonalbumin proteinuria). Whole-exome trio analysis revealed a de novo heterozygous missense pathogenic variant in a conserved locus of EPB41L1 (c.1857G>C; p.Glu619Asp; NM_012156.2). This is the first description of kidney involvement in a patient with EPB41L1 pathogenic variant. Echogenic kidneys, hypomagnesemia with hypocalciuria, and nonalbumin proteinuria suggest tubulointerstitial involvement. Demonstrating that kidney involvement is secondary to the EPB41L1 variant would broaden the spectrum of cerebrorenal syndromes.
