# Transcriptional regulation by Errb (Nr3b2) in stria vascularis

## Content

Transcriptional profiles were compared in microdissected lateral walls of the inner ears from Errb mutant mice and wild type littermate controls. The goal is to identify transcriptional targets of Errb and candidate genes for inner ear diseases. Keywords: genetic mutant analysis
