# PDGFRA activating mutations in gastrointestinal stromal tumors.

PDGFRA activating mutations in gastrointestinal stromal tumors.

Heinrich MC(1), Corless CL, Duensing A, McGreevey L, Chen CJ, Joseph N, Singer 
S, Griffith DJ, Haley A, Town A, Demetri GD, Fletcher CD, Fletcher JA.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine, Department of Pathology, Oregon Health & Science

Most gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GISTs) have activating mutations in the 
KIT receptor tyrosine kinase, and most patients with GISTs respond well to 
Gleevec, which inhibits KIT kinase activity. Here we show that approximately 35% 
(14 of 40) of GISTs lacking KIT mutations have intragenic activation mutations 
in the related receptor tyrosine kinase, platelet-derived growth factor receptor 
alpha (PDGFRA). Tumors expressing KIT or PDGFRA oncoproteins were 
indistinguishable with respect to activation of downstream signaling 
intermediates and cytogenetic changes associated with tumor progression. Thus, 
KIT and PDGFRA mutations appear to be alternative and mutually exclusive 
oncogenic mechanisms in GISTs.
