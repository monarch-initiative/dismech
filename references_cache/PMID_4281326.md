# Pilosebaceous abnormalities in Apert type acrocephalosyndactyly.
**Authors:** Solomon LM, Cohen MM Jr, Pruzansky S
**Journal:** Birth Defects Orig Artic Ser (1971)

## Content

1. Birth Defects Orig Artic Ser. 1971 Jun;7(8):193-5.

Pilosebaceous abnormalities in Apert type acrocephalosyndactyly.

Solomon LM, Cohen MM Jr, Pruzansky S.

Forearm acne is uncommon in the general population and comedones and pustules
are rare. Forearm acne was observed in more than 70% of the Apert syndrome
patients suggesting that steroid sensitivity of certain tissue and organs and
tissue disposition of androgens may be fruitful areas for investigation in the
Apert syndrome.
