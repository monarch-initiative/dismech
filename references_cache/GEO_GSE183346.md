# High throughput sequencing data of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS) mouse model

## Content

We found that in the mouse OHSS model, certain drug treatment can improve the symptoms of OHSS. The results of high-throughput sequencing showed that the cell connection between ovarian vessels was strengthened.
