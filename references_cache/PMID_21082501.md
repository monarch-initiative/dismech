Ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome: definition, incidence, and classification.

Author information:

Ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS) is a iatrogenic complication of 
controlled ovarian stimulation. Although considered uncommon, the severe form is 
potentially fatal. Different clinical classifications have been developed 
through the years. No matter whether it is termed "grade C" or "critical" OHSS, 
life-threatening events may develop. Hence it is prudent to recognize the 
population at risk. Several risk factors for OHSS have been identified; some are 
considered major risk factors such as polycystic ovary syndrome; others are less 
well defined. Preventive measures could be taken before and during the treatment 
cycle while taking into consideration the patient's characteristics and the 
various treatment options and approaches that currently exist.
