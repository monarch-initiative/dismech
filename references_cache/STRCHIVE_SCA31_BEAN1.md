## Disease description

Spinocerebellar ataxia type 31 (SCA31) is a very rare subtype of autosomal dominant cerebellar ataxia type III (ADCA type III) characterized by the late-onset of cerebral ataxia, dysarthria and horizontal gaze nystagmus, and that is occasionally accompanied by pyramidal signs, tremor, decreased vibration sense and hearing difficulties [@mondo:0007296].

## Mechanism

RNA toxicity and gain of function leading to neurodegeneration [@pmid:36371266]. Role in heterochromatin or chromosomal structure theorized [@omim:117210].

## Age of onset

Typical: 56-62 [@pmid:36563608]; Range: 8-83 [@pmid:23331413].

## Prevalence

- Details: Has been found in Japanese populations [@omim:117210] and then once respectively in a Korean and a Chinese family [@pmid:36563608].

## Details

This locus is a novel STR-containing insertion, not present in reference genome; the pathogenic threshold (110-760) is based on the pure repeat of the pathogenic motif within the insertion [@pmid:19878914].
