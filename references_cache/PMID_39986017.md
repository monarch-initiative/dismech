Ohdo syndrome Say-Barver-Biesecker-Young-Simpson (SBBYS) variant is a rare 
autosomal dominant disorder characterized mainly by intellectual disability, 
developmental delays, contractures of the knees and hips contractures, thyroid 
dysfunction, and dysmorphic appearance. The Ohdo syndrome SBBYS variant is 
caused by heterozygous loss of function mutation in the KAT6B gene. The 
peripheral blood mononuclear cells from a patient carrying heterozygous 
frameshift mutation of the KAT6B gene were reprogrammed using the 
CytoTune-iPS2.0 Sendai Reprogramming Kit. The mutation in the KAT6B gene causes 
the abnormal protein variant. The established human induced pluripotent cell 
line allow proper in vitro disease modelling of Ohdo syndrome SBBYS variant.
