# ORPHA:698090  Ophthalmological abnormalities-facial dysmorphism-intellectual disability syndrome

**ORPHA:698090** — Ophthalmological abnormalities-facial dysmorphism-intellectual disability syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- BRPF1-related neurodevelopmental disorder

## Definition

A rare genetic syndrome characterized by developmental delay, intellectual disability, ophthalmological abnormalities and dysmorphic facial features such as ptosis and round face.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
