# Nonsyndromic Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Overview.
**Authors:** Adam MP, Bick S, Mirzaa GM, Wallace SE, Amemiya A, Cirino AL, Channaoui N, Ho C

## Content

The purpose of this overview is to: 1.. Define the clinical characteristics of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM); 2.. Review the genetic causes of nonsyndromic HCM; 3.. Review the differential diagnosis of nonsyndromic HCM; 4.. Provide an evaluation strategy to identify (when possible) the genetic cause of nonsyndromic HCM in a proband; 5.. Review the management of HCM; 6.. Inform genetic counseling of family members of an individual with nonsyndromic HCM.
