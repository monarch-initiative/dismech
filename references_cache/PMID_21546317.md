# Video/EEG recording of myoclonic absences in GLUT1 deficiency syndrome with a hot-spot R126C mutation in the SLC2A1 gene.

Glucose transporter type 1 deficiency syndrome (GLUT1DS) is an inborn error of 
brain energy metabolism characterized by impaired glucose transport into the 
brain. A classic phenotype comprising epilepsy, mental retardation, an often 
paroxysmal disorder, and several subtypes has been described. Although typical 
absences are frequent in GLUT1DS, myoclonic absence seizures are rarely 
reported. Here we describe a novel Turkish patient with a hot-spot mutation 
(R126C) in the SLC2A1 gene who presented with unusual myoclonic absence epilepsy 
and paroxysmal shivering. The case is discussed in view of eight other cases 
carrying the R126C mutation.
