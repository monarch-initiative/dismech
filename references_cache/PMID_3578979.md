Acute arsenic ingestion.

Levin-Scherz JK, Patrick JD, Weber FH, Garabedian C Jr.

A 21-year-old man presented in shock after ingesting 2 g of arsenic trioxide. He
died within 37 hours despite intensive treatment that included intramuscular
dimercaprol and hemodialysis. Hemodynamic and laboratory data are presented
illustrating the multisystem toxicities of inorganic arsenic. Hemodialysis,
previously described as an effective therapeutic adjunct, was shown to be
ineffective in this case.
