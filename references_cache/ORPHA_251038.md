# ORPHA:251038  3q29 microduplication syndrome

**ORPHA:251038** — 3q29 microduplication syndrome (Malformation syndrome, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Trisomy 3q29

## Definition

3q29 microduplications are recently described chromosomal abnormalities with unclear clinical significance.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal dominant
- Not applicable

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Infancy
- Age of onset: Neonatal
