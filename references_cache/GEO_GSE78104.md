# lncRNA and mRNA expression data in peripheral blood sampled from patients with Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

## Content

The aim of the study is to identify the global messenger RNA (mRNA) and long noncoding RNA (lncRNA) expression profiling in peripheral blood from thirty patients with Obsessive Compulsive Disorders (OCD) and thirty paired normal controls.
