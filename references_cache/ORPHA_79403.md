# ORPHA:79403  Junctional epidermolysis bullosa with pyloric atresia

**ORPHA:79403** — Junctional epidermolysis bullosa with pyloric atresia (Disease, Disorder)

## Synonyms

- Carmi syndrome
- JEB with pyloric atresia
- JEB-PA

## Definition

A severe form of junctional epidermolysis bullosa (JEB) characterized by generalized blistering at birth and congenital atresia of the pylorus and rarely of other portions of the gastrointestinal tract.

## Inheritance

- Autosomal recessive

## Natural history

- Age of onset: Antenatal
- Age of onset: Neonatal
