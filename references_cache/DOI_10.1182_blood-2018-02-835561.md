# Patients with CD3G mutations reveal a role for human CD3γ in Treg diversity and suppressive function
**Authors:** Jared H. Rowe, Ottavia M. Delmonte, Sevgi Keles, Brian D. Stadinski, Adam K. Dobbs, Lauren A. Henderson, Yasuhiro Yamazaki, Luis M. Allende, Francisco A. Bonilla, Luis I. Gonzalez-Granado, Seyma Celikbilek Celik, Sukru N. Guner, Hasan Kapakli, Christina Yee, Sung-Yun Pai, Eric S. Huseby, Ismail Reisli, Jose R. Regueiro, Luigi D. Notarangelo
**Journal:** Blood (2018)
**DOI:** [10.1182/blood-2018-02-835561](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2018-02-835561)

## Content

Key Points
CD3γ-deficient patients manifest T-cell phenotypic and functional defects that are especially prominent in Treg cells. The peripheral T-cell repertoire of CD3γ-deficient patients is restricted, with molecular signatures of self-reactivity.
