Key inclusion & exclusion criteria
Inclusion criteria: 1. The patient meets the diagnostic criteria of movement disorders;
2. Voluntary acceptance of bacterial community transplantation therapy and contraindication of sterile group transplantation therapy;
3. Informed consent was given by the patient and his/her family and informed consent was filled in.
Exclusion criteria: 1. Pregnant or lactating women;
2. Patients with end-stage diseases;
3. Cases who are participating in or have participated in other clinical trials within 3 months;
4. Previous history of intestinal pathogen infection;
5. Oral antibiotics or proton pump inhibitors (PPI) in recent 2 weeks and immunosuppressed.
