# Codex assessment of the OpenScientist report

## Verdict

**Supported.** The report is dependable on its central conclusion: ATP7B
dysfunction, failed biliary copper excretion and ceruloplasmin loading, and
hepatic copper retention are the established initiating mechanism of Wilson
disease. Gene-transfer, hepatocyte-replacement, knockout-model, chelation, and
transplant evidence converge on that core.

The assessment does not endorse every downstream ranking or curation lead.
Several statements move from strong plausibility to claims that the cited
designs did not test.

## Material corrections

- **The pan-India cohort does not justify “ATP7B as the sole causative gene in
  every cohort.”** Its 128 clinically diagnosed cases included genetically
  unresolved and incompletely resolved groups, and the paper reports secondary
  gene-burden signals (PMID:39535360). Wilson disease is ATP7B-defined, but the
  empirical cohort claim is still false.
- **Rescue supports causality without proving universal formal necessity and
  sufficiency.** ATP7B gene transfer and normal-hepatocyte repopulation correct
  copper handling in LEC rats (PMID:11961729; PMID:11273771), and liver
  transplantation normalizes human copper indices (PMID:17889170). Those
  interventions do not test every manifestation in every tissue.
- **Mitochondrial injury is central in hepatic Wilson disease, but “principal
  downstream effector” is not a comparative result.** The meta-analysis pools
  heterogeneous human and animal hepatic endpoints and does not partition
  causal contributions among death pathways (PMID:41480142).
- **QSM is not element-specific.** Regional susceptibility and serum-ferritin
  correlations motivate an iron hypothesis but do not establish iron
  co-deposition or its causal contribution (PMID:39233127).
- **The ontology lead is wrong.** GO:0006534 is an obsolete cysteine-metabolic
  process identifier, not ceruloplasmin biosynthesis.
- **Search-volume claims are not auditable.** The committed report exposes 39
  unique PMIDs while stating that more than 155 papers were reviewed.

## Curation consequence

Retain the canonical ATP7B copper-retention hypothesis. Treat penetrance,
exchangeable-copper toxicity, mitochondrial dominance, brain iron, non-hepatic
ATP7B functions, and newer regulated-cell-death labels as separately scoped
claims. None of the report's new papers or ontology terms should be promoted
from this assessment alone; normal evidence and reference validation still
apply.

The authoritative structured dispositions are in
`openscientist-assessment-by-codex.yaml`.
