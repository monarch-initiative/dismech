# Assessment of the OpenScientist miR-214–BCL2L11 report

## Overall assessment

**Verdict: weakly supported and unresolved.**

The report is right that this is not a validated CNS-specific or clinical
cisplatin-resistance mechanism. It is also right to emphasize the non-CNS
NCCIT model, forced miR-214 expression, cross-sectional patient data, and the
absence of paired resistant tumors. Its treatment of the seed experiment,
however, is incomplete enough to affect the strength of the in-vitro judgment.
The weakly-supported/unresolved verdict is intentional because the distinctive
claim is CNS-specific or clinical resistance, not merely an NCCIT in-vitro
response modifier.

## Material evidence omitted by the report

The report says the seed study contains only an
overexpression-to-protein-reduction observation and repeatedly lists a missing
BCL2L11 phenocopy. The full text of PMID:29036598 reports that BCL2L11
knockdown increased cisplatin resistance in NCCIT cells (Figure 3E), with a
caspase-3 readout (Figure 3F). MiR-214 overexpression increased cisplatin
survival in Figure 3B but did not change etoposide survival (reported as data
not shown), and Supplementary Figure S6 shows similar baseline proliferation.
These controls make the in-vitro miR-214/BCL2L11/cisplatin chain stronger and
more drug-specific than the report allows.

The experiment remains limited. It does not supply endogenous miR-214
loss-of-function, a disease-context 3′UTR binding assay, miRNA-insensitive
BCL2L11 rescue, a second intracranial model, orthotopic validation, or a
response-linked patient cohort.

## Overstated counterevidence

The report calls the cisplatin-survival claim contradicted in the correct
disease context. A neuroblastoma result is not the correct germ-cell-tumor
context. Testicular embryonal-carcinoma evidence for OCT4–NOXA establishes a
strong competing determinant of platinum sensitivity, but an alternative
apoptotic dependency does not refute coexistence of a BIM-dependent response
modifier. The appropriate disposition is limited in-vitro support, not
refutation.

## What remains unresolved

The clinical claim is still unsupported. The human data compare histologic
classes rather than resistant and sensitive CNS NGGCTs, and methylation is
strongly structured by differentiation state. Direct miR-214 binding to
BCL2L11 also remains unconfirmed in this disease context.

## Curation implication

Keep the hypothesis `EMERGING` and explicitly label it an NCCIT-derived
response-modifier candidate. Retain both the miR-214 overexpression result and
the BCL2L11-knockdown phenocopy as in-vitro evidence, but do not promote the
axis to an endogenous, CNS-specific, or clinical resistance mechanism.
