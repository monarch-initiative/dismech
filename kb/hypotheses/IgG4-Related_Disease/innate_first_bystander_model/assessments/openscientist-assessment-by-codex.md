# Assessment of the OpenScientist innate-first bystander report

## Overall assessment

**Verdict on the distinctive mechanism: weakly supported / unresolved.**

The report finds real and important innate biology, but the evidence does not
yet establish its proposed ordering in human IgG4-related disease. Its strongest
causal sequence is from repeated poly(I:C) administration in an autoimmune
pancreatitis mouse model. Its strongest genetic model produces autoimmune
sialadenitis after constitutive TLR7 activation, but does not reproduce the
IgG4, vascular, fibrotic, or systemic hallmarks needed to make it a faithful
IgG4-RD model.

The report is right that a purely bystander adaptive response is incompatible
with the observed oligoclonality, somatic hypermutation, self-reactivity, and
clonal CD4-positive CTL expansion. That correction, however, removes a defining
part of the seed hypothesis. The replacement two-phase model remains a coherent
research hypothesis whose central transition has not been observed.

## What the report gets right

### Innate pathways are genuine mechanistic leads

[PMID:21971969](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21971969/) and
[PMID:22744834](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22744834/) show that stimulation
of innate receptors on monocytes or basophils can promote BAFF-associated IgG4
production in cell systems. [PMID:39264798](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39264798/)
provides temporal and perturbational evidence for a cDC–T-cell–pDC loop in a
poly(I:C)-induced mouse model. These findings justify retaining innate activation
as an upstream or parallel possibility.

### A pure adaptive-bystander account is untenable

[PMID:24815737](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24815737/) directly demonstrates
oligoclonal, extensively mutated, self-reactive plasmablast responses and new
clones at relapse. [PMID:26971690](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26971690/)
demonstrates clonally expanded CD4-positive CTLs. The established adaptive
response is therefore selected, not simply a polyclonal passenger response.

### The report identifies the decisive missing observation

No longitudinal human study shows an early innate, polyclonal state progressing
to the later oligoclonal adaptive state. That unobserved transition is the
distinctive edge of the proposed two-phase model.

## Where the report overstates the evidence

### Mouse ordering is not human ordering

The human arm of PMID:39264798 measured serum chemokines cross-sectionally in 33
patients. It did not observe lesion initiation, cell recruitment order, or a
preclinical-to-clinical transition. The paper itself frames the same human loop
as possible; the report upgrades this to strong temporal validation.

### The Slc29a3 model is not specific for IgG4-RD

[PMID:41332187](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41332187/) establishes that
endogenous-nucleoside-driven TLR7 activation can cause autoimmune sialadenitis
in Slc29a3-deficient mice. Shared chemokines in human salivary glands are a
useful bridge, but the model also resembles a feature shared with Sjögren
syndrome and does not reproduce systemic IgG4-RD pathology.

### “Three independent pathways” misclassifies a review

The NOD2 and basophil experiments are primary cell studies.
[PMID:27744509](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27744509/) is a review, not a
third independent primary experiment. None of these sources demonstrates three
redundant pathways operating in vivo or establishes that they precede the
adaptive response in patients.

### Treatment and biomarker results do not prove the claimed mechanisms

The MITIGATE trial strongly supports CD19-positive B-lineage cells as a
therapeutic target, but anti-CD19 efficacy does not identify antigen-specific
adaptation as the necessary function of that compartment.
[PMID:32938972](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32938972/) supports IFN-alpha and
IL-33 as activity-associated biomarkers; correlation and steroid responsiveness
do not prove that they are causal rather than downstream markers.

## Curation implication

Retain the hypothesis as `ALTERNATIVE` and preserve the proposed longitudinal
repertoire and spatial tests. Do not encode a human innate-first causal edge,
describe the Slc29a3 model as sufficient for IgG4-RD, infer adaptive necessity
from anti-CD19 efficacy, or label IFN-alpha/IL-33 non-epiphenomenal.

The current disease entry already contains several of these report-derived
interpretations. A separate disease-YAML correction should narrow those
explanations while preserving the verified component evidence; this
assessment-only change intentionally does not perform that promotion or repair.
