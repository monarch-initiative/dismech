# ME/CFS exertion-perfusion model — OpenScientist assessment

Assessed: 2026-09-05T00:07:34+00:00 · Provider: openscientist · Assessor: codex

**Overall verdict: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED**

The report correctly retains an emerging, heterogeneous neurovascular-muscle-immune hypothesis and recognizes conflicting CPET and cytokine evidence. Source-level review finds material errors: a missed 2022 sodium MRI pilot and randomized acute pyridostigmine study, a 31% small-fiber-neuropathy proportion misreported as approximately 50%, review-as-primary ultrastructural evidence, absent healthy EM controls, long-COVID-to-ME/CFS transfer, and cross-sectional/cohort comparisons described as temporal or causal tests. Plasma metabolomics, CSF cohorts, TRPM3 assays and mouse ATG13 perturbation are informative within their actual scopes. A cited microbiome source is a preprint, and DecodeME genetic data already existed. The report supplies a literature synthesis, not auditable provider analyses: 29 numbered PMID entries, no search export for the claimed 72-paper corpus, no raw data, code or supplied figure files. The disease YAML incorporates valid findings and corrected clinical care while preserving uncertainty about intracellular calcium loading, causal ordering and subtype boundaries. An omitted 2022 patient serum-vesicle/microglial assay adds in vitro bioactivity evidence without establishing mtDNA-specific causality or human brain exposure.

## integrated-verdict — RETAINED

The integrated model remains emerging and partly supported.

Multiple physiological observations are credible, but they were measured in different populations with different assays. A necessary sequential route from muscle perfusion through ions, metabolites and immune states to delayed PEM is unproven. Retain clinical PEM while qualifying the proposed molecular explanation.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/), [PMID:36494667](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36494667/), [PMID:35526605](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35526605/), [PMID:42212259](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42212259/).

## sodium-mri-absence — REJECTED

The report falsely states that no ME/CFS sodium MRI study exists.

PMID:36494667 was published in 2022, before this June 2026 report. Six female cases and six age/BMI/sex-matched controls underwent 3T sodium MRI before and after three minutes of plantar flexion with 40-minute recovery imaging. Baseline tissue sodium and selected recovery values differed. The proposed MRI experiment therefore extends existing pilot work rather than filling a complete absence.

Sources: [PMID:36494667](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36494667/).

## sodium-compartment-limit — QUALIFIED

Existing tissue sodium imaging does not prove intracellular calcium overload.

The sodium MRI pilot is real, but its 2.44-ms echo time emphasizes long-lived signal and cannot separate intracellular from extracellular sodium; the authors estimate only about 30% of the short-lived component remains. It did not directly measure myocyte calcium, NCX reversal or calcium-mediated mitochondrial injury. Correct the absence claim without adopting the review's stronger intracellular interpretation.

Sources: [PMID:36494667](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36494667/), [PMID:39727052](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39727052/).

## sfn-denominator — REJECTED

Small-fiber neuropathy was present in 31% of the cited ME/CFS cohort.

The 160 ME/CFS biopsies yielded 31%, while approximately 50% refers to background POTS/fibromyalgia studies in the introduction. Misattribution changes the apparent strength and generality of the upstream mechanism.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/).

## icpet-selection — QUALIFIED

The low-filling-pressure result is conditioned on cohort selection.

Investigators screened 1516 iCPETs and selected patients with RAP below 6.5 mmHg, no intrinsic cardiopulmonary disease and available skin biopsies. The 160-versus-36 contrast cannot estimate the prevalence of low RAP in unselected ME/CFS. Low-flow impaired venous return and high-flow impaired extraction are distinct phenotypes.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/).

## sfn-causal-bridge — QUALIFIED

Skin-fiber loss does not establish shunting as its downstream effect.

Denervation severity did not correlate with exertional measures. Skin biopsy and systemic extraction measurements support a proposed neurovascular relationship, not demonstrated autonomic denervation, direct capillary shunt imaging or a causal SFN-to-PEM chain.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/).

## em-source-type — QUALIFIED

The purported direct EM citation is a narrative review.

PMID:39727052 collects and interprets other studies. Direct sources are PMID:38338957 for CFS-versus-PCS ultrastructure and PMID:38177128 for paired exercise biopsies in long COVID. Labeling the review high-confidence primary human EM obscures their methods and limitations.

Sources: [PMID:39727052](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39727052/), [PMID:38338957](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38338957/), [PMID:38177128](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38177128/).

## em-controls — QUALIFIED

The primary EM study lacks a healthy microscopy comparator.

PMID:38338957 compared EM samples from 14 CFS and 14 PCS participants. Healthy historical controls were used for OXPHOS, not EM. Cristae integrity and morphology differed between syndromes, but the study cannot directly establish abnormality versus healthy EM, causation by calcium, or absence of PCS pathology. Disease duration, activity and other cohort differences remain plausible explanations.

Sources: [PMID:38338957](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38338957/).

## exercise-biopsy-population — QUALIFIED

The paired biopsy study is long-COVID evidence with restricted transfer to general ME/CFS.

PMID:38177128 studied 25 people with WHO-defined long COVID and PEM and 21 recovered controls, not a mixed general ME/CFS cohort. The paired biopsy design provides genuine post-exercise evidence, but OXPHOS capacity decreased in both groups; disease-specific conclusions depend on other measures and interactions. Neither all long COVID nor every ME/CFS subtype can be equated with this sample.

Sources: [PMID:38177128](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38177128/).

## myotube-context — QUALIFIED

Cultured-cell persistence does not establish a genetic intrinsic defect.

Nine CFS and eleven control donor cultures showed reduced glucose-supported oxidation but preserved galactose and fatty-acid use. Culture selection, epigenetic memory and prior systemic exposure can persist. The study supports a retained substrate-specific cellular phenotype, not universal OXPHOS/FAO failure or exclusion of systemic origins.

Sources: [PMID:33106563](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33106563/).

## vascular-context — QUALIFIED

FMD and PORH differences do not establish exercising-muscle ischemia.

The cited paper is by Sandvik and colleagues, not Scherbakov. Thirty-nine RituxME participants had lower brachial FMD and skin PORH; these measures did not correlate, and rituximab did not improve vascular or clinical outcomes over placebo. Different assay beds support vascular abnormalities but not the proposed muscle-perfusion-to-PEM causal link.

Sources: [PMID:36730360](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36730360/).

## rbc-assay-limit — QUALIFIED

Erythrocyte stiffness is an ex vivo observation, not direct in vivo obstruction.

PMID:30594919 studied sixteen case-control pairs with a microfluidic device. It did not measure patient capillary obstruction, oxygen delivery or delayed PEM. Diagnostic use was a proposal, not validated sensitivity/specificity.

Sources: [PMID:30594919](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30594919/).

## purine-time-window — QUALIFIED

The seven-day PEM measure is not a serial exercise-provocation experiment.

The 47-case study relates biochemical concentrations to self-reported recent PEM. Hypoxanthine was lower with severity. It does not provide a prospective seven-day exercise time course, isotopic flux, calorimetry or direct muscle-source tracing; methylhistidine and mannitol interpretations are not definitive proof of muscle destruction or barrier leakage.

Sources: [PMID:31277442](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31277442/).

## csf-independent-cohorts — QUALIFIED

CSF before/after comparisons used independent groups.

PMID:39941050 compared independent non-exercise and post-exercise cohorts, not paired lumbar punctures. Post-exercise samples followed two submaximal bouts on consecutive days. Concentration differences cannot establish within-person consumption/generation, flux, white-matter injury or a causal muscle-to-brain bridge.

Sources: [PMID:39941050](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39941050/).

## naviaux-count — QUALIFIED

The Naviaux study had 45 cases, not 84 cases.

PMID:27573827 enrolled 84 total subjects: 45 cases and 39 controls. Sex-stratified classifiers were derived in a small study; reported diagnostic accuracy is not equivalent to prospective independent clinical validation.

Sources: [PMID:27573827](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27573827/).

## metabolite-direction — QUALIFIED

Plasma concentration profiles are not direct metabolic shutdown or flux measurements.

Reduced abundance of many selected metabolites supports altered metabolic state, while dauer is an analogy. The report combines this hypometabolic interpretation with a separate hypermetabolic interpretation of PEM without direct flux measurements or a longitudinal demonstration of transition. Do not curate a proven shutdown-to-hypermetabolism sequence.

Sources: [PMID:27573827](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27573827/), [PMID:31277442](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31277442/).

## cbfv-referral-scope — QUALIFIED

Cerebral flow velocity and specialty-cohort proportions require qualification.

PMID:41576003 is a retrospective autonomic-referral cohort (143 long COVID, 170 ME/CFS, 73 controls). Flow velocity is not volumetric cerebral flow and its abnormality frequency cannot be generalized to unselected populations. It does not demonstrate peripheral metabolite amplification into CNS symptoms. A separate 429-case extracranial Doppler study directly assessed volumetric flow, including normal HR/BP subgroups.

Sources: [PMID:41576003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41576003/), [PMID:32140630](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32140630/).

## central-physiology — QUALIFIED

Central activation is a complementary physiological finding, not proof of psychiatric causation or central sensitization.

PMID:8423875 demonstrated reduced voluntary activation during a specific maximal isometric task with normal peripheral measures in its older CFS cohort. It did not establish a disorder of belief, voluntary effort avoidance, nociceptive central sensitization or the mechanism of delayed multi-system PEM.

Sources: [PMID:8423875](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8423875/).

## older-muscle-negative — RETAINED

The older biopsy study also found no significant EM abnormalities.

PMID:9527150 included 105 biopsies and 41 EM samples. Histometry did not generally show expected inactivity changes and EM was negative. Preserve both the negative structural result and evidence against reducing the disease to deconditioning; differences in sampling and case criteria require further work.

Sources: [PMID:9527150](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9527150/).

## cpet-meta-specificity — QUALIFIED

The meta-analysis does not make every CPET parameter a validated PEM test.

PMID:33327624 reports lower day-two mean measures and highlights a significant case-control difference at workload at VT. The -10.8 and -33.0 values are differences between groups at the two tests, not universal within-patient declines. Assay specificity and generalization need validation. The 84-case/71-control primary study supports impaired recovery in its cohort, including matched aerobic-capacity pairs.

Sources: [PMID:33327624](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33327624/), [PMID:38965566](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38965566/).

## cpet-negative-study — RETAINED

The 2026 study meaningfully limits universality without negating clinical PEM.

PMID:42212259 enrolled 58 cases and 25 sedentary controls and required PEM (VAS at least 3). It used multiple VT criteria with expert adjudication. No day-two VO2 decline was detected, while perceived exertion and chronotropic response differed. The paper mentions preliminary symptom-monitoring differences whose full analysis is separate; a negative CPET decline is not evidence that patients lack PEM or disability.

Sources: [PMID:42212259](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42212259/).

## immune-dynamic-limit — RETAINED

The report appropriately preserves negative cytokine evidence.

PMID:10226888 sampled through 24 hours with greater patient fatigue but no group immune differences; PMID:24027260 found no clinically significant cytokine upregulation after exercise or sleep deprivation. Single-cell platelet changes in PMID:38232699 are specific associations, not confirmation of a generalized cytokine cascade.

Sources: [PMID:10226888](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10226888/), [PMID:24027260](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24027260/), [PMID:38232699](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38232699/).

## nk-trpm3-scope — QUALIFIED

NK-cell calcium experiments do not validate the skeletal-muscle ion hypothesis.

PMID:42177403 used ten cases and ten controls and found compartment- and stimulus-specific calcium effects, including higher mitochondrial influx under one condition. PMID:39483457 is a meta-analysis of cytotoxicity, not a TRPM3 perturbation study. PMID:31057538 found no NK functional differences in a large biobank cohort. These data support research phenotypes, not a universal diagnostic abnormality or muscle calcium-overload proof.

Sources: [PMID:42177403](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42177403/), [PMID:39483457](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39483457/), [PMID:31057538](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31057538/).

## atg13-model — QUALIFIED

ATG13 perturbation is mouse evidence, not an established human ME/CFS cause.

PMID:41591477 uses Atg13-depleted mice and macrophage assays. It supports autophagy/redox/inflammatory coupling and post-treadmill weakness. It does not establish a causal human susceptibility variant, the temporal definition of human PEM or the entire perfusion/ion/CNS chain. Curate under an explicitly model-scoped hypothetical mechanism and animal model.

Sources: [PMID:41591477](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41591477/).

## genetic-architecture — QUALIFIED

ME/CFS genetic data existed before the report.

The August 2025 DecodeME preprint reports eight loci in GWAS using up to 15,579 cases and 259,909 controls of European genetic ancestry. HLA associations in 426 Norwegian cases were published in 2020. These sources do not connect each hypothesis node to an effector gene, but invalidate a blanket absence of genetic architecture data. DecodeME remains a preprint; nearest-gene annotations are not causal assignments.

Sources: [DOI:10.1101/2025.08.06.25333109](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.08.06.25333109), [PMID:32210306](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32210306/).

## pyridostigmine-omission — REJECTED

A randomized acute hemodynamic perturbation already existed.

PMID:35526605 (2022), registered NCT03674541, randomized 45 patients to 60 mg pyridostigmine or placebo between iCPETs separated by 50 minutes. Relative peak VO2, cardiac output and filling-pressure responses improved. It directly tests part of the neurovascular pathway; it does not establish delayed PEM rescue or the complete molecular sequence. The abstract reports an internally inconsistent sign for its treatment-effect confidence interval, so this assessment does not reuse that interval.

Sources: [PMID:35526605](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35526605/), [clinicaltrials:NCT03674541](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03674541).

## registered-mechanistic-trial — QUALIFIED

Registered trials narrow the report's claimed intervention gap.

The narrow absence of a trial measuring every proposed node may hold. However, NCT03674541 already tested acute neurovascular physiology, and NCT06366724 (LIFT, registered before the report) tests pyridostigmine and low-dose naltrexone in ME/CFS with OI. The August 2026 LIFT publication is a later protocol, not outcome evidence. As of 2026-09-04 LIFT is RECRUITING; no efficacy result should be inferred from registration.

Sources: [clinicaltrials:NCT03674541](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03674541), [clinicaltrials:NCT06366724](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06366724), [DOI:10.1186/s13063-026-09943-6](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-026-09943-6).

## microclot-interpretation — QUALIFIED

Extravascular muscle amyloid does not support intravascular capillary plugging.

The report labels the microclot model speculative, appropriately. Its primary long-COVID exercise-biopsy source further found deposits outside capillaries, no capillary-density difference, and no corresponding lactate evidence for tissue hypoxia. These results constrain that particular occlusion explanation without ruling out all vascular abnormalities.

Sources: [PMID:36043493](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36043493/), [PMID:38177128](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38177128/).

## autoantibody-interpretation — QUALIFIED

Receptor autoantibody concentration correlations do not demonstrate receptor dysfunction.

PMID:32247028 is a hypothesis paper. PMID:33889154 correlates sCD26, antibody levels and clinical variables in subgroups and could not confirm sCD26 diagnostic suitability. Functional receptor assays, target engagement and controlled intervention are needed before using the antibody association as a causal vasomotor edge.

Sources: [PMID:32247028](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32247028/), [PMID:33889154](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33889154/).

## microbiome-source-type — QUALIFIED

The microbiome citation is a preprint and does not prove barrier leakage.

PMID:40980765 is an ArXiv preprint with thesis-style chapters, not a new peer-reviewed multi-study causal confirmation. Peer-reviewed primary studies PMID:36758521 and PMID:36758522 support altered microbial pathways and butyrate-related measures. Diet, IBS, medication, duration and reverse causation matter; microbial/serum associations do not establish a gut-leak-to-PEM mechanism.

Sources: [PMID:40980765](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40980765/), [PMID:36758521](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36758521/), [PMID:36758522](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36758522/).

## comparative-ranking — NEEDS_VERIFICATION

The report does not establish that this is the most comprehensive or best explanatory framework.

No prespecified comparison, model-fit analysis or head-to-head causal test is provided. Broad coverage of many associated findings is not evidence of comparative validity; overlapping alternatives and unexplained heterogeneity remain.

## subtype-boundaries — QUALIFIED

Proposed SFN/CPET/post-infectious subgroups are not validated mechanistic boundaries.

Lack of a day-two CPET decline or skin biopsy abnormality does not establish CNS primacy, autoimmunity or absence of a peripheral mechanism. Studies use different sampling frames; prospective joint phenotyping, replication and treatment interaction tests are needed to establish clinically useful subgroups.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/), [PMID:42212259](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42212259/), [PMID:8423875](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8423875/).

## vasodilator-design — QUALIFIED

A nonspecific vasodilator is not a selective test of improved muscle oxygen use.

This is a predicted outcome, not a result. Low preload and high-flow impaired extraction may respond differently; vasodilation can alter blood pressure, shunting and central perfusion. Predefine target engagement, adverse hemodynamic responses, carryover and washout, symptom outcomes beyond 24 hours, multiplicity and subgroup interaction tests. Improvement would support a pathway only if mediator changes and off-target alternatives are addressed.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/), [PMID:35526605](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35526605/).

## temporal-profiling-design — QUALIFIED

Dense profiling is useful but temporal correlation alone is insufficient for causality.

The proposed schedule can characterize timing and heterogeneity. It cannot by itself prove metabolite-to-immune-to-CNS direction because common causes, feedback, circadian effects, activity, medication and selection can confound associations. Use prespecified endpoints, matched sampling, within-person models and independent validation; interpret mediation cautiously.

Sources: [PMID:38232699](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38232699/), [PMID:10226888](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10226888/).

## sodium-design — QUALIFIED

The proposed sodium study must extend existing work with compartment and task controls.

Build on PMID:36494667 rather than claim first application. Optimize echo time and compartment modeling, measure activation and workload, include mobility/activity controls and repeated symptom trajectories. Tissue sodium and pH do not directly quantify calcium flux or NCX direction; orthogonal tissue assays and perturbation are required.

Sources: [PMID:36494667](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36494667/).

## biopsy-design — QUALIFIED

Paired biopsies can test association and injury dynamics without automatically proving upstream perfusion causation.

No provider experiment was executed. Existing paired long-COVID biopsies already address part of this question. Match biopsy timing, task load, baseline activity and assay normalization; distinguish case-by-time interaction from changes in both groups and control injury from the procedure itself. Simultaneous physiology and targeted perturbation are needed for direction.

Sources: [PMID:38177128](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38177128/), [PMID:38338957](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38338957/).

## multimodal-selection — QUALIFIED

A single invasive battery risks excluding severe disease and distorting subgroup inference.

The nominal n>=100/50 target is not an endpoint-specific power calculation. Severity-adapted visits, tolerability, noninvasive options, cumulative exertional burden, missingness and recruitment bias must be planned. Unsupervised clusters require stability testing and external replication; they are not automatically biological subtypes.

Sources: [PMID:33577778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33577778/), [PMID:42212259](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42212259/).

## peripheral-vesicle-bioassay — QUALIFIED

An existing patient serum-vesicle/microglial experiment narrows the peripheral-to-CNS evidence gap.

PMID:36153118 (2022) reports exercise-associated increases in patient serum vesicle-associated mtDNA and IL-1beta release from cultured human microglia exposed to patient preparations. Published figure captions describe pooled donor preparations and assay replicates; they do not establish independent donor-level replication. The accessible earlier author preprint uses total extracellular-vesicle isolation, commercially purchased healthy-control sera without a matched exercise series, and no mtDNA depletion/add-back or BBB transport experiment. Those detailed methods are preprint evidence because final full methods were inaccessible. The assay supports preparation bioactivity, not mtDNA necessity, muscle source, disease-specific exercise response, blood-brain-barrier passage or human CNS/PEM causality.

Sources: [PMID:36153118](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36153118/), [DOI:10.21203/rs.3.rs-387687/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-387687/v1).
