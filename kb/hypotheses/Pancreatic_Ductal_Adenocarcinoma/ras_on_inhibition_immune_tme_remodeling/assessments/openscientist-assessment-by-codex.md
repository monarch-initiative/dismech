# OpenScientist assessment: ras_on_inhibition_immune_tme_remodeling

Updated 4 September 2026; supersedes and expands the 31 July assessment. The structured YAML is authoritative.

The central preclinical immune-remodeling hypothesis is supported, but its antigen-presentation ownership, daraxonrasib agent gap and generalized Treg-relief framing are flawed. The report missed direct 2025 RMC-7977/daraxonrasib PDAC evidence. Subsequent August evidence adds Treg recruitment and context-specific CTLA4 combination efficacy, qualifying generic checkpoint synergy. Patient immune mediation and checkpoint-combination efficacy remain unestablished in the cited clinical trials. This September review retains and expands the useful July assessment, reviews the complete report and citation sidecar, and distinguishes contemporaneous omissions from subsequently published evidence. Literature findings were independently verified and the disease YAML revised; no provider computation was reproduced.

## central-preclinical-spine — RETAINED

The report's conclusion is correct and is more directly supported than its evidence inventory indicates. PMID:40057911 tested RAS(ON) multiselective inhibition in immunocompetent PDAC models, including daraxonrasib, and reported fewer suppressive myeloid cells, more T cells, T-cell/cDC dependence, and deeper and more durable responses with immunotherapy.

[PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/), [PMID:37782788](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37782788/), [PMID:37625401](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37625401/)

## direct-daraxonrasib-pdac-omission — REJECTED

PMID:40057911 is a directly matched PDAC study. Its full text reports immunocompetent PDAC experiments with the tool compound RMC-7977 and the investigational drug daraxonrasib, including a daraxonrasib-plus-anti-PD-1 study. It found increased tumor MHC-I, decreased MDSC-like subsets, increased CD4/CD8 T cells, T-cell/cDC-dependent regression, and deeper, more durable immunotherapy combinations. The NSCLC study PMID:41670434 is supportive but is not the strongest available drug-class/disease match.

[PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/), [PMID:41670434](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41670434/)

## clinical-combination-gap — RETAINED

PMIDs 42223072 and 42090791 report daraxonrasib trials without a randomized checkpoint-combination or paired tissue immune-contexture endpoint. Registry entries supply trial context, not negative proof of all ongoing or future trials. The prior assessment claimed a current exhaustive registry search without a committed query record; that absence claim is narrowed here. The experimental combinations remain preclinical evidence.

[PMID:42223072](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42223072/), [PMID:42090791](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42090791/), clinicaltrials:NCT06625320

## antigen-presentation-ownership — REJECTED

PMID:32376951 and PMID:36977556 establish autophagy/NBR1 and FAK control of MHC-I, but neither comparison establishes dominance over RAS signaling or independence from it. PMID:40057911 directly observed increased MHC-I on remaining PDAC tumor cells after RMC-7977. The causal mediation and interaction among RAS, autophagy, and FAK remain to be separated; the report's categorical ownership inference is unsupported.

[PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/), [PMID:32376951](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32376951/), [PMID:36977556](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36977556/)

## treg-edge — QUALIFIED

The July assessment correctly noted that PMID:40057911 measured FoxP3-positive CD4 Tregs and did not attribute its RMC-7977-associated CD4 increase to that population; this did not show Treg depletion or disarming. Subsequent DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75960-3 reports Treg recruitment under MRTX1133/daraxonrasib and CTLA4-associated reprogramming. The YAML replaces an unsupported generalized relief node with this contextual adaptation.

[PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/), DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75960-3

## cdk8-daraxonrasib-scope — QUALIFIED

PMID:42436354 directly shows initial immune infiltration followed by CDK8-CXCL2/FAS-mediated reversal during long-term MRTX1133 treatment. For daraxonrasib, it reports CDK8 upregulation in resistant PDX tumors, not the complete temporal immune-remodeling mechanism. The durability warning is important, but agent-specific mechanistic equivalence remains an inference.

[PMID:42436354](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42436354/)

## independence-of-support — QUALIFIED

PMID:36824971 is the preprint version of the study later published as PMID:37625401 and should not be counted as an independent replication. PMID:38727236 used SOS1 plus MEK inhibition and found that durable benefit required myeloid activation with a CD40 agonist before checkpoint blockade. The central conclusion is supported, but the regimens and independence of the sources must be stated accurately.

[PMID:36824971](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36824971/), [PMID:37625401](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37625401/), [PMID:37782788](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37782788/), [PMID:38727236](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38727236/)

## retracted-alternative-citation — REJECTED

PubMed marks PMID:41329731 as retracted in May 2026. PNAS published a corrected version in June 2026 under PMID:42224594. The mechanistic alternative can be retained only by citing and reviewing that corrected record; the retracted PMID should not be treated as evidence.

[PMID:41329731](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41329731/), [PMID:42224594](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42224594/)

## human-immune-readout-gap — RETAINED

PMID:42223072 establishes improved survival and progression-free survival in previously treated mPDAC. It did not test checkpoint blockade or the proposed MHC-I, myeloid, T-cell, or Treg pharmacodynamic changes, so those clinical causal steps remain open.

[PMID:42223072](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42223072/)

## newer-treg-recruitment — QUALIFIED

The July caution against asserting Treg depletion was appropriate. Subsequent DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75960-3 directly finds recruitment of regulatory, effector and exhausted T cells under MRTX1133 or daraxonrasib. CTLA4 blockade reprogrammed effector Tregs toward a naive state and improved immune control in these models. This supports a context-dependent counter-regulatory recruitment node rather than generalized Treg relief; abundance and suppressive function are distinct endpoints.

DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75960-3, [PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/)

## checkpoint-partner-specificity — QUALIFIED

PMID:40057911 demonstrated daraxonrasib plus anti-PD1 benefit in one PDAC system, whereas the subsequent Nature Communications study found CTLA4 blockade specifically effective compared with PD1, Tim3, Lag3, Vista and 4-1BB combinations in its models. Different regimen, timing and model conditions preclude treating either as a universal checkpoint ranking. Both are preclinical; no routine human combination is inferred.

DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75960-3, [PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/)

## autophagy-publication-independence — QUALIFIED

PMID:32376951 is the primary Nature study. PMID:32459143 is an Autophagy commentary by overlapping authors about that recent study, not another independent Nature experiment. The NBR1 lysosomal MHC-I mechanism is supported by the primary evidence; two citation identifiers do not imply two independent replications.

[PMID:32376951](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32376951/), [PMID:32459143](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32459143/)

## fak-kinase-and-subtype — QUALIFIED

The primary FAK study shows a kinase-independent function requiring nuclear FAK. The suppression was lost in an extreme squamous phenotype. Its genetic depletion and STAT3 interactions do not establish that a FAK kinase inhibitor reproduces antigen-presentation rescue in every PDAC subtype or that the pathway operates independently of RAS in all settings.

[PMID:36977556](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36977556/)

## usp20-direction-and-clinical-scope — QUALIFIED

PMID:42392864 supports KRAS-G12D/EGR1-driven USP20 stabilization of HMGCR, cholesterol accumulation and CD8 exhaustion, plus ULK1/autophagy-mediated adaptation under G12D inhibition. Thus active USP20 promotes suppression; relief follows inhibition, not activation of USP20. Human multiomics anchors associations, while causal perturbation and MRTX1133/USP20-inhibitor/PD1 combinations are model experiments, not a clinical daraxonrasib combination.

[PMID:42392864](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42392864/)

## sos1-mek-rebound-context — QUALIFIED

PMID:38727236 concerns combined SOS1/MEK inhibition and reports IL6-associated fibroblast/myeloid rebound with impaired dendritic-cell quality. Adding a CD40 agonist was required before checkpoint blockade produced the reported durable tumor-free outcomes. The report diagram should not assign that complete sequence to daraxonrasib or generic checkpoint blockade alone.

[PMID:38727236](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38727236/)

## ras-activity-human-association — QUALIFIED

PMID:37782788 links KRAS activity and immune/stromal states in human profiling and tests pathway inhibitors with immune therapy in preclinical systems. A bulk or single-cell association does not establish that every myeloid, stromal and T-cell effect is mediated by a single RAS-MAPK causal chain. KRAS is frequent but not universal, and KRAS-wild-type PDAC is a valid disease subset.

[PMID:37782788](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37782788/), [PMID:37404765](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37404765/)

## allele-and-unselected-response — QUALIFIED

The cited PMID:42226008 is a review of allele-associated architecture, not a randomized comparison proving immune-treatment effect modification. General PDAC checkpoint response estimates below 5% must distinguish unselected/microsatellite-stable cases from the rare MSI-high/dMMR treatment-sensitive subset. Molecular selection and immune-state composition are different stratifiers.

[PMID:42226008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42226008/), DOI:10.1136/jitc-2021-004485

## corrected-nonimmune-alternative — QUALIFIED

Corrected PMID:42224594 supports pharmacologic triple targeting and genetic RAF1/EGFR/STAT3 ablation in mouse/PDX systems. It provides a combination strategy without an added checkpoint antibody, but the absence of an immune drug does not prove absence of an immune contribution to efficacy. This is preclinical durability, not demonstrated human cure or clinical tolerability.

[PMID:42224594](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42224594/)

## trial-news-not-replication — QUALIFIED

The randomized trial PMID:42223072 establishes clinical efficacy. The report PMID:42226659 did not resolve in an independent NCBI fetch; the matching quoted wording is in Cancer Discovery news DOI:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-NW2026-0062. That secondary news item describes the same trial, not independent replication or immune pharmacodynamics. The August acquired-resistance ctDNA cohort PMID:42581230 adds molecular evolution data but no paired tissue immune-contexture or randomized checkpoint-combination endpoint. The unresolved PMID is retained here only as the object of the source audit, not a validated supporting citation.

PMID:42223072, DOI:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-NW2026-0062, PMID:42581230

## proposed-tests-not-results — RETAINED

All five proposed tests remain proposals in the provider record, with no executed input data, code or results. The Treg and antigen-presentation tests need redesign in light of direct existing evidence: quantify function and composition, distinguish kinase inhibition from FAK depletion, test interaction rather than exclusive pathway ownership, and compare clinically relevant exposure and checkpoint partners.

[PMID:40057911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40057911/), [PMID:36977556](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36977556/), DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-75960-3

## Artifact and analysis audit

The Markdown report and citation sidecar are present. Linked HTML and PDF reports are absent. No raw dataset, code or quantitative execution record was supplied. All 5 discriminating tests are future proposals, recorded as SKIPPED. Independently checked publication evidence supports curated claims but does not establish provider search completeness.
