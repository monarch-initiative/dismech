## Executive Judgment

**Verdict: WEAKLY / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED — the causal (driver *or* amplifier) claim remains unproven, and the injury-associated biomarker-only null is currently the more parsimonious reading of the exact-ARVC evidence.**

The hypothesis rests on three empirical pillars: (1) anti-DSG2 antibodies occur in clinically defined ARVC; (2) they have functional activity in vitro (gap-junction dysfunction, cohesion loss, DSG2 cleavage); (3) their level tracks disease features. Pillar 1 is robust in human ARVC cohorts. Pillars 2 and 3 are real but do **not** yet discriminate a *causal antigen-specific* effect from (a) total polyclonal IgG activity, (b) reactivity to other intercalated-disc (ICD) antigens (notably N-cadherin), (c) generic proteolytic/catalytic IgG activity, (d) a downstream intracellular GSK-3β/p38 signaling node that is *also* activated cell-intrinsically by desmosomal variants, and (e) assay/comparator/cutoff choices.

The decisive caveats are:

- **Disease specificity fails against the most relevant comparator.** In an independent multicenter study, anti-DSG2 titers did **not** differ between ARVC and myocarditis/DCM, and had **no** ARVC clinical correlates (PMID:39597880). The favorable diagnostic/prognostic study (PMID:42233375) is real-world and internally strong (AUC 0.823; 100% specificity at OD>0.774; death/transplant HR 4.56) **but used only non-inflammatory RV-overload comparators (Ebstein/Eisenmenger) plus healthy controls**, and its own authors state specificity versus other non-ischemic cardiomyopathies "remains to be established." The two are reconcilable, not contradictory: anti-DSG2 separates ARVC from healthy and pressure/volume-overloaded RV, but not from inflammatory myocardial disease.
- **The animal model that anchored the original specificity claim now fails to replicate.** Two 2025–2026 Boxer-dog cohorts (PMID:41351822; PMID:42160918) find anti-DSG2 present in healthy dogs and non-discriminatory (AUC 0.53–0.60), while troponin/NT-proBNP discriminate and track arrhythmia burden.
- **No study delivers the causal-grade experiments** — antigen-specific depletion/add-back of native antibody, monoclonal reconstruction, epitope-rescue of the patient IgG effect, Fc/complement dissection, intact-animal passive transfer, or longitudinal pre-injury (antecedent) antibody sampling.
- The **prognostic** association in PMID:42233375 is with cardiac death/transplant (progressive dysfunction) and **not** with arrhythmic events — a pattern the authors interpret as the antibody "reflecting progressive myocardial dysfunction," i.e., compatible with the biomarker-only null.
- **The antigen-specificity premise itself is unconfirmed.** The "disease-specific DSG2 epitope" underpinning the specificity claim comes from a single study (PMID:30239670) and has no independent reproduction (searched 2026-07-26), while two other groups could not detect anti-DSG2 binding by their own ELISAs and observed N-cadherin co-reactivity (PMID:37450050, 39597880). Cross-lab assay disagreement means part of the "signal" may be assay/epitope-artifactual — a concern that sits upstream of all causal interpretation.
- **The only mechanistically-supported, partly-actionable immune axis in exact desmosomal models is innate (NF-κB/NLRP3), and it responds to therapy that does not target the antibody** (PMID:34345905); human immune-directed therapy signal is DSP-restricted and empirical (PMID:39786454). No anti-DSG2-directed intervention or trial exists.

Bottom line: the evidence supports **testing** an emerging causal hypothesis and is sufficient to keep it EMERGING, but does **not** warrant wiring a causal pathophysiology edge, defining a clinical biomarker as ARVC-specific, or proposing immune-directed therapy. If forced to weight driver vs amplifier vs epiphenomenon, current exact-ARVC data most economically fit **injury-dependent epiphenomenon, with an injury-dependent amplifier not excluded.**

## Mechanistic Causal Chain (hypothesis vs evidence)

```
[Desmosomal variant: PKP2/DSG2/DSP/JUP]
        │  STRONG (genetics establishes upstream cause)
        ▼
[Impaired desmosomal adhesion / ICD instability]  ── STRONG (PMID:32376797, 30239670)
        │
        ├───────────────► [Cell-intrinsic NFκB / GSK-3β inflammation]  ── COMPETING BRANCH, growing support
        │                          │  (PMID:42193878) inflammation may PRECEDE structure
        │                          ▼
        │                  [Myocyte necrosis / injury] ── STRONG in model (PMID:19635863)
        │                          │
        ▼                          ▼
[Exposure of cryptic DSG2 / ICD epitopes]  ── INFERRED (proposed in 42233375 discussion; not demonstrated)
        │
        ▼
[Anti-DSG2 (and anti-N-cadherin / anti-ICD) antibody formation]  ── OBSERVED in ARVC (PMID:30239670, 42233375)
        │                                                            but NOT ARVC-specific (PMID:39597880, 35764120)
        │
        ▼  ◄── THE CONTESTED EDGE ──►
[Antigen-specific functional injury: adhesion/gap-junction impairment, Fc/complement]
        │  WEAK/PARTIAL: polyclonal effect in a minority of fractions, multi-antigen,
        │  via general GSK-3β/p38 node; no depletion/add-back, no monoclonal, no Fc/complement test,
        │  no intact-animal transfer (PMID:37450050, 42219531)
        ▼
[Accelerated remodeling, arrhythmia, progression]
        │  Prognostic association exists (death/transplant HR 4.56) but NOT with arrhythmia,
        │  and is confounded with injury severity (PMID:42233375)
        ▼
[Clinical ARVC phenotype]
```

**Strong links:** genotype → adhesion impairment → arrhythmia (structural/electrical). **Inferred links:** injury → cryptic-epitope exposure → antibody. **Missing/contested link:** antibody → *antigen-specific* injury with necessity/sufficiency. **Temporal direction (antecedent vs reactive) is untested** in humans.

## Alternative Models

1. **Injury-first / biomarker-only null (leading competitor, downstream consequence).** Desmosomal injury (necrosis, PMID:19635863) exposes DSG2/ICD epitopes → antibodies are a *reactive* readout. Explains: non-specificity vs myocarditis/DCM and post-COVID (39597880, 35764120), death/transplant (not arrhythmia) prognosis (42233375), and injury markers out-performing antibody in dogs (42160918). This is the hypothesis's own stated null and is currently well supported.
2. **Cell-intrinsic inflammatory model (parallel/upstream, PMID:42193878).** Variants activate cardiomyocyte NFκB/GSK-3β directly; inflammation precedes structure. Notably, this *also* explains the GSK-3β-dependent cohesion loss in PMID:42219531 **without** invoking antibody causality — the antibody may simply nudge a pathway that the genotype already drives.
3. **Innate-immune / inflammasome (DAMP, NLRP3) model (parallel/upstream, PMID:41448261, 34345905).** "Hot phases" with aseptic inflammation; in the **exact Dsg2-mutant mouse**, NF-κB/NLRP3/CD68 are elevated and anti-inflammatory therapy improves function/arrhythmia **without** targeting anti-DSG2 (PMID:34345905). Human immune-directed therapy signal is DSP-restricted and empirical (PMID:39786454, 39786662). Should be tested as an upstream/parallel branch, **not** assumed to feed a linear DAMP→anti-DSG2 cascade.
4. **Broad ICD-autoantigen / AIDA model (alternative to DSG2-centric view, PMID:39597880).** The clinically correlated signal in ARVC was AIDA (anti-intercalated-disc antibodies by IFL), not anti-DSG2 by ELISA — suggesting DSG2 is one of several ICD autoantigens and possibly not the functionally dominant one.
5. **Injury-dependent amplifier (the surviving pro-hypothesis variant).** Antibody is generated after injury but then worsens adhesion/coupling. Compatible with in-vitro data but unproven for *native, antigen-specific* antibody in vivo.

## Discriminating Tests

1. **Antigen-specific depletion/add-back (highest yield, lowest cost).** Deplete anti-DSG2 from patient IgG on immobilized DSG2-EC3/4; test cohesion/gap-junction in hiPSC-CMs before/after depletion and after monoclonal add-back. *Driver/amplifier predicts loss of effect on depletion and restoration on add-back; null predicts residual effect from remaining IgG (e.g., anti-N-cadherin).*
2. **Longitudinal antecedent cohort.** Serial anti-DSG2 + high-sensitivity troponin + CMR in genotype-positive/phenotype-negative relatives. *Driver predicts antibody rise before first injury; amplifier/null predicts antibody follows troponin/CMR change.*
3. **Head-to-head specificity study.** ARVC vs biopsy-proven myocarditis vs DCM vs sarcoid vs athlete, single harmonized assay, blinded pre-registered cutoff. *Resolves the 42233375 vs 39597880 comparator discrepancy.*
4. **Fc/complement dissection + passive transfer.** Intact IgG vs F(ab')₂, complement-depleted media, FcR-null cardiomyocytes; passive transfer of purified patient anti-DSG2 into WT and Dsg2/Pkp2-variant mice with telemetry/histology. *Establishes mechanism class and in-vivo necessity/sufficiency.*
5. **Pathway-epistasis test.** Compare anti-DSG2-induced cohesion loss vs genotype-driven cohesion loss under GSK-3β/p38 inhibition. *If both fully rescued by the same node, the antibody is acting through a shared downstream mechanism the genotype already engages — favoring parallel/epiphenomenon over independent driver.*
6. **Therapeutic dissection (antibody-removal vs innate-blockade).** In a phenotype-positive cohort or exact Dsg2-mutant model, compare antigen-specific immunoadsorption/B-cell depletion (removes anti-DSG2) against NF-κB/NLRP3 inhibition (leaves antibody intact). *If only innate blockade improves phenotype — as PMID:34345905 already suggests — the antibody is not the actionable driver; if antibody removal is required, the amplifier/driver model gains ground.*

## Limitations of this search

- Two pivotal papers (PMID:42233375, PMID:39597880) had no abstract in the PubMed tool; content was retrieved from NCBI efetch and PMC BioC full text and should be curator-verified against source.
- Search was literature-only (no primary data supplied); effect-size claims are as reported by source studies, not independently recomputed.
- Some competing-model support is review-level (PMID:42193878, 41448261, 42406223) and labeled as such; primary preclinical NFκB/injury data (e.g., PMID:19635863) anchor the injury-first branch.
