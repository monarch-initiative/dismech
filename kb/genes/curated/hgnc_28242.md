## Normal function

ai-gene-review has no completed review of HPDL, so the function stated here is
the one the KB records. HPDL is a mitochondrial enzyme that converts
4-hydroxyphenylpyruvate into 4-hydroxymandelate, an intermediate in the
synthesis of the coenzyme Q10 head group; it is not one of the COQ proteins of
the biosynthetic complex but supplies a precursor upstream of them.

## Causative records

The genetic record describes a spectrum from juvenile-onset pure spastic

infantile end has its own MONDO class, neurodevelopmental disorder with
progressive spasticity and brain white matter abnormalities, which no entry
curates yet; it is queued as a stub.

HPDL disease under hereditary spastic paraplegia, not under coenzyme Q10
deficiency, and that entry records the decision in its review notes.
