## Normal function

COX16 is a small inner-membrane assembly factor that binds newly made COX2 and recruits
the SCO1 metallochaperone to it, promoting COX2 metallation and the joining of the COX1
and COX2 modules. It is not a Complex IV subunit and has no cytochrome-c oxidase activity.
