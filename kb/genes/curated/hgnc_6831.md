## Normal function

MANBA encodes lysosomal beta-mannosidase, the exoglycosidase that removes the
single beta-1,4-linked mannose at the core of N-linked glycans, one of the last
steps of their lysosomal degradation. The KB binds it to beta-mannosidase
activity, marked decreased, on both of its nodes. ai-gene-review has no
completed review for the gene, so the ingest layer contributes
<span class="result" data-code="len(g.core_function_terms())">0<span class="method"></span></span>
