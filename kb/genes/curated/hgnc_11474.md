## Normal function

SURF1 is an inner-membrane assembly factor that acts on early, COX1-containing
intermediates of Complex IV. It is not a subunit of the mature enzyme and has no
cytochrome-c oxidase activity. ai-gene-review asserts no molecular function for it and
places it in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV assembly<span class="method"></span></span>,
as part of the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('in_complex')">mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV pre-assembly complex<span class="method"></span></span>.
