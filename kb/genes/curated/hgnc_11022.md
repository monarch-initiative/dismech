## Normal function

SLC35A2 encodes the Golgi UDP-galactose transporter, the nucleotide-sugar
transporter that moves UDP-galactose from the cytosol into the Golgi lumen
where galactosyltransferases use it; it is the only known Golgi transporter
for this donor. The KB binds UDP-galactose transmembrane transporter activity,
marked decreased. ai-gene-review has no completed review for the gene
(<span class="result" data-code="len(g.core_function_terms())">0<span class="method"></span></span>
