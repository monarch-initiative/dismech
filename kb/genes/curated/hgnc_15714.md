## Normal function

LRPPRC, with its partner SLIRP, binds mitochondrial mRNAs and stabilises them, promoting
their polyadenylation and translation. It is not part of Complex IV; its loss lowers the
mtDNA-encoded Complex IV subunits indirectly. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular
function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">mRNA binding<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">negative regulation of mitochondrial mRNA catabolic process; mitochondrial mRNA polyadenylation; and regulation of mitochondrial translation<span class="method"></span></span>.
