## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives NEU1's core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">exo-alpha-sialidase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">glycoprotein catabolic process; N-acetylneuraminate catabolic process; and oligosaccharide catabolic process<span class="method"></span></span>
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">lysosomal lumen; and lysosomal membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.
NEU1 is the lysosomal sialidase that removes terminal sialic acid from
sialylated glycoproteins and oligosaccharides, the first step in their
lysosomal degradation. It works in a complex with the protective
protein/cathepsin A (CTSA), which it needs for activity and stability.
