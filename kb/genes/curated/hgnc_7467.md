## Normal function

MTTP encodes the large subunit of the microsomal triglyceride transfer protein,
which works with protein disulfide isomerase in the lumen of the endoplasmic
reticulum of enterocytes and hepatocytes to load lipid onto nascent
apolipoprotein B. Vertebrate MTP transfers both phospholipids and
triacylglycerols. ai-gene-review has no completed review for the gene, so the
function layer on this page is empty
