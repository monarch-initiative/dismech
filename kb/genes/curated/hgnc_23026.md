## Normal function

GNPTG encodes the gamma subunit of GlcNAc-1-phosphotransferase, the
alpha2beta2gamma2 Golgi enzyme that adds the first GlcNAc-1-phosphate of the
mannose 6-phosphate tag to soluble acid hydrolases. The catalytic site lies in
the alpha/beta subunits encoded by GNPTAB; the gamma subunit is non-catalytic
and helps present the hydrolases' high-mannose glycans to it. The KB therefore
binds the complex's phosphotransferase activity on the GNPTG node, with a
preferred term saying that the gamma subunit enhances phosphorylation of a
subset of hydrolases. ai-gene-review has no completed review for the gene
(<span class="result" data-code="len(g.core_function_terms())">0<span class="method"></span></span>
terms).
