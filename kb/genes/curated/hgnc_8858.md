## Normal function

PEX3 is an integral peroxisomal membrane protein that acts as the docking
factor for PEX19. PEX19 binds newly made peroxisomal membrane proteins in the
cytosol, and PEX3 receives the loaded PEX19 at the membrane so that those
proteins can be inserted. It is one of the three peroxins, with PEX16 and
PEX19, without which no peroxisomal membrane forms at all. ai-gene-review
