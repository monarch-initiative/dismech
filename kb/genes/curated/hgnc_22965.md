## Normal function

PEX26 is a tail-anchored peroxisomal membrane protein and the membrane anchor of
the receptor export module. It binds PEX6 and through it recruits the PEX1-PEX6
AAA+ ATPase to the peroxisome, where that ATPase extracts the import receptor
PEX5 after each round of import. PEX26 has no ATPase activity of its own.
