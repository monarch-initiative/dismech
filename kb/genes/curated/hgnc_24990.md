## Normal function

COA3 (MITRAC12) is a small inner-membrane assembly factor that, with COX14, binds and
stabilises newly made COX1 and couples its synthesis to Complex IV assembly. It is not a
Complex IV subunit and has no cytochrome-c oxidase activity. ai-gene-review asserts no
molecular function and places it in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV assembly; and positive regulation of mitochondrial translation<span class="method"></span></span>.
