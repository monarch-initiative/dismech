## Normal function

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">FMN binding; [methionine synthase] reductase (NADPH) activity; and flavin adenine dinucleotide binding<span class="method"></span></span>.

cobalamin from the inactive cob(II)alamin state to the methylated form, which
keeps the enzyme working.
