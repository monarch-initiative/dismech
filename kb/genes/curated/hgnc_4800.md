## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives its core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">3-hydroxy-2-methylbutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase activity; (3S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase (NAD+) activity; tRNA binding<span class="method"></span></span>:
