## Normal function

PEX1 is one of the two AAA+ ATPases of the receptor export module. With PEX6 it
forms a heterohexamer, anchored to the peroxisomal membrane by PEX26, that uses
ATP hydrolysis to pull the monoubiquitinated import receptor PEX5 out of the
membrane so that it can carry another load of matrix protein. ai-gene-review
gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protein transporter activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">protein import into peroxisome matrix, receptor recycling<span class="method"></span></span>
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">peroxisomal membrane; and cytosol<span class="method"></span></span>.
