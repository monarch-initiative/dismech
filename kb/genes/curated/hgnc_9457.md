## Normal function

PLPBP, formerly PROSC, encodes the pyridoxal 5'-phosphate homeostasis protein
(PLPHP), a PLP-binding protein with no known enzymatic activity that is
thought to keep the reactive cofactor available to PLP-dependent enzymes while
holding free PLP low.
