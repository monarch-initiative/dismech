## Normal function

SCO1 is an inner-membrane copper metallochaperone that delivers copper to the
CuA site of MT-CO2. It is an assembly factor, not a Complex IV subunit, and has no
cytochrome-c oxidase activity. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">copper chaperone activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV assembly<span class="method"></span></span>.
