## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives MMADHC's core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">molecular carrier activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">cobalamin metabolic process<span class="method"></span></span>.
MMADHC is an adapter rather than an enzyme: its C-terminal domain forms a
complex with cobalamin-loaded MMACHC, engaging the cofactor through a
cobalt-sulfur bond, and the complex sends cobalamin either to methionine
synthase in the cytosol or to the mitochondrial adenosylcobalamin pathway.
