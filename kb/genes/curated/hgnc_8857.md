## Normal function

PEX16 is one of the three peroxins, with PEX3 and PEX19, that insert proteins
into the peroxisomal membrane. It is the membrane receptor for complexes of
PEX19 with newly made PEX3, and it works from the endoplasmic reticulum as well
as from peroxisomes. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">molecular adaptor activity<span class="method"></span></span>,

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">peroxisomal membrane; and endoplasmic reticulum membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.
