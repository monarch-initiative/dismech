## Normal function

PEX13 is the membrane peroxin of the docking and translocation module through
which cargo-loaded PEX5 enters the peroxisome. Its SH3 domain binds the WxxxF/Y
motifs of PEX5, a motif just after it binds PEX14, and its tyrosine- and
glycine-rich YG domain forms a selective phase in the membrane through which
PEX5 carries folded cargo. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protein-macromolecule adaptor activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
contributing to
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('contributes_to_molecular_function')">protein transmembrane transporter activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
and acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">protein import into peroxisome matrix; protein import into peroxisome matrix, docking; and protein import into peroxisome matrix, translocation<span class="method"></span></span>.
