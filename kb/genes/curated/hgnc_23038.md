## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives LMBRD1 no core molecular function, only processes:
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">cobalamin transport; and protein localization to lysosome<span class="method"></span></span>,
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">lysosomal membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.
That absence is informative. The gene's product, LMBD1, was first proposed as
the lysosomal cobalamin exporter, but reconstituted in liposomes it shows no
transport activity; its job is to bring the exporter ABCD4 from the endoplasmic
reticulum to the lysosome.
