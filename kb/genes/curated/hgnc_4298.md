## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives GLB1's core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">beta-galactosidase activity; and extracellular matrix binding<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">ganglioside catabolic process; glycoprotein catabolic process; and keratan sulfate proteoglycan catabolic process<span class="method"></span></span>

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">lysosomal lumen<span class="method"></span></span>.
The review attributes the two activities to two products of the gene: the
lysosomal enzyme that removes terminal beta-galactose from glycoconjugates,
and a catalytically inactive splice product that binds elastin and laminin.
The KB models only the first. Of the processes, ganglioside and keratan
sulfate catabolism correspond to the two allelic disease groups, GM1 ganglioside
storage and keratan sulfate storage.
