## Normal function

COA8 (formerly APOPT1) is a matrix-side inner-membrane factor needed for Complex IV
assembly and stability; it is not a Complex IV subunit and has no cytochrome-c oxidase
activity. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">enzyme binding<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV assembly; and protein stabilization<span class="method"></span></span>.
