## Normal function

Despite its HGNC name, the KB does not model MAGT1 as a magnesium transporter.
ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">disulfide oxidoreductase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
contributing to
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('contributes_to_molecular_function')">dolichyl-diphosphooligosaccharide-protein glycotransferase activity<span class="method"></span></span>
in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('in_complex')">oligosaccharyltransferase complex B<span class="method"></span></span>.
MAGT1 is an ER-lumenal thioredoxin homologue in the STT3B form of the
oligosaccharyltransferase; its active site is required for STT3B to
glycosylate acceptor sites that are bracketed by disulfides or contain
cysteine.
