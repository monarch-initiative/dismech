## Normal function

TACO1 was named a translational activator of COX1, but the KB records it as a
mitochondrial translation elongation factor, the counterpart of bacterial EF-P and
eukaryotic eIF5A, that relieves mitoribosome stalling at polyproline stretches; the
polyproline-rich COX1 and COX3 depend on it. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular
function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">mitochondrial ribosome binding<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">positive regulation of mitochondrial translation; mitochondrial translation; and mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV assembly<span class="method"></span></span>.
