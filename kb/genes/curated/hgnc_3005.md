## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives DPM1's core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">dolichyl-phosphate beta-D-mannosyltransferase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">dolichol phosphate mannose biosynthetic process<span class="method"></span></span>
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">endoplasmic reticulum membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.
DPM1 is the catalytic subunit of dolichol-phosphate-mannose (Dol-P-Man)
synthase, which transfers mannose from GDP-mannose to dolichyl phosphate. It
has no transmembrane domain of its own and is held on the endoplasmic
reticulum membrane by DPM3, while DPM2 raises the complex's activity.
Dol-P-Man is the mannose donor for protein O-mannosylation, N-glycan precursor
extension and GPI-anchor synthesis.
