## Normal function

A transfer RNA has no protein product, so ai-gene-review carries no core
functions for it, and the KB does not yet place MT-TP on a mechanism node.

## How the KB records it

MERRF Syndrome records MT-TP with relationship type
<span class="result" data-code="g.relationship('MERRF Syndrome')">unknown<span class="method"></span></span>
and variant origin
<span class="result" data-code="g.variant_origin('MERRF Syndrome')">germline<span class="method"></span></span>:
GeneReviews lists MT-TP among the mitochondrial tRNA genes described in a
subset of MERRF, but the entry cites no MT-TP MERRF case. Most reported MT-TP
variants present as mitochondrial myopathy or progressive external
ophthalmoplegia, neither of which has a dismech entry yet.

## Where the layers disagree

ClinGen classifies MT-TP for
<span class="result" data-code="g.clingen_diseases()">1<span class="method"></span></span>
disease, the lumped mitochondrial disease class, at the Definitive tier; that
class is broader than MERRF, so the tier is not copied onto the entry.
