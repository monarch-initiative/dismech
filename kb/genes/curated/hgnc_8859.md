## Normal function

PEX6 is the second AAA+ ATPase of the receptor export module. It forms an
alternating heterohexamer with PEX1, is recruited to the peroxisomal membrane
by PEX26, and uses ATP hydrolysis to extract monoubiquitinated PEX5 from the
membrane after cargo delivery. ai-gene-review records
<span class="result" data-code="len(g.core_function_terms())">0<span class="method"></span></span>
