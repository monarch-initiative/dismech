## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives MMACHC's core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">FAD binding; cobalamin binding; cyanocobalamin reductase (cyanide-eliminating) (NADP+) activity; glutathione binding; and transferase activity, transferring alkyl or aryl (other than methyl) groups<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">cobalamin metabolic process<span class="method"></span></span>.
MMACHC is the cytosolic chaperone that receives every incoming form of
cobalamin and strips its upper ligand: it removes cyanide from cyanocobalamin
by reductive decyanation, and the methyl or adenosyl group from the
alkylcobalamins by a glutathione-dependent transfer, so that one common
intermediate can be rebuilt into methylcobalamin for methionine synthase and
adenosylcobalamin for methylmalonyl-CoA mutase.
