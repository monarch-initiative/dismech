## Normal function

PEX14 is the docking peroxin of the peroxisomal membrane. Its N-terminal
domain binds the WxxxF/Y motifs of the import receptor PEX5, and it also binds
PEX19, so it is where cargo-loaded PEX5 first engages the membrane before
PEX13 lets it across. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular
functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protein-macromolecule adaptor activity; and beta-tubulin binding<span class="method"></span></span>,

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">protein import into peroxisome matrix; and peroxisome transport along microtubule<span class="method"></span></span>.
