## Normal function

PCSK9 is a secreted hepatocyte protein that binds the LDL receptor and diverts
it to lysosomal degradation. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular functions
as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">low-density lipoprotein particle receptor binding; and serine-type endopeptidase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">negative regulation of low-density lipoprotein particle clearance; positive regulation of low-density lipoprotein particle receptor catabolic process; and protein autoprocessing<span class="method"></span></span>,
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">cell surface; endoplasmic reticulum; endosome; and extracellular region<span class="method"></span></span>
(<span class="result" data-code="len(g.core_function_terms())">10<span class="method"></span></span>
terms in all). The endopeptidase activity is spent on the protein's own
prodomain in the endoplasmic reticulum, which stays bound as an inhibitor, so
the secreted form acts on the receptor by binding rather than by cleavage.
