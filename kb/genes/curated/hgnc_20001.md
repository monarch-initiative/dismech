## Normal function

PCSK9 is a secreted hepatocyte protein that binds the LDL receptor and diverts
it to lysosomal degradation. The ai-gene-review record for PCSK9 is being
completed upstream, and the pinned ingest snapshot does not yet carry its core
functions, so this page makes no claim about them.
