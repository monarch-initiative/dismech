## Normal function

COX14 (formerly C12orf62) is a small inner-membrane assembly factor that binds newly
made COX1 in the ribosome nascent-chain complex and couples COX1 synthesis to Complex IV
assembly. It is not a Complex IV subunit and has no cytochrome-c oxidase activity.
