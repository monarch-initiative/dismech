## Drug-target record

Primary Hyperoxaluria Type 1 names HAO1 only through a treatment, and records
<span class="result" data-code="g.relationship('Primary Hyperoxaluria Type 1')">no genetic record<span class="method"></span></span>
for it: the gene is the target of
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.treatments()">Lumasiran<span class="method"></span></span>,

gene. The glycolate oxidase deficiency entry is what makes that strategy
plausible: its evidence includes a healthy adult with a lifelong homozygous
HAO1 knockout and raised plasma and urinary glycolate.
