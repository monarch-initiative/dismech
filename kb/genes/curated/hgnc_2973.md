## Normal function

DNM1L encodes dynamin-related protein 1 (DRP1), the cytosolic dynamin-family
GTPase that receptors such as MFF recruit to the outer membrane of mitochondria
and peroxisomes. There it polymerises into a filament and uses GTP hydrolysis
to constrict into rings that divide the organelle. The KB records this activity
as GTPase activity; ai-gene-review has no ingested review of the gene yet.

## Where the layers disagree

ClinGen classifies DNM1L as Definitive for encephalopathy due to mitochondrial
and peroxisomal fission defect, the parent class that holds both fission
encephalopathies, and as Limited for Leigh syndrome. Neither is the entry's own
disease term, so the entry records no tier and notes the assertion instead. The
allelic DNM1L optic atrophy, optic atrophy 5, has no entry yet and is queued as
a stub.
