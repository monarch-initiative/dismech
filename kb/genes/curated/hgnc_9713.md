## Normal function

PEX19 is the predominantly cytosolic chaperone and import receptor for
peroxisomal membrane proteins. It binds their membrane targeting signals as
they are made, keeps them soluble, and delivers them to PEX3 at the
peroxisomal membrane. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protein carrier chaperone; and peroxisome signal sequence receptor activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">protein import into peroxisome membrane; peroxisome membrane biogenesis; and protein targeting to peroxisome<span class="method"></span></span>.
