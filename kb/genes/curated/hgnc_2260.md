## Normal function

COX10 is heme O synthase: it farnesylates protoheme (heme B) to heme O, the first step
of heme A synthesis, which COX15 completes. Heme A is the cofactor of the COX1 catalytic
centre, so COX10 is a biosynthetic assembly factor rather than a Complex IV subunit.

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protoheme IX farnesyltransferase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">heme A biosynthetic process<span class="method"></span></span>.
