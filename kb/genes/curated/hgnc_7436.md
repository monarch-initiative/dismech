**<span class="result" data-code="g.symbol()">MTHFR<span class="method"></span></span>**
(<span class="result" data-code="g.name()">methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase<span class="method"></span></span>)
is named by several disorder entries in two quite different roles: as the
cause of a rare recessive homocystinuria, and as the common thermolabile
c.677C>T variant that modifies or predisposes to other conditions.

## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives MTHFR's core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">flavin adenine dinucleotide binding; and methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase (NADPH) activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">L-methionine biosynthetic process; and tetrahydrofolate interconversion<span class="method"></span></span>.
MTHFR reduces 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate to 5-methyltetrahydrofolate, the
methyl donor for remethylation of homocysteine to methionine. The KB's MTHFR
nodes bind the same NADPH-dependent reductase activity as decreased.

## Susceptibility and modifier records

The common variant is typed as susceptibility in entries including
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.includes('Anencephaly', 'Spina Bifida Cystica', 'Migraine with aura', 'Methotrexate Toxicity', 'Fetal Valproate Syndrome', relationship='susceptibility')">Anencephaly; Spina Bifida Cystica; Migraine with aura; Methotrexate Toxicity; and Fetal Valproate Syndrome<span class="method"></span></span>.
In Fetal Valproate Syndrome the effect sits in the mother's genome rather
than the child's. Ariboflavinosis types MTHFR as a
<span class="result" data-code="g.relationship('Ariboflavinosis')">modifier<span class="method"></span></span>:
the thermolabile enzyme loses its FAD cofactor more easily, so low riboflavin
status impairs one-carbon metabolism mainly in TT homozygotes, on the node
<span class="result" data-code="g.node('Ariboflavinosis', 'Reduced MTHFR Activity')">Reduced MTHFR Activity<span class="method"></span></span>.
In Anencephaly and Methotrexate Toxicity the gene sits on
<span class="result" data-code="g.node('Anencephaly', 'Disrupted Folate One-Carbon Metabolism')">Disrupted Folate One-Carbon Metabolism<span class="method"></span></span>

<span class="result" data-code="g.node('Methotrexate Toxicity', 'Tetrahydrofolate Depletion')">Tetrahydrofolate Depletion<span class="method"></span></span>
