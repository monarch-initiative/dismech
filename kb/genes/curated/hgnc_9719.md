## Normal function

PEX5 is the cycling cytosolic receptor for matrix proteins carrying the type-1
peroxisomal targeting signal (PTS1); its long isoform, PEX5L, is also the
co-receptor that carries PEX7-bound PTS2 cargo. ai-gene-review gives its core
molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">peroxisome signal sequence receptor activity; and protein carrier activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">protein import into peroxisome matrix; protein import into peroxisome matrix, docking; and protein import into peroxisome matrix, receptor recycling<span class="method"></span></span>,
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">cytosol; and peroxisomal membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.
The three process terms are the successive steps of one cycle the review
describes: cargo recognition in the cytosol, docking at the PEX13/PEX14
complex, and extraction back to the cytosol for another round.
