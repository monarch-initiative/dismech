## Normal function

PEX5 is the cycling cytosolic receptor for matrix proteins carrying the type-1
peroxisomal targeting signal (PTS1); its long isoform, PEX5L, is also the
co-receptor that carries PEX7-bound PTS2 cargo. The ai-gene-review record in the
pinned ingest has no core functions yet; the upstream review has since been
completed, and its core functions will appear here once the ingest is refreshed.
