## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives AIFM1's core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">DNA binding; NADH dehydrogenase activity; protein-membrane adaptor activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">mitochondrial disulfide relay system; positive regulation of apoptotic process<span class="method"></span></span>.
In healthy cells AIF is an FAD-dependent NADH oxidoreductase anchored in the
mitochondrial intermembrane space, where it holds the CHCHD4 import receptor
of the disulfide relay at the inner membrane; the DNA-binding, pro-apoptotic
function belongs to AIF released into the nucleus.
