## Normal function

A transfer RNA has no protein product, so ai-gene-review carries no core
functions for it. The disease entry's mechanism node binds the molecular
function GO gives a transfer RNA, triplet codon-amino acid adaptor activity, as
decreased.

## Where the layers disagree

ClinGen classifies MT-TL2 for
<span class="result" data-code="g.clingen_diseases()">2<span class="method"></span></span>
diseases: Definitive for the lumped mitochondrial disease class, and Disputed
for Leigh syndrome, which the Leigh Syndrome entry agrees with by not naming
the gene. Most reported MT-TL2 variants cause progressive external
ophthalmoplegia or myopathy, which have no dismech entry yet.
