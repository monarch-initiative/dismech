## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives DPM3's core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protein-membrane adaptor activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">dolichol phosphate mannose biosynthetic process<span class="method"></span></span>

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">endoplasmic reticulum membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.

whose coiled-coil domain tethers the catalytic DPM1 subunit to the endoplasmic

complex has no activity.
