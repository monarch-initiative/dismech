## Normal function

PHYH is the iron- and 2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenase that starts the
alpha-oxidation of phytanic acid, the 3-methyl-branched fatty acid that cannot
be beta-oxidised directly. It is a peroxisomal matrix enzyme imported by the
PTS2 receptor PEX7, which is why PHYH and PEX7 defects converge on the same
disease. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">phytanoyl-CoA dioxygenase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">fatty acid alpha-oxidation<span class="method"></span></span>
in the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">peroxisomal matrix<span class="method"></span></span>.
