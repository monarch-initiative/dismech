## Normal function

ai-gene-review gives OPA1's core molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">GTPase-dependent fusogenic activity; membrane bending activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
acting in
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">cristae formation; mitochondrial fusion; mitochondrial inner membrane fusion<span class="method"></span></span>
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">mitochondrial crista; mitochondrial inner membrane<span class="method"></span></span>.
OPA1 is the dynamin-like GTPase of the inner mitochondrial membrane: it fuses
inner membranes and shapes cristae.

## Causative records

All five entries type OPA1 as causative with a germline origin
(<span class="result" data-code="len(g.disorders('causative', 'germline'))">5<span class="method"></span></span>),
and between them they cover a dose-dependent allelic series.

- Autosomal Dominant Optic Atrophy places the gene on
  <span class="result" data-code="g.node('Autosomal Dominant Optic Atrophy', 'OPA1 Haploinsufficiency')">OPA1 Haploinsufficiency<span class="method"></span></span>,
  with a <span class="result" data-code="g.functional_impact('Autosomal Dominant Optic Atrophy')">loss of function<span class="method"></span></span>
  impact; that node binds the fusogenic GTPase activity as decreased.
- Autosomal Dominant Optic Atrophy Plus, the syndromic dominant form, places
  it on
  <span class="result" data-code="g.node('Autosomal Dominant Optic Atrophy Plus', 'OPA1 Loss of Function and Impaired Mitochondrial Fusion')">OPA1 Loss of Function and Impaired Mitochondrial Fusion<span class="method"></span></span>

  <span class="result" data-code="g.node('Autosomal Dominant Optic Atrophy Plus', 'Mitochondrial DNA Instability and Secondary mtDNA Deletions')">Mitochondrial DNA Instability and Secondary mtDNA Deletions<span class="method"></span></span>,
  which it shares with MFN2.
- Behr Syndrome records the biallelic form, with the gene on
  <span class="result" data-code="g.node('Behr Syndrome', 'Biallelic OPA1 Variants')">Biallelic OPA1 Variants<span class="method"></span></span>
  and on the splicing and protein-abundance nodes that follow it.
- Mitochondrial DNA Depletion Syndrome 14B (Cardioencephalomyopathic Type) records the homozygous extreme on
  <span class="result" data-code="g.node('Mitochondrial DNA Depletion Syndrome 14B (Cardioencephalomyopathic Type)', 'Destabilization of the OPA1 Dynamin-like GTPase')">Destabilization of the OPA1 Dynamin-like GTPase<span class="method"></span></span>.
- Auditory Neuropathy names OPA1 on its shared
  <span class="result" data-code="g.node('Auditory Neuropathy', 'Auditory Nerve Dyssynchrony')">Auditory Nerve Dyssynchrony<span class="method"></span></span>

Every OPA1 node that binds an activity binds a GTPase term; only the
haploinsufficiency node uses the specific fusogenic term the review gives, and
the others use the generic GTPase activity. Two entries record investigational
treatments aimed at OPA1

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.shared_processes()">cristae formation; mitochondrial fusion<span class="method"></span></span>

ClinGen classifies OPA1 for
<span class="result" data-code="g.clingen_diseases()">2<span class="method"></span></span>

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.clingen_without_entry()">OPA1-related optic atrophy with or without extraocular features<span class="method"></span></span>,
