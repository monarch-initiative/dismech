## Normal function

MAN2B1 encodes lysosomal acid alpha-mannosidase, the class II exoglycosidase
that removes alpha-1,2-, alpha-1,3- and alpha-1,6-linked mannose residues during
the stepwise lysosomal degradation of N-linked glycans. The KB binds it to
alpha-mannosidase activity, marked decreased, on both of its nodes.
