the cell by clathrin-mediated endocytosis. ai-gene-review gives its core
molecular functions as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">low-density lipoprotein particle receptor activity; and very-low-density lipoprotein particle receptor activity<span class="method"></span></span>,

<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('directly_involved_in')">low-density lipoprotein particle clearance; plasma lipoprotein particle clearance; and receptor-mediated endocytosis involved in cholesterol transport<span class="method"></span></span>,
at the
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('locations')">clathrin-coated pit; early endosome; and plasma membrane<span class="method"></span></span>
(<span class="result" data-code="len(g.core_function_terms())">8<span class="method"></span></span>

from the surface through the coated pit to the endosome where the ligand is
released.
