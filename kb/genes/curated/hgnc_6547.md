## Normal function

LDLR is the hepatocyte receptor that binds apoB-100 on LDL and carries it into
the cell by clathrin-mediated endocytosis. The ai-gene-review record for LDLR
is being completed upstream, and the pinned ingest snapshot does not yet carry
its core functions, so this page makes no claim about them.
