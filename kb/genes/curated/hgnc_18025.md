COA6 is an intermembrane-space thiol-disulfide oxidoreductase of the copper delivery
pathway to the CuA site of COX2. It is an assembly factor, not a Complex IV subunit, and has
no cytochrome-c oxidase activity. ai-gene-review gives its core molecular function as
<span class="result" data-compare="names" data-code="g.core_function_terms('molecular_function')">protein-disulfide reductase activity<span class="method"></span></span>,
