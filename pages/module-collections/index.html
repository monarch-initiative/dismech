Hallmarks of Aging

Mechanisms of the Epilepsies

The mechanism modules that upstream epilepsy entries reach on their way to recurrent seizures. Epilepsy is not one mechanism: distinct lesions in inhibitory-interneuron development, presynaptic vesicle release, postsynaptic scaffolding, cortical growth signaling, and repeat-RNA toxicity each drive a different route to the same endpoint, and each has its own treatment logic. The collection groups those upstream lanes together with the shared excitation-inhibition convergence module they feed. Membership records that a module is an evidenced route to seizures or to excitation-inhibition imbalance in at least one curated DisMech entry; it does not claim the lanes are exhaustive or mutually exclusive, and a single disorder commonly conforms to the convergence module plus one lane.
