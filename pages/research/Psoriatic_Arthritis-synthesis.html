Narrative

Overview

All three providers frame psoriatic arthritis as a complex, immune-mediated inflammatory musculoskeletal disease of psoriatic disease, converging on a genetic-susceptibility-plus-environmental-trigger model in which dysregulated innate and adaptive immunity target the synovium, the enthesis, the skin, and bone. Falcon and perplexity both used the mechanism-focused pathophysiology template and produced narrative, mechanistic accounts; asta is a retrieval-only corpus dump whose 19 papers and 20 snippets supply supporting evidence rather than a synthesized argument. Falcon organizes the disease around an enthesis-initiated, cytokine-amplified process integrated through JAK/STAT signaling; perplexity is the broadest and most granular, walking through genetics, the IL-23/IL-17 axis, TNF, JAK/STAT, individual innate and adaptive cell populations, bone remodeling, the enthesis, metabolism, epigenetics, angiogenesis, and clinical phenotypes; asta's retrieved literature adds histopathology, cytokine profiling, and a strong cardiometabolic-comorbidity thread.

Agreement

The providers agree on the core mechanistic backbone. The IL-23/IL-17 axis is treated as the dominant/central inflammatory pathway, amplified by TNF, with Th17 and innate-like cells producing IL-17 that acts on stromal and skeletal cells. Enthesitis and the synovio-entheseal complex are recognized as a primary/initiating lesion driven in part by mechanical stress. The paradoxical bone phenotype — simultaneous osteoclast-driven erosion and new bone formation via RANKL and Wnt-antagonist (DKK1/sclerostin) modulation — is a shared, PsA-distinguishing feature. Genetic susceptibility is anchored in HLA class I / antigen processing (notably HLA-B27) plus non-MHC IL-23-pathway loci (IL23R, IL12B, TYK2). JAK/STAT is described as a cytokine convergence hub and validated drug target, and TNF-alpha as a central destructive driver and anti-TNF target.

Divergence

Divergence is coverage-driven, not contradictory — no direct conflicts were found. Falcon uniquely commits to the enthesitis-first (GRAPPA) framing and the JAK2/TYK2/STAT3 unifying-hub argument, and it is the only provider foregrounding the DKK1/SOST Wnt-antagonist bone axis by name, but it is silent on the ~30% psoriasis-to-PsA epidemiology and on comorbidity. Perplexity contributes by far the most detail and several elements the others omit entirely — CD8+ tissue-resident memory T cells with shared skin/joint clones, metabolic reprogramming/glycolysis, epigenetics, angiogenesis, complement, Toll-like receptors, and quantified clinical phenotypes (oligo/polyarticular, dactylitis, axial). Asta's distinctive value is the retrieved evidence base: the ~30% conversion figure, the cardiometabolic comorbidity burden, and synovial histopathology (IL-17+ mast cells increased in PsA vs RA). As a retrieval-only provider, asta supports mechanistic claims only partially, earning mostly PARTIAL stances where falcon/perplexity are CONCORDANT.

Integration

The concordant mechanistic backbone is ready for promotion into the disorder YAML: the IL-23/IL-17 axis as the central pathway, enthesitis / synovio-entheseal complex as an initiating lesion, the RANKL-erosion / Wnt-DKK1/sclerostin new-bone paradox, HLA class I (HLA-B27) plus IL23R/IL12B/TYK2 genetics, the JAK/STAT convergence hub with JAK/TYK2 inhibitors, and TNF-alpha with anti-TNF therapy. The ~30% psoriasis-to-PsA epidemiology (perplexity + asta) is a clean, two-provider prevalence claim suitable for the prevalence/description block. Each promoted claim should be re-grounded on verified primary literature (fetch-reference on the underlying PMIDs/DOIs) before it enters kb/disorders.

Not integrated (leads)

Two findings are retained as leads rather than promoted. The cardiometabolic comorbidity burden (finding 8) rests on a single provider (asta) and belongs in a comorbidity entry with its own verified evidence rather than in the core pathophysiology. The mast-cell finding (finding 9) — IL-17+ mast cells enriched in PsA synovium versus RA — is supported by asta and perplexity but is a finer-grained cellular claim best held as a lead pending confirmation of the synovial-biopsy evidence. Perplexity-only threads (CD8+ Trm skin-joint clonal sharing, glycolytic reprogramming, epigenetics, angiogenesis, complement, TLRs) were not elevated to harmonized findings and remain research leads.
