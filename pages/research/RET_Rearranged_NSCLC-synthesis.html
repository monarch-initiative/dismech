Narrative

Overview

Both providers frame RET-rearranged NSCLC as a rare (~1-2%) but highly actionable fusion-driven lung adenocarcinoma subtype, enriched in younger, female, never/light-smoking patients, with constitutive RET kinase signaling, selective RET tyrosine kinase inhibition (selpercatinib, pralsetinib), frequent CNS metastasis, an immune-cold microenvironment, and acquired resistance as the major curated concepts.

Agreement

Both identify RET-rearranged NSCLC as a fusion-driven adenocarcinoma with KIF5B-RET as the dominant partner (followed by CCDC6 and NCOA4), constitutive ligand-independent RET signaling through RAS-MAPK, PI3K-AKT, JAK-STAT, and PLCgamma, and strong first-line benefit from selpercatinib (LIBRETTO-431 PFS 24.8 vs 11.2 months). They converge on the immune-cold/low-TMB microenvironment with limited immunotherapy benefit, the CNS-metastasis propensity, and resistance via on-target RET G810 solvent-front mutations plus MET/bypass signaling.

Divergence

Falcon emphasized the selective RET inhibitor treatment landscape, diagnostic modality comparison (RNA-based NGS preferred), first-line selpercatinib and real-world RET-TKI cohorts, and a KIF5B-RET transgenic mouse model. OpenScientist added deeper molecular and resistance detail: RET protein-domain and structural biology, co-occurring TP53/CDKN2A alterations, an expanded bypass-resistance catalog (MET/KRAS amplification, AXL/IGF-1R, SMARCA4 loss), next-generation RET inhibitors, and the physiologic role of wild-type RET. Quantitative differences are minor (median age 63 vs 58 years; baseline brain-metastasis fraction ~25% vs 6.7%) rather than contradictions.

Integration

Integrated into kb/disorders/RET_Rearranged_NSCLC.yaml: the driver mechanism and fusion-partner spectrum, the multi-pathway effector signaling, the epidemiology/demographic enrichment, the CNS-metastasis phenotype, the immune-cold microenvironment and weak immunotherapy signal, the RET inhibitor resistance node (G810R/S/C plus MET/KRAS bypass), and the first-line selpercatinib LIBRETTO-431 evidence.

Not integrated (leads)

Next-generation investigational RET inhibitors, experimental RET/SRC and RET/MET combination strategies, fine-grained adverse-event management, and the contextual normal-RET developmental biology were retained as research leads rather than promoted to curated disease mechanisms.
