Narrative

Overview

This is a single-provider synthesis by necessity rather than by design. Of three providers attempted, only claude_code produced usable output. Its mechanistic account of Mitchell-Riley syndrome is accurate and well structured, and it correctly separates the two arms of RFX6 biology — the endoderm-patterning/islet-developmental arm and the enteroendocrine arm with its congenital GLP-1 deficiency — which is the organizing insight the curated pathograph adopts.

Agreement

Not assessable. Cross-provider agreement requires at least two usable reports; falcon produced none and asta produced an off-topic one.

Divergence

Also not assessable in the intended sense. The only divergence worth recording is between the report and the primary literature it cites: the report's mechanistic conclusions were consistently reproducible against fetched abstracts, but its bibliographic layer was not. Two citation defects are recorded under the claude_code provider's identifier_issues, and the report's own closing section volunteers that several PMIDs were unverified. The practical lesson is that a report can be scientifically reliable and bibliographically unreliable at the same time, which is exactly the case the verify-every-identifier rule exists for.

Integration

Eight findings were promoted to kb/disorders/Mitchell-Riley_Syndrome.yaml, covering the RFX6 loss-of-function trigger, primitive-gut-tube endoderm patterning, the NEUROG3-downstream islet block, the pancreatic-endoderm specification failure, beta-cell insulin and calcium-channel maintenance, the enteroendocrine/GLP-1 arm, liraglutide, and the allelic series. Every one was re-anchored to an independently fetched abstract; no evidence snippet was taken from the report text.

Not integrated (leads)

Three findings were retained as leads: the Finnish founder allele, which belongs to the heterozygous MODY entity rather than to this syndrome; exocrine pancreatic insufficiency, which is plausible but uncited in the report and absent from the HPO annotation set; and the 3-of-4 cholestasis fraction with its liver-transplant detail, which is not in the cited abstract. The mouse enterochromaffin/serotonin expansion was integrated only as a HUMAN_MODEL_MISMATCH discussion, not as asserted human mechanism.

Off-pattern in one respect: all 59 other syntheses in research/ compare two or more providers, and the ProviderReport class was designed around that. This one declares three providers of which two produced nothing usable, so the agreement/divergence narrative slots are largely empty by circumstance. It is recorded anyway because the provenance is worth keeping — a later reader should be able to see that falcon was attempted and failed on credit, not that it was never tried.
