Narrative

Overview

All three providers converge on the same disorder identity: SEDC is an autosomal dominant type II collagenopathy caused by heterozygous COL2A1 variants that disrupt the alpha-1(II) chain of hyaline-cartilage collagen. They differ sharply in method and depth. Falcon (Edison) is a focused pathophysiology synthesis built around the intracellular mechanism — misfolded procollagen-II, ER retention, and the ER-stress / UPR / apoptosis cascade — and it deliberately foregrounds a recent conceptual tension in that cascade. Perplexity (sonar-deep-research) is the most comprehensive: it walks the full arc from gene structure and mutation classes through cellular ER stress, growth-plate and endochondral-ossification failure, matrix biology, and the multisystem (skeletal, ocular, auditory, cervical-spine, respiratory) clinical course. Asta is retrieval-only — a ranked list of papers and snippets with no second-stage synthesis — so it frames SEDC mainly through the broader, genetically heterogeneous spondyloepiphyseal-dysplasia spectrum rather than through a single mechanistic narrative.

Agreement

The three reports agree strongly on the disease's genetic and clinical core. COL2A1 is the causal gene and inheritance is autosomal dominant (all three); glycine substitution within the Gly-X-Y triple-helical repeat is the predominant mutation class, which perplexity quantifies (~74%) and asta and falcon corroborate qualitatively. The clinical picture is consistent across providers: disproportionate short-trunk short stature, platyspondyly, and epiphyseal dysplasia, with frequent extraskeletal involvement (myopia, retinal detachment, sensorineural hearing loss, cleft palate). Falcon and perplexity additionally agree on the same rare-disease prevalence (~3.4 per million) and, at the mechanistic level, on ER retention of misfolded procollagen as the central cellular defect and on a defective, sparse, thin-fibril extracellular matrix.

Divergence

The genuine conflict is over ER-stress signaling. Falcon foregrounds the Yammine human iPSC-derived cartilage model of COL2A1 p.Gly1170Ser, which shows ER procollagen storage WITHOUT a coupled canonical UPR — and without increased apoptosis — in clinically relevant heterozygotes, and it explicitly argues that UPR readouts are not reliable proxies for collagen-misfolding burden. Perplexity, drawing largely on the R992C transgenic mouse, presents canonical UPR activation (IRE1a/PERK/ATF6, CHOP-driven apoptosis) as the central, established mechanism and does not acknowledge the storage-without-UPR result. These stances are recorded as CONTRADICTORY (falcon CONCORDANT vs. perplexity CONTRADICTORY on the reframing). Part of the tension is that the two lean on different model systems (iPSC heterozygote vs. mouse) and allele dosages. Beyond that conflict, divergence is mostly coverage: perplexity uniquely details the dominant-negative vs. haploinsufficiency dichotomy, the DDRGK1/UFMylation ER-homeostasis axis, and the late clinical course (early-onset osteoarthritis, ~40-year total hip arthroplasty, cervical myelopathy risk); falcon uniquely surfaces the iPSC "ER storage disorder" reframing; and asta uniquely maps the heterogeneous SED spectrum and its other causal genes (SEDL/TRAPPC2, TRPV4, MBTPS1, MGP) as a differential frame. Asta is otherwise SILENT on the intracellular mechanism, having not retrieved the mouse/iPSC mechanistic papers.

Integration

Promote the tri-provider consensus into the disorder YAML: the COL2A1 autosomal-dominant type II collagenopathy etiology, the predominant glycine-substitution / dominant-negative mutation mechanism, the misfolded-procollagen ER-retention cellular defect, the ER-stress-linked chondrocyte apoptosis and growth-plate / endochondral-ossification failure (as a MODEL_ORGANISM-supported node, given its mouse basis), the defective sparse-fibril cartilage ECM, the short-trunk/platyspondyly/epiphyseal plus extraskeletal phenotype set, and the ~3.4 per million prevalence. These are well corroborated and ready for evidence-backed curation once the underlying PMIDs/DOIs are fetch-verified.

Not integrated (leads)

Retain as leads rather than promoting outright: (1) the model-dependent UPR question — whether clinically relevant heterozygous SEDC involves canonical UPR activation (mouse) or ER storage without a coupled UPR (human iPSC) — is an unresolved human-model-fidelity tension best captured as a knowledge-gap / HUMAN_MODEL_MISMATCH lead rather than asserted as settled mechanism; and (2) perplexity's DDRGK1/UFMylation-stabilized IRE1a axis and its implied therapeutic angle, which is a single-provider mechanistic lead not yet corroborated here. Both should await primary-literature verification before entering the disorder YAML.
