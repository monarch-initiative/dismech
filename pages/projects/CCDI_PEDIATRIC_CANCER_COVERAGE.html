CCDI and WHO Pediatric Cancer Coverage

Purpose

The NCI Childhood Cancer Data Initiative (CCDI) codes pediatric, adolescent and young adult (AYA) cancer diagnoses in a flat category list and a long value list, both of which are hard to compute over. The UNC submission to the CCDI Innovation Challenge, Track 1 ("Computable Pediatric, Adolescent, and Young Adult Cancer Categories for CCDI"), proposes reconciling those vocabularies through Mondo and, in its Aim 4, linking each pediatric Mondo class to its dismech record: the driver lesion, the cell of origin, and the GO processes the lesion disrupts. That mechanism layer is only as good as dismech's coverage of the pediatric entities, so this project measures that coverage against the vocabularies the proposal names and tracks the work to close the gaps.

This project is the coverage-and-alignment companion to CHILDHOOD_CANCER.md, which covers the wider CCDI and MCI integration strategy, and NEUROBLASTOMA.md, which covers one tumour family in depth.

Findings that affect the mechanism layer

  1. The SMARCB1 pooling example has no cell of origin to pool on. Rhabdoid Tumor and Atypical Teratoid Rhabdoid Tumor describe SMARCB1 loss in detail but bind no Cell Ontology term anywhere, so scripts/check_cancer_origin.py derives no cell of origin for either. Epithelioid Sarcoma does derive one (CL:0000134).
  2. Ewing sarcoma already records its unresolved origin as two candidates (CL:0000134 mesenchymal stem cell and CL:0000333 migratory neural crest cell), which is the representation the proposal describes.
  3. Several core pediatric entries bind cell types but mark no origin node: Medulloblastoma SHH Activated, Hepatoblastoma, Osteosarcoma, Ependymoma, Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia, Juvenile Myelomonocytic Leukemia, Pleuropulmonary Blastoma and Desmoplastic_Small_Round_Cell_Tumor.
  4. There is no overall acute myeloid leukemia entry. IDH Mutant AML uses the generic AML Mondo term (MONDO:0018874) as its disease_term, so an identifier lookup for "Acute myeloid leukemia, NOS" lands on the IDH-mutant entry. Pediatric KMT2A-rearranged and NUP98-rearranged AML exist only as subtypes of Acute_Megakaryoblastic_Leukemia.
  5. There is no overall rhabdomyosarcoma entry, so CCDI's "Rhabdomyosarcoma, NOS" has nothing to resolve to.
  6. Adrenocortical carcinoma has no entry, although it is a CCDI value, a WHO paediatric entity and a core Li-Fraumeni tumour.

Before creating any of these, check the stub queue and open claim issues, and decide entry versus subtype by the cancer granularity ladder (design decisions §3a): a histologic or WHO-defined molecular entity is an entry, a biomarker stratum is a subtype by default.

The Mondo and NCIt identifiers in the CCDI value table come from an automatic OLS search and have not been curated; treat them as leads, not mappings. The dismech_status column was reviewed by hand, but a location of the form File[subtype] asserts only that the subtype exists, not that its content is complete.
