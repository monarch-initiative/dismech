Neuroblastoma Family Project

Overview

This project tracks the curation of Neuroblastoma and the 21 entries around it. It is the neuroblastoma part of the childhood-cancer work in CHILDHOOD_CANCER.md and of the Tier 4 and Tier 13 rows of CANCER.md. Individual changes are tracked in dismech#14976.

Scope: where each kind of thing is recorded

The family follows the cancer granularity ladder in design decisions §3a (docs/explanation/design-decisions.md).

Rung In this family Where it is recorded
Histologic entity Neuroblastoma (the Schwannian stroma-poor category of the International Neuroblastoma Pathology Classification), ganglioneuroblastoma, ganglioneuroma Three entries, Neuroblastoma (MONDO:0005072), Ganglioneuroblastoma (MONDO:0005035) and Ganglioneuroma (MONDO:0005033), collected in Peripheral_Neuroblastic_Tumors
Biomarker or risk stratum MYCN-amplified and high-risk neuroblastoma has_subtypes on Neuroblastoma, beside the adrenal gland subclass (MONDO:0006076)
Stage INRGSS L1, L2, M and MS stages: on Neuroblastoma; relapsed or refractory disease is a progression: phase, not an entry
Germline predisposition ALK- and PHOX2B-related susceptibility, and twelve syndromes with a raised neuroblastoma risk Separate entries, collected in Neuroblastoma_Predisposition_Syndromes
Mechanism Proliferative signalling, evasion of growth suppressors, replicative immortality, invasion and metastasis conforms_to from Neuroblastoma nodes to the four hallmark modules of those names; no mechanism is an entry

Neuroblastoma's MONDO and NCIT classes also contain tumours the entry does not cover. Olfactory neuroblastoma and CNS neuroblastoma with FOXR2 activation have their own entries; retinal neuroblastoma has none. The entry's notes carry the full scope statement.

Stub decisions

  • Neuroblastoma, susceptibility to, 2 and 3 are curated, as PHOX2B-Related_Neuroblastic_Tumor_Susceptibility (MONDO:0700041) and ALK-Related_Neuroblastic_Tumor_Susceptibility (MONDO:0013083).
  • Neuroblastoma, susceptibility to, 1 (MONDO:0009741, KIF1B): stubs/Neuroblastoma_Susceptibility_To_1.yaml is BLOCKED at LOW priority, with its entry type left UNDECIDED. The germline KIF1B link rests on one family, and the follow-up study of that family (PMID:33112832) moved its pheochromocytoma predisposition onto a germline MAX variant. No gene-disease validity body has classified the pair. The stub's notes carry the full reasoning.
  • Myeloid sarcoma (MONDO:0006861): stubs/Myeloid_Sarcoma.yaml is DEFERRED at LOW priority. It was nominated as a tumour that can be mistaken for neuroblastoma, but the fifth-edition WHO haematolymphoid classification (PMID:35732831) treats it as a tissue-based manifestation of acute myeloid leukaemia rather than an entity. The KB has no general acute myeloid leukaemia entry to hold it as a subtype.

CNS_Neuroblastoma_FOXR2-Activated binds no cell type at all.
