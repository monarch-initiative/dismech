Leukodystrophies

Heritable disorders of central nervous system white matter whose primary pathology lies in glial cells or the myelin sheath, as defined by the 2015 expert consensus case definition: oligodendrocytes, astrocytes, microglia or other non-neuronal cells, with or without peripheral nerve involvement. The mechanisms span myelin structural proteins, lysosomal and peroxisomal lipid metabolism, astrocyte and microglial dysfunction, ion and water homeostasis, and the translational stress response.

Why this grouping

Membership follows the consensus classification of Vanderver et al. 2015 (PMID:25649058), which defined leukodystrophy by the cell type primarily involved rather than by organelle or by whether the disease progresses. The same paper set apart a second class, the genetic leukoencephalopathies: heritable disorders with significant white matter abnormality but primary neuronal involvement or prominent systemic disease (mitochondrial disorders such as MELAS and MNGIE, GM1 and GM2 gangliosidosis, the neuronal ceroid lipofuscinoses, the mucopolysaccharidoses). These are deliberately excluded, even where MONDO files them under leukodystrophy, as are inherited vasculopathies such as CADASIL and COL4A1/2 disease. The grouping is therefore narrower than MONDO:0019046, which is why the MONDO mapping is a broadMatch. Members remain separate Disease entries because the causal mechanisms differ; the hypomyelinating subset is nested as its own grouping because it shares a primary failure of myelin formation.

MONDO alignment & provenance

MONDO's leukodystrophy class also contains mitochondrial, glycosylation and other disorders that the 2015 consensus classifies as genetic leukoencephalopathies, so the MONDO class is broader than this grouping.
