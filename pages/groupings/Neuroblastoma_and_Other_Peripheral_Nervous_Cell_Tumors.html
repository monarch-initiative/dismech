ICCC-3 group IV: neuroblastoma and other peripheral nervous cell tumors

Group IV of the International Classification of Childhood Cancer, third edition (ICCC-3), a classification designed for international, population-based epidemiological studies and cancer registries. The ICCC-3 classifies tumours coded according to the International Classification of Diseases for Oncology, third edition (ICD-O-3), into 12 main groups, which are split into 47 subgroups. Group IV is named "Neuroblastoma and other peripheral nervous cell tumors". In the main classification table as the SEER programme of the United States National Cancer Institute publishes it, subgroup (a), "Neuroblastoma and ganglioneuroblastoma", is ICD-O-3 morphology 9490 and 9500, and subgroup (b), "Other peripheral nervous cell tumors", is morphology 8680-8683, 8690-8693, 8700 and 9520-9523, each with the site range C000-C809, the widest the table uses. Subgroup (b) also takes morphology 9501-9504 at the sites C000-C699, C739-C768 and C809. Five DisMech entries cover tumours that these codes place in the group: Neuroblastoma, Ganglioneuroblastoma and CNS Neuroblastoma, FOXR2-Activated in subgroup (a), and Olfactory Neuroblastoma and Pheochromocytoma and Paraganglioma in subgroup (b).

Why this grouping

Grouped by registry convention. The members are the DisMech entries whose tumours the ICCC-3 code table assigns to one group, so the grouping records which tumours childhood cancer statistics count together. A registry group is a coding convention and not a mechanism: this grouping asserts no shared driver lesion, pathway or origin among its members, and none of its evidence is evidence for one. Membership follows the code table and not the group's name. The table lists four-digit ICD-O-3 morphology codes, and the SEER page carries a caution that benign and borderline tumours belong only in group III and in subgroup X(a). Two consequences follow. Ganglioneuroma is not a member, although morphology 9490 is in subgroup (a), because the code recorded for it is 9490/0, a benign one (see notes). Pheochromocytoma and Paraganglioma is a member only for the tumours a registry records with malignant behaviour, and which tumours those are has changed with the coding edition (see that member below). CNS Neuroblastoma, FOXR2-Activated is a member by the code rule alone. The group's name speaks of peripheral tumours, and this is a tumour of the central nervous system. But the table gives morphology 9500 to subgroup IV(a) with the site range C000-C809 and has no separate row for intracranial sites, so a tumour coded 9500 is counted in this group wherever it arises, and 9500/3 is the code the Disease Ontology records for this tumour (see that member's notes and the discussion in this file). The grouping overlaps the Peripheral Neuroblastic Tumors grouping and cuts differently. That grouping follows a pathology classification and holds neuroblastoma, ganglioneuroblastoma and ganglioneuroma. This one follows the registry table, so it leaves ganglioneuroma out and takes in olfactory neuroblastoma, pheochromocytoma and paraganglioma, and the FOXR2-activated neuroblastoma of the central nervous system, all of which the rationale of that grouping records as non-members. The members stay separate Disease entries because the registry group says nothing about their biology, treatment or outcome.

A member is a Disease entry for a tumour whose ICD-O-3 morphology code the ICCC-3 main classification table assigns to group IV, for the tumours registered with malignant behaviour. Stated as NECESSARY only. How much of this entry the group covers depends on the coding edition in force when a tumour was registered. Under the ICD-O-3 codes that the NCI Thesaurus records, adrenal gland pheochromocytoma is 8700/0 and paraganglioma is 8680/1, a benign and a borderline behaviour code. The caution on the table keeps benign and borderline tumours out of group IV, so only the tumours coded as malignant, 8700/3 and 8680/3, are counted, and the group covers the entry in part. Later coding removed that limit: ICD-O-3.2 lists paraganglioma, NOS as 8680/3 for cases diagnosed in 2021 and after, and SEER tells registrars to report pheochromocytoma as 8700/3.

Does a primary coding source give CNS neuroblastoma, FOXR2-activated the ICD-O code 9500/3, and do registries in practice count the tumour in group IV(a)?
The CNS Neuroblastoma, FOXR2-Activated entry is a member on two grounds: the table gives morphology 9500 to subgroup IV(a) with its widest site range and has no separate row for intracranial sites, and the Disease Ontology records 9500/3 on its term for the tumour (DOID:0080906). Two things would make the row firmer. First, the code is read from one ontology cross-reference. MONDO and the NCI Thesaurus record no ICD-O code on their own terms for the tumour (MONDO:0859597 and NCIT:C186547, read through OLS4 on 10 November 2026), and no primary coding source for it is held in the reference cache, so the code is recorded in the member's notes and not as quoted evidence. Second, the table states a rule, and no source read for this grouping reports how registries have classified tumours with this diagnosis. A citable coding source, or a registry report that names the tumour, would settle both.
