Channelopathies (Ion Channel Disorders)

A curated union of dismech disorders caused by dysfunction of an ion channel or of a protein that directly regulates one: a pore-forming channel subunit (voltage-gated sodium, potassium, calcium and chloride channels, inward rectifiers, ligand-gated GABA-A, glycine and glutamate receptors, ryanodine receptors, store-operated calcium channels) or an accessory or regulatory partner such as a channel beta subunit, KCNE peptide, calmodulin, calsequestrin or STIM1. Members span the heart, skeletal muscle, central and peripheral nervous system, transporting epithelia and the immune system, and each carries a channelopathy_category tag naming its principal organ system.

Why this grouping

Grouped on a shared molecular lesion, ion channel dysfunction, rather than on a phenotype or organ system; the organ system is recorded per member by channelopathy_category. Two criteria blocks make the grouping checkable. The first is NECESSARY: every member must curate, somewhere in its pathophysiology, a GO molecular function under ion channel activity (GO:0005216) or ion channel regulator activity (GO:0099106), so a member whose entry records no channel-level activity is reported as a contradiction. The regulator branch is deliberate, because long QT syndrome from KCNE1, SCN4B or calmodulin variants and STIM1 deficiency are channelopathies caused by proteins that are not pores. The second is NECESSARY_AND_SUFFICIENT and ties membership to the channelopathy_category tag, so a newly tagged entry is reported as a candidate and the tag and the grouping cannot drift apart. Disorders of ion pumps are excluded: the Na+/K+-ATPase disorders (ATP1A3-related CAPOS syndrome and alternating hemiplegia of childhood) carry a P-type transporter activity, not a channel activity, and are not members. Broad umbrella entries whose genes are mostly not channels (Genetic Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathy, Congenital Insensitivity to Pain, Familial Atrial Fibrillation) and acquired channel blockade (Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity) are also left out. The criteria check the entry's own curated GO terms; they do not check that the causal genes carry the corresponding annotations in GO. Inherited_Arrhythmia_Syndromes overlaps the cardiac members but is not nested here: it is defined by conformance to the cardiac repolarization module and includes calcium-handling disorders and a sick sinus syndrome entry that are not tagged as channelopathies.

Membership criteria

NECESSARY  (member ⇒ criteria)
Every member curates a GO molecular function under ion channel activity (GO:0005216) or ion channel regulator activity (GO:0099106) on at least one pathophysiology node.

    • HAS MOLECULAR FUNCTION monoatomic ion channel activity GO:0005216
      Ion channel activity (GO:0005216) or a descendant.
    • HAS MOLECULAR FUNCTION ion channel regulator activity GO:0099106
      Ion channel regulator activity (GO:0099106) or a descendant.
NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT  (member ⇔ criteria)
A disorder is a member if and only if it carries a channelopathy_category classification (cardiac, skeletal muscle, neurological, epithelial or immune channelopathy).

  each carries a channelopathy_category tag naming its principal organ system.
grouping_basis:
- SHARED_MECHANISM
- SHARED_GENE_FAMILY
grouping_rationale: >-
  Grouped on a shared molecular lesion, ion channel dysfunction, rather than on
  a phenotype or organ system; the organ system is recorded per member by
  channelopathy_category. Two criteria blocks make the grouping checkable. The
  first is NECESSARY: every member must curate, somewhere in its
  pathophysiology, a GO molecular function under ion channel activity
  (GO:0005216) or ion channel regulator activity (GO:0099106), so a member whose
  entry records no channel-level activity is reported as a contradiction. The
  regulator branch is deliberate, because long QT syndrome from KCNE1, SCN4B or
  calmodulin variants and STIM1 deficiency are channelopathies caused by
  proteins that are not pores. The second is NECESSARY_AND_SUFFICIENT and ties
  membership to the channelopathy_category tag, so a newly tagged entry is
  reported as a candidate and the tag and the grouping cannot drift apart.
  Disorders of ion pumps are excluded: the Na+/K+-ATPase disorders
  (ATP1A3-related CAPOS syndrome and alternating hemiplegia of childhood) carry
  a P-type transporter activity, not a channel activity, and are not members.
  Broad umbrella entries whose genes are mostly not channels (Genetic
  Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathy, Congenital Insensitivity to Pain,
  Familial Atrial Fibrillation) and acquired channel blockade (Local Anesthetic
  Systemic Toxicity) are also left out. The criteria check the entry's own
  curated GO terms; they do not check that the causal genes carry the
  corresponding annotations in GO. Inherited_Arrhythmia_Syndromes overlaps the
  cardiac members but is not nested here: it is defined by conformance to the
  cardiac repolarization module and includes calcium-handling disorders and a
  sick sinus syndrome entry that are not tagged as channelopathies.
