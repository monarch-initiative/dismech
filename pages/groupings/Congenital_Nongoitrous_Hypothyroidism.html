Congenital Nongoitrous Hypothyroidism (CHNG series)

The congenital nongoitrous hypothyroidisms are the numbered OMIM series CHNG1-9, collected in MONDO as hypothyroidism, congenital, nongoitrous. What defines the series is a negative finding: thyroid hormone action is deficient from birth and the thyroid is not enlarged, because nothing drives TSH-dependent thyroid growth: the TSH signal is absent, the gland cannot respond to it, or hormone production is intact and the defect lies in the target tissues. The members fall into three mechanistically unrelated families. In the primary forms the thyroid itself fails to develop or to respond to TSH (TSHR, PAX8, NKX2-5). In the central forms the hypothalamic-pituitary drive is missing, so TSH is low or ineffective (TSHB, TRHR, TBL1X, IRS4). In the resistance form hormone is made but the alpha thyroid hormone receptor cannot respond to it (THRA).

Why this grouping

Grouped because MONDO and OMIM group them: this is a transcription of the CHNG numbered series, whose organising principle is congenital hypothyroid physiology without goitre rather than a shared mechanism. Primary thyroid dysgenesis, central hypothyroidism and receptor-level hormone resistance are all represented, so SHARED_MECHANISM would overstate what the members have in common - hence SHARED_PHENOTYPE plus CLINICAL_CONVENTION. The members are kept where they are already curated: the primary forms as gene-defined subtypes of Congenital Hypothyroidism, the TRHR, TBL1X and IRS4 forms as subtypes of Central Congenital Hypothyroidism, and the TSHB and THRA forms as their own Disease entries. The grouping gives the MONDO class a home without forcing a single pathograph over three unrelated mechanisms.

MONDO alignment & provenance

The grouping concept is the MONDO hypothyroidism, congenital, nongoitrous class, whose nine direct subclasses are the CHNG series. exactMatch records the intended conceptual alignment; a MONDO subclass without a dismech entry is a curation gap rather than evidence that the grouping is only a close match.

Both parent entries are anchored on broader MONDO classes (congenital hypothyroidism, central congenital hypothyroidism), so they are listed as SUBTYPE members and only their named CHNG subtypes fall inside this grouping. just grouping-mondo-consistency reads a SUBTYPE member's parent term, so it reports those two members as outside MONDO:0000045; each of their listed CHNG subtypes is a direct subclass of it. The ninth, MONDO:0012360 (congenital nongoitrous hypothyroidism 3), is defined by MONDO only as autosomal dominant resistance to thyrotropin linked to chromosome region 15q25.3-q26.1, with no causal gene identified, so it has no curatable mechanism and is recorded here as a gap.

Membership criteria

NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT  (member ⇔ criteria)
A disorder belongs to this grouping if and only if MONDO places it under hypothyroidism, congenital, nongoitrous (MONDO:0000045). The grouping is a transcription of the CHNG series, so placement in that class is both necessary and sufficient; it is deliberately not extended to congenital hypothyroidism without goitre that MONDO files outside this class.
  • HAS MAPPING
    The member's disease term, or for a SUBTYPE member the named subtype's term, is an is-a descendant of MONDO:0000045 (hypothyroidism, congenital, nongoitrous).
Clinically, every member presents from birth with deficient thyroid hormone action and no goitre. This is recorded as the group's defining description rather than as machine-evaluable logic: the THRA member has near-normal circulating thyroid hormone with tissue-level hypothyroidism, and the central members are annotated as central hypothyroidism rather than under a common HP class, so no single phenotype term covers every member without over- or under-reaching.
