mTORopathies (Hereditary mTORC1 Hyperactivation Disorders)

Hereditary disorders in which a germline variant in a regulator of the mTORC1 pathway leaves mTORC1 constitutively active. The lost regulators are the TSC complex (TSC1, TSC2), PTEN, the GATOR1 complex (DEPDC5, NPRL2, NPRL3), the KICSTOR component SZT2, and the Rag GTPase RRAGC. The shared output is hamartomatous overgrowth, focal cortical dysplasia and epilepsy. A somatic second hit in the lesion is common. mTOR inhibition is an established treatment in tuberous sclerosis complex; for the other members its use is preclinical or limited to case reports.

Why this grouping

Grouped on a shared signalling lesion rather than on phenotype or organ. The members are classified elsewhere as tumour predisposition syndromes (PTEN hamartoma tumour syndrome), focal epilepsies (the GATOR1 disorders) or developmental and epileptic encephalopathies (SZT2), so no organ-based class brings them together. Members stay separate Disease entries because they lose different regulators upstream of mTORC1, which sets their spectrum: TSC and PTEN disorders are multisystem hamartoma syndromes, while the GATOR1 disorders are largely confined to the brain. The criterion is NECESSARY, not NECESSARY_AND_SUFFICIENT. The module also takes acquired and neoplastic conformers that are not hereditary mTORopathies, so conformance alone does not admit a disease. Excluded on that basis: lymphangioleiomyomatosis and perivascular epithelioid cell neoplasm (TSC-associated neoplasms that also occur sporadically) and the Peutz-Jeghers polyp (a lesion). Peutz-Jeghers syndrome (STK11) is left out as a boundary case: STK11 acts on mTORC1 through AMPK, but the syndrome is defined by polyposis and cancer risk, not by mTORC1-driven cortical or hamartomatous disease. No MONDO class covers this grouping. MONDO has single diseases on the pathway (MONDO:0976294 ragopathy, MONDO:0100572 MTOR-related overgrowth spectrum) but no parent. It is proposed as proposal 12 in docs/reports/mondo-mechanism-classification-proposal-round-2-2026-10-23.md.
