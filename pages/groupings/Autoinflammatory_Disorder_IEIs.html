Autoinflammatory Disorder IEIs (IUIS Table 7)

IUIS Table 7 — autoinflammatory disorders. These inborn errors of immunity drive sterile, antigen-independent inflammation through unrestrained innate signalling — inflammasome activation and IL-1beta release, constitutive type I interferon production, or dysregulated NF-kappaB — rather than through autoantibodies or autoreactive lymphocytes. The clinical signature is recurrent or continuous fever, rash, serositis and arthropathy with a raised acute-phase response and no identifiable infection or high-titre autoantibody.

Where this grouping sits

Nested in Inborn Errors of Immunity

Why this grouping

Grouped on the IUIS Table 7 pattern of monogenic sterile innate inflammation. Members are kept as separate Disease entries because the deregulated innate circuit differs, and that difference is what determines treatment: CINCA is inflammasome/IL-1beta-driven and answers to IL-1 blockade, whereas COPA syndrome is a type I interferonopathy driven by failed STING retrieval and does not, and ADA2 deficiency is a secreted-enzyme defect treated with TNF blockade rather than either. Grouping them keeps the inflammasome, interferon and NF-kappaB arms of autoinflammation visible as siblings rather than collapsing them into a single "periodic fever" bucket. The grouping follows IUIS's own three-section split of Table 7, so it also carries members whose presentation is not a periodic fever at all — cherubism's jaw-restricted osteolysis, DITRA's pustular psoriasis, H syndrome's histiocytosis — which is the point: they share the innate-inflammatory mechanism, not the syndrome shape.

MONDO alignment & provenance

skos:broadMatch MONDO:0019751 · autoinflammatory syndrome

IUIS Table 7 aligns with MONDO autoinflammatory syndrome (MONDO:0019751), but broadMatch rather than exactMatch: MONDO's class is phenotype-defined and admits non-monogenic and acquired autoinflammatory disease, whereas IUIS Table 7 is restricted to germline monogenic inborn errors. The MONDO term is therefore strictly wider than this grouping.

MONDO consistency: consistent Re-checked 2026-09-13 against the full member list with `runoak -i sqlite:obo:mondo descendants -p i MONDO:0019751`, which returns 128 classes. Fifteen of the seventeen members resolve inside that set, including COPA syndrome, which MONDO carries as autoimmune interstitial lung disease-arthritis syndrome (MONDO:0014629). Two do not: A20 haploinsufficiency (MONDO:0100222) and H syndrome (MONDO:0011273). Both are nonetheless IUIS Table 7 rows, so the divergence is MONDO's placement of those two classes rather than a membership error here, and it is exactly the kind of gap the broadMatch predicate leaves room for. Membership in this grouping is decided by the iuis_category criterion below, not by MONDO closure.

A20 Haploinsufficiency DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
A20 is the ubiquitin-editing enzyme that switches NF-kappaB off downstream of TNF, IL-1 and Toll-like receptors, so haploinsufficiency leaves the brake only half applied and NF-kappaB output chronically raised. This is the NF-kappaB arm of Table 7 in its purest form, with no inflammasome sensor and no interferon signature to explain the phenotype. Clinically it presents as early-onset orogenital ulceration, uveitis and arthralgia, and it was first described as a dominantly inherited form of Behcet disease before being recognised as an inborn error of immunity. Aicardi-Goutieres Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The prototype of the type I interferonopathy arm, and the member where the lesion is in nucleic-acid housekeeping rather than in a receptor: defective clearance or mis-sensing of cytosolic DNA and RNA species leaves an endogenous ligand permanently available to the interferon-inducing sensors. That single mechanism is reached through nine different genes, which is why this entry is a lumped series rather than a single genotype. What distinguishes it clinically from every other Table 7 member is the target organ: intracranial calcification, leukodystrophy and chronic CSF lymphocytosis that mimic congenital infection, rather than fever and serositis. Blau Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Gain-of-function variants in the NOD2 nucleotide-binding domain drive constitutive NF-kappaB signalling from a cytosolic NOD-like receptor that, unlike NLRP3 and NLRP1, does not nucleate an inflammasome. The output is organised granuloma rather than an IL-1beta fever: the triad is granulomatous dermatitis, boggy synovitis with camptodactyly, and uveitis. This is also the member that has to be held apart from a common polygenic disease, since the NOD2 variants that raise Crohn disease risk are loss-of-function and sit in a different part of the protein. Differentiating mechanism

COPA Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Heterozygous COPA variants impair COPI-mediated retrograde Golgi-to-ER trafficking, so the SURF4 adapter fails to retrieve STING; STING accumulates and signals ligand-independently. The type I interferonopathy arm of Table 7, and the member that reaches autoinflammation through a housekeeping vesicular-transport defect rather than an immune sensor, presenting as interstitial lung disease with arthritis and autoantibodies. Deficiency of Adenosine Deaminase 2 DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The only member whose defective product is a secreted enzyme rather than an intracellular sensor, adaptor or signalling node. Loss of extracellular adenosine deaminase activity, and of the growth-factor function of the same protein, skews monocytes toward a proinflammatory phenotype and destabilises endothelium, so the disease presents as medium-vessel vasculitis: livedo racemosa, early-onset lacunar and haemorrhagic stroke, and a polyarteritis-nodosa-like picture. It is also the Table 7 member most clearly separated by treatment, answering to TNF blockade rather than IL-1 blockade, and it can add hypogammaglobulinaemia and marrow failure that overlap the antibody-deficiency and bone-marrow-failure tables. H Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Loss of the equilibrative nucleoside transporter hENT3 disables nucleoside efflux from lysosomes and mitochondria in myeloid cells, and the resulting inflammation arrives as tissue histiocytic infiltration rather than as a circulating cytokine fever. That makes it the Table 7 member whose lesion is a transporter and whose read-out is a histiocytosis, with cutaneous hyperpigmentation, hypertrichosis and induration as the defining signs. SLC29A3 is allelic across a spectrum that also includes pigmented hypertrichotic dermatosis with insulin-dependent diabetes and Faisalabad histiocytosis, which IUIS records in the same row. Muckle-Wells Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The intermediate rung of the NLRP3 spectrum, sharing its gain-of-function inflammasome mechanism with familial cold autoinflammatory syndrome and CINCA. It is distinguished from the former by attacks that are not cold-triggered and from the latter by the absence of neonatal central nervous system and epiphyseal disease. Its own defining late complications are progressive sensorineural hearing loss and AA amyloidosis, which is what makes early IL-1 blockade a disease-modifying rather than purely symptomatic treatment here. STING-Associated Vasculopathy with Onset in Infancy DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The gain of function is in the sensing adaptor itself: mutant STING adopts an active conformation and signals without ligand, so interferon induction no longer requires any accumulated nucleic acid. That makes SAVI the direct counterpart of COPA syndrome, which reaches the same STING node from the opposite direction by failing to retrieve it from the Golgi. What distinguishes SAVI clinically is the acral small-vessel vasculopathy, which can progress to ulceration and tissue loss, combined with early-onset interstitial lung disease. TMEM173 hgnc:27962

TNF Receptor-Associated Periodic Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Cysteine-disrupting TNFRSF1A variants cause the 55-kD TNF receptor to misfold and be retained in the endoplasmic reticulum instead of reaching the surface and being shed, so the primary lesion is protein folding and trafficking rather than inflammasome or interferon signalling. IUIS places it accordingly in the non-inflammasome section, even though the resulting phenotype is a periodic fever indistinguishable at first glance from the inflammasome members. Attack duration is the most useful bedside discriminator: episodes typically run one to three weeks rather than days, with migratory myalgia under an overlying rash and periorbital oedema. Cherubism DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Stabilising SH3BP2 variants leave the adaptor resistant to degradation, so macrophage and osteoclast NF-kappaB and TNF signalling run hot. Cherubism is the one Table 7 member whose inflammation is anatomically confined: symmetric osteolytic giant-cell lesions of the mandible and maxilla, with no fever, no serositis and no systemic acute-phase response. Familial Mediterranean Fever DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The archetypal inflammasome member, but built on pyrin rather than NLRP3: recessive loss-of-function MEFV variants remove the autoinhibition that normally keeps the pyrin inflammasome silent. Because pyrin is regulated through RhoA and the microtubule cytoskeleton, this is the member defined by colchicine responsiveness, which no other Table 7 disorder shares. Attacks are short, typically one to three days of fever with serositis, and the untreated long-term risk that distinguishes it is AA amyloidosis. MEFV hgnc:6998

Familial Cold Autoinflammatory Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The mildest end of the NLRP3 cryopyrin-associated periodic syndrome spectrum, and the only member with an external physical trigger: attacks follow generalised cold exposure within about two hours, rather than arising unprovoked. Its mechanism is shared with Muckle-Wells syndrome and CINCA, so what separates the three is threshold and target tissue rather than pathway. The entry also carries the genetically distinct cold-urticarial and inflammasome-adjacent disorders caused by NLRP12, PLCG2 and NLRP1, which reach a similar phenotype through separate circuits. Mevalonate Kinase Deficiency DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The only member that is simultaneously an inborn error of metabolism: biallelic MVK loss of function blocks the isoprenoid branch immediately downstream of mevalonate, and the resulting shortage of geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate leaves RhoA unprenylated, which releases pyrin. It therefore converges on the same pyrin inflammasome as familial Mediterranean fever while starting from a metabolic rather than an immune lesion, and IUIS itself records the link from the block to the inflammation as not fully resolved. Residual enzyme activity sets the phenotype on a continuous scale from hyper-IgD syndrome to mevalonic aciduria with neurological involvement, which none of the other members show. MVK hgnc:7530

Proteasome-Associated Autoinflammatory Syndrome DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Reaches the type I interferon arm through proteostasis failure rather than nucleic-acid sensing: loss-of-function defects in immunoproteasome subunits and proteasome assembly factors let ubiquitinated substrates accumulate, and the ensuing cell-intrinsic stress response drives chronic interferon signalling. That distinguishes it from Aicardi-Goutieres syndrome and SAVI, which start from a nucleic-acid ligand and a sensor respectively. The clinical signature is equally distinctive: annular violaceous plaques evolving to progressive lipodystrophy with joint contractures, a combination no other member produces. PSMB8 hgnc:9545

Psoriasis 14, Pustular DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
Loss of the interleukin-36 receptor antagonist leaves IL-36 alpha, beta and gamma signalling unopposed on keratinocytes, which drives IL-8 release and massive neutrophil recruitment into the epidermis. The lesion is therefore an absent brake on an IL-1-family cytokine rather than an overactive sensor, the same logic IUIS applies to DIRA in the neighbouring row. What sets it apart from the rest of Table 7 is the compartment: recurrent sterile pustular flares confined largely to skin, rather than fever with serositis or vasculopathy. IL36RN hgnc:15561

Spondyloenchondrodysplasia DISEASE
Differentiating mechanism
The only member that is both an interferonopathy and a skeletal dysplasia, because one enzyme serves both roles. Loss of tartrate-resistant acid phosphatase leaves osteopontin phosphorylated, which drives type I interferon through plasmacytoid dendritic cells, while the same enzyme loss disturbs the vertebral and metaphyseal growth plates and produces platyspondyly with enchondroma-like lesions. Clinically it adds prominent lupus-like autoimmunity and spastic paraparesis, so it sits at the intersection of the interferon arm and the bone nosology rather than cleanly inside either. - member: Psoriasis 14, Pustular
  member_type: DISEASE
  differentiating_mechanisms:
  - description: >-
      Loss of the interleukin-36 receptor antagonist leaves IL-36 alpha, beta and

- member: Spondyloenchondrodysplasia
  member_type: DISEASE
  differentiating_mechanisms:
  - description: >-
      The only member that is both an interferonopathy and a skeletal dysplasia,
      because one enzyme serves both roles. Loss of tartrate-resistant acid
      phosphatase leaves osteopontin phosphorylated, which drives type I
      interferon through plasmacytoid dendritic cells, while the same enzyme loss
      disturbs the vertebral and metaphyseal growth plates and produces
      platyspondyly with enchondroma-like lesions. Clinically it adds prominent
      lupus-like autoimmunity and spastic paraparesis, so it sits at the
      intersection of the interferon arm and the bone nosology rather than
      cleanly inside either.

- member: STING-Associated Vasculopathy with Onset in Infancy
  member_type: DISEASE
  differentiating_mechanisms:
  - description: >-
      The gain of function is in the sensing adaptor itself: mutant STING adopts
      an active conformation and signals without ligand, so interferon induction
      no longer requires any accumulated nucleic acid. That makes SAVI the direct
      counterpart of COPA syndrome, which reaches the same STING node from the
      opposite direction by failing to retrieve it from the Golgi. What
      distinguishes SAVI clinically is the acral small-vessel vasculopathy, which

      interstitial lung disease.

- member: TNF Receptor-Associated Periodic Syndrome
  member_type: DISEASE
  differentiating_mechanisms:
  - description: >-
      Cysteine-disrupting TNFRSF1A variants cause the 55-kD TNF receptor to
      misfold and be retained in the endoplasmic reticulum instead of reaching
      the surface and being shed, so the primary lesion is protein folding and
      trafficking rather than inflammasome or interferon signalling. IUIS places
      it accordingly in the non-inflammasome section, even though the resulting
      phenotype is a periodic fever indistinguishable at first glance from the
      inflammasome members. Attack duration is the most useful bedside
      discriminator: episodes typically run one to three weeks rather than days,
      with migratory myalgia under an overlying rash and periorbital oedema.
