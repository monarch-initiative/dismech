Inherited Distal Renal Tubular Acidoses

Shared distal acid-secretion defect; compare SLC4A1 exchange with ATP6V1B1/ATP6V0A4 proton-pump defects.

Shared mechanism Shared phenotype

Why this grouping

The four numbered series members share defective distal urinary acidification. Basolateral anion exchange and apical proton pumping are different components of the acid-secretion machinery; erythroid and inner-ear involvement distinguish members further. The union is scoped to these four curated entities, not every acquired or newly discovered genetic cause of distal renal tubular acidosis, and does not impose recessive inheritance on every pump-related presentation.
