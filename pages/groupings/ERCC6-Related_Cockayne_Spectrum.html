ERCC6-related Cockayne spectrum (with or without COFS)

The disorders caused by biallelic variants in ERCC6, the gene encoding the Cockayne syndrome B protein (CSB) that couples nucleotide excision repair to transcription. They form one severity and onset continuum. Cockayne syndrome type B covers the mild later-onset form (CS III), the classic form (CS I) and the severe infantile form (CS II). CS II already shows growth failure and eye anomalies at birth and partially overlaps COFS. Cerebrooculofacioskeletal syndrome 1 (COFS1), with prenatal-onset microcephaly, congenital cataracts and neurogenic arthrogryposis, is the most severe end. MONDO:0100506 treats this ERCC6 continuum as a single disease, and ClinGen's Definitive ERCC6 assertion is made against that term. dismech curates the two parts inside two separate disease entries, and this grouping joins them.

Why this grouping

The axis is a single gene, and SHARED_GENE_FAMILY fits: every member is caused by variants in ERCC6. The grouping exists because the knowledge base splits this continuum at a different point from MONDO and ClinGen. dismech files ERCC6 Cockayne syndrome as the molecular type B row of Cockayne_Syndrome, next to the ERCC8 type A row, because Cockayne syndrome is one clinical disease with two causal genes. It files ERCC6 COFS as the COFS1 row of COFS_Syndrome, next to the ERCC2, ERCC5 and ERCC1 forms, because COFS is one prenatal-onset phenotype with four causal genes. Both splits follow the phenotype. MONDO:0100506 instead cuts by gene: its definition lumps CS type B, COFS1 and de Sanctis-Cacchione syndrome into one ERCC6 spectrum, and ClinGen's Definitive ERCC6 assertion is made against that term. Neither cut is wrong. The grouping lets a reader who starts from the gene find both rows without merging the two disease entries or duplicating their content. The boundary stops at the neurodevelopmental, progeroid end of ERCC6 disease. UV-sensitive syndrome 1 is also caused by biallelic ERCC6 variants, but it is cutaneous photosensitivity without the growth failure or neurodegeneration of Cockayne syndrome. MONDO:0100506 does not include it, and MONDO files UV-sensitive syndrome 1 (MONDO:0010909) under UV-sensitive syndrome instead, so it is not a member.

MONDO alignment & provenance

skos:exactMatch MONDO:0100506 · Cockayne spectrum with or without cerebrooculofacioskeletal syndrome

MONDO defines this class as the autosomal recessive condition caused by ERCC6 variants, and its definition says it lumps Cockayne syndrome type 2/B, cerebrooculofacioskeletal syndrome 1 and de Sanctis-Cacchione syndrome into one spectrum. That union is the concept curated here, and the match rests on that lump sentence. The definition's phenotype sentence (growth failure at birth, little or no postnatal neurologic development, death usually by age five) describes only the severe infantile end, while the type B member also covers the classic and mild forms. MONDO does not place the members' own terms under this class: MONDO:0019570 (Cockayne syndrome type 2, defined as Cockayne syndrome caused by ERCC6) is a sibling of MONDO:0100506 under MONDO:0016006 Cockayne syndrome, and MONDO:0008955 (cerebrooculofacioskeletal syndrome 1) sits under MONDO:0008926 COFS syndrome. So an ancestor walk from the members will not reach the mapped class. That gap is in MONDO's hierarchy, not in the membership. MONDO:0019570 also overlaps heavily with MONDO:0100506, which makes it a candidate for an upstream request to place 0019570 and 0008955 under 0100506.
