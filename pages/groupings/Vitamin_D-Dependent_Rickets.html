Vitamin D-Dependent Rickets (VDDR)

The vitamin D-dependent rickets (VDDR) are a small series of monogenic disorders in which rickets arises because the calcitriol signal fails, while dietary vitamin D intake and sunlight exposure are adequate. Each member interrupts the vitamin D axis at a different point: hepatic 25-hydroxylation (type 1B, CYP2R1), renal 1-alpha-hydroxylation (type 1A, CYP27B1), receptor transduction (type 2A, VDR), response-element occupancy downstream of a normal receptor (type 2B), or catabolic clearance of the metabolites (type 3, CYP3A4). They converge on the calciopenic arm of the defective skeletal mineralization module, and they diverge sharply in biochemistry and treatment, which is why they are grouped rather than merged.

Why this grouping

Grouped on a shared pathway rather than a shared gene family: the members are united by lesions at different points of one signalling axis, not by homology. They are deliberately kept as separate Disease entries because the differences are diagnostically and therapeutically decisive rather than cosmetic. Calcitriol is LOW in types 1A, 1B and 3 but HIGH in types 2A and 2B; 25-hydroxyvitamin D is normal in type 1A but low in types 1B and 3; type 1A responds to physiological calcitriol, type 1B needs lifelong high-dose vitamin D, and types 2A and 2B do not respond to any dose of hormone and require calcium bypass instead. Type 2A alone carries alopecia. Merging them into one entry with subtypes would force a single causal chain across a synthetic block, a receptor block and a catabolic gain of function, which are not the same mechanism. The name is a double misnomer that the grouping preserves rather than corrects, because it is the term the literature and the ontologies use: "dependent" denotes dependence on the active hormone rather than on dietary vitamin D, and types 2A and 2B are vitamin D-RESISTANT, the mechanistic inverse of types 1A and 1B. Curators should read the type number, not the series name.

A disorder belongs to the vitamin D-dependent rickets if it produces rickets through failure of the calcitriol signal in the presence of adequate vitamin D supply: it has a pathophysiology node conforming to the calciopenic arm of the defective skeletal mineralization module AND it presents with rickets.

    • HAS PHENOTYPE Rickets HP:0002748
      Presents with rickets.
NECESSARY  (member ⇒ criteria)
A disorder is NOT a vitamin D-dependent rickets if its rickets arises from renal phosphate wasting with normal parathyroid hormone (the hypophosphatemic rickets, which enter the defective skeletal mineralization module through its phosphopenic trigger arm), or from failure to clear a mineralization inhibitor (hypophosphatasia, which enters through the inhibitor-excess arm). Every member enters through the CALCIOPENIC arm specifically.

notes: >-

  same mineralization failure through renal phosphate wasting with normal
  parathyroid hormone, and they are vitamin D-resistant in the trivial sense that

  it is the common acquired disorder these entities are defined against. All of
  them conform to `defective_skeletal_mineralization`, which is the level at which
