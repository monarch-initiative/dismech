Membrane Contact Site Disorders (Organelle Tether and Lipid-Transfer Disorders)

Hereditary disorders caused by loss or mis-regulation of a protein that holds two organelle membranes together at a membrane contact site, or that moves lipids or calcium across one. The contact can join the endoplasmic reticulum to mitochondria, the plasma membrane, the Golgi, lipid droplets, peroxisomes or endolysosomes. Losing it disturbs lipid supply between organelles, calcium transfer into mitochondria, and organelle shape and position. The members are overwhelmingly neurological: motor neuron disease, ataxia, chorea, leukodystrophy with retinal dystrophy, and neurodevelopmental disorders.

Why this grouping

Grouped on a shared subcellular mechanism, the failure of communication between organelles, rather than on phenotype or on the organelle affected. It cuts across organelle-based classes on purpose: ACBD5 deficiency also belongs with the peroxisomal disorders, and Wolfram syndrome with the mitochondrial and endoplasmic reticulum disorders. Those classes describe a failure inside one organelle; this one describes the failure of the connection between two. Members stay separate Disease entries because they differ in which organelle pair is affected and which function of the contact fails: the tether itself, lipid transfer across it, or calcium transfer across it. The criterion is NECESSARY, not NECESSARY_AND_SUFFICIENT. It characterizes every listed member and is for auditing the list. It must not be used to close the class: several disorders of contact-site proteins are not yet curated (BLTP1, ESYT and ORP family genes) or are curated without a conforming node (VPS13B in Cohen syndrome), and entries such as MFN2-related Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease are candidates whose nodes do not yet conform. No MONDO class covers this grouping (OLS search of MONDO for "membrane contact site", "organelle contact" and "lipid transfer", 2026-10-23). It is proposed as a mechanism-based class in docs/reports/mondo-mechanism-classification-proposal-round-2-2026-10-23.md.
