COX4I1-Related COX Deficiency

Mendelian MONDO:0033651 Pathograph 47 Show in embeddings browser Mitochondrial Disease Inborn Error of Metabolism

COX4I1-related mitochondrial complex IV deficiency is an autosomal recessive disorder caused by biallelic COX4I1 variants. Reduced abundance or function of the COX4-1 structural and regulatory subunit impairs complex IV assembly and respiratory-chain function. Presentations range from severe growth failure with chromosome breakage and mild hematologic abnormalities to infantile encephalopathy with regression and epilepsy, or later progressive spastic-dystonic motor and cognitive impairment with Leigh-like basal ganglia lesions. Normal lactate, normal chromosome-breakage studies, or initially normal development do not exclude the disorder. Nuclear DNA-repair defects and replication-associated DNA damage have been demonstrated in cell models; excess reactive oxygen species alone does not explain this branch of the disease mechanism.
