CDK19-Related Disorder

Mendelian MONDO:0030059 Pathograph 47 Show in embeddings browser Autosomal dominant intellectual disability Neurodevelopmental disorder CDK8-kinase module-associated disorder

CDK19-related disorder is an autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental condition associated primarily with de novo missense variants in the kinase domain of CDK19. Developmental and speech impairment, hypotonia, variable craniofacial differences, ophthalmologic abnormalities and behavioral features occur with or without epilepsy. Some patients have severe developmental and epileptic encephalopathy with infantile spasms; seizures are not obligatory. Functional effects depend on allele and assay: Gly28Arg reduces and Tyr32His increases kinase activity in biochemical assays, while fly complementation varies with the tissue and degree of endogenous Cdk8 depletion. Altered transcriptional regulation and cytoplasmic mitochondrial/synaptic dysfunction are candidate contributors, but neither is fully established in patient neurons. Management is individualized and supportive. Antioxidant findings remain confined to experimental flies.
