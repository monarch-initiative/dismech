Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease, axonal, type 2JJ

Mendelian MONDO:0976227 Pathograph 5 Show in embeddings browser hereditary disease

Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2JJ is an adult-onset axonal sensorimotor peripheral neuropathy caused by heterozygous variants in BAG3, the co-chaperone of chaperone-assisted selective autophagy (CASA). BAG3 works with HSPB8 and Hsp70 to collect mechanically damaged and misfolded proteins, carry them to the microtubule organizing center and commit them to autophagosomal degradation; when that route fails, insoluble aggregates accumulate. CMT2JJ is therefore a proteostasis disease presenting as a neuropathy, rather than a defect of myelin, axonal transport or tRNA charging. Its most instructive feature is genotypic: the recurrent CMT2JJ allele p.Pro209Ser and the well-known myofibrillar myopathy allele p.Pro209Leu affect the same codon in the same IPV motif, yet one produces a peripheral neuropathy and the other a severe childhood myopathy with cardiomyopathy. The BAG3 phenotypes overlap rather than partition cleanly, and this entry is scoped to the neuropathy while recording that overlap explicitly.

Why do p.Pro209Ser and p.Pro209Leu — substitutions at the same BAG3 codon — produce a peripheral neuropathy and a childhood myofibrillar myopathy with cardiomyopathy respectively?

This is the most informative open question in BAG3 biology and it bears directly on how this entry should be read. Two substitutions at codon 209, differing by one nucleotide, give diseases that differ in affected tissue, age of onset and severity. Nothing published explains the difference, and the aggregation-and-sequestration mechanism does not distinguish them: PMID:32472079 found that all BAG3_Pro209 mutants acquire the same toxic gain of function, which is why the CASA node in this entry is ESTABLISHED rather than inferred from the myopathy allele. The node that is PROVISIONAL is `Misfolded Protein Aggregation in Peripheral Neurons`, and for an unrelated reason — the aggresome work was done in cell lines, not in nerve tissue from a patient. So what needs explaining is not a gap in the characterised mechanism but why one shared mechanism produces two diseases. Nor is the partition clean — a p.Pro209Leu patient has been reported with a CMT-like axonal polyneuropathy and no cardiomyopathy, so the two alleles may produce overlapping spectra with different centres of gravity rather than separate diseases. Resolving this would establish whether CMT2JJ is properly a distinct entity or the neuropathy-predominant end of one BAG3 proteinopathy.

Pathophysiology

4
BAG3 IPV Motif Variant
Heterozygous variants affect the conserved IPV motif region of BAG3, the surface through which it binds the small heat shock protein HSPB8. The recurrent CMT2JJ allele is p.Pro209Ser (c.625C>T), reported in three families and then independently in a Chinese family, and proposed on that basis to be a hotspot with more than one ethnic origin. It sits at the same codon as the myofibrillar myopathy allele p.Pro209Leu (c.626C>T) — adjacent nucleotides, one codon, two diseases.

Chaperone-Assisted Selective Autophagy Failure
BAG3 is the co-chaperone of chaperone-assisted selective autophagy: with HSPB8 and Hsp70 it transports misfolded proteins to the microtubule organizing center for degradation in autophagosomes. The Pro209 mutants do not merely fail at this — they actively subvert it, relocating CASA-complex chaperones into insoluble aggresomes and trapping ubiquitinylated client proteins there so they cannot be cleared. This was demonstrated for the Pro209 mutant class including p.Pro209Ser, which is why this node is ESTABLISHED rather than inferred from the myopathy allele.

Misfolded Protein Aggregation in Peripheral Neurons
Aggresome formation and client entrapment are directly demonstrated for the Pro209 mutants, so the aggregation itself is not in doubt. What remains PROVISIONAL is specifically the peripheral-neuron step: the aggresome work was done in cell lines, no study has shown aggregates in nerve tissue from a CMT2JJ patient, and the supporting argument that aggregation is what damages the axon leans additionally on the analogy with HSPB1 p.Pro182Leu in CMT2. A closer corroboration than that analogy is that HSPB8 — one of the small heat-shock proteins the Pro209 motif binds directly — itself causes axonal CMT2 when mutated, so the client side of this chaperone relationship is independently established as a peripheral-neuropathy mechanism. The gradual loss of protein homeostasis these mutants produce is the plausible route to axonal degeneration; it has not been observed in the affected tissue.

BAG3-related distal hereditary motor neuronopathy
Overlapping Features A third neuropathy phenotype of the same gene, and the closest differential to CMT2JJ. The truncating variant p.Val505GlyfsTer6 causes an adult-onset, slowly progressive, symmetric distal motor neuronopathy with absent Achilles reflexes and neurogenic changes on biopsy, in a family of eight affected individuals with mean onset at 46 years. The larger European series links nonsense and frameshift BAG3 variants to this distal motor phenotype and to isolated dilated cardiomyopathy, as against the missense IPV-motif alleles of CMT2JJ and myofibrillar myopathy.

notes: >-
  Scope and boundaries. This entry covers CMT2JJ (MONDO:0976227), the
  neuropathy-predominant BAG3 phenotype. It deliberately does not restate the
  BAG3 cardiomyopathy or myofibrillar myopathy pathographs, which are already
  curated elsewhere in this knowledge base — MFM6 as a subtype of
  `Myofibrillar_Myopathy`, and BAG3 dilated cardiomyopathy across several
  `Dilated_Cardiomyopathy` entries. Both are curated here as differential
  diagnoses instead, because distinguishing them is the actual clinical task.

  Do not curate this as a clean allelic partition. It is tempting to write BAG3
  up as one gene with three tidy phenotypes selected by allele. The evidence does
  not support that: p.Pro209Ser and p.Pro209Leu sit at the same codon, and a
  p.Pro209Leu patient has been reported with a CMT-like axonal polyneuropathy
  and no cardiomyopathy. The relationship between allele and phenotype in this
  gene is unresolved and is recorded as an open discussion.

  The lesion is a toxic gain of function, not a loss. This is worth stating
  plainly because the intuitive reading of a co-chaperone disease is
  haploinsufficiency, and it is wrong here: the Pro209 mutants acquire a new
  property, dragging CASA-complex chaperones and ubiquitinylated clients into
  insoluble aggresomes where they cannot be cleared. `functional_impact_category`
  is `GAIN_OF_FUNCTION` accordingly. A practical consequence: therapeutic
  strategies that overexpress wild-type BAG3 to boost autophagy — an active
  literature in other diseases — are not applicable here and must not be curated
  as treatments for this entry.

  Why one node remains PROVISIONAL. Aggresome formation itself is directly
  demonstrated for the Pro209 class including p.Pro209Ser, so the CASA node is
  ESTABLISHED. What is not established is the peripheral-neuron step: the
  aggresome work is cell-line based and no study has examined nerve tissue from a
  CMT2JJ patient. That gap is unusually tractable, since sural nerve biopsy is
  already part of the diagnostic workup.

  A note for anyone running a deep-research report on this disease. The standard
  NEC gene check is blind here: BAG3 is the causal gene for CMT2JJ, MFM6, dilated
  cardiomyopathy and a distal hereditary motor neuropathy alike, so a report that
  has drifted to myofibrillar myopathy will be saturated with "BAG3" and score a
  confident PASS. The discriminator is the substitution, not the gene — ask
  whether the report says Pro209**Ser** or Pro209**Leu**, and treat childhood
  onset, rigid spine or cardiac transplant as signs it is about MFM6.

  Evidence base. Small: two index families, one independently co-segregating
  family, one atypical-phenotype case report, and a review. No animal model, no
  functional study of the p.Pro209Ser allele itself, and no treatment evidence,
  so no treatments or animal_models blocks are curated.
