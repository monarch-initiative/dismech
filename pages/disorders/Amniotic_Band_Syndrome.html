Amniotic Band Syndrome

Congenital MONDO:0015167 Pathograph 29 Show in embeddings browser

Amniotic band syndrome is a sporadic, heterogeneous congenital disruption sequence. In prototypic band-positive cases, amnion rupture or detachment is proposed to expose fibrous strands that tether or constrict fetal parts, producing rings, edema, deformation, impaired distal perfusion, tissue loss, or amputation. Earlier disruption can be associated with severe craniofacial or neural defects, but no single extrinsic mechanism explains every case and no universal genetic cause is established.

Evidence-calibrated clinical definition
A congenital disruption sequence ranging from localized limb constriction rings and distal tissue loss to severe early multisystem defects. Fibrous bands are characteristic when present, but they are not asserted as a universal initiating lesion.
