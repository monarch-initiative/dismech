COX15-Related COX Deficiency

Mendelian MONDO:0014051 Pathograph 29 Show in embeddings browser Mitochondrial Disease Inborn Error of Metabolism

COX15-related COX deficiency is an autosomal recessive mitochondrial disease caused by biallelic variants affecting heme A synthase. Reduced heme A availability impairs complex IV biogenesis and respiratory function. The clinical spectrum includes rapidly fatal neonatal cardioencephalomyopathy and more slowly progressive Leigh syndrome spectrum with developmental impairment, movement disorders and variable cardiac involvement. Residual enzyme activity and assembled complex IV vary between tissues and alleles. Muscle-specific mouse models reveal additional mitochondrial quality-control defects and experimental intervention responses that have not established efficacy in patients.
