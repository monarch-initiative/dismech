16p11.2 Deletion Syndrome

Genetic MONDO:0012756 Pathograph 31 Show in embeddings browser hereditary disease chromosomal disorder

The proximal recurrent 16p11.2 BP4-BP5 deletion is a heterozygous copy-number loss of approximately 600 kb. Reduced dosage of multiple genes produces variable neurodevelopmental, neurologic, and metabolic manifestations. Speech and language impairment and motor coordination difficulties are prominent; most carriers do not meet criteria for intellectual disability. Autism and seizures affect a minority, and obesity commonly develops through childhood. The reciprocal duplication and the distal BP2-BP3 deletion are distinct genomic disorders.
