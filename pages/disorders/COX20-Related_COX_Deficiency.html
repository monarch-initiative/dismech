COX20-Related COX Deficiency

Mendelian MONDO:0033645 Pathograph 55 Show in embeddings browser Mitochondrial Disease Inborn Error of Metabolism

COX20-related mitochondrial complex IV deficiency is an autosomal recessive disorder caused by biallelic COX20 (FAM36A) variants. COX20 stabilizes newly synthesized COX2 and supports its maturation and incorporation into complex IV. Splice defects and protein-destabilizing variants reduce functional COX20. The clinical spectrum includes infantile hypotonia, childhood-onset sensory-predominant neuropathy, sensory or cerebellar ataxia, dysarthria, dystonia, and variable visual, motor and cognitive involvement. Progression can cause loss of independent walking, although severity and tempo vary. Normal lactate, brain MRI, early nerve-conduction studies or fibroblast respiratory assays do not exclude the disorder.
