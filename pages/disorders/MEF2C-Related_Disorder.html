MEF2C-Related Disorder

Mendelian MONDO:0013266 Pathograph 12 Show in embeddings browser Neurodevelopmental Disorder

MEF2C-related disorder is an autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental disorder with developmental delay, intellectual disability, severe speech impairment, hypotonia, seizures, and stereotypic movements. Loss of functional MEF2C can follow coding variants or deletions involving the gene. A separate group of structural variants leaves MEF2C coding sequence intact but disrupts its upstream regulatory domain, including the MEF2C-AS1 long noncoding RNA. These regulatory cases support reduced MEF2C expression, while the relative contributions of enhancer disconnection and lncRNA disruption remain incompletely resolved. Large multigene deletions can add effects from other genes and are not assumed to produce a purely MEF2C-mediated phenotype.
