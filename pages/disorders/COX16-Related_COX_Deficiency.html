COX16-Related COX Deficiency

Mendelian MONDO:0859160 Pathograph 27 Show in embeddings browser Mitochondrial Disease Inborn Error of Metabolism

COX16-related complex IV deficiency is an autosomal recessive mitochondrial disorder associated with loss of the COX16 assembly factor. COX16 assists SCO1 recruitment to newly synthesized COX2 and subsequent joining of the COX2 and COX1 assembly modules. Deficiency reduces COX2 stability, mature complex IV and respiratory capacity. The founding report described neonatal metabolic disease with different subsequent courses: death in the first days of life or progressive cardiac disease with survival into infancy. Clinical evidence remains limited, while patient-cell complementation and engineered-cell studies support the molecular mechanism. Copper rescue in engineered cells did not reproduce in the tested patient fibroblasts.
