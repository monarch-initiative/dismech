TRIM71-Associated_Developmental_Disorder

Mendelian MONDO:0032862 Pathograph 18 Show in embeddings browser Congenital Hydrocephalus

TRIM71-associated developmental disorder (TADD; OMIM 618667, congenital communicating hydrocephalus 1) is an autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental disorder caused mostly by de novo missense variants in TRIM71, the RNA-binding protein encoded by the let-7 microRNA target lin-41. Affected children have variable ventriculomegaly or congenital hydrocephalus, developmental delay, dysmorphic features and other structural brain defects, including corpus callosum dysgenesis and white matter hypoplasia; some also have hearing loss with middle and inner ear malformations. Recurrent arginine substitutions sit at homologous positions of the NHL repeats that bind target mRNAs. In mouse models the patient variant reduces binding to normal targets, neuroepithelial stem cells differentiate too early, the cortex is hypoplastic and mechanically hypercompliant, and the ventricles enlarge secondarily, without a primary defect in cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) production, flow or absorption.

1. Disease information

TRIM71 (LIN41) encodes an E3 ubiquitin ligase and RNA-binding protein. It has a RING, B-box and coiled-coil (RBCC/TRIM) region and a C-terminal NHL β-propeller domain, and it regulates developmental gene expression post-transcriptionally. Heterozygous variants cause a neurodevelopmental syndrome with variable ventriculomegaly, congenital hydrocephalus (CH), developmental delay, dysmorphic features and other structural brain defects, including corpus callosum abnormalities.

Identifiers - OMIM:618667 ("congenital hydrocephalus 4", per PanelApp). - MONDO:0032862 (as given in the task). - Orphanet, ICD-10/11 and MeSH identifiers: not retrieved.

Synonyms: HYDCC1 and TADD (from the task), plus "TRIM71-associated developmental disorders". The 2024 Brain paper coined the name TADD, per the search summaries.

Data source: aggregated, from research exome cohorts and case series rather than EHR.

Key reference - Duy PQ et al., Brain 2024;147(12):4292-4305, PMID:38833623, DOI 10.1093/brain/awae175, PMC11629693. Title: "TRIM71 mutations cause a neurodevelopmental syndrome featuring ventriculomegaly and hydrocephalus."

  • Causal factor: genetic. Heterozygous de novo, and in some families rare inherited, protein-altering TRIM71 variants (Duy 2024).

6. Mechanism / pathophysiology

Causal chain (mouse and cell-line steps are demonstrated; human steps are inferred):

  1. A heterozygous TRIM71 missense variant, usually in the NHL domain, arises de novo or is inherited (human genetics).
  2. The variant impairs binding of TRIM71 to target mRNAs such as CDKN1A, or mislocalizes TRIM71 away from P-bodies (cell lines, Duy 2024).
  3. Loss of post-transcriptional repression leads to premature differentiation of neuroepithelial cells (mouse, Duy 2022).
  4. Premature differentiation results in reduced neurogenesis and cortical hypoplasia (mouse, Duy 2022).
  5. A hypoplastic cortex is hypercompliant, which leads to secondary ventricular enlargement without a primary CSF-circulation defect (mouse and biomechanical modelling, Duy 2022). Applying this to human patients is inferred.
  6. The outcome is ventriculomegaly or hydrocephalus, with corpus callosum and white matter abnormalities and developmental delay (human phenotype). The link from step 5 to the white matter and callosal findings is inferred.
