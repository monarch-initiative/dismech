CDK8-Related Disorder

Mendelian MONDO:0032897 Pathograph 68 Show in embeddings browser Autosomal dominant intellectual disability Neurodevelopmental disorder CDK8-kinase module-associated disorder

CDK8-related disorder is an autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental condition associated primarily with heterozygous kinase-domain missense variants. Most reported variants arose de novo, but transmission from an affected mother is documented. Developmental and speech delay, variable intellectual disability, hypotonia and behavioral difficulties occur with craniofacial differences and variable cardiac, ocular, auditory and gastrointestinal anomalies. Tested variants reduce kinase output while retaining protein stability and module interactions; residual activity differs between alleles. A dominant-negative mechanism has been proposed but not established, and simple haploinsufficiency should not be assumed. Management addresses the individual clinical findings; experimental CDK8/19 inhibition is a developmental perturbation model, not an established therapy for this disorder.
