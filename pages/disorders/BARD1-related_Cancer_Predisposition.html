BARD1-related Cancer Predisposition

Mendelian MONDO:0700267 Pathograph 12 Show in embeddings browser

BARD1-related cancer predisposition is an autosomal dominant susceptibility caused by heterozygous germline loss-of-function variants in BARD1, which encodes the obligate RING-domain heterodimeric partner of BRCA1. The BRCA1-BARD1 heterodimer is an E3 ubiquitin ligase and a mediator of RAD51-dependent homologous recombination. Female breast cancer is the established manifestation, at moderate absolute risk, with enrichment for estrogen-receptor-negative and triple-negative tumors and for earlier onset in familial series. Rare germline loss-of-function variants are also enriched in childhood neuroblastoma, which ClinGen lumps into this gene-disease entity. Ovarian cancer is not part of the entity: ClinGen curates BARD1 and familial ovarian cancer separately and classifies that relationship as Disputed. Some carrier-associated tumors show somatic loss of the remaining allele with a homologous-recombination-deficiency phenotype, while heterozygous loss-of-function cell models already show reduced repair efficiency. Common BARD1 polymorphisms associated with high-risk neuroblastoma in GWAS are a separate, non-Mendelian susceptibility signal.

Two signals must be kept apart. Common BARD1 polymorphisms at 2q35 are associated with high-risk neuroblastoma in GWAS; this is polygenic susceptibility, not this Mendelian entity. Separately, rare germline pathogenic or likely pathogenic BARD1 variants and BARD1-disrupting copy-number variants are enriched in neuroblastoma patients, and ClinGen lumps neuroblastoma into this entity on that case-control evidence. The case-control odds ratios are wide (OR 32.30, 95% CI 6.44 to 310.35 in 786 patients; OR 6.3, 95% CI 2.4 to 13.9 in a pediatric pan-cancer analysis whose cohort overlap with the first study was not assessed here). Absolute childhood penetrance is not reported in these sources, and no source used here gives a neuroblastoma surveillance recommendation for carrier children.

Pathophysiology

5
Germline BARD1 Loss of Function
Mechanism confidence: Established
Heterozygous germline nonsense, frameshift, copy-number and selected missense variants reduce functional BARD1. BARD1 is required for the ubiquitin ligase activity of the BRCA1-BARD1 RING heterodimer; individually, BRCA1 and BARD1 have very low ligase activity in vitro. ClinGen describes the pathogenic mechanism as loss of function with disrupted BRCA1 binding and loss of DNA damage response. In isogenic neuroblastoma and RPE1 cells, single-allele truncating variants were sufficient to reduce repair efficiency (haploinsufficiency).

Somatic BARD1 Second-Hit Inactivation
Mechanism confidence: Provisional
Loss of the wild-type BARD1 allele is documented in a subset of carrier tumors but has not been shown to be universal. In a French case-control study, two of seven interpretable carrier breast tumors (both triple-negative primaries) showed loss of heterozygosity with an HRD phenotype, a metastasis showed LOH without HRD, and non-triple-negative primaries had neither. In a child with a germline BARD1 frameshift and refractory neuroblastoma, mono-allelic expression of the variant and loss of BARD1 protein on immunohistochemistry indicated biallelic loss in the tumor.

6. Mechanism (ordered causal chain)
