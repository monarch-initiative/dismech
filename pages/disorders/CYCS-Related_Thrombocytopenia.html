CYCS-Related Thrombocytopenia

Mendelian MONDO:0012775 Pathograph 27 Show in embeddings browser Inherited thrombocytopenia Mitochondrial Disease

CYCS-related thrombocytopenia (thrombocytopenia 4, THC4) is an autosomal dominant inherited platelet disorder caused by heterozygous CYCS variants, including missense substitutions and an in-frame deletion. Most reported families have isolated mild-to-moderate thrombocytopenia with normal platelet size and little bleeding, although severe platelet-count reduction and neonatal intracranial hemorrhage have been reported. Cytochrome c participates in mitochondrial electron transfer and apoptosome activation. Functional effects differ by variant and experimental system: several variants reduce respiration or enhance apoptosis, whereas Thr20Ile increases respiration and reduces caspase activation in engineered megakaryoblasts. How these effects disrupt platelet production remains incompletely resolved.
