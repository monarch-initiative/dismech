ACAN-Related Short Stature Spectrum

Mendelian MONDO:1060149 Pathograph 21 Show in embeddings browser hereditary disease Skeletal Dysplasia

A dominantly inherited spectrum caused by heterozygous variants in ACAN, the gene encoding aggrecan - the large proteoglycan that gives cartilage its swelling pressure and load-bearing stiffness. Aggrecan is present in both growth-plate and articular cartilage, and that single fact organizes the whole entry: one gene dose produces a growth arm (short stature with accelerated skeletal maturation and early growth cessation) and a joint arm (premature osteoarthritis, osteochondritis dissecans, degenerative disc disease), and which arm brings a family to attention is largely how the historical disease names were assigned. The spectrum was described piecemeal - spondyloepiphyseal dysplasia Kimberley type in one South African kindred, familial osteochondritis dissecans in a five-generation Swedish family, then autosomal dominant short stature with advanced bone age - and only later recognized as one condition. Two mechanisms feed it. Roughly half of reported variants truncate the core protein, so the matrix runs on a reduced dose of normal aggrecan; the rest are missense substitutions that cluster in the C-type lectin repeat of the C-terminal G3 domain, where they impair both secretion and binding to the matrix ligands that anchor aggrecan into the network. This entry is scoped to the heterozygous forms. The biallelic severe extreme, spondyloepimetaphyseal dysplasia aggrecan type, is a MONDO child of the same spectrum term but is curated at depth as its own dismech entry and is deliberately not absorbed here.

Discussions and Knowledge Gaps

5
Is one spectrum entry with two subtypes the right granularity for the dominant ACAN disorders, or should SED Kimberley type and SSOAOD be separate entries?
OPEN QUESTION acan_spectrum_lumping
Attached to
has_subtypes#SEDK has_subtypes#SSOAOD disease#ACAN-Related Short Stature Spectrum
They have separate MONDO terms, separate OMIM numbers and separate historical literatures, which is the usual argument for separate entries. Lumped here for four reasons. One gene, one allelic series, and no genotype-phenotype rule - the largest cohort found no correlation between mutation type and joint disease. One mechanism: the same cohort concluded that different variants impair growth plate chondrogenesis similarly. MONDO itself has since minted a parent class over them. And the boundary is already leaking in the literature: familial osteochondritis dissecans was described as its own entity, is now placed under the SSOAOD OMIM entry, and Sillat et al. simply label their families "fOCD/SSOAOD". What would argue for splitting is a demonstrated mechanistic difference between the two presentations rather than a difference in how they were ascertained - the G3-clustering of osteochondritis-dissecans alleles is the one hint of that, and it is not yet enough.
Do the G3 C-type lectin missense alleles act simply by reducing functional aggrecan dose, or by a distinct mechanism that the haploinsufficiency model does not capture?
CONTROVERSY acan_haploinsufficiency_vs_g3
Attached to
pathophysiology#Aggrecan Deficiency in the Cartilage Extracellular Matrix pathophysiology#Impaired Secretion and Matrix Ligand Binding of G3-Domain Variant Aggrecan pathophysiology#Aggrecan Retention in the Chondrocyte Endoplasmic Reticulum
The cohort argument for a single dosage mechanism is that the growth phenotype does not track the mutation class, and it is a good argument for the growth arm. It sits awkwardly against three findings on the joint arm. First, the osteochondritis-dissecans alleles cluster in the G3 C-type lectin repeat rather than being distributed like the truncating alleles, which is not what pure dosage predicts. Second, the G3 variant proteins lose binding to tenascin-R and the fibulins, so what reaches the matrix is qualitatively different and not merely scarcer. Third, patient-derived chondrocytes retain variant aggrecan in the endoplasmic reticulum, which is a gain-of-dysfunction route with no counterpart in a truncating allele. The observations are not in conflict about the data, only about the summary: a truncating allele and a G3 missense allele can plausibly converge on the growth plate while diverging at the joint. Distinguishing them needs joint outcomes compared between allele classes in a cohort powered for it, which no study has done.
Is variant aggrecan secreted into patient cartilage or not?
CONTROVERSY acan_secretion_versus_presence
Attached to
pathophysiology#Impaired Secretion and Matrix Ligand Binding of G3-Domain Variant Aggrecan pathophysiology#Aggrecan Retention in the Chondrocyte Endoplasmic Reticulum
Three results, and they do not obviously agree. Mass spectrometry of aggrecan purified from patient cartilage found the p.V2303M protein present in the tissue, which was read as excluding a secretion defect. Patient-derived chondrocytes later showed aggrecan accumulating in the endoplasmic reticulum with a marked absence of it from the extracellular matrix. Direct secretion measurement in three further G3 missense families then found reduced - not absent - secretion, for recombinant protein and for full-length aggrecan from patient cartilage alike. The reconciliation the entry adopts is that secretion is reduced rather than abolished, so the protein is detectable in tissue and still under-supplied, and that the in vitro system exaggerates the deficit because differentiating chondrocytes secrete aggrecan far faster than resting ones. That is a plausible reading, not a demonstrated one; nobody has measured aggrecan concentration in patient cartilage against controls.
Does growth hormone, with or without bone age suppression, improve adult height in this condition?
KNOWLEDGE GAP acan_growth_promoting_therapy_evidence
Attached to
treatments#Recombinant Human Growth Hormone treatments#Bone Age Suppression with a GnRH Analogue or Aromatase Inhibitor pathophysiology#Premature Hypertrophic Chondrocyte Maturation and Early Epiphyseal Fusion
Every reported treatment outcome is an intermediate one. Growth hormone series report height SDS gain or growth velocity over one to three years; the gonadotropin-releasing hormone analogue and aromatase inhibitor data report bone age progression halted in three patients between them. No study reports adult height against an untreated comparison, and the one near-adult figure - treated adults at -2.5 SDS against untreated at -3.0 - is a retrospective comparison of five people with sixty-five. The cohort's own authors say a randomised controlled trial would be required. The mechanistic case for combining the two is strong and specific to this disease: the height deficit accrues from early growth plate closure, so an agent that delays closure targets the responsible node while growth hormone only pushes on the compromised one. That is a rationale for a trial, not a substitute for one.
Does the premature hypertrophic maturation seen in aggrecan-null animals actually occur in the growth plate of a human heterozygote?
HUMAN MODEL MISMATCH acan_heterozygous_growth_plate_evidence
Attached to
pathophysiology#Premature Hypertrophic Chondrocyte Maturation and Early Epiphyseal Fusion phenotypes#Accelerated skeletal maturation
The mechanism this entry gives for advanced bone age - abnormal hedgehog, FGF and BMP signalling driving early chondrocyte hypertrophy, hence early vascular invasion and ossification - comes from chick and mouse growth plates lacking aggrecan entirely. Complete aggrecan loss is lethal postnatally in mice, cattle and chick, whereas the human disease is a heterozygous state compatible with a normal lifespan, so the models sit at a different point of the dose-response curve by a wide margin, not a small one. On the human side there is no growth plate histology at all: the evidence is a radiographic bone age and an age at growth cessation. The gap matters for treatment, because bone age suppression is aimed squarely at this node. Resolving it would need growth plate tissue from an affected individual, which is not obtainable, or a heterozygous animal model characterised for maturation timing - which is the tractable route and has not been reported.

Heterozygous ACAN Variants
The trigger, and it is deliberately named for the gene rather than for one lesion, because two mechanistically different allele classes sit under it. Roughly half of reported variants truncate the aggrecan core protein - frameshifts, nonsense alleles and splice-site changes - including the single-base-pair insertion in the exon 12 variable repeat region that founded the series in the SEDK kindred. The remainder are missense substitutions, and these are not scattered: those linked to osteochondritis dissecans cluster in the C-type lectin repeat of the C-terminal G3 domain. The two classes are carried to separate downstream nodes below because what they do to the protein is not the same thing.

Impaired Secretion and Matrix Ligand Binding of G3-Domain Variant Aggrecan
What the C-type lectin missense alleles actually do, and the part of the story that took three studies and changed twice. The G3 C-type lectin repeat is how aggrecan engages the surrounding network - tenascins and fibulins - and the p.V2303M substitution abolishes those interactions in binding assays. Both results stand: the protein is present, and less of it gets out.

Longitudinal bone growth is produced at the growth plate by chondrocyte proliferation, hypertrophy and matrix production, then replacement of that cartilage by bone. Aggrecan is a critical component of the matrix the plate is built from, so a reduced dose degrades the template and the mechanical environment the chondrocytes work in at the same time. This is the growth arm of the spectrum, and it is the same lesion as the joint arm one tissue over.

Premature Hypertrophic Chondrocyte Maturation and Early Epiphyseal Fusion
The step that explains the entry's most counter-intuitive feature. Short stature usually comes with a delayed bone age; here it comes with an advanced one. In aggrecan-deficient chick growth plates the hedgehog, FGF and BMP signalling that paces the plate is abnormal and chondrocytes hypertrophy early, and because hypertrophy is what triggers vascular invasion and ossification, the plate both matures and closes ahead of schedule. Clinically the advanced bone age is therefore a readout of reduced remaining growth potential rather than a separate lesion - which is why these children stop growing at a median of 12 years despite normally timed puberty, and why predicting their adult height from bone age fails.

Differential Diagnoses

5

Conditions with similar clinical presentations that must be differentiated from ACAN-Related Short Stature Spectrum:

Idiopathic short stature
Overlapping Features Not a mimic so much as the label these patients carry until someone sequences them. What should prompt the test is short stature with a normal endocrine workup plus any of brachydactyly, an advanced bone age, a family history of early growth cessation, or a family history of early-onset osteoarthritis.
Spondyloepimetaphyseal dysplasia, aggrecan type
Overlapping Features The biallelic form of the same gene, with severe short stature and a combined spondylo-epi-metaphyseal radiographic picture. Recessive rather than dominant, and curated as its own dismech entry. It is the reason this entry states its heterozygous scope explicitly.
SHOX deficiency disorders
Overlapping Features The other common monogenic cause of mildly disproportionate short stature reached through an endocrine clinic, and the one ACAN is most often tested alongside. Distinguished by Madelung deformity and mesomelia, and by a bone age that is not advanced.
Spondyloepiphyseal dysplasia congenita
Overlapping Features COL2A1-related, and the differential for the SEDK end of the spectrum. Congenital rather than postnatal growth failure, with ocular and hearing involvement that the type II collagenopathies carry and this condition does not.
Sporadic osteochondritis dissecans
Overlapping Features Far commoner than the familial form and usually attributed to repetitive joint loading in an athletic adolescent. Short stature in the patient or the family, or lesions in several joints, is what should raise the familial possibility.

  rationale: >-

    invasion and ossification - comes from chick and mouse growth plates lacking
    aggrecan entirely. Complete aggrecan loss is lethal postnatally in mice, cattle

    from an affected individual, which is not obtainable, or a heterozygous animal
