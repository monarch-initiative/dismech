FASTKD5-Related COX Deficiency

Mendelian MONDO:0980755 Pathograph 25 Show in embeddings browser Mitochondrial Disease Inborn Error of Metabolism

FASTKD5-related COX deficiency (MC4DN24) is an autosomal recessive mitochondrial RNA-processing disorder caused by biallelic FASTKD5 variants. FASTKD5 is an endonuclease required at noncanonical junctions of mitochondrial polycistronic RNA. Impaired CO1 mRNA maturation reduces CO1 translation and complex IV assembly and activity. The founding series describes three unrelated individuals with Leigh syndrome spectrum, ranging from severe infantile disease with fatal decompensation to childhood developmental delay and adult-onset movement and ocular abnormalities. Residual-function patient fibroblasts show predominantly complex IV deficiency, whereas muscle assays and complete-knockout cell models can show broader respiratory-chain abnormalities. The small series does not establish population frequencies or a reliable genotype-based prognosis.
