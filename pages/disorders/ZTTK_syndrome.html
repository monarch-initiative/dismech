ZTTK syndrome

Mendelian MONDO:0014936 Pathograph 31 Show in embeddings browser hereditary disease neurodevelopmental disorder

ZTTK syndrome is a multisystem neurodevelopmental disorder caused by heterozygous loss-of-function variants in SON. The core disease mechanism is SON haploinsufficiency with downstream disturbance of RNA processing and neurodevelopmental programs, producing intellectual disability and broader congenital anomaly phenotypes.

Autosomal dominant inheritance, usually de novo HP:0000006
ZTTK syndrome is autosomal dominant. Almost all probands reported to date whose parents have undergone molecular genetic testing have a de novo pathogenic SON variant. Each child of an affected individual has a 50% chance of inheriting the variant, and prenatal or preimplantation genetic testing is possible once the familial variant is known.
