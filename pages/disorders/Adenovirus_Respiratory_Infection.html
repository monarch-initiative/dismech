Adenovirus Respiratory Infection

Infectious Disease Pathograph 16 Show in embeddings browser Viral Respiratory Infection

Adenovirus respiratory infection is an acute infection caused by human adenoviruses, non-enveloped double-stranded DNA viruses with type-dependent tissue tropism. Most disease in immunocompetent people is mild and self-limited, but adenovirus can cause pharyngitis, conjunctivitis, and lower respiratory infection. Severe pneumonia occurs particularly in young children, crowded congregate settings such as military training, and people with impaired immunity; survivors of severe pediatric pneumonia can develop chronic respiratory sequelae including bronchiolitis obliterans.

Adenoviral DNA Polymerase Activity
Adenoviral DNA polymerase copies the double-stranded DNA genome. The active diphosphorylated metabolite of cidofovir interacts with viral DNA polymerase, making this replication step a pharmacologic vulnerability, although clinical evidence for cidofovir in adenovirus disease remains limited.

Innate Antiviral Response
Intrinsic restriction factors, pattern-recognition receptors, and cytosolic DNA sensors mount an antiviral response to adenovirus and trigger interferon. Adenovirus early proteins and virus-associated RNAs counter several of these defenses, so the net response varies by cell and host context.

Medical Actions

3
Supportive and Respiratory Care
Action: Supportive Care NCIT:C15747
Most respiratory adenovirus infections are self-limited. Severe pneumonia may require supplemental oxygen, ventilatory support, and other organ support; no adenovirus-specific drug is approved.
Show evidence (2 references)
PMID:37476615 SUPPORT Other
"Until now, there is no specific drug for adenovirus pneumonia, so in clinical practice, current treatment comprises antiviral drugs, respiratory support and bronchoscopy, immunomodulatory therapy, and blood purification."
The pediatric review supports respiratory and organ-supportive management in severe disease.
PMID:28114362 SUPPORT Human Clinical
"Most patients in our study were treated empirically with antibiotics, and most HAdV pneumonia cases were self-limiting without an antiviral agent."
This outbreak cohort supports the self-limited course in most immunocompetent patients.
Intravenous Cidofovir for Selected Severe Disease
Action: Pharmacotherapy NCIT:C15986
Agent: cidofovir NCIT:C1600
Intravenous cidofovir is used off label in some transplant centers for severe, progressive, or disseminated adenovirus disease. This is not an approved general treatment for uncomplicated respiratory infection, prospective randomized efficacy data are absent, and nephrotoxicity limits use.
Mechanism Target:
INHIBITS Adenoviral DNA Polymerase Activity — Intracellular cidofovir diphosphate interacts with viral DNA polymerase, and cidofovir inhibits replication across tested adenovirus serotypes in vitro.
Show evidence (2 references)
PMID:8701600 SUPPORT Other
"The mechanism of action of HPMPC is based upon the interaction of its active intracellular metabolite, the diphosphorylated HPMPC derivative HPMPCpp, with the viral DNA polymerase."
This review directly supports the polymerase-target mechanism.
PMID:15865216 SUPPORT In Vitro
"Susceptibility to cidofovir was comparable between species with inhibition of replication of all tested serotypes in a narrow dose range (IC50=17-81 microM)."
This in-vitro study directly supports cidofovir inhibition of adenovirus replication.
Show evidence (2 references)
PMID:30859626 SUPPORT Other
"The use of antiviral therapy is not supported by prospective randomized clinical trials. However, intravenous cidofovir is considered the standard practice for treatment of severe, progressive, or disseminated adenovirus disease in most transplant centers."
Transplant guidance supports only the selected severe-disease use and explicitly records the lack of prospective randomized trials.
PMID:28114362 SUPPORT Human Clinical
"It would be unsafe to offer cidofovir to all HAdV pneumonia patients because of the risk of renal toxicity."
This clinical outbreak report supports restricting cidofovir rather than treating all cases.
Live Oral Adenovirus Type 4 and 7 Vaccine
Action: vaccination MAXO:0001017
In U.S. military recruits, the live oral type 4/type 7 vaccine directly prevented type-4 febrile acute respiratory disease. Type 7 was not expected to circulate during the trial, so its primary endpoint was neutralizing-antibody seroconversion rather than clinical efficacy. This is a targeted prevention strategy, not treatment of established disease.
Show evidence (2 references)
PMID:23623865 SUPPORT Human Clinical
"Vaccine efficacy was 99.3% (95% CI, 96.0-99.9; P<0.001)."
The phase 3 randomized trial directly quantifies efficacy against the ADV-4 febrile-ARD endpoint in military recruits.
PMID:23623865 SUPPORT Human Clinical
"The primary endpoints were prevention of febrile ARD due to ADV-4 and seroconversion of neutralizing serum antibodies to ADV-7, which was not expected to circulate in the study population during the course of the trial."
The trial design distinguishes direct type-4 clinical efficacy from the type-7 immunogenicity endpoint.
