COX6B1-Related COX Deficiency

Mendelian MONDO:0033637 Pathograph 50 Show in embeddings browser Mitochondrial Disease Inborn Error of Metabolism

Autosomal recessive COX6B1-related mitochondrial complex IV deficiency spans neonatal encephalomyopathy with hydrocephalus and cardiomyopathy and childhood-onset progressive cavitating leukodystrophy. Biallelic missense variants impair a nuclear-encoded structural subunit of cytochrome c oxidase. Protein abundance and residual enzyme activity vary by allele and tissue: loss of stable incorporation can occur despite preserved total protein. Human cell models identify both an early role in MT-CO2 maturation or incorporation and a later role in holoenzyme stability. ATP depletion is context dependent, and secondary respiratory-chain defects in complete knockout cells should not be assumed in patients.
