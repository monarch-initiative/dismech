Basel-Vanagaite-Smirin-Yosef Syndrome

Mendelian MONDO:0014643 Pathograph 110 Show in embeddings browser Autosomal recessive intellectual disability Neurodevelopmental disorder Mediator complex-associated disorder

Basel-Vanagaite-Smirin-Yosef syndrome is an autosomal recessive neurodevelopmental disorder associated with biallelic MED25 variants. MED25 encodes a subunit of the Mediator transcriptional coactivator complex. Intellectual and speech impairment accompany variable ocular, brain, cardiac, palatal, skeletal, and growth abnormalities; epilepsy and hypotonia are also reported. The allelic spectrum includes missense, truncating, and splice-altering variants. Tyr39Cys impairs MED25 association with Mediator in a cell-based assay, while specific splice variants alter RNA processing. These results do not establish a single molecular defect for all alleles or a resolved cellular pathway to every clinical manifestation.

Homozygous and compound heterozygous MED25 variants are associated with a variable developmental syndrome. Both missense and truncating alleles have been described, and splice defects have been demonstrated for particular intronic variants. Functional consequences and the strength of pathogenicity evidence differ between alleles.

The c.1965+1dup allele generates intron-16 one-base retention and exon-16 skipping in minigene assays.
