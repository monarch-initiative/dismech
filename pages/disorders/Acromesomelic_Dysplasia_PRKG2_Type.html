Acromesomelic Dysplasia PRKG2 Type

Mendelian MONDO:0030553 Pathograph 28 Show in embeddings browser Skeletal Dysplasia

Acromesomelic dysplasia, PRKG2 type is a rare autosomal recessive skeletal dysplasia caused by biallelic PRKG2 variants that impair cGMP-dependent protein kinase II (cGKII). Reported presentations range from marked acromesomelic shortening and brachydactyly to a predominantly spondylometaphyseal dysplasia without obvious acromesomelia, and an attenuated presentation with moderate short stature. Vertebral, metaphyseal, digital and femoral-neck abnormalities vary between and within families. Human-variant cell assays demonstrate deficient regulation of FGF2-induced RAF/ERK signaling, while rodent experiments implicate impaired hypertrophic chondrocyte differentiation through distinct SOX9 and GSK3β-related routes. These experimental molecular findings have not been measured directly in patient growth plates.
