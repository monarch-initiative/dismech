Menopausal Estrogen Withdrawal Module

Neuroscience Aging Module menopausal_estrogen_withdrawal 7 disorders Pathograph 10
A conserved endocrine module representing the loss of ovarian estrogen output and its consequences for every estrogen-responsive tissue at once. Exhaustion of the ovarian follicle pool removes the granulosa-cell source of estradiol and inhibin B; loss of inhibin B and then estradiol feedback disinhibits pituitary gonadotropin secretion and raises FSH; and the fall in circulating estradiol withdraws estrogen-receptor signalling from bone, myocardium and coronary endothelium, urogenital connective tissue and the hypothalamus simultaneously. That receptor-level withdrawal - "Loss of Estrogen Receptor Signalling in the Target Tissue" - is the key conformance target: upstream the route into the follicle pool differs (lifelong atresia in natural menopause, a genetic or iatrogenic step reduction in premature ovarian insufficiency), and downstream the lesion differs by tissue, but the lost receptor signal is the shared step. The module also carries the hypothalamic KNDy arm, the one consequence whose mechanism is conserved rather than tissue-specific, and with it the neurokinin-3 receptor antagonist drug-target pattern. Conforming disorder entries substitute their own tissue, cell type and lesion while preserving the follicle-pool to receptor-withdrawal chain. Intended conformers: Osteoporosis and Heart_Failure (whose "Postmenopausal Oestrogen Withdrawal" and "Loss of Myocardial Oestrogen Receptor Signalling" nodes correspond to this module's systemic and receptor nodes respectively), Pelvic_Organ_Prolapse (at the systemic node only - see notes), and the premature ovarian insufficiency entries Fragile_X-Associated_Primary_Ovarian_Insufficiency and Premature_Ovarian_Failure_19, in which the identical withdrawal happens decades early. Complementary to, and deliberately not overlapping with, two sibling modules: osteoporosis_bone_resorption owns the RANKL/OPG bone arm downstream of this module, and sex_steroid_driven_proliferation is this module's opposite sign - sustained receptor signalling driving proliferation rather than its withdrawal.

Notes

This is a mechanism module, not a specific disease. Disorder entries reference individual nodes via conforms_to (for example "menopausal_estrogen_withdrawal#Loss of Estrogen Receptor Signalling in the Target Tissue"). Do NOT create a "Menopause" or "Perimenopause" Disease entry. Menopause is a life stage, not a disorder: MONDO has no term for natural menopause or perimenopause, HP files HP:0040406 Perimenopausal under past medical history and offers HP:6000314 Perimenopausal onset and HP:6000315 Postmenopausal onset only as onset modifiers, and GO:0042697 menopause is a phase term GO itself puts in the gocheck_do_not_annotate subset, carrying a comment that it should not be used for direct annotation - so no node in this module binds it, and none should. What is curated as disease is the premature or iatrogenic case (MONDO:0001119 premature menopause) and the organ consequences (MONDO:0008159 postmenopausal osteoporosis, MONDO:0001410 postmenopausal atrophic vaginitis), each of which conforms to this module rather than replacing it. Where the boundary with osteoporosis_bone_resorption falls. That module keeps the whole bone arm - remodeling imbalance, the RANKL/OPG shift, osteoclastogenesis, resorption outpacing formation - and already names estrogen deficiency as one of its upstream drivers. This module supplies that driver and stops there. It carries no bone cell type, no bone process and no bone node, and a curator wanting the bone mechanism should conform to the bone module, not extend this one. The same rule holds for every other tissue: the myocardial fibrosis chain belongs in Heart_Failure, the pelvic matrix chain in Pelvic_Organ_Prolapse. This module ends at the receptor. The hypothalamic KNDy arm is a node here rather than a module of its own, and the reason is the module bar rather than the arm's importance. Arcuate KNDy neurons hypertrophy and raise neurokinin B and kisspeptin expression when estrogen is withdrawn, NKB infusion reproduces flushes in healthy premenopausal women, and NK3R antagonists reduce vasomotor symptoms - a complete chain with its own drug target. But a separate module needs two or more disorders funnelling through a rate-limiting node of its own, and every candidate conformer reaches this arm through the same estrogen withdrawal already modelled here, so it would have one real mechanism and no independent conformers. Keeping it as a node also keeps it where a curator will find it: beside the receptor node it depends on. If vasomotor curation later needs the thermoregulatory steps this node deliberately omits - the narrowed thermoneutral zone, the median preoptic relay, cutaneous vasodilatation and sweating - that is the point to factor a vasomotor module out, and this node is where it would attach. Note also that congenital TACR3 or TAC3 loss of function causes hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, which is not this mechanism and must not be conformed to this node. Iatrogenic withdrawal is in scope, as the same mechanism entered at a different point, but only when it is entered at the follicle pool. Bilateral oophorectomy removes the pool outright and chemotherapy or pelvic radiation destroys it; both are step reductions of exactly the kind the cited follicle-dynamics model treats as advancing the menopausal threshold, the downstream chain from the granulosa node onward is identical, and the international POI guideline covers iatrogenic POI within the same condition. Two caveats a conforming entry must observe. Surgical withdrawal is abrupt and also removes ovarian androgen output, which natural menopause substantially preserves, so a surgical conformer is not simply an accelerated natural one. And GnRH-agonist, aromatase-inhibitor and SERM "medical menopause" does NOT enter at the follicle pool: those agents act centrally on gonadotropin drive, or on peripheral aromatisation, or at the receptor itself, so such an entry conforms at the systemic or receptor node and must not claim the follicle-pool trigger. Not curated as an Xogenesis module. No pathological material anatomical entity is formed anywhere in this chain - the terminal state is the absence of a signal - so no OGMS/MPATH/UBERON lesion anchor applies and none is asserted. Node granularity follows the single-value biological_scale discipline, and two places in this chain needed splitting because of it. The fall in circulating estradiol (ORGANISM) and the loss of receptor occupancy in a target tissue (CELLULAR) are separate nodes, which is also how Heart_Failure splits its two oestrogen nodes and is what lets those two nodes conform here one-to-one. The granulosa secretory failure (CELLULAR) is likewise separate from the follicle-pool exhaustion above it (TISSUE). Spelling: node names and prose here use "estrogen" and "estradiol", matching the GO, HGNC and NCIT labels bound below. Conforming entries written in British English ("oestrogen", "oestradiol" - Heart_Failure and Pelvic_Organ_Prolapse both are) are not inconsistent with the module and need no renaming; both spellings appear in this paragraph so that either one finds the module by search.

Ovarian Follicle Pool Exhaustion
Mechanism confidence: Established
trigger
The non-renewable primordial follicle reserve falls below the number needed to sustain ovulatory cycles. In natural menopause this is the endpoint of lifelong atresia, which accelerates in the final decade of menstrual life; in premature ovarian insufficiency the reserve is established normally and then lost faster, or is reduced in a step by oophorectomy, chemotherapy or radiation. The route in is the main substitution a conforming entry makes; the state this node describes - an ovary with essentially no recruitable follicles - is the same in every case.

Loss of Granulosa-Cell Estradiol and Inhibin B Output
Mechanism confidence: Established
amplifier
Granulosa cells of growing follicles aromatise androgen to estradiol and secrete inhibin B, and both outputs fall with the size of the recruitable cohort. The two are listed on one node because they are products of the same cell population lost together, but they are not lost on the same schedule: inhibin B secretion is already reduced in older ovulatory women whose estradiol is still normal or high, which is why the feedback arm downstream moves years before the systemic-estradiol arm does.

Hypergonadotropic Feedback Disinhibition
Mechanism confidence: Established
effector
Pituitary gonadotropes lose the negative feedback that ovarian inhibin B and estradiol normally impose, and FSH rises disproportionately to LH. This is a parallel arm, not a step on the way to the tissue consequences: the hypergonadotropic state is what makes the endocrine diagnosis, and the sequence of the two cohort-measured curves tells the mechanism apart from a purely estradiol-driven one - FSH begins to rise about six years before the final menstrual period, while mean estradiol does not move until about two years before it, so the early disinhibition is attributable to inhibin B rather than to estradiol. No node here asserts a direct action of FSH on bone or any other peripheral tissue; that hypothesis is outside this module's scope and is not curated as an edge.

Loss of Estrogen Receptor Signalling in the Target Tissue
Mechanism confidence: Established
central effector
Estrogen receptor alpha and beta in a responsive cell population lose their ligand, and the transcriptional and membrane-initiated programmes they maintain fall away. This is the rate-limiting, tissue-agnostic step and the key conformance target of the module: above it the route to hormone loss differs by disorder, below it the lesion differs by tissue, and this is the one step every conformer shares. The receptors themselves are intact and ligand-competent, so the activity is reduced rather than absent or altered - which is also what makes the withdrawal pharmacologically reversible in principle. Which cell population is the substrate is the main tissue-specific substitution: cardiac fibroblast and cardiac endothelial cell in Heart_Failure's "Loss of Myocardial Oestrogen Receptor Signalling", the osteoblast and osteocyte lineage for the bone arm that osteoporosis_bone_resorption owns, vaginal and urogenital connective tissue cells, and the arcuate KNDy neurons of the node below. No cell type is bound on this node, deliberately, because naming one would fix the choice the module exists to leave open.

Hypothalamic KNDy Neuron Disinhibition
Mechanism confidence: Established
effector
KNDy neurons of the arcuate (infundibular) nucleus, which co-express kisspeptin, neurokinin B and dynorphin and are normally restrained by estradiol, hypertrophy and raise neurokinin B and kisspeptin expression once that restraint is gone. This is the one consequence arm whose mechanism is conserved rather than tissue-specific, which is why it sits in the module while the bone, myocardial and urogenital arms do not. The node is confined to the neuronal state; the thermoregulatory output of that state - a narrowed thermoneutral zone, cutaneous vasodilatation, sweating - is a whole-organism process at a different scale and is curated as a phenotype in the conforming entry, not bundled in here.

Convergent Multi-System Estrogen Deficiency
Mechanism confidence: Established
consequence
The clinical state of estrogen deficiency, affecting bone, the cardiovascular system, the urogenital tract, thermoregulation and the central nervous system at once. What this node asserts is only the convergence - that one withdrawn signal produces a multi-system picture rather than a single-organ lesion, which is the reason this module exists instead of four unrelated upstream nodes. It is not the place to curate any of those consequences: each tissue's lesion belongs in the conforming disease entry, and the bone arm belongs in osteoporosis_bone_resorption. Conformers normally attach at the receptor node above, not here.

Agent: fezolinetant NCIT:C171845 NCI Thesaurus (NCIT) Relation: this treatment uses this therapeutic agent This treatment uses fezolinetant (NCIT:C171845). NCIT:C171845 is a therapeutic agent from the NCI Thesaurus.
Platform: Small molecule
Blocking NK3R on the disinhibited KNDy population treats the vasomotor arm without replacing the withdrawn hormone. This is the module's drug-target design pattern, and it is a strong test of the KNDy node: the antagonist acts nowhere else in the chain - the follicle pool, the gonadotropin rise and the systemic estradiol fall are all untouched - so symptomatic benefit localises the mechanism to that node. It also makes the arm treatable where estrogen is contraindicated, which is why agents of this class are used for withdrawal produced by endocrine therapy for breast cancer. The therapeutic agent recorded here is fezolinetant, a selective NK3R antagonist. Elinzanetant, cited below for the endocrine-therapy population, is a dual neurokinin-1/neurokinin-3 antagonist rather than a second selective NK3R agent, so its benefit supports this target without isolating it; the trials cited do not separate the NK1 contribution.
Mechanism target:
INHIBITS Hypothalamic KNDy Neuron Disinhibition — The antagonist blocks NK3R-mediated transmission from the disinhibited KNDy neurons, suppressing this node's output while leaving the upstream hormonal state unchanged.
