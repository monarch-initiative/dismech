Normal function

ai-gene-review gives MSMO1's core molecular function as C-4 methylsterol oxidase activity, acting in cholesterol biosynthetic process. MSMO1 is the oxidative half of sterol C4-demethylation: a non-heme di-iron enzyme of the endoplasmic reticulum membrane that oxidises a C4 methyl group to a carboxylate, which NSDHL then removes.
