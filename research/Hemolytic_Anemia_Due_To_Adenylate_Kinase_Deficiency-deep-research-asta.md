    > Adenylate kinase (AK) is ubiquitously present across various organisms, serving as a vital monomeric enzyme essential for normal cellular growth, differentiation, motility, and metabolic processes in living organisms [1]. As a pivotal enzyme in the regulation of cellular adenine nucleotide homeostasis, AK modulates the cell cycle, proliferation, and energy distribution through the AK→adenosine monophosphate (AMP)→AMP-activated protein kinase signaling cascade [2].
