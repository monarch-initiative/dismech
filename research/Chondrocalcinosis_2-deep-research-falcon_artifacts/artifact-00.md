| Two U.S. CCAL2 pedigrees with autosomal-dominant CPPD | Across three generations, 9 relatives were clinically affected and 15 were unaffected. All affected relatives were heterozygous for the **ANKH p.Pro5Thr (P5T)** variant; three younger carriers had not yet developed CPPD. The variant was absent from 204 control alleles. In US-A, recurrent pseudogout began in the third decade; in US-B, approximately half of affected relatives had acute attacks superimposed on chronic joint disease. (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000067) | Primary, genotype-specific human evidence for CCAL2. The young asymptomatic carriers support age-dependent expression and preclude an inference of complete penetrance from this cross-sectional study. The sample was too small to estimate population prevalence or variant penetrance precisely. | [10.1002/art.11133](https://doi.org/10.1002/art.11133), 2003 |
| 2023 ACR/EULAR CPPD classification validation cohort | The validation cohort included 251 definite CPPD cases and 162 mimickers. The complete framework—entry and exclusion rules, sufficient criteria, and a score **>56** when needed—had **99.2% sensitivity and 92.5% specificity**. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000064, pqac-00000065) | Validated for symptomatic CPPD generally, not specifically for genetically confirmed CCAL2.
