# African Histoplasmosis Deep Research Note

## Scope and source mode

No external deep-research provider was used for this follow-up pass. This local
Codex review re-read the cached African histoplasmosis sources to check whether
the first curation missed severity, dissemination, host-context, diagnosis, and
phenotype-frequency evidence.

## Cached sources re-read

- PMID:32887981 — a 1993-2019 review of 94 well-documented Hcd cases.
- PMID:33956817 — a Republic of Congo literature review dominated by African
  histoplasmosis.
- PMID:18217546 — a review and case histories for Hcd infections in people with
  HIV.
- PMID:15538817 — a classic African histoplasmosis review.
- PMID:30334708 — the Kimpese, Democratic Republic of the Congo, HIV-negative
  case series.

## Findings added to the entry

The cached sources extend the initial localized skin/bone/lymph-node curation
in three directions:

- **Dissemination** — PMID:33956817 defines disseminated Hcd infection as at
  least two noncontiguous body sites and reported disseminated infection in a
  majority of Republic of Congo Hcd cases with classifiable extent. PMID:32887981
  reported disseminated forms in 60.6% of the 1993-2019 cases.
- **Severity** — PMID:32887981 reported 23.4% mortality by the end of follow-up
  across the 94 recent well-documented cases.
- **HIV host context** — PMID:32887981 reported 20.8% HIV co-infection, and
  PMID:18217546 found that most well-described HIV-infected Hcd cases had poor
  immunologic status and disseminated infection.

PMID:32887981 also supports a progression note for long latency, because some
patients became symptomatic decades after leaving an endemic area.

## Phenotype and diagnostic updates

PMID:33956817 provides frequencies for the dominant Congo presentations: skin
lesions, lymph nodes, and bone lesions. Fever was added from the 1993-2019 case
review as a feature differentiating HIV-coinfected cases from cases in people
living without HIV.

The histopathology block keeps the 7-15 µm size range by adding the Kimpese
immunohistochemistry paragraph, while the existing 10-15 µm paragraph continues
to support routine histopathology and fungal stains. Diagnosis remains centered
on histopathology because the classic review explicitly called for a specific
serologic test rather than describing one as established.

## Treatment update

The amphotericin B / itraconazole row was softened. PMID:32887981 supports
these as the best therapeutic alternatives, and PMID:18217546 explains that
severity-based management in Hcd is extrapolated from histoplasmosis due to
variety capsulatum because Hcd clinical trials have not been performed.
