| Prognosis | **Broad-LVNC fact:** outcomes are driven more by LVEF than trabeculation burden. Across 2,501 patients, cardiovascular mortality was 1.92 and ventricular arrhythmia 2.17 per 100 person-years. **PRDM16-specific:** deletion was associated with death, transplant, or VAD (p=0.04).
