Species-specific name: Renal cancer syndrome, nodular dermatofibrosis and kidney disease, renal cysts and nodular dermatofibrosis

Species-specific symbol: RCND

Inheritance: A pedigree analysis by Lium and Moe (1985) implicated autosomal dominant inheritance, which was confirmed by Moe et al. (1998).

Molecular basis: The first causal mutation was discovered via a comparative positional cloning approach. Building on the mapping results described in the Mapping section above, Lingaas et al. (2003) narrowed the candidate region homologous to human HSA17p, which includes the BHD gene, mutations in which cause Birt–Hogg–Dubé syndrome, which is very similar to the canine disorder. Sequencing of the canine BHD gene showed that this disorder in German Shepherd dogs is due to an A>G base substitution in exon 7 of the BHD (folliculin) gene (omia.variant:77), leading to a H255R amino-acid substitution in a highly conserved region. Using genetic variant nomenclature as of 2015, the causative variant would be denoted as c.764A>G or p.His255Arg. The previous gene symbol BHD has changed to FLCN.

Schneiter et al. (2026) performed illumina short-read whole genome sequencing of a Chihuahua with renal cysts and nodular dermatofibrosis. Investigation of the FLCN candidate gene revealed a heterozygous deletion of 9092 bp (omia.variant:1934) haboring five coding exons of FLCN. As haploinsufficiency for FLCN has been shown to cause Birt–Hogg–Dubé syndrome in humans and RCND in dogs, the authors proposed this deletion as candidate causal variant in the studied dog.

Clinical features: As summarised by Lingaas et al. (2003), "The disease is characterized by bilateral, multifocal tumors in the kidneys, uterine leiomyomas and nodules in the skin consisting of dense collagen fibers". Ciccarelli et al. (2019) reported a case of "Unilateral renal cystadenocarcinoma and nodular dermatofibrosis in a mixed-breed dog carrying . . . [the p.His255Arg] FLCN gene mutation".

Breeds: Australian Shepherd (Dog) (VBO_0200095), Beagle (Dog) (VBO_0200131), Bichon Frise (Dog) (VBO_0200163), Boxer (Dog) (VBO_0200210), Chihuahua (Dog) (VBO_0200338), German Shepherd Dog (Dog) (VBO_0200577), Golden Retriever (Dog) (VBO_0200610), Mixed Breed (Dog) (VBO_0200902).
