HP ID: HP:0005584
Label: Renal cell carcinoma
Definition: Renal cell carcinoma (also known as hypernephroma) is the most common form of kidney cancer arising from the proximal renal tubule. The tissue of origin for renal cell carcinoma is the proximal renal tubular epithelium. Renal cell carcinoma can be classified as 1) clear cell carcinoma, 2) papillary carcinoma, 3) chromophobe renal carcinoma, and 4) collecting duct carcinoma. A type of carcinoma of the kidney with origin in the epithelium of the proximal convoluted renal tubule.
Synonyms: Hypernephroma; Renal carcinoma; Cancer starting in small tubes in kidneys

HP ID: HP:0011798
Label: Renal oncocytoma
Definition: Renal oncocytoma is seen in Birt-Hogg-Dube syndrome (OMIM 135150). A renal tumor originating from an oncocyte, which is an epithelial cell characterized by an excessive amount of mitochondria, resulting in an abundant acidophilic, granular cytoplasm.
