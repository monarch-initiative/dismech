1: Birt-Hogg-Dube Syndrome
Autosomal dominant neoplastic syndrome characterised by genodermatosis, lung
cysts, spontaneous and recurrent PNEUMOTHORAX; and RENAL CANCER. It is associated
with mutations in the folliculin protein gene (FLCN protein).
Year introduced: 2011
