Oncocytomas are benign epithelial tumors that are also rare. Recently, there have been a few case reports of BHD with a parotid oncocytoma that appears to have a BHD phenotype. Here we document the eighth known case and describe the magnetic resonance imaging features of the parotid oncocytoma, which mimicked Warthin's tumor. Radiologists should be aware of the association between these rare disorders.

Birt-Hogg-Dubé syndrome (BHD) is a rare autosomal dominant disease characterized by skin fibrofolliculomas, pulmonary cysts, spontaneous pneumothoraces, and renal cancers. Oncocytomas are benign epithelial tumors that are also rare.

It is difficult to distinguish between oncocytomas and Warthin's tumors; however, radiologists should be aware of this association and consider parotid oncocytoma as a differential diagnosis if they detect a parotid mass similar to Warthin's tumor in BHD. If additional imaging is to be recommended, a dedicated renal-protocol MRI would be the choice, alone or in addition to chest CT.
