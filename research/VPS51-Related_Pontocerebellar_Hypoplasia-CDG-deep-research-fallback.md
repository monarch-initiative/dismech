## Evidence Scope Used For Curation

- PMID:30624672 for the original biallelic VPS51 case, reduced assembled
  GARP/EARP complexes, mannose-6-phosphate receptor redistribution, lysosomal
  swelling, and the clinical phenotype including developmental delay,
  microcephaly, hypotonia, epilepsy, cortical visual impairment,
  pontocerebellar abnormalities, failure to thrive, liver dysfunction, lower
  extremity edema, and dysmorphic features.

  fibroblast proteomics showing vesicular, lysosomal, and mitochondrial pathway
  disruption, and additional phenotypes including thin corpus callosum, severe
  intellectual disability, epilepsy, microcephaly, hearing loss, and dysphagia.
- PMID:34161137 for the mechanistic link between GARP disruption and abnormal
  cellular and serum glycoprotein glycosylation.

## Curation Conclusions

The supported model is biallelic VPS51 dysfunction causing combined GARP/EARP
tethering-complex deficiency. Patient-cell evidence supports impaired
endosome-derived cargo sorting, lysosomal abnormalities, and downstream Golgi
glycoprotein processing defects. The curated phenotype set should include the
core neurologic and growth features plus sensory, hepatic, edema, dysmorphic,
hearing, and swallowing features reported in the cached literature.
