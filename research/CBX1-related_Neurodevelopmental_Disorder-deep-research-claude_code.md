## 6. Mechanism and pathophysiology
Ordered causal chain, with each step marked as demonstrated or inferred:
1. A de novo missense variant arises in the *CBX1* chromodomain (demonstrated by sequencing).
2. The mutant HP1β can no longer bind repressive histone marks or localize to heterochromatin (demonstrated in cell assays, per the summary).
3. Because HP1β dimerizes, the mutant protein sequesters wild-type HP1β and other HP1 proteins, so one mutant allele leads to loss of function beyond 50% (**inferred**; the authors' proposal).
4. Facultative heterochromatin becomes dysfunctional, chromatin opens, and H3K27me3-marked genes are derepressed (demonstrated in patient lymphoblastoid cells; not shown in neural tissue).
5. Aberrant neurodevelopmental gene regulation leads to developmental delay, hypotonia and autistic features (**inferred**; no neuronal data found).

A possible branch involves the mouse *Cbx1* chromodomain mutant, which showed longer latency-to-peak responses. The authors suggest synaptic delay or myelination deficits. This is hypothesis-level.
