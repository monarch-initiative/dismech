# OLIDA cross-walk against kb/disorders

Generated by `scripts/olida_crosswalk.py`. Regenerate rather than hand-editing.

Resolves the `digenic_grouping_olida_crosswalk` proposed experiment on
`kb/groupings/Digenic_and_Oligogenic_Disorders.yaml`.

**Name matching is a screen, not a verdict.** OLIDA's disease vocabulary is
ICD-10-flavoured and does not align term-for-term with MONDO, so a match is a
lead to confirm against the OLIDA entry and its literature, and an absent match
is not proof that no dismech entry covers the concept.

**A high score is not a membership argument.** The score rates the
evidence behind the variant COMBINATION, not the claim that the DISEASE
requires two loci. Cystinuria sits at the maximum score here and is
correctly a non-member: the International Cystinuria Consortium
(PMID:15635077) found the digenic type AB genotype in two of 164 families
and reported it raising aminoaciduria values rather than causing the
stone-forming disease. Read the primary literature before binding a term;
the rule is requirement, not severity.

Score floor: OLIDA `final_metascore` >= 0 (0-3; higher is stronger
genetic plus functional evidence).

`Matched on` is how the pairing was made and how much it is worth:
`omim` is strongest, `name` is an entry-name hit, and `synonym`/`subtype`
frequently mean the OLIDA concept is a sibling or a part of that entry
rather than the entry itself - check those before acting on them.
