## Curation Conclusions

The supported model is biallelic GOLGA2/GM130 loss impairing cis-Golgi
organization, intracellular protein transport, secretion/sorting, and
microtubule-related developmental functions. Human and zebrafish evidence
support a neurodevelopmental and neuromuscular phenotype with microcephaly,
seizures, developmental delay, hypotonia, strabismus, thin corpus callosum,
failure to thrive, myopathy or muscular dystrophy, and elevated creatine kinase.
