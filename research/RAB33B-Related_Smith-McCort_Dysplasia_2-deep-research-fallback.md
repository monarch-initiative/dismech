## Curation Conclusions

The supported model is biallelic RAB33B dysfunction causing a Golgi Rab GTPase
defect with Golgi mislocalization, reduced protein stability, impaired
autophagy-associated membrane trafficking, and downstream osteoclast/protein
glycosylation abnormalities. The phenotype should focus on
spondylo-epi-metaphyseal skeletal dysplasia and short stature while preserving
the reported distinction that classic Smith-McCort dysplasia has normal
cognition and no microcephaly.
