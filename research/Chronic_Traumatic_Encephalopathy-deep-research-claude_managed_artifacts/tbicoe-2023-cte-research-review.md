TDP-43 pathology is transmembrane protein 106b (TMEM106B). Exploratory genetic analysis by Bieniek et al. (2015) revealed a lower proportion of minor allele homozygotes with CTE compared to those without CTE pathology (Bieniek et al., 2015).
