# ICD-10-CM F07.81: Postconcussional syndrome - Billable: Yes; valid on HIPAA claims October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027 (FY 2027) - Classification: Mental and behavioural disorders > Mental disorders due to known physiological conditions (F01-F09) > Personality and behavioral disorders due to known physiological condition (F07)

## Also documented as - Chronic traumatic encephalopathy - Late effect of traumatic injury to brain - Postconcussion state - Postconcussion syndrome - Post-concussional personality disorder - Traumatic encephalopathy

F07.81 is a billable ICD-10-CM diagnosis code for postconcussional syndrome. It is valid on HIPAA claims for fiscal year 2027 (October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027) and groups to MS-DRG 102 through 103. Coders also document this condition as chronic traumatic encephalopathy.
